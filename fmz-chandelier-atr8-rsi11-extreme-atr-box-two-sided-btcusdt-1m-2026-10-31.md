## Signal — Gate 1, fully pinned derivative

Index completed UTC minute bars `t = 0, 1, ...` with real Binance BTC-USDT futures `O[t], H[t], L[t], C[t]` (`V[t]` is never read — no volume input of any kind). All inputs pinned at source defaults: `ATR 8 / ATR multiplier 3 / RSI 11 / oversold 20 / overbought 80 / stop 2×ATR / take-profit 1×ATR` (any other values are a different, unpinned rule).

- **ATR `A[t]` (t ≥ 7):** Wilder-RMA ATR over 8 bars (Pine `ta.atr` — recursive: needs the 8-bar true-range seed plus convergence margin; `na` before index 7). True range uses `H/L/C` only, never volume.
- **Chandelier lines (t ≥ 7):** `CE_LONG[t] = max(H[t−7..t]) − 3 × A[t]`; `CE_SHORT[t] = min(L[t−7..t]) + 3 × A[t]` (exact 8-bar extremum windows, no seed state beyond availability; `na` before index 7).
- **RSI `R[t]` (t ≥ 10):** Wilder-RMA RSI over 11 closes (Pine `ta.rsi` — recursive: needs the 11-bar seed plus convergence margin; `na` before index 10).
- **Edge triggers (Pine v5 `ta.crossover`/`ta.crossunder` — strictly one-bar edges):** gold edge `G[t] = C[t−1] <= CE_LONG[t−1] AND C[t] > CE_LONG[t]`; death edge `D[t] = C[t−1] >= CE_SHORT[t−1] AND C[t] < CE_SHORT[t]`.
- **Full predicates (t ≥ 10 by construction, actionable only t ≥ 70 — see warmup):** long predicate `LONG[t] = G[t] AND R[t] < 20`; short predicate `SHORT[t] = D[t] AND R[t] > 80` (file-literal two-conjunct predicates on both sides — breakout edge plus RSI extreme, no third conjunct of any kind). A single bar cannot print both (gold and death edges against opposite lines with opposite RSI rails are mutually exclusive — disclosed).
- **Latched box legs (fixed at the signal bar's close and ATR, never moved):** on a long signal at bar `s`, `STOP_LONG = C[s] − 2 × A[s]`, `TP_LONG = C[s] + 1 × A[s]`; on a short signal, `STOP_SHORT = C[s] + 2 × A[s]`, `TP_SHORT = C[s] − 1 × A[s]` (2×/1×ATR are ATR-unit distances, never ticks or percents; levels are tickSize-snapped at 0.10 — the record's sole precision pin).
