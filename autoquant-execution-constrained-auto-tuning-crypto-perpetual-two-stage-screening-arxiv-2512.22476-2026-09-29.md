## Economic mechanism

### Source-reported

- The paper's central claim is an **evaluation-validity claim, not a return premium**. Its stated narrow question is: "under a strict 4-hour perpetual-futures protocol, does execution-aware, double-screened configuration selection reduce the performance inflation and parameter fragility that commonly arise under naive one-stage tuning and simplified costs?"
- The source positions AutoQuant as a **configuration-selection and traceability protocol for a pre-specified signal family** over four liquid perpetual contracts (BTC/USDT anchor, plus ETH/USDT, SOL/USDT, AVAX/USDT as bounded portability checks). It is explicit that "the emphasis is not on inventing a new signal family" and that the contribution "is methodological and architectural".
- **Four stated contributions (Section 1):** (1) formulating configuration selection as an auditable expert-system-style decision-support problem in which execution semantics, funding visibility, trading costs and feasibility constraints are encoded as explicit system rules; (2) a policy-driven pipeline combining Bayesian search with a two-stage double-screening protocol under strict `t+1` execution semantics and explicit cost-mis-specification scenarios; (3) a traceability layer of deterministic machine-readable artifacts, accounting-invariant checks and offline-to-live consistency mechanisms; (4) a BTC/USDT anchor case plus three cross-asset replications showing how the framework "exposes cost-driven performance inflation, window fragility, and residual overfitting risk under a unified strict protocol".
- **Stated knowledge base (Table 2):** STRICT4H execution semantics; funding alignment; cost/constraint profiles; screening policies - "declarative rules that prevent look-ahead and enforce feasibility; explicit cost-scenario grid and window semantics". Inference engine = Stage I Bayesian optimization (TPE) plus Stage II policy-driven double-screening. Explanation interface = deterministic exports, accounting invariants, full-chain consistency checks. Governance overlay = guard triggers plus invariant monitoring with ok/watch/kill decisions.
- **Stated headline empirics (abstract):** "fee-only and zero-cost backtests materially inflate apparent performance relative to fully costed runs with funding and slippage"; "two-stage screening does not guarantee higher returns"; in the BTC anchor case and several replications it "more often surfaces lower-drawdown or less extreme alternatives under identical strict semantics"; "the experiments use small-account simulations under linear costs and exclude market impact and institutional capacity constraints".
- **Stated scope limit:** the cross-asset results "serve as portability checks for the validation protocol rather than broad market validation", and Stage (iii) (live-style replay) "is treated as a traceability pattern rather than as evidence of long-horizon live performance".

### Research interpretation

- Falsifiable restatement of the source's hypothesis: **under a fixed 4-hour perpetual-futures signal family and a fixed search budget, (a) accounting for fees only, or for no costs at all, inflates measured CAGR and Sharpe relative to an accounting that also charges slippage and the realised funding stream; (b) selecting the single best in-sample trial (one-stage) produces a configuration that is fragile on held-out windows, so a two-stage screen that filters on held-out rolling windows and a cost-scenario grid selects a configuration with shallower drawdowns and lower switch density; (c) enforcing strict `t+1` execution and carry-forward-only funding visibility is not cosmetic - it changes which configurations look viable.**
- Mechanism roles, stated so each can be ablated independently:
  - **Regime / policy layer:** strict `t+1` execution (signal at bar close affects PnL only from the next bar) plus no-look-ahead funding alignment (only funding values visible at each bar timestamp, carried forward, never backfilled).
  - **Primary signal:** a normalised fast/slow EMA divergence on the 4-hour close, squashed to [0,1] with a `tanh` transform, blended with an auxiliary mean-reversion/breakout channel.
  - **Confirmation / smoothing:** signal pre-averaging, an ADX/regime gate, and confirmation plus turnover controls (named in the Table 23 module ablation).
  - **Risk / exit block:** minimum holding bars, post-exit cooldown, ATR stop-loss and take-profit multipliers, account exposure cap, and a funding-bias entry-threshold escalator.
  - **Selection layer:** Stage I Bayesian search on one training window, Stage II double screening over held-out rolling windows crossed with a cost-mis-specification grid, then a fixed ex-ante filter.
- The economic channel the source invokes for the *inflation* half of the claim is **cost and turnover drag plus selection bias**: repeated search over configurations on one window picks candidates whose in-sample edge is partly luck, and simplified cost models hide how much of the gross edge the friction consumes. The channel invoked for the *look-ahead* half is that same-bar execution lets a decision see the bar it is priced on.
- **Where the source's own evidence does not support its stated mechanism:** the funding-aware rules (a headline knowledge-base item) ablate to approximately zero effect (contradiction 4), and the same-bar (`t+0`) execution convention does **not** systematically inflate performance in the reported snapshot (Table 26/27: median uplift -0.018, only 36.7% of trials positive). Both are recorded as negative evidence rather than silently dropped.
- Competing explanations the source does not exclude: the protocol's benefit may be **risk-control selection rather than alpha selection** (the two-stage rule never wins on return); the cost-inflation result is measured on one deliberately chosen stress-test configuration; window effects (2021-2022 drawdown regime) may dominate selection; the linear cost model itself may understate real friction.
- This is **already crypto evidence** (USDT-denominated perpetual contracts on centralised exchanges), so it is a native crypto result rather than a port. See Crypto portability.

## Signal

The source does publish an explicit signal form for the anchor family, but it declares parts of it implementation-specific. What is printed:

- **Formation timestamp:** 4-hour bars indexed by **UTC bar-open timestamp**; the signal is computed at the **close of bar t** from information available at that time, and under STRICT4H it "can affect position and profit-and-loss only from bar t+1 onward". Same-bar execution is prohibited by construction.
- **Signal equations (Section 2.2, equations 1-3):**
  - momentum block: `m_t(theta_momentum) = 0.5 * (1 + tanh((EMA_fast,t - EMA_slow,t) / (sigma(theta_momentum) * EMA_slow,t)))`
  - blend: `C_t = w_mom * m_t + (1 - w_mom) * a_t`, where `a_t` is the auxiliary (mean-reversion / breakout) channel
  - position rule: `Signal_t = +1 if C_t >= tau_long`, `-1 if C_t <= 1 - tau_short`, `0 otherwise`
- **Funding-bias entry threshold (equation 4):** `tau_long(t) = tau_base + kappa * max(|fr_t| - theta_bias/10000, 0)`, where `fr_t` is the 8-hour funding rate carried forward onto the 4-hour grid, `theta_bias` is the activation threshold and `kappa` the slope.
- **Hyperparameter space (Table 3, "Range (default)", Stage I Optuna bounds):** `ema_fast` 6-32 (12); `ema_slow` 20-96 (26), required longer than `ema_fast`; `ema_threshold` 0.0005-0.0060 (0.003) as the entry threshold on the EMA spread; `bb_period` 10-30 (20); `bb_dev` 1.0-2.5 (2.0); `min_hold_bars` 1-6 (1); `cooldown_hours` 0-8 (0); `atr_period` 0-30 (0, 0 disables ATR exits); `atr_k_sl` 0-3 (0) and `atr_k_tp` 0-5 (0); `max_exposure_abs` 0-5 (0); `funding_bias_thr_bps` 0-10 (0); `funding_bias_k_thr_per_bps` 0-0.005 (0). Defaults are fallback values, not tuned results.
- **Stage I search (Section 2.3):** Tree-structured Parzen Estimator via Optuna, **N_opt = 40 TPE trials** on the BTC training window (120 trials for the auxiliary volatility-momentum family; 60-evaluation budgets in the same-budget optimizer benchmark); scalar objective `J(theta) = r_ann`, the cost-adjusted annualised net return under the baseline cost profile; "risk and fragility controls are instead enforced in Stage II as explicit, auditable policy constraints".
- **Stage II double screening (Section 2.4):** the fixed Stage I pool is re-evaluated with **no re-optimisation** on (i) held-out rolling windows of **2,000 bars stepped forward by 500 bars**, and (ii) a cost-mis-specification grid `taker_bps in {3,4,6}` x `fund_mult in {0.5,1.0,1.5}` = **9 scenarios**. In the reproducible BTC snapshot this is 9 scenarios x top 40 candidates over the combined Training+Validation window of **7,262 4-hour bars = 360 strict backtest runs**, plus long-horizon and blind hold-out stress evaluations.
- **Ex-ante stable-candidate filter (Table 7):** `mean_monthly_true >= 0.005` (mean monthly geometric return across scenarios), `min_monthly_true >= 0.0` (worst-scenario monthly geometric return), `maxDD_mean <= 0.30`, `switch_density_mean <= 0.12`; survivors are then ranked lexicographically by mean monthly geometric return descending and mean MaxDD ascending, and the **top K = 5** are retained. The source states these rules "are not tuned post hoc on a per-experiment basis".
- **Window definitions (Section 4, BTC/USDT, half-open intervals):** Training `2019-09-08 - 2021-01-01` (Stage I only); Validation `2021-01-01 - 2023-01-01` (Stage II selection, not Stage I); Long-horizon `2019-09-08 - 2025-10-14` (diagnostics and cost stress only); Blind post-ETF `2024-01-01 - 2025-10-14` (post-hoc only); the interval `2023-01-01 - 2024-01-01` is "intentionally left unused". ETH windows: train `2021-12-31 - 2023-01-01`, validation `2023-01-01 - 2024-01-01`, hold-out `2024-01-01 - 2025-11-24`; SOL/AVAX use the same definitions with hold-out through `2025-12-16`.
- **Holding period / cadence:** one decision per closed 4-hour bar is implied by the STRICT4H grid; minimum holding `min_hold_bars` 1-6 bars and cooldown 0-8 hours are searched parameters. `Expected` holding period, re-entry rules after an exit, and tie handling between simultaneous channel signals are `not stated in source`.
- **Position sizing:** executed signed nominal exposure `pi_t` in `{-1,0,+1}`-style units after "ex-ante caps, smoothing, and other fixed risk controls", bounded by `max_exposure_abs` and by the account constraints in Execution assumptions. The exact cap values, smoothing window and the blend weight `w_mom`, the auxiliary channel `a_t`, the thresholds `tau_long`/`tau_short` and the sigma normaliser are **not printed** - the source says "the details of this construction are implementation-specific". **The signal is therefore `underspecified` for independent reconstruction from the text alone**; it is reproducible only if the (non-public) replication package is obtained.
- **Everything a researcher would still have to choose - entry price beyond bar close, order type, fill model, the numerical caps, `w_mom`, `a_t`, `tau`, the venue stream used per asset, and any capacity filter - is `research-proposed`, not source-reported.**

## Required data

- **Instrument / market type:** USDT-margined **perpetual futures** contracts on **BTC/USDT** (anchor), **ETH/USDT**, **SOL/USDT**, **AVAX/USDT**.
- **Venue:** "major centralized-exchange perpetual-futures endpoints", implemented from **Binance, Bybit and OKX** perpetual data; the cleaned series used in each experiment is **a single timestamp-aligned venue stream per asset** (not a volume-weighted cross-venue synthetic), with the exact local venue/source path recorded only in the regeneration configuration - **which venue produced each published series is `data gap`**.
- **Timeframe:** **4-hour OHLCV bars**, built either directly from exchange candle fields or by aggregating 1-hour bars into 4-hour bars; bar-open timestamps in UTC.
- **Fields:** open, high, low, close, volume; an **8-hour funding-rate series** aligned to the 4-hour grid as a step function carried forward between funding timestamps (with a conservative constant fallback only when a realised funding timestamp is missing); per-bar signal, exposure, market return, fee, slippage, funding and net-return ledger columns (Table 25 artifact schema).
- **Sample sizes (Table 8, BTC/USDT):** Core `2019-09-08 - 2021-12-31`, **5,069** 4-hour returns, mean 0.00043, std 0.01615, min -0.21046, max 0.14734; Long `2019-09-08 - 2025-10-14`, **13,368** 4-hour returns, mean 0.00026, std 0.01309, min -0.21046, max 0.14734. Other assets: ETH `2021-12-31 - 2025-11-24`, SOL and AVAX `2021-01-01 - 2025-12-16` (exact bar counts per asset `data gap`).
- **Point-in-time / leakage:** strict `t+1` execution, funding carry-forward with no backfill from future funding updates, Stage I confined to the training window, Stage II restricted to validation for selection, blind post-ETF window used only post-hoc, and all Stage II thresholds "fixed ex ante prior to evaluation on the long-horizon or blind hold-out windows". Whether the exchange OHLCV reflects last-trade, mark or index pricing, cross-venue aggregation rules and contract-specification changes are named in Section 7 as **unresolved data-construction choices**.
- **Missing data:** the system "does not interpolate or fill missing OHLCV bars"; observations failing sanity, de-duplication and continuity checks are removed; imputation is explicitly avoided. Stale/suspended handling beyond that is `not stated in source`.
- **Funding / fee / spread needs:** funding is both an input (aligned rate series) and a per-bar cost term; taker fee is searched/scenario-varied in bps; **the baseline slippage magnitude in bps, the bid-ask spread, borrow and any maker/liquidity rebate are never printed** - `data gap`, never zero.

## Execution assumptions

- **Source-reported:**
  - **Signal-to-order timing:** strict `t+1` - the exposure applied to bar `t`'s close-to-close market return is derived from `S_{t-1}` after caps, smoothing and fixed risk controls. Same-bar `t+0` is run only as a diagnostic (Table 26).
  - **Return accounting:** `r_t_raw = pi_t * r_t_mkt`; costs are applied on exposure changes at bar boundaries, proportional to `|Delta pi_t|`; `r_t_net = r_t_raw - C_fee,t - C_slip,t - C_fund,t` (equation 7/9). Annualisation factor `AF = 2190` (6 bars/day x 365); Sharpe annualised from 4-hour returns with a **constant annual risk-free rate of 3%**, treated as descriptive because 4-hour crypto returns are autocorrelated and heavy-tailed.
  - **Cost grid:** taker fee `3 / 4 / 6` bps on notional turnover crossed with funding multipliers `0.5 / 1.0 / 1.5` (9 scenarios), described as optimistic and pessimistic perturbations "around a conservative baseline" - **the baseline cost profile's own fee, slippage and funding numbers are not printed** (`data gap`). Additional pessimistic stress scenarios multiply transaction-related costs by `2x` and `3x`, plus a funding-off stress.
  - **Funding model:** charged bar by bar from the aligned 8-hour series scaled to bar duration, using only values visible at each timestamp; a conservative constant-rate fallback is used only when the realised series is unavailable.
  - **Account constraints (Section 7):** initial capital **10,000 USDT**, maximum leverage **5x**, notional cap **50,000 USDT**. Reported annualised returns and Sharpe ratios are explicitly "small- to mid-sized positions in a frictional but non-impact world".
  - **Order type, fill model, latency:** **not stated in source** for the backtest. The live-style executor "maps that target exposure to exchange orders while enforcing the same leverage and notional caps ... and logging fills"; no order type, queue position or latency model is printed.
  - **Third-party replay semantics:** a Backtrader cheat-on-close replay of the same signal reproduces the vectorised raw-return series to max `2.75e-6` over 2,190 bars (RMSE `2.94e-7`, correlation `1.000`, Table 28); a full Backtrader event-loop adapter with the explicit cost/funding ledger reproduces net returns to max `5.44e-5` (RMSE `4.68e-6`) with rank correlation `1.000` across top-5 candidates and five rolling windows (max monthly delta `3.14e-4`); a Freqtrade futures-backtest adapter run reports 65 trades from 265 exposure changes, trade PnL 137.87% and max account drawdown 16.19% (Table 29).
- **`data gap` - never treated as zero:** market impact (explicitly excluded, linear cost model only), venue-specific liquidity constraints, partial fills and failed orders, bid-ask spread and spread crossing, queue position, order type, in-backtest latency, participation and capacity, turnover cost beyond the linear term, borrow, the numerical slippage baseline, leverage and margin/liquidation modelling, and any cross-venue aggregation. The source itself states that "nonlinear market impact and venue-specific liquidity constraints are left to future execution models" and that capacity conclusions must not be drawn.
