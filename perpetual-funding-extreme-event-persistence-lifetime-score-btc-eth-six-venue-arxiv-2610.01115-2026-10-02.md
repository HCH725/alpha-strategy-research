## Economic mechanism

### Source-reported

The paper's stated question is whether a trading signal can accumulate enough statistical evidence for reliable deployment before its economic value decays (Section 1). Statistical evidence and remaining opportunity are put on one Kullback-Leibler scale: in the canonical Gaussian experiment Theorem 3.1 gives the largest deployment probability by information time A as `pi_alpha(A) = Phi(sqrt(A) - z_{1-alpha})` and feasibility at level alpha with power 1-beta iff `A >= A_crit(alpha, beta) = (z_{1-alpha} + z_{1-beta})^2`, equivalently in KL units `I_life >= I_crit(alpha, beta) = (z_{1-alpha} + z_{1-beta})^2 / 2`; with alpha 0.05 and power 0.90 the paper prints `A_crit = 8.5638473507...` and `I_crit = 4.2819236753...` nats, against the generic data-processing bound `kl(0.9, 0.05) = 2.3762054...` nats. Under exponential decay with zero hurdle (Section 5.4) `I_life = mu_0^2 / (4 sigma^2 lambda)`, so with instantaneous Sharpe `S = mu_0 / sigma` and half-life `h_{1/2} = log 2 / lambda` the Alpha Survival Frontier is `h_{1/2} >= 2 log 2 [z_{1-alpha} + z_{1-beta}]^2 / S^2`; Section 5.5 then charges Bonferroni search breadth M through `I_crit(M) = [z_{1-alpha_F/M} + z_{1-beta}]^2 / 2`. Section 6 makes lifetime endogenous: equilibrium destroys `D_i = m_i J_i` of excess lifetime information with `m_i = a_i kappa_i (1 - 1/N_i) / (a_i kappa_i + psi_i)`, rising with crowding effectiveness and competitive breadth and falling with research and implementation cost. Two empirical exercises are presented as illustrations of separate implications (Section 1): a cross-sectional anomaly benchmark in Section 6.3 and the funding-rate study in Section 7.

For the trading-relevant illustration the source asks a narrow question (Section 7): whether a **pre-outcome estimate of signal lifetime contains information about subsequent persistence beyond current signal magnitude**, in perpetual-futures funding rates whose predictability, cross-venue structure and persistence are attributed to references [36] to [39]. The design treats an extreme funding observation as a signal whose magnitude is known but whose remaining life is unknown, fits the pre-event history with a zero-intercept AR(1), converts the fitted decay rate and residual scale into a lifetime-information estimate using the same `mu_0^2 / (4 sigma^2 lambda)` form, normalizes it by `I_crit(0.05, 0.10)`, and ranks events by that score.

### Research interpretation

The falsifiable mechanism this record captures is **crowded positioning in a perpetual book that decays slowly enough for the funding transfer itself to persist**, with the ranking hypothesis that a persistence estimate adds information about the next seven days beyond the size of the extreme:

```text
Regime: an extreme-funding event, defined by the source as current absolute funding
        at or above its trailing 95th percentile over a continuous 180-day
        (540-settlement) pre-event history, with a seven-day cooldown
Primary signal (source-reported): lifetime score L = I_life / I_crit(0.05, 0.10),
        with I_life = f_t^2 / (4 sigma_eps^2 lambda) and lambda = -log(phi_hat)
        from the zero-intercept AR(1) fit; compared against the contemporaneous
        edge magnitude z_edge = |f_t| / s_f,t and the sign-oriented historical
        t-statistic t_hist
Outcome variable (source-reported): Y7d = sum over k = 1..21 of sgn(f_t) * f_{t+k},
        the next-seven-day sign-oriented funding, plus same-sign survival length
Research-proposed trade (NOT source-reported): at each event, hold the
        funding-receiving position for the following 21 settlements, selecting
        events by descending L, with a delta-neutral perp/spot construction
Sizing, entry price, hedge ratio, venue choice, cost schedule: research-proposed
```

Under the persistence reading, a positive `Y7d` means the side that collects funding at the event keeps collecting it for about a week, so a carry entered at the event earns the printed cashflow; under the competing reading, `L` is a monotone re-encoding of `|f_t|` and the fitted `lambda` and the whole effect is ordinary magnitude persistence that competition will close. The source does not adjudicate: it reports rank associations only, states explicitly that "The reported quantities measure persistence", and never converts them into a return, a Sharpe or a net result. The record therefore treats the mechanism as a hypothesis, treats the event definition as source-reported and everything about how a position would actually be taken as `research-proposed`, and does not assume each component contributes (ablation is required, see Falsification plan).

## Signal

All rules below are source-reported unless explicitly labeled `research-proposed` or `research-defined`.

- **Formation timestamp (source-reported):** every event regressor uses only information available at event time t; the source states "Every event-level regressor uses only information available at time t; the exercise is retrospective" (Section 7.1). Settlements are observed at eight-hour funding settlements. Whether the funding rate of the settlement immediately after t is already observable at t is `not stated in source` - a point-in-time `data gap` that any replication must settle from venue documentation.
- **Lookback (source-reported):** `W = 540` eight-hour settlements (180 days) of continuous pre-event history; `K = 21` settlements (seven days) of complete outcome window; a seven-day cooldown between events; the AR(1) is fitted on `j = t-W, ..., t-2` as `f_{j+1} = phi f_j + eps_{j+1}` with the one-step residuals supplying `sigma_eps`; the sign-oriented historical t-statistic uses `g_j = sgn(f_t) f_j` over `j = t-W, ..., t-1`.
- **Event trigger (source-reported):** an event requires the continuous 180-day pre-event history, `|f_t|` at or above its trailing 95th percentile over that window, the seven-day cooldown and a complete seven-day outcome window; the sample contains 545 events, 543 of which have a finite lifetime score (the finiteness condition is `0 < phi_hat < 1`).
- **Signal value (source-reported):** `lambda_hat = -log(phi_hat)`, `I_life = f_t^2 / (4 sigma_eps^2 lambda_hat)`, `L = I_life / I_crit(0.05, 0.10)` with `I_crit = 4.2819236753...` nats; the source notes the normalization "is monotone and leaves all rank statistics unchanged; it does not itself constitute a statistical certificate". Comparators: `z_edge = |f_t| / s_f,t` where `s_f,t` is the sample standard deviation of the W pre-event observations, and `t_hist = mean(g) / (sd(g) / sqrt(W))`.
- **Outcome (source-reported):** `Y7d = sum_{k=1..21} sgn(f_t) f_{t+k}` and the same-sign survival length `S_sign` counting consecutive post-event settlements that keep the event-time sign, capped at 21, stopping at the first `k` with `sgn(f_t) f_{t+k} <= 0`.
- **Long entry / short entry (research-proposed):** the source specifies no trade. A replication would receive funding by holding the perpetual short and the spot long when `f_t > 0` (longs pay shorts), and the mirror construction when `f_t < 0`, for the 21 settlements following the event, entering only events above a chosen `L` rank. Both the direction convention and the hedge are `research-proposed`; the source's own sentence frames the payoff as a basis trade ("Net basis-trade returns additionally depend on ...").
- **Exit / holding period (research-proposed):** fixed exit at settlement 21, or earlier exit on the first settlement that flips the event-time sign (the source's `S_sign` stopping rule is reported but not proposed as an exit rule).
- **Position sizing (not stated in source):** `data gap`; equal notional, vol targeting, Kelly fraction and leverage are all `research-proposed`, and the census shows `kelly` 0 and `leverage` 0 occurrences.
- **Parameters (source-reported):** 180-day window, 95th-percentile threshold, seven-day cooldown, seven-day outcome, AR(1) zero intercept, `I_crit(0.05, 0.10) = 4.2819236753...`, calendar-month cluster bootstrap with 5,000 resamples for the Section 7.2 intervals, a three-month block bootstrap for the double-residualized statistic, twelve asset-venue series for the leave-one-out check, and 109 events per top quintile.
- **Underspecified:** the calendar window of the funding sample, the data vendor and ticker mapping, the timezone and settlement-alignment convention, venue-by-venue event counts, the treatment of venues whose contracts run on 1-hour or 4-hour funding grids, the threshold sensitivity of the 95th percentile, and any rule for selecting events across the twelve series simultaneously. All are `not stated in source`.
- **Signal definition status:** event definition, `L`, `z_edge`, `t_hist`, `Y7d` and `S_sign` are source-reported; direction, hedge, sizing, entry price, exit and venue selection are `research-proposed`; all falsification cutoffs are `research-defined`.

### Negative evidence

1. The paper's own cross-sectional equilibrium benchmark fails to support the mechanism it is built to illustrate: Spearman `0.059` with 95 percent interval `[-0.086, 0.199]` over 205 predictors, OLS slope `0.0058` with `t = 0.24`, and the source's own words "contains little information" (Section 6.3).
2. The paper's own BTCUSDT 57-event holdout fails its pre-specified joint criterion: `beta_A = -1.741 x 10^-5` with a lower bound of `-3.002 x 10^-5` (not positive) and `beta_B = 2817.99` with a lower bound of `-9857.24` (not positive); a failed joint criterion is then read in the source as "consistent with implementation technology being a central state variable", which is an interpretation of a failure, not a confirmation.
3. The reported outcome is signed funding cashflow, not return: the source states that net basis-trade returns additionally depend on execution fees, spot/perpetual hedge drift, borrow, liquidation, collateral funding, slippage and venue credit risk, and that "The reported quantities measure persistence."
4. No cost model exists anywhere in the empirical sections (census: `commission` 0, `bid-ask` 0, `leverage` 0, `latency` 0, `limit order` 0, `market order` 0, `taker` 0, `maker` 0, `fill` 0, `kelly` 0; `slippage` 1 and `liquidation` 1 only inside the list of omissions).

8. The exercise is retrospective and in-sample: no out-of-sample split, no walk-forward, no frozen forward window, no live or paper test, no venue holdout.
9. No multiple-testing control is applied to the funding study's own family of reported statistics (three rank correlations, two residualizations, two quintile splits, one leave-one-out range); Bonferroni appears only in the theoretical Section 5.5 and never in Section 7.

11. Universe breadth is two assets (BTC-USDT, ETH-USDT) across six venues and one funding grid (eight-hour); no other coins, no 1-hour or 4-hour contracts, no weekly settlements, no decentralized venues.
12. Only 543 analysed events over twelve series, clustered in calendar time - the very need for calendar-month cluster bootstrap and a three-month block bootstrap is itself evidence of strong dependence that a naive test would overstate.
13. The 95th-percentile trigger, the 180-day window, the seven-day cooldown and the 21-settlement horizon are fixed without justification and without any reported sensitivity analysis over those four choices.
14. Persistence is modelled as a zero-intercept AR(1) over 540 settlements with a Gaussian residual scale; no model comparison, no long-memory or regime-switching alternative, and no confidence interval for `phi_hat` or `lambda_hat` is reported, although `L` is entirely driven by them.
15. The source itself warns that the normalization "does not itself constitute a statistical certificate", so `L >= 1` must not be read as certification, yet `L` is the only threshold-like quantity a practitioner would reach for.
16. The outcome horizon of 21 settlements equals the cooldown, so events are non-overlapping by construction; overlapping designs, which would raise effective sample size and dependence at once, are untested.

19. Extreme funding is a crowded-book condition; the trade that receives it requires holding a hedge through the same stressed interval in which liquidation cascades, borrow and collateral terms move, and the source models none of that.
20. A simple competing explanation is not excluded: sign and magnitude autocorrelation of funding is already documented in the paper's own references [36] to [39], so `L` may add nothing beyond `|f_t|` and a decay estimate - exactly what the drop to `0.1278` suggests.

22. No capacity, borrow-cost, turnover or market-impact analysis exists for the corresponding trade; Section 8 lists these as "natural candidates for measuring implementation technology" rather than measuring them.

## Falsification plan

Every threshold below is `research-defined`; every operational choice not specified by the source is `research-proposed`. Each gate states data, sample, metric, threshold and action.

- **F1 - Printed-value reproduction.** Rebuild the event sample from six-venue eight-hour funding histories and recompute the Section 7.2 statistics. Fail if the event count is not 545 (or 543 finite-score) under the stated definitions, or if `rho_S(L, Y7d)`, `rho_S(z_edge, Y7d)` or `rho_S(t_hist, Y7d)` differs from `0.4756`, `0.3833`, `0.3606` by more than `0.02` each, or if the ordering `L > z_edge > t_hist` does not hold. Action: report the reproduction as failed, reclassify all quoted associations as unverified, and stop before any economic test.
- **F2 - Sample-window and data-availability gate (primary go/no-go).** Establish the actual calendar window, vendor, ticker map and settlement timestamps behind the twelve series, and confirm that a 180-day continuous history exists for each series over that window. Fail if the window cannot be established, if any of the six venues lacks continuous eight-hour settlement history for the stated design, or if the events cannot be dated. Action: keep the record research-only, mark the published sample non-reconstructible, and require a documented replacement sample before any test proceeds.
- **F3 - Point-in-time gate.** For every event, verify that the funding rate of the first settlement after t (the `k = 1` term of `Y7d`) is observable at t under venue publication rules. Fail if that rate is only known after t for any venue. Action: recompute `Y7d` from `k = 2` onward and treat any inflation of the reported association as look-ahead; if the association vanishes, close the hypothesis.
- **F4 - Incremental-value gate.** Out of sample, regress `rank(Y7d)` on `rank(z_edge)` and test the residual association of `rank(L)`. Fail if the incremental Spearman is below `0.10` or its block-bootstrap 95 percent interval includes `0`. Action: conclude the lifetime score adds no information beyond magnitude and drop the ranking layer from any hypothesis.
- **F5 - Quintile monotonicity gate.** Sort events into five equal bins by `L` and compute mean `Y7d` and positive-event share per bin. Fail if the bins are not monotonically increasing, or if the Q5 minus Q1 difference in positive share is not significant at the 5 percent level by a calendar-month cluster bootstrap. Action: treat the top-quintile result as a single-bin artifact.
- **F6 - Sign-symmetry gate.** Split events by `sgn(f_t)` and require positive mean `Y7d` in both halves, and require the same-sign survival to exceed the opposite-sign survival in both halves. Fail if either half has non-positive mean `Y7d`. Action: conclude the effect is drift or one-sided crowding in one funding regime, not persistence.
- **F7 - Venue robustness gate.** Recompute the incremental statistic leaving out each venue in turn. Fail if any leave-one-venue-out value falls below `0.15`, versus the source's in-sample range `0.2529` to `0.3092`. Action: identify the dominating venue and treat the effect as venue-specific.
- **F8 - Asset robustness gate.** Compute the association separately on BTC-USDT and ETH-USDT. Fail if either point estimate is at or below `0`. Action: restrict any claim to a single asset and downgrade confidence.
- **F9 - Subperiod stability gate.** Split the dated event sample into calendar-year halves (`research-defined` split chosen before seeing results). Fail if at least two subperiods have association at or below `0`, or if more than `60` percent of events fall in a single calendar year. Action: classify the effect as regime- or episode-concentrated and require a fresh forward window.
- **F10 - Multiple-testing gate.** Enumerate the reported statistic family (three rank correlations, two residualizations, two quintile splits, one leave-one-out range) plus every variant the replication runs, and apply Benjamini-Hochberg at `q = 0.10`, reporting also the Bonferroni family-wise bar the paper itself defines in Section 5.5. Fail if the incremental statistic `0.2824` does not survive `q = 0.10`. Action: report the persistence claim as selection-contaminated and downgrade confidence.
- **F11 - Mechanism placebo gate.** Permute event-time funding signs across venues within calendar month and recompute `rho_S(L, Y7d)` 1,000 times; separately replace the signed outcome with the absolute-value outcome to test magnitude persistence. Fail if the permuted 95th percentile reaches `0.15`, or if the signed association disappears once `|f_t|` persistence is controlled. Action: conclude the result is generic magnitude clustering, not sign persistence, and reject the carry hypothesis.
- **F12 - Economic gate.** Convert the top-quintile `L` events into the research-proposed funding-receiving basis position and charge a cost ladder of `0, 1, 2, 5, 10` basis points per round trip on both legs plus a hedge-drift term. Fail if net Sharpe is below `0` at `2` basis points, or if net cumulative funding over the sample is not positive at `5` basis points. Action: conclude the persistence does not survive execution, stop, and keep the record as documentation of a persistence fact only.
- **F13 - Framework cross-check.** Reproduce the paper's own Section 6.3 checks (205 predictors, Spearman `0.059`, `beta_A` and `beta_B` on the 57-event BTCUSDT holdout). Fail if the pooled Spearman stays below `0.10` in absolute value or the joint criterion still fails. Action: record that the paper's equilibrium/implementation mechanism is unsupported, so no crowding-adjusted decay prior may be borrowed from it.
- **F14 - Frozen forward window.** Freeze the design from 2026-10-02 forward for at least 12 months of new settlements, with no change to any frozen parameter. Fail if the forward incremental Spearman is at or below `0` or forward net carry at `2` basis points is at or below `0`. Action: close the hypothesis as falsified.

**Global no-retuning rule.** A failed gate may not be rescued by adjusting anything on this frozen list: the 180-day / 540-settlement window, the trailing 95th-percentile trigger, the seven-day cooldown, the 21-settlement outcome horizon, the zero-intercept AR(1) order, `I_crit(0.05, 0.10) = 4.2819236753`, the sign-orientation convention, the six venues and two assets, the eight-hour settlement grid, the calendar-month and three-month bootstrap schemes with 5,000 resamples, the quintile construction, the cost ladder points, and the choice of signed funding cashflow as the outcome. Falsification thresholds themselves may only be tightened, never loosened, after first failure.

## Crypto portability

`direct`, economic viability unproven.

The source is crypto-native: it studies BTC-USDT and ETH-USDT perpetual-futures funding on six centralized venues, so no porting of the mechanism is required and the census shows `btc` 3, `eth-usdt` 1 and `perpetual` 6 occurrences in the body. What is directly portable is the measurement itself: the funding payment stream is observable, the event definition needs only the funding history, and the outcome variable is a cashflow the receiving side actually earns.

What remains unproven is economics, not portability: (a) the source reports rank association with cashflow and never a return, Sharpe or net result, so no profitability claim transfers; (b) venue-specific funding grids - many contracts run on 1-hour or 4-hour schedules rather than eight hours - would change `W`, `K`, the percentile threshold and the cooldown, a material signal-construction change the source does not address; (c) the delta-neutral spot leg introduces borrow, spread and hedge-drift risk that the source lists as unmodelled, and shorting the perpetual requires margin and survives neither liquidation cascades nor auto-deleveraging; (d) 24/7 sessions mean settlement epochs differ across venues and timezone alignment is unstated; (e) venue fragmentation, listing churn and delistings can remove exactly the crowded contracts where extreme funding appears; (f) collateral funding, withdrawal and custody risk on six venues are outside the source entirely. Crypto portability is not authorization to trade.

- Scope limitation: two assets, six centralized venues, one funding grid, one horizon, one event definition; absence of negative results elsewhere is not evidence of robustness.
