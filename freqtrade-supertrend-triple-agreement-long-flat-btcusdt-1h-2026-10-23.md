## Economic mechanism

### Source-reported

The source claims only what its code prints: hold long exposure while three buy-parameterized supertrend gauges (multipliers 4/7/1, ATR periods 8/9/8) all print `'up'`; stand aside to flat while three sell-parameterized supertrend gauges (multipliers 1/3/6, ATR periods 16/18/18) all print `'down'`. A four-rung time-decaying ROI ladder (8.7% → 5.8% → 2.9% → 0% at 0/372/861/2221 minutes) harvests decaying profit targets, a fixed −26.5% stoploss backstops every position, and an immediately-active 5% trailer (armed from entry, ratcheting under the peak once price advances 14.4% — the `trailing_only_offset_is_reached = False` semantic, preserved verbatim) trails every position. The file ships no sample, window, trade count, or cost basis — this record adopts no source-reported performance (see Evidence).

### Research interpretation

Six-gauge agreement filter, long-only. Each supertrend gauge is a trailing ATR band that flips state only when price closes through it — a tight gauge (multiplier 1, period 8) flips on ordinary noise while a wide gauge (multiplier 7, period 9) flips only on structural breaks. The entry demands unanimity across the tight/mid/wide buy triplet: a rally that flips the tight gauge but leaves the wide gauge pointing down buys nothing, so entries concentrate on moves strong enough to drag every buy gauge into `'up'` on the same bar. The exit is deliberately asymmetric — it reads a different, slower triplet (periods 16/18/18) and demands unanimous `'down'`: a stall that flips only the fast sell gauge holds the book until the ROI ladder, the trailer, or the stop. The failure mode is plain: in a chopping tape the triplets rarely agree, so the book sits flat for long stretches (opportunity cost, not loss); in a grinding trend that never prints unanimous sell-side agreement, the position rides until the wide −26.5% stop or the trailer — the stop is 3× the opening ROI rung, so in ordinary tape exits run through the ROI ladder or the trailer first. The bet is on broad ATR-band agreement resolving upward at frozen six-gauge geometry on `1h` BTCUSDT, not on oscillators, volume prints, calendar effects, or any second timeframe.
