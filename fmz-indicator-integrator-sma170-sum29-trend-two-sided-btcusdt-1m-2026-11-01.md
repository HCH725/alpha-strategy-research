## Economic mechanism

### Source-reported

Deviation-area trend tracking: the distance between price and its long average is accumulated over a summation window, and the sign of that accumulated area decides the held side — positive area means price has lived above its average (uptrend, hold long), negative area the reverse. The prose frames this as an integral concept that smooths single-bar noise at the cost of lag, admits sensitivity to both window parameters, warns against violent-event bars and high-volatility symbols, and suggests RSI/volume/ML extensions (all unported future work — disclosed). The prose names a 200-bar average while the code ships 170 (contradiction recorded; 170 governs), and praises backtest profits with zero numbers (contradiction recorded; nothing adopted). The stanza window 2023-10-09→2023-10-16 is a seven-day 1m sample — cited as provenance, never as performance evidence.

### Research interpretation

Sign-state holder on an accumulated deviation area, event-persistent, two-sided. Unlike single-bar sign systems (`roc-sign-state` — raw ROC sign, long-flat, 1d; `force-index-100-zero-state` — single-bar force level, 1d), this machine never reads a one-bar value for direction — every decision integrates 30 consecutive (close−SMA170) deviations, so an isolated spike cannot flip the state unless it moves the whole 30-bar area across zero. Unlike threshold-band systems (`cumulative-rsi-dual-threshold` — summed RSI with dual breakout thresholds, long-only, 1h; `mass-index-threshold-latch` — summed range with threshold latch), there is no band and no latch: the gate is the bare zero line with an explicit `sum==0` hold, and exits are the opposite sign itself rather than a band reclaim. Unlike cross-event machines (the MA-cross family — no cross ships here) and unlike agreement-gated HPotter combos (`combo-123-reversal-fisher-transform` — stoch-streak AND Fisher-slope, 4h), there is a single scalar with no second leg and no flat-by-disagreement (flat exists only pre-warmup or on the measure-zero `sum==0`). Five-axis distinction from the nearest pool cousins holds on mechanism (30-bar deviation-area sign-state vs single-bar oscillator sign vs summed-RSI dual-threshold breakout), signal construction (close−SMA170 accumulation with 170/29 windows vs RSI sums vs ROC sign), horizon (1m source-native vs 1h/1d), exits (opposite-sign id-specific closes with all-`na` no-op exit legs vs band reclaims), and source identity (FMZ 429484 integrator vs Noro/Coppock/KST lineages).
