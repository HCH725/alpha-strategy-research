## Economic mechanism

### Source-reported

Coarse mean-reversion/momentum building blocks. The paper frames its universe coarsely: `one can think of alpha signals as based on mean-reversion or momentum`, where `a mean-reversion alpha has a sign opposite to the return on which it is based`. Alpha#52 is a low-floor shift block: the trailing five-day minimum of `low` against its own print five bars earlier, signed so a fallen floor prints positive. The paper prints the formula and the delay-1 next-day class and nothing else about its behaviour. The paper ships no stop, target, or risk leg of any kind.

### Research interpretation

Five-day low-floor shift fade, two-sided overall. Write the single-asset score exactly as the pinned code reduces (bit-exact equivalence verified this cycle — max absolute difference `0.0` over 2,372 live bars and 81 tape-C bars): `score[t] = -delta(ts_min(low[t], 5), 5) × 1.0 × ts_rank(volume[t], 5)`, i.e. sign `= sign(ts_min[t-5] − ts_min[t])`. When the trailing five-day low floor undercuts its print five bars ago (new lower low — breakdown), the book goes long the next open (fade expecting snap-back); when the floor lifts (higher low — strength), the book goes short (fade expecting give-back). The `1.0` rank leg is dead warmup (240-bar validity gate, never a signal) and the `ts_rank(volume, 5)` leg is strictly positive scale (magnitude only, ignored by the single-unit book) — both disclosed, neither moves direction. Unlike sibling worldquant-alpha23 (a high-vs-mean gate with a structural flat regime — a gate object), this rule never gates: every defined bar takes a side. Unlike sibling worldquant-alpha24 (a 100-day slow-ratio splice — a trend object), this rule reads only a 5/5 low window. Unlike the Donchian/breakout pool kin (20-bar extreme breakouts with stop/time exits — breakout objects), this rule trades the *change* of the floor, not the breach of it, with flip-or-flatten as the sole exit. The failure mode is plain: the live window flips 311 entries / 310 exits across 2,367 post-warmup bars (~0.26 flips per bar) with no guardrail and no cost awareness — calmer than siblings worldquant-alpha47 (~0.37/bar), worldquant-alpha57 (~0.51/bar) and worldquant-alpha41 (~0.94/bar), but still a naked shift with no smoothing, plus a 241-bar dead-warmup tax that consumes ~9% of BTC history — disclosed, not upgraded. Score magnitude (floor-shift distance times volume rank) is ignored (single-unit book), so dollar/point scale never sizes anything.

## Signal

Exact rule as pinned (any other series, anchor, window, divisor, negation, fill, frame, book, or tie treatment is a different, unpinned rule):

- Inputs (paper A.2): `low[t]` live on completed `1d` bars only, plus `volume[t]` for positive scale only and `close[t]`-derived `returns[t]` for validity only (their two legs collapse single-asset as pinned — values never move direction, finiteness/scale only). `open[t]`, `high[t]`, and `vwap[t]` are never read by any order-gating line, not even for validity. No adv/returns-level/market-cap/sector beyond the pinned legs, no cross-sectional rank, no second asset or timeframe is read by any order-gating line.
- Level (paper Appendix A lines 479–480, pinned impl lines 454–459, bit-exact single-asset reduction proven this cycle): `score[t] = -delta(ts_min(low[t], 5), 5) × rank((ts_sum(returns[t], 240) − ts_sum(returns[t], 20)) / 220) × ts_rank(volume[t], 5)` — undefined exactly when any read input is non-finite per the NaN-propagation rule (any missing bar in the 240-returns window, any non-finite low/volume print, or the `returns[0]` seed makes the whole score NaN — deterministically flat, never a fabricated side), never seeded or backfilled.
- Direction mapping (source-grounded, fenced in Negative evidence 1): `score[t] > 0` → long signal; `score[t] < 0` → short signal; `score[t] == 0` → flat signal (fires only on the exact print `ts_min[t] == ts_min[t-5]` — the dead-constant tape proves this path prints all-flat; zero such bars across the full 2,612-bar live window, disclosed); non-finite → NaN → flat signal by record rule, never interpolated. Both sides fire from the paper's own sign convention — neither side is disabled. The entry fills at the next bar's open, one single unit, only if the book is not already on that side (see position rule).
- Exit: the first opposite-side or flat signal reverses-to or flattens the full unit at the next-bar open — flip-or-flatten is the sole exit path by construction (a long holding into a short signal reverses across the next open, counting one exit plus one entry; into a flat signal it closes flat). No stop-loss, no take-profit, no trailing, no time exit (the paper ships no risk leg — carried over exactly, disclosed in Provenance adaptation (4), never upgraded into a source claim).
- Direction: two-sided overall (fallen-floor longs; lifted-floor shorts; exact-tie or undefined prints flat).
- Position rule (predeclared researcher pin): single-unit long-or-short-or-flat; an already-positioned book treats same-side signal bars as no-ops (no pyramiding, no averaging, no scaling by shift distance or volume rank); flat-regime bars while already flat are no-ops. Re-entry re-arms on the next signal bar after a flat exit (no cooldown specified — explicitly none, not invented). Pre-first-signal bars (before the adopted warmup completes) and any NaN-score bars are flat by record rule.
