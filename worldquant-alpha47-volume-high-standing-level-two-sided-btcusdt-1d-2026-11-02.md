## Economic mechanism

### Source-reported

Coarse mean-reversion/momentum building blocks. The paper frames its universe coarsely: `one can think of alpha signals as based on mean-reversion or momentum`, where `a mean-reversion alpha has a sign opposite to the return on which it is based`. Alpha#47 is a volume-scaled high-standing level block: the day's volume against its 20-day average, times the day's high against its 5-day mean, minus one. The paper prints the formula and the delay-1 next-day class and nothing else about its behaviour. The paper ships no stop, target, or risk leg of any kind.

### Research interpretation

Volume-confirmed high-standing level, two-sided overall. Write the single-asset score exactly as the pinned code reduces (bit-exact equivalence verified this cycle — max absolute difference `0.0` on both the sine tape and the full 2,612-bar live window): `score[t] = (volume[t] / sma(volume[t], 20)) * (high[t] / sma(high[t], 5)) - 1.0`. The first ratio asks whether today traded heavier than its 20-day habit; the second asks whether today's high stands above its 5-day mean. When their joint product exceeds one (`score > 0`), volume confirms the high standing and the book goes long the next open; when it falls short (`score < 0`) — quiet days, sagging highs, or both — the book goes short. Unlike sibling worldquant-alpha23 (a gated level that goes structurally flat whenever the high sits at-or-below its 20-bar mean — a gate object with a flat regime), this rule never gates: every defined bar takes a side, long or short, from the same subtraction. Unlike sibling worldquant-alpha28 (a `scale(` of an adv-correlation plus midpoint tilt — a scale/correlation object whose single-asset correlation legs degenerate), this rule correlates nothing and every live leg genuinely moves the sign. Unlike sibling worldquant-alpha7 (an adv-gated time-rank contrarian — a rank object), this rule ranks nothing live. The failure mode is plain: the live window flips 963 entries / 962 exits across 2,588 post-warmup bars (~0.37 flips per bar) with no guardrail and no cost awareness — calmer than siblings worldquant-alpha41 (~0.94/bar) and worldquant-alpha57 (~0.51/bar), but still a naked level with no smoothing — disclosed, not upgraded. Score magnitude is ignored (single-unit book), so the quote-currency scale of the ratios never sizes anything.

## Limitations

- Naked level with no guardrail: the live window flips 963 entries / 962 exits across 2,588 post-warmup bars (~0.37 flips per bar) — the record's principal risk is churn against house taker fees and modeled funding with no smoothing before the next print, by the carried-over paper design.
- Single-print noise on the high leg: the high-standing ratio reads one bar against a 5-day mean with no averaging on the numerator — single-print wicks swing the product bar to bar — disclosed, not smoothed.
- Volume-spike sensitivity: a one-day volume eruption inflates the product regardless of price conviction and can pin the book long through the 20-day habit decay — disclosed, not dampened.
- Next-open latency idealization: source-native delay-1 assumes trading `on the day subsequent`; any live gap between the prior close print and the realized open fill differs by construction — disclosed, research-only.
- Performance evidence is absent: nothing here is calibrated, fitted, or tuned to any backtest.
- Author scope: the paper presents production formulaic alphas as research objects, not a vetted retail trading system — this record is research-only and claims no live suitability.
