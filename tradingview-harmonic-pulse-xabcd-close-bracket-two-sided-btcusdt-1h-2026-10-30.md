## Economic mechanism

**Source-reported:** Five zigzag swing pivots completing a textbook harmonic (Gartley/Bat/Butterfly/Crab) Fibonacci structure mark a D-point reversal setup; the stop sits beyond X, the target is a fixed multiple of that risk. The author warns this is discretionary pattern recognition, not a proven edge.

**Research hypothesis only:** Completed multi-pivot Fibonacci-conformant structures on BTC-USDT 1h may precede short-horizon reversals often enough to study with a symmetric bracket. Exit on completed-close stop/target touch, opposite-structure close, and nothing else. This is **untested**, not a claim of expectancy or fidelity to the author's backtest.

## Signal

1. **Market and data:** One chronologically sorted, **real** Binance `BTC-USDT` spot UTC `1h` OHLCV feed. Raw `T[t]` is exchange candle **opening epoch seconds**. Require exact 3600-second UTC-aligned consecutive timestamps, no duplicates or gaps, finite positive O/H/L/C, `L<=min(O,C)<=max(O,C)<=H`, finite base-asset `V>=0`, and actual observed prices. A candle is complete/available only at `E[t]=T[t]+3600`. Reject missing/incomplete/invalid data before any engine `dropna`; no forward fill or synthetic candles.
2. **Warmup with no carry-forward:** Bars `0..99` are warmup: **no pivot is stored and no evaluation occurs**; all three pivot arrays are definitionally empty at the end of bar 99. Eligibility starts at `t>=100`. Because nothing is stored before eligibility, no pre-warmup structure can recur later — the first actionable set is whatever the post-warmup pivot stream first completes. This is a disclosed researcher lifecycle rule answering closed-PR #537 blocker (1) structurally.
3. **Pivot storage (t>=100 only):** `pivHigh=ta.pivothigh(high,4,4)`, `pivLow=ta.pivotlow(low,4,4)` (4 left / 4 right; a pivot at bar `b` confirms only at close of `b+4` — causal with a 4-bar lag, as the author warns). When defined, push `(price, bar_index-4, dir)` with `dir=+1` (high) / `-1` (low): empty array appends; same-direction push **replaces** the last entry iff strictly more extreme (higher high / lower low), keeping its new bar; opposite-direction push appends. Cap 20 entries (shift oldest). High is processed **before** low on the same bar (source order). `newPivot = defined(pivHigh) OR defined(pivLow)`. Display markers use `offset=-4` (plot only, feeds no rule).
4. **Single evaluation event:** At most one evaluation per bar, and only when `cnt>=5 AND newPivot`. Take the five most recently stored pivots oldest→newest as X,A,B,C,D with prices `Xp..Dp`, bars `Xb..Db`, and `dDir` = newest stored direction; `isBullish = (dDir==-1)`. No per-bar re-scan of older windows: a stored quintuple is judged only on the bar its fifth pivot arrives.
5. **Ratio scoring (exact windows, tol=0.10):** `XA=|Ap-Xp|`, `AB=|Bp-Ap|`, `BC=|Cp-Bp|`, `CD=|Dp-Cp|`, `XD=|Dp-Xp|`; require `XA>0 AND AB>0 AND BC>0` (else no evaluation; `CD==0` is allowed and simply scores 0 since every lower bound below is positive). `abR=AB/XA`, `bcR=BC/AB`, `cdR=CD/BC`, `xdR=XD/XA`; `f_in(v,lo,hi) = v>=lo AND v<=hi` (boundary equality passes). Gartley: ab `[0.518,0.718]`, bc `[0.282,0.986]`, cd `[1.03,1.718]`, xd `[0.686,0.886]`. Bat: ab `[0.282,0.6]`, bc `[0.282,0.986]`, cd `[1.518,2.718]`, xd `[0.786,0.986]`. Butterfly: ab `[0.686,0.886]`, bc `[0.282,0.986]`, cd `[1.518,2.718]`, xd `[1.17,1.718]`. Crab: ab `[0.282,0.718]`, bc `[0.282,0.986]`, cd `[2.14,3.718]`, xd `[1.518,1.718]`. Each pattern scores 0–4 (disabled patterns score 0; all four are enabled here); `bestScore` = max; `matched = bestScore>=3`. Name resolves by fixed priority `Gartley > Bat > Butterfly > Crab` on ties (first `==bestScore` in that chain wins — deterministic).
6. **Trade gating and bracket construction:** `tradeDirection` pinned `"Both"`: `longOk = isBullish`, `shortOk = NOT isBullish` (mutually exclusive). `cooled = na(lastEntryBar) OR (bar_index-lastEntryBar)>=cooldownBars(5)` (equality passes; `lastEntryBar` starts na). `stop = isBullish ? Xp-0.25*ATR14[t] : Xp+0.25*ATR14[t]`; `risk=|Dp-stop|`; `target = isBullish ? Dp+2.0*risk : Dp-2.0*risk`. **Disclosed integrity skips (source-silent fail-safes):** require defined `ATR14[t]`; require `risk>0`; LONG additionally requires `C[t]>stop`, SHORT requires `C[t]<stop` (strict; equality fails) — otherwise the close is already through the stop (the known bullish Crab/Butterfly `xdR>1` defect where `stop` prints above entry) and the event is discarded, consuming no cooldown. A skipped event sets nothing.
7. **Entry scheduling and fill:** On a gated signal at `E[t]` while FLAT with no pending action, queue one entry for the **next completed bar t+1**, decision frozen at t. On `E[t+1]`, if real valid `C[t+1]` exists and modeled cash `>=Q*C[t+1]*(1+f)`, issue exactly one `MARKET` order for `Q=Decimal("0.001") BTC` at that row's **actual close** (historical simulator idealization of the source's next-open fill — disclosed), `f=Decimal("0.001")`. Record fill index `e=t+1`, modeled price `P=C[e]`, and the frozen `stop`/`target`. Set `lastEntryBar:=t`. Insufficient cash consumes the signal and stays FLAT; a missing price, unexpected partial fill, rejected executor, or inconsistent ledger **aborts** rather than inventing a fill.
8. **Exit signals and priority:** On each **subsequent** completed bar `u>e` while holding and no pending exit, evaluate in order: (a) stop touch — LONG `C[u]<=stop` / SHORT `C[u]>=stop`; (b) target touch — LONG `C[u]>=target` / SHORT `C[u]<=target`; (c) opposite-direction fresh `matched` event on bar u — treated as **close-only** (never a same-bar flip). First true predicate records the sole exit reason and queues one full-`Q` close for the **following completed bar u+1** at its **real C[u+1]**. Stop-before-target priority is a disclosed researcher pin (source intrabar touch order is unknowable). No time exit, trailing, or signal-flat beyond (a)–(c): the source ships none, and none is added. An adverse move between signal and next close can cause loss **greater than** the reference risk. If exit price is missing, do not fabricate; mark run incomplete. No exit check on the entry-fill bar.
9. **Position/confirmation/re-entry:** States `FLAT`, `LONG_PENDING`, `LONG`, `LONG_CLOSE_PENDING`, `SHORT_PENDING`, `SHORT`, `SHORT_CLOSE_PENDING` — one slot, one side, at most one pending executor (`pyramiding=1`: no adds). A confirmed close credits `Q*C_exit*(1-f)` (long) or the symmetric short proceeds and returns FLAT; unconfirmed closes do not erase inventory — reconcile or abort on failure. After a confirmed close, the next entry requires a **later fresh** `matched` event (opposite events while holding only close; events while pending are discarded). No new entry on a fill/exit bar. No DCA, safety orders, compounding, cooldown beyond rule 6, leverage, margin, borrowing, futures, shared capital, maker/limit orders, discretionary stop/TP/trailing, or hidden controller defaults.
10. **End of history:** Never assume a next bar exists. An entry/exit pending beyond the last complete bar remains unfilled and explicitly reported. Any still-held position is marked at the **last actual valid completed close**, separately from realized PnL; no synthetic final liquidation. Indicator arithmetic is chronological float64; size and modeled cash/fees use Decimal with 28-digit precision. Threshold comparisons exactly as specified; no rounded comparisons or fitted parameters.
