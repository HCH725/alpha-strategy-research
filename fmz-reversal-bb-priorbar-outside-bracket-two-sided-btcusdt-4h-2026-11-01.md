## Signal — Gate 1, fully pinned derivative

Index completed UTC 4-hour bars `t = 0, 1, ...` with real Binance BTC-USDT futures `O[t]`/`C[t]` for entries, `H[t]`/`L[t]` for bracket-level construction and post-hoc resolution only (`V[t]` never read — disclosed). A bar with missing/nonfinite/non-positive prices, or with `H < max(O,C)` / `L < min(O,C)`, is invalid; never interpolate. All constants are hard-coded source literals (zero `input.` ships — there is nothing to fence; any other value on any of them is a different, unpinned rule).

- **Bands (Pine v4 causal `sma`/`stdev`; `stdev` is the biased population form):** `B[t]=mean(C[t-19..t])` (na before index 19), `D[t]=popstdev(C[t-19..t])` (na before 19), `U[t]=B[t]+2·D[t]`, `Lw[t]=B[t]-2·D[t]`.
- **Full predicates (actionable only t ≥ 120, evaluated at bar `t`'s close from the prior bar's prints):** long level `LE[t]=(O[t-1]<Lw[t-1]) AND (C[t-1]<Lw[t-1])`; short level `SE[t]=(O[t-1]>U[t-1]) AND (C[t-1]>U[t])` — the short close leg reads the CURRENT band `U[t]` exactly as the file prints it (never symmetrized to `U[t-1]` — disclosed). Pre-warmup bars are flat by record rule. Strict-inequality misses, equality-edge bars, na bars and zero-variance bars satisfy no predicate — an explicit neither-fire hold unless a resting bracket independently resolves.
- **Latched bracket (fixed at the signal bar, never moved):** on a long signal at bar `s`, `SL=min(L[s-9..s])`, `TP=max(H[s-9..s])`; on a short signal, `SL=max(H[s-9..s])`, `TP=min(L[s-9..s])` (single-argument extremes default to the low/high series — reference-verified this cycle; levels are tickSize-snapped at 0.10 — the record's sole precision pin). Levels never move after arming. A newly armed pair is eligible for resolution from bar `s+1` onward (orders created at the signal close cannot fill on the signal bar itself — pinned).
- **Exit precedence (explicit researcher pin, same class as the merged Simons/dual-EMA9/21 records):** each held bar resolves the latched box first-touch against the bar's `H/L` with the **stop-first** same-bar rule (both touched → stop price wins). An opposite level predicate arriving while holding REVERSES the book at the next open (close at next-open, open opposite at the same next-open, old resting pair cancelled, new pair armed from the new signal bar) — Pine `pyramiding = 0` default reversal pinned as an explicit gate. A same-side level while holding is a no-op (default single-unit pin). The closed side's resting stop+limit order dies with the position (Pine exit-order lifecycle — pinned, never smoothed).
