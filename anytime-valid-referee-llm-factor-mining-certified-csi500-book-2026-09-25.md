## Economic mechanism

### Source-reported

The paper's object is an **adaptive, feedback-coupled candidate stream**: a controller emits factor candidates `(family, parameter)` every 21 days, the market reveals one day of outcomes at a time, and today's candidate depends on everything observed so far (Section 3). It splits quantitative factor research into jobs and assigns them: the agent **proposes**, diagnoses and authors its own diagnostic instruments; a **frozen referee that the agent cannot touch judges**; an execution layer converts admitted factors into positions and is never seen by either (Figure 1, Sections 4-6). The framework is named *governed self-evolution*.

The referee is three betting procedures built on one property — a test supermartingale can be read at any stopping time (Ville's inequality, Section 4.1):

1. **Admission bet (Section 4.2).** Each candidate's post-submission daily cross-sectional Spearman rank-IC stream `X_t in [-1,1]` is whitened (`X̃_t = X_t - ρ̂_t X_{t-1}` with a shrunk, capped, past-only `ρ̂_t`) and staked with the plug-in aGRAPA stake clipped at `λ_max,t = φ/(1+ρ̂_t)` with cap fraction **φ = 0.8**. The null is `E[X̃_t | G_{t-1}] <= 0`. Raw (unwhitened) betting admits 6.0% of zero-marginal-edge factors over ten years at AR(1) ρ=0.2 and 15.4% at ρ=0.4 against a nominal 5%; whitening restores false-admission rates of 1.8 / 2.0 / 2.4% at ρ = 0 / 0.2 / 0.4 (Table 7, Appendix B).
2. **Online e-BH admission (Section 4.3).** Benjamini-Hochberg with capital instead of p-values over a **frozen universe of `N_v = 2000` slots per epoch**, level **α = 0.05**, unit weights; the k-th admission bar is `N_v/(k·α)` (40,000 for the first admission, 20,000 for the second), and a candidate's capital is **frozen at its admission-day value**, so admissions are never revoked. A campaign uses about 490 slots; expired/retired candidates keep their slots; a resubmitted near-copy opens its own slot, so repetition only spends slots and cannot raise FDR above α. Expected wait `T ≈ ln(N_v/(k·α)) · 2σ²/μ²` at the Kelly stake: at the run parameters the first admission of an edge-0.03 factor takes about **546 days**, and at the viability threshold edge 0.015 it takes **1434 days** (1356 for uncapped Kelly) — "no better bettor can shorten the wait near the threshold."
3. **Retirement e-detector (Section 4.4).** Betting against the viability floor `δ_t = δ(1-ρ̂_t)` on `Y_t = δ_t - X̃_t`, with a **new e-process restarted every day after admission** and statistic `M_t = Σ_{j<=t} W_t^(j)`; alarm at `M_t >= A` with the pre-registered **A\* = 1260** trading days (five years). The bound `E_H0[τ_A] >= A` is an average run length and is "vacuous over a ten-year campaign", so reported false-retirement rates are simulation results (0.5-1.5% on ten-year alive streams at ρ<=0.2, 8.5% at ρ=0.4); median detection delay 204-322 days by decay shape vs 181-337 for a Monte-Carlo-calibrated CUSUM (Table 7).

**Guarantees (Sections 4.3-4.5).** FDR over everything ever admitted is at most α **at every stopping time for any proposal policy**, provided the proposer holds no information about the post-submission stream; expected run length to a false retirement is at least A\*. The execution layer is provably downstream and changes none of these guarantees (Proposition 2, Section 5.4): every book can be re-booked from the recorded decision log, and all 220 LLM cells reproduced their referee-level metrics exactly.

**Three deliberately leaky referees** (Section 7.1) each break one assumption the way common practice does: **peeking** (one-sided t-test on the post-submission mean, checked daily after 60 observations, admit at first `p <= α`, i.e. the practitioner's rolling `t > 1.65` rule — breaks optional stopping), **adaptive threshold** (wealth threshold relaxes with candidate age, `α^-1 e^(-age/τ)` — breaks Ville's fixed threshold), and **no gate** (admit every candidate after a fixed 126-trading-day incubation — tests nothing). **Three controllers** (Section 6): C0 round-robin, C1 discounted-UCB bandit over families, C2 LLM controller with direction / diagnosis-after-retirement / probe authoring roles.

**Viability vs certification (Section 4.2, Section 5).** The break-even edge is `δ = 2c·TO/κ`, with per-side cost `c = 15 bp`, one-way turnover `TO`, and the market-level IC-to-return conversion **κ̄ = 0.018** (measured once over the whole panel: 0.016-0.019 for the four book-carrying families). At 15 bp the family break-evens are **value 0.010, liquidity 0.012, volatility 0.017, momentum 0.031, short-horizon reversal 0.074** — an order of magnitude apart — so the single registered **δ = 0.015** is explicitly "a signal-quality floor, not a per-family economic threshold"; whether a certified edge pays is the execution layer's separate question.

**Execution layer (Section 5).** Each deployed factor forms a **quintile long-short sleeve** (long top fifth, short bottom fifth) on its own calendar; the holding frequency is chosen from five desk frequencies — weekly, fortnightly, monthly, bi-monthly, quarterly — scored on the trailing 250 days with `c = 15 bp` and `κ̄ = 0.018`, at first deployment and then at most once every **B = 250** trading days, moving at most one grid step; **daily formation is excluded by design** (costs < 1% per year for the two families that would favour it). A sleeve whose `max_h net(h) <= 0` is certified but **shelved**. Traded sleeves are aggregated at **equal notional**, book one-way turnover is **capped at 25% per day**, an **index-futures leg hedges index beta**, and costs are **15 bp per side on stocks and 5 bp on the hedge, charged on position changes**.

### Research interpretation

- **Primary (governance) hypothesis, falsifiable:** on an adaptive, feedback-coupled stream of factor candidates, the number of false admissions is set by the *adjudication procedure*, not by the proposer — a frozen anytime-valid referee (e-process + online e-BH over a frozen slot budget + daily-restarted e-detector) holds FDR at α against scripts, bandits and LLMs alike, while proposer intelligence appears only in yield and instrument-making. Mechanism class: **selection-bias / multiple-testing control under optional stopping**, i.e. an information-friction claim about research process, not a price-prediction claim.
- **Secondary (market) hypothesis, falsifiable:** a book that holds only factors certified on **daily** rank-IC will be positive-but-thin net of 15 bp and will **trail** an ungated book, for two structural reasons the source identifies: (i) **certification latency** (~500 trading days of dead capital) and (ii) **horizon mis-sorting** — the bar rewards the statistic strongest *per day*, which in CSI 500 is high-turnover short-horizon reversal, while the family that pays most (momentum, IC rising from 0.001 at 1 day to 0.009 at 63 days) cannot be certified at all. In falsifiable terms: **validity and investability are separate axes**, and a portfolio Sharpe ratio is evidence neither for nor against a certificate.
- **Cost-of-validity mechanism:** selectivity and wait are the *same knob* (`N_v/(k·α)`): shrinking the universe, lowering α, or certifying the traded horizon instead of the daily one are the only levers that shorten the wait (Section 9).
- Do not read this record as a profitable strategy claim; the source itself states "The book is an instrument, not a strategy" (Appendix B) and "The portfolio results support no claim of investability" (Section 9).

## Signal

All items below are **source-reported** unless marked `research-proposed` / `research-defined`.

- **Formation timestamp and tradability.** Candidate signals are functions of data known at the close of day `t-1`; the outcome on day `t` is the cross-sectional Spearman rank correlation between the signal at the close of `t-1` and the return on `t`, over the point-in-time universe `U_t`. Everything used on day `t` must be measurable w.r.t. `G_{t-1}` (the "alignment invariant", Section 3). Timezone of the exchange clock is **not stated in source** -> `data gap`.
- **Universe `U_t` (Appendix E).** Stocks whose **index membership was known at `t-1`**, that are tradable and **not limit-locked**: a name that closed at its price limit (`|Δp| >= 9.5%`) on `t-1` is excluded from the day-`t` universe; halts are missing values; membership uses the latest index-weight snapshot dated at or before the previous trading day; prices are vendor-adjusted by the cumulative factor; fundamentals become visible on their announcement date with a **400-day staleness cut**.
- **Lookback / parameter grids (Appendix E, Table 26).** The library is fixed in advance and enumerable, freezing the hypothesis universe as Proposition 1 requires; each submission draws one specification from the grid. Nine families (the four-family library is the first four): **Momentum** `log(P_{t-1-s}/P_{t-1-s-L})`, `L in [40,250]`, skip `s in [0,21]`, 4,642 specs; **Short-horizon reversal** `-log(P_{t-1}/P_{t-1-L})`, `L in [3,30]`, turnover-scaled or not, 56 specs; **Volatility** negative of std / downside std / max of daily returns over `L in [10,130]`, 363 specs; **Liquidity** negative mean turnover over `L in [10,130]` or Amihud illiquidity, 242 specs; **Value** book/earnings/sales/dividends to price smoothed over `L in [1,21]`, 84; **Size** negative log market cap, 42; **Quality** ROE / quarterly ROE / ROA / gross or net margin, 315; **Growth** net profit / revenue / EPS growth y/y, 189; **Investment** negative asset or equity growth y/y, 126. Sign conventions are fixed per family (`++` / `--` after sign flip).
- **Submission cadence and budget.** Four submissions every 21 days; campaigns start in **2016, 2017, 2018 and 2019** and last seven to ten years; five seeds per cell; one epoch with the library frozen; about 490 of the 2,000 slots used per campaign.
- **Entry (certificate, not order).** A candidate is admitted on day `t` iff its frozen capital `E_c(t) >= 1/(k*·α·γ_c)` with `k*` the online-e-BH fixed point; capital only ever rises, so admissions are permanent. `α = 0.05`, `γ_c = 1/2000`.
- **Entry (portfolio order).** The book's target positions come from the admitted set plus the execution layer's sleeve calendars. Execution timing is only stated **relatively**: Appendix B defines the lag convention as "executes the target book decided on day `t` on day `t+1`. It does not execute at the signal's own close", which the source prints as a *variant* of the registered book — so the registered convention executes at the signal's own close and the lag convention delays by one day (residual wording ambiguity in what "the signal's own close" keys to -> `underspecified`). The **carry** convention holds a halted position at its last price and credits the whole gap on the reopening day (Appendix B, Table 6). No order-type, queue-position, partial-fill or latency model exists -> `data gap`.
- **Sizing.** One unit notional per sleeve on its own calendar; traded sleeves combined at equal notional; book one-way turnover capped at 25% per day; index-futures leg hedges index beta; no leverage, gross-exposure or margin schedule is stated -> `data gap` (leverage/margin not modelled).
- **Exit / retirement.** A deployed factor is retired when `M_t >= A* = 1260`; it is **shelved** (stays certified, not traded) when `max_h net(h) <= 0` on the trailing 250-day window; the holding-frequency decision itself may be revisited on first deployment and then **at most once every `B = 250` trading days**, moving at most one grid step. What happens to a name that leaves the index mid-hold is implied by the point-in-time universe but **not printed as an explicit exit rule** -> `underspecified`.
- **Holding period / rebalance cadence.** One of weekly / fortnightly / monthly / bi-monthly / quarterly per sleeve; daily formation excluded by design; overlapping positions across sleeves are combined by equal notional with the turnover cap.
- **Parameters (all pre-registered unless noted).** `α = 0.05`, `N_v = 2000`, `δ = 0.015`, `A* = 1260`, cap fraction `φ = 0.8`, `κ̄ = 0.018` (**in sample** — measured once over the whole panel), `c = 15 bp`, hedge cost `5 bp`, cool-down `B = 250` (selected by the pre-stated "never worst, least churn" rule, not by Sharpe), `ρ̂` shrunk/capped, half-sample band for κ̄ `[0.013, 0.026]` flipping the traded-or-shelved decision for 3.3-7.2% of the 51,531 calibrated sleeves (almost all reversal: 14-19%).
- **Reconstruction status.** The referee, execution layer and book are exactly specified in the text; the **proposer's prompts, model versions and decoding settings are in Appendix D / recorded logs that are not public** -> the LLM arm is `underspecified` from the public record alone.

### Negative evidence

1. **The certified book's alpha is statistically zero.** Newey-West t **never reaches one** in any certified four-family group (α = +0.25 to +1.13 bp/day) and the deflated Sharpe ratio over the study's 108 groups is only **0.49-0.69** — the source's own bar for surviving a search of that size is "near one".
2. **The certified book underperforms the ungated book in every start year** (+0.33..+0.50 vs +0.59..+0.77 net Sharpe); certification is a net *cost* to the book, not a source of return.
3. **A same-patience fixed-horizon comparator builds a better book** (+0.59..+0.65 vs +0.40..+0.49, +0.12..+0.24 in every start year), so anytime-valid sequentiality adds verdict-timing and adaptive-proposer validity, **not** portfolio value at equal patience.
4. **Certification latency is an information bound, not an engineering gap:** median ~500 trading days for a true factor (1434 days at the δ threshold by the source's own formula), the certified book is below water until year six, and 99-100% of certified holding windows sit after 2020-01-02 — the wait is paid in *regimes*, not days.
5. **Horizon mis-sorting:** the daily bar admits high-turnover reversal (net economics worst: daily-reformed reversal nets -19.6%/yr; family break-even 0.074) and excludes momentum (+1.69 bp/day when ungated, 892 sleeves held vs 6 certified) — the certificate is "right about momentum on its own terms" and "wrong for the book".
6. **The headline 5-11x / 7-17x advantage is label-dependent.** Judging only post-admission days moves frozen from 11.7 to 17.6; relabelling against each family's own break-even collapses the advantage to **2-3x** (96.0 vs 180.4 / 197.4 / 276.2).
7. **The registered label itself "leans towards the frozen referee"** — its window starts at submission and overlaps the admission window, which the source flags as favouring late admit-and-correct procedures (Section 9).
8. **No held-out period beyond the walk-forward, one market, four start years sharing most of their data** (Section 9).
9. **Knowledge-cutoff leakage is not addressed by the guarantee:** the LLM arms on real data are **replay outcomes**, outside Proposition 1's hypothesis, so their yield advantages are not instances of the guarantee (Sections 4.5, 7.1, 9).
10. **Cost model is incomplete:** 15 bp/side flat, **no borrow fee**, no spread, slippage, impact, latency, participation or capacity model, and short selling is assumed for an A-share quintile with no borrow list; the certified book already **crosses zero at 50 bp/side (-0.12)** and is only +0.17 at 30 bp.
11. **`κ̄ = 0.018` is in sample** (the single input measured over the whole panel), the execution layer was **constructed after a first pass over the recorded cells**, and books were not re-booked across the κ half-sample band (flips 3.3-7.2% of 51,531 sleeve decisions, 14-19% of reversal sleeves).
12. **No statistical significance is attached to the portfolio alpha**; measured maximum drawdown is **21-25% certified**, capacity and drawdown are explicitly not modelled, and missing returns are booked as zero (12.5% of member-stock days in 2015).
13. **Guarantee scope gaps:** the marginal-null guarantee under autocorrelation is "measured, not proved"; the near-duplicate cluster merge is available but **not exercised**; the retirement bound is vacuous over one campaign, so all reported false-retirement rates are simulation results.
14. **Not ablated:** the LLM controller's diagnosis role is not ablated on its own; the fixed-horizon comparator is a counterfactual on the frozen cell's candidate stream, not an arm with its own campaign; LLM coverage is partial (Table 23).
15. **Reproducibility:** no public code, no public run manifests/prompts/model calls (request-only from the corresponding author), commercial market data that cannot be redistributed — and the authors themselves write that the book "is an instrument, not a strategy" and "supports no claim of investability".
16. **Publication status:** single unrefereed v1 preprint, no journal reference, no peer review stated; the whole design (δ, α, A\*, grids, analysis plan) is pre-registered inside the paper but **not externally time-stamped**, so pre-registration is a self-report.

## Falsification plan

All thresholds below are **research-defined** and all operational choices not printed by the source are **research-proposed**. Each test names data, sample/regime, metric, decision rule and the action on failure.

- **F1 — Second-market transport of the governance claim** (`research-defined`). Data: point-in-time constituents of a second broad index (e.g. CSI 300 or a US small/mid-cap index), >= 7 years, >= 1,500 names, same point-in-time rules. Metric: frozen-vs-no-gate false-admission ratio under a **script** proposer with the registered label. Threshold: ratio >= 2.0 and frozen count <= 50% of no-gate. **Action on failure:** drop the mechanism to "single-market artefact"; keep the referee as a statistical tool, not as evidence for the division-of-labour claim.
- **F2 — Label-overlap audit** (`research-defined`). Re-judge every admitted factor using **only post-admission days** and per-family break-even thresholds. Threshold: frozen-vs-leaky advantage must stay >= 3.0x under post-admission labelling and >= 1.5x under break-even labelling (source reports 5-11x and 2-3x respectively). **Action on failure:** the headline advantage is an artefact of label construction; record the claim as unsupported.
- **F3 — Finite-sample validity / placebo** (`research-defined`). Data: 1,000 circular-shift (and AR(1)-preserving block-shuffle) placebo draws of the post-submission rank-IC streams under the null. Metric: frozen-referee false-admission rate and |z| of the observed rate vs the placebo distribution. Threshold: null false-admission rate <= 8% (source measures 1.8/2.0/2.4% at ρ = 0/0.2/0.4) and |z| < 2.0. **Action on failure:** the anytime-valid guarantee does not hold in finite samples on this data; suspend certificate-based admission.
- **F4 — Cost ladder with borrow and spread** (`research-proposed` cost lines; `research-defined` thresholds). Re-book every certified and ungated book at **0/15/25/30/50 bp per side**, plus **50 bp/yr stock-loan fee on the short leg** and a paid-spread (mid-to-ask) variant, with participation capped at **20% ADV**. Threshold: certified net Sharpe > 0 at 15 bp and no sign flip below 30 bp; certified-vs-ungated ordering must not reverse. **Action on failure:** withdraw "does not lose money at the registered cost"; the certificate has no investable expression.
- **F5 — Alpha significance floor** (`research-defined`). Sample: >= 7 years point-in-time, CH-3-style three-factor regression with Newey-West lag >= 5, plus BH q < 0.10 over all book-level tests and deflated Sharpe >= 0.95 across the whole group grid. Threshold: certified-book alpha |t| >= 2.0 **and** DSR >= 0.95 (source: t < 1, DSR 0.49-0.69). **Action on failure:** no portfolio claim is permitted; the record stays a governance-only finding.
- **F6 — Horizon-matched certification ablation** (`research-proposed` rule, `research-defined` threshold). Certify the **traded-horizon** statistic `mean_{k=1..h} IC(k)` for each desk frequency instead of daily IC(1), all else frozen. Threshold: certified book net Sharpe improves by >= 0.15 vs the registered book **and** momentum admissions rise from 0.5 to >= 3 per campaign. **Action on failure:** reject the paper's "horizon mismatch" explanation of the Sharpe gap and re-attribute it to latency or to family economics.
- **F7 — Same-patience comparator horse race** (`research-defined`). Run the 500-day one-sided t-test + BH(0.05) comparator as a **stand-alone arm with its own campaign** (not a counterfactual on the frozen stream). Threshold: if it beats the frozen referee by > 0.10 net Sharpe in >= 3 of 4 start years (source: +0.12..+0.24 in 4 of 4), then anytime-valid sequentiality is **not** the value driver for the book. **Action:** adopt the fixed-horizon procedure for portfolio construction; retain the e-process referee only for verdict-on-any-day and adaptive-proposer validity.
- **F8 — Proposer-invariance test** (`research-defined`). Cross at least one additional proposer family (genetic/grammar-guided miner, human desk) with frozen and leaky referees on identical slots and seeds. Threshold: frozen-referee false-admission count must vary by < 2x across proposers while leaky arms vary by >= 3x (source: proposer changes the count 2-2.5x on leaky arms, 0 on frozen). **Action on failure:** the "referee alone sets false admissions" claim fails.
- **F9 — Live forward window with post-cutoff models** (`research-defined`). Run the LLM controller **live** on a window that post-dates every model's training cutoff, five seeds, four submissions/21 days, at least 24 months. Threshold: LLM yield advantage over the discounted-UCB bandit must be > 0.02 and positive in >= 3 of 4 blocks. **Action on failure:** the replay yield advantage was knowledge-cutoff leakage; strip the LLM from the claim.
- **F10 — κ and execution-layer out-of-sample audit** (`research-defined`). Freeze `κ̄`, the five-frequency grid and `B = 250` on data ending at the original campaign end, then re-book forward only. Threshold: the traded-or-shelved decision must flip for < 5% of sleeves and certified net Sharpe must stay within ±0.10 of the frozen value (source's in-sample κ band already flips 3.3-7.2%, concentrated in reversal at 14-19%). **Action on failure:** the execution layer is overfit to the calibration window; re-estimate prospectively before any investability discussion.

## Crypto portability

**unproven.**

The *procedure* (per-candidate e-process, online e-BH over a frozen slot budget, daily-restarted e-detector, execution layer separated from certification) is market-agnostic in form and could in principle be pointed at crypto cross-sectional signals. The **empirical content is not**: every reported number comes from CSI 500 point-in-time A-share constituents with daily rank-IC, a 9.5% limit-move tradability screen, an index-futures beta hedge, and a fundamental library (book/earnings/quality/growth/investment) that has no crypto equivalent.

Crypto-specific portability risks: **24/7 sessions** remove the close-of-t-1 -> return-on-t clock and the "previous trading day" alignment invariant; **perpetual funding, mark/index price, liquidation and borrow** are entirely absent from the source's cost model (and the source already omits equity borrow fees), while crypto short legs face venue-specific borrow/edge pricing; **venue fragmentation and survivorship** break the single point-in-time index-membership universe; **limit-move screens** have no analogue (halts are continuous), so the null stream's autocorrelation structure — which the whitening step exists to fix — would be different; the **~500-day certification latency** at daily-rank-IC noise (σ ≈ 0.12) would face much faster signal decay and regime turnover in crypto. The certification-latency-vs-horizon-mismatch hypothesis is a **ported hypothesis**, not crypto evidence.
