## Economic mechanism

### Source-reported

Multi-EMA trend-following with momentum-continuation entries (strategy README: `uses the alignment of three exponential moving averages`; `Entries are taken when price bounces off the fast EMA while all three EMAs are properly aligned, indicating strong trend momentum`; `Trading Style: Momentum continuation`): in a stacked uptrend the long leg buys the cross back above the fast EMA; in a stacked downtrend the short leg sells the cross back below the fast EMA; either leg exits when the fast EMA crosses the mid EMA (alignment breaks). There is deliberately no stop, no target, no trailing exit, and no flat state beyond pre-signal and post-exit gaps — the order block ends with the two `strategy.close` guards and no risk leg ships anywhere, by design gap rather than by hidden leg.

### Research interpretation

Alignment-gated fast-EMA-cross trend-continuation, two-sided with stack-break exits — the first ribbon record in the pool. Unlike two-line cross systems that trade the moment one line passes another, this rule never trades an EMA-EMA cross for entry: the alignment inequalities are a standing permission (the stack must already be ordered), and the only entry trigger is price crossing the fast EMA — so the system buys pullback resolutions inside established trends rather than the trend birth itself. Unlike the pool's Guppy record, the stack is three EMAs with strict pairwise ordering (not 22 rank-ordered lines with a latch), and the exit is a single fast-under/over-mid cross (not an opposite-transition reversal). Note the two pinned asymmetries, stated plainly: first, a close crossing the fast EMA against a disordered or oppositely ordered stack fires nothing — counter-trend and chop crosses are ignored by construction, so sideways markets with tangled EMAs produce long flat stretches; second, the exit needs the fast EMA to travel all the way across the mid EMA — a trend that stalls without unstacking is held with no profit-taking leg, and a leg that extends vertically keeps no trailing guard. The bet is on ordered-stack persistence at frozen `9/21/55` geometry, not on any breakout extreme, volume print, calendar effect, or second timeframe.
