## Economic mechanism

The original indicator highlights a wick probing a previously confirmed swing level but failing to close beyond it. This **researcher's** trading hypothesis is to enter a single spot long after a sell-side (low) raid and leave it after a buy-side (high) raid. A raid is a price-pattern observation, **not** proof of institutional stop orders, liquidity or profitable reversals. This model can hold through large adverse moves because all protective exits are deliberately disabled.

## Signal — fully frozen derived rules

- Input is one uninterrupted sequence of **completed** Binance spot BTC-USDT UTC daily OHLCV rows indexed `t=0,1,...`. All OHLC values must be finite and strictly positive, `L<=min(O,C)<=max(O,C)<=H`, volume finite and nonnegative. Consecutive exchange **open** timestamps must differ by exactly 86400 seconds. Missing, duplicate, nonmonotonic, invalid or partial candles **abort the run** before feature joins/dropna; no interpolation, fabricated price or compressed bar count.
- `P=5`. At the close of bar `t>=10`, consider candidate pivot center `j=t-5`. A strict **low** pivot exists iff `low[j] < low[k]` for **every** `k` in `[j-5,j+5]` except `j`; a strict **high** pivot exists iff `high[j] > high[k]` for all other `k` in that window. Equal competing extrema invalidate the candidate. A pivot at `j` is not knowable before completed bar `j+5`; it is never backdated as a trade signal. No use of later data.
- Keep `activeLow=(price,pivotIndex,confirmedIndex)` and `activeHigh` or NONE, **one per side**. On each completed bar, first evaluate the *previously active* levels, then discover/replace each side with any **newly confirmed** pivot; a new pivot becomes sweep-eligible **only on the following bar**. Latest confirmed pivot replaces the previous level even if the previous level was unswept. No active level is inferred from future bars or historical drawings.
- For the preexisting activeLow: `SSL[t]=(low[t] < activeLow.price && close[t] >= activeLow.price)`; if true consume that level once. Else if `close[t] < activeLow.price`, invalidate it. Otherwise retain. For activeHigh: `BSL[t]=(high[t] > activeHigh.price && close[t] <= activeHigh.price)`; if true consume it once. Else if `close[t] > activeHigh.price`, invalidate it. Otherwise retain. Strict wick breach; equality of close with the level **qualifies** as rejection, equality of wick with level does not. Sweep and break are mutually exclusive for the same side. No volume filter or intrabar order.
- **Warmup:** bars `t=0..49` build/consume pivot levels and validate data but issue no orders. First eligible signal is completed bar `t=50`; first possible simulated fill is `t=51`. Initial state FLAT, no inherited position. If both SSL and BSL occur on the same bar, consume both levels but generate **no trade signal** (ambiguous two-sided sweep).
- Trading state `FLAT | LONG` plus `PENDING_BUY | PENDING_SELL | NONE`. When FLAT, no pending and an unambiguous SSL occurs on an eligible bar `s`, queue exactly one BUY for **the next completed bar `s+1`**. When LONG, no pending and an unambiguous BSL occurs, queue one full SELL for `s+1`. Other-side sweeps still consume their levels but cannot cause an order in the wrong state. No orders on the signal bar. On the scheduled fill bar, process/consume levels and discover new pivots as normal, but suppress **new signals** to prevent a same-bar round trip. Clear pending after its one attempted fill; no deferred retry.
- A successful, full, validated BUY fill changes FLAT -> LONG; a successful full SELL of the recorded unit changes LONG -> FLAT. Invalid/missing price, missing successor bar, failed/partial/unmatched fill, insufficient cash or mismatched quantity: **fail closed**; no phantom exposure, no synthetic fill and no re-armed pending order. A new trade requires a fresh subsequent eligible sweep. Terminal pending is unfilled, and terminal LONG is marked to the last observed close, not forcibly liquidated.
- There are **no** stop-loss, take-profit, trailing, timed exit, opposite short, pyramiding, DCA/safety orders, averaging, partial exits, compounding, discretionary filters, cooldown, concurrent market, leverage, hedge, borrowing or liquidation instructions. This omission is an explicit risk-bearing research design, not a hidden Hummingbot default.
