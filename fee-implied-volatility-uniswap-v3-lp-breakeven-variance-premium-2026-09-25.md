## Economic mechanism

### Source-reported

The source builds one chain of identities and one empirical illustration:

1. **Theta of active liquidity equals LVR** (Proposition 3): writing a concentrated-liquidity position as a short strip of European options and taking the short-maturity concentration of Black-Scholes theta gives aggregate theta per unit time `Theta_LP(sigma) = (1/2) sigma^2 P^2 |V''(P)| = sigma^2 L sqrt(P) / 4`, which is exactly the instantaneous loss-versus-rebalancing of the same liquidity in Milionis–Moallemi–Roughgarden–Zhang. The source's phrasing: "theta and LVR are one function of volatility" that differ only in which volatility is plugged in (pricing volatility for theta, realized volatility for LVR).
2. **Depth density** `delta(P) = dy/d ln P = L sqrt(P)/2` (Equation 20) so that `Theta_LP(sigma) = (1/2) sigma^2 delta`.
3. **Fee implied volatility** (Definition 1, Equations 22–23): over a window `[t0,t1]` of length `Delta` years, the constant volatility at which aggregate theta of active liquidity equals realized fee income `F = gamma*V` is `sigma_fee^2 = 2 gamma V / (Delta * delta_bar)`. Units work out to an annualized volatility with no further scaling (365-day year, `Delta = 1/365` for daily windows). Remark 3: `sigma_fee^2` is four times the fee rate times the annualized turnover of the virtual numeraire reserve `2 delta = L sqrt(P)`.
4. **Breakeven reading** (Proposition 4, Equations 25–27): with `Pi = F - (1/2) integral sigma_t^2 delta_t dt` (the LP's value relative to a rebalancing strategy, "up to corrections that vanish with the fee"), substituting Definition 1 gives `Pi = (1/2) integral delta_t (sigma_fee^2 - sigma_t^2) dt`. So **the LP beats rebalancing over a window if and only if `sigma_fee^2` exceeds the depth-weighted average realized variance**, and the fee-to-LVR ratio `alpha = F / integral (1/2) sigma_t^2 delta_t dt = sigma_fee^2 / sigma_bar^2` is the AMM counterpart of the profit of a delta-hedged short option: fees are theta collected at `sigma_fee`, LVR is gamma cost paid at realized volatility. `alpha` is unbounded above and `alpha > 1` exactly when LPs outperform rebalancing.
5. **When it estimates external volatility** (Section 7.2, Proposition 5, Corollary 1): under A1 (constant external vol/drift), A2 (costless continuous arbitrage keeping `|ln(S/P)| <= g = ln(1 + gamma/(1-gamma))`), A3 (all swaps are arbitrage swaps), A4 (constant depth), `E[sigma_fee^2] = kappa(gamma) (1 + rho) sigma^2` with `kappa = gamma_bar / ln(1 + gamma_bar) = 1 + gamma_bar/2 + O(gamma_bar^2)` and `|rho| <= 2|mu|g/sigma^2`; for the ETH/USDC 30 bps pool `kappa ~ 1.0015` and even with 100%/yr drift and `sigma^2 = 0.3`, `|rho| ~ 0.02`. Corollary 1: the fee tier cancels beyond `kappa(gamma)`; arbitrage fee income equals `kappa(1+rho)` times LVR so LPs break even against rebalancing up to order-gamma terms; and `sigma_fee` is a consistent estimator of `sigma` **in that benchmark**.
6. **Two opposing biases** (Section 7.3, Equations 29–32): partitioning swaps into arbitrage volume `V_A` and other volume `V_N` gives the exact identity `sigma_fee^2 = sigma_fee,A^2 (1 + V_N/V_A)`, so **uninformed flow inflates `sigma_fee` by the ratio of total to arbitrage volume**, while gas costs, priority fees, arbitrage latency and discrete block times widen the effective band `g_eff > g` and **deflate it**: `sigma_fee,A^2 ~ kappa (g/g_eff) sigma^2`. Combining, `alpha ~ kappa(gamma) (g/g_eff) (1 + V_N/V_A)`. The block correction is `g_eff ~ g + beta sigma sqrt(tau)` with `beta = |zeta(1/2)|/sqrt(2 pi) ~ 0.5826`; in the source's simulations at `sigma = 55%` per year with 12-second blocks it matches `E[sigma_TV^2]/sigma^2` to within 0.2% in all three original tiers, implying a downward `sigma_fee` bias of about 15% (5 bps tier), 3% (30 bps tier) and under 1% (1% tier). Because the fee tier shapes who trades a pool, cross-tier differences in `sigma_fee` measure `V_N/V_A` and `g_eff` rather than differences in `sigma` (Section 9.5).
7. **Economic interpretation the source states:** the excess of fees over LVR "is the AMM counterpart of a variance risk premium: it is the compensation LPs require to supply liquidity, and in the benchmark it is paid by uninformed flow. Unlike its options market counterpart it is measured on realized rather than quoted quantities" (Section 8).

### Research interpretation

The falsifiable hypothesis this record normalizes (all operationalization below is **`research-proposed`**, not printed by the source):

- **H1 — DEX-native variance-premium harvest (`research-proposed`).** On a concentrated-liquidity pool, `alpha = fee income / LVR` is an expected-above-one quantity because uninformed flow pays fees without contributing to the external variance term. Roles: *state variable* = `sigma_fee` computed from pool depth and fee flow; *cost benchmark* = LVR at reference realized variance; *entry* = provide concentrated liquidity while the trailing estimate of `alpha` is expected above 1; *exit* = withdraw or widen when it is expected below 1; *risk layer* = LVR, repositioning gas, MEV/JIT adverse selection (none of which the source prices). The source's own Table 1 is consistent with H1 on one pool (median `alpha_hat = 1.18`, `alpha_hat > 1` on 68.26% of days) but does not test it as a trading rule.
- **H2 — oracle-free realized-vol proxy (`research-proposed`, evidence partial).** `sigma_fee` (or the liquidity-free `sigma_TV`) is a pool-native estimator of realized volatility usable without any external oracle; the source reports only a levels correlation of 0.87 and explicitly says the informative tests — correlation of changes and forecasting content beyond own lags — are the ones still to be run (Section 8 and research direction 3 in Section 10.3). Those tests are therefore `research-defined` below, not source-reported.
- **H3 — identification caveat as a first-class claim.** From pool data alone, `sigma_fee` is exact only as a *breakeven* volatility; treating it as external `sigma` requires classifying swaps against a reference price to estimate `V_N/V_A` and `g_eff` (Section 7.4). Any strategy that uses `sigma_fee` as if it were CEX implied volatility is therefore unsupported by the source (Section 10.2).

Do not assume each component contributes alpha: H1 (LP premium) and H2 (vol proxy) can fail independently, and the record keeps them separately falsifiable.

## Signal

**Source-reported (estimator definitions only — the source specifies no trading rule):**

- **Formation timestamp / tradability:** `sigma_fee` is computed over a completed window `[t0,t1]`; it uses only swaps and pool state inside that window, so it becomes available at `t1` (block-level pool state, on-chain, UTC). It is a *realized* quantity over the window, not a forward quote (Section 10.2). Timezone/availability convention beyond "block level pool state" is not stated (`underspecified`).
- **Lookback / window:** window length `Delta` in years with a 365-day year "since the market trades continuously"; the illustration uses daily windows, `Delta = 1/365` (Section 9.2). `V[t0,t1]` = swap input converted to numeraire at execution price; `delta_bar = (1/Delta) integral delta_u du` with `delta_u` from **block-level pool state** (not end-of-window snapshots).
- **Depth density (Units, Section 9.1):** `delta = L sqrt(P)/2` when the numeraire is token1 and `delta = L/(2 sqrt(P))` when the numeraire is token0, with `P` the raw pool price, converted with token decimals (mainnet ETH/USDC pools: token0 = USDC, token1 = WETH). Remark 4 explicitly forbids reading tick liquidity as raw Uniswap `L` (dimensionally inconsistent and decimals-dependent); the equivalent `sigma_fee = 2 gamma sqrt(V/(Delta D_bar))` form uses `D` = numeraire depth over a band of log width `2 gamma`, which for the three original tiers equals one tick spacing to first order.
- **Liquidity-free variant (Section 9.3, Proposition 6):** `sigma_TV^2 = 2 gamma_bar TV / Delta` where `TV = sum_i |Delta ln P_i|` runs over swaps in the window (each swap moves the pool log price monotonically; mints/burns do not move price). Needs only the fee rate and post-swap pool price — no liquidity data.
- **Tradeable comparison the source prints (Section 8):** `alpha_hat_RV(t) = (sigma_fee(t)/RV(t))^2`, the empirical counterpart of `alpha`, with `RV` the realized volatility of ETH spot over the same window.
- **Long/short entry, exit, holding period, re-entry, sizing, parameters:** **not specified by the source — `underspecified`.** The source states no entry threshold, no exit rule, no holding period, no position sizing, no rebalance cadence for a strategy, and no parameter is tuned (there is nothing to tune in the estimator itself beyond window length and fee tier).
- **Scout operationalization, all `research-proposed` (explicitly not source-reported):** *entry* — supply a concentrated range on the pool when the trailing 7-day median `alpha_hat_RV` (computed from data through `t-1`) exceeds 1.05; *exit* — withdraw or widen to full range when the trailing 7-day median falls below 1.00 or when the reference-price RV over the position window makes ex-post `alpha < 1`; *holding* — minimum 7 days, maximum 30 days, daily re-evaluation; *sizing* — fixed fraction of a pre-declared LP budget per pool, capped at a `research-defined` 20% of pool TVL; *re-entry* — after a 2-day cooldown following any exit. Thresholds (1.05 / 1.00 / 7-day / 30-day / 20% TVL) are `research-defined` scout choices chosen only to make the hypothesis falsifiable; the source supports none of them.
- **Alternate, non-LP use (`research-proposed`):** use `sigma_fee` as an oracle-free RV state variable for volatility timing against a quoted options venue. The source warns (Section 10.2) that comparing `sigma_fee` with a CEX implied-vol index compares a realized breakeven with a forward quote and the gap mixes the variance risk premium with flow-composition and friction noise — so this use is a hypothesis, not a source claim.

- **Section 10.3 research directions the source itself sets:** (1) classify swaps against a reference price to estimate `V_N/V_A` and `g_eff` and form the corrected estimator `sigma_hat = sigma_fee * (kappa g/g_eff (1 + V_N/V_A))^(-1/2)`; (2) test fee-tier invariance on ETH/USDC at 5 and 30 bps after the block correction; (3) compare `sigma_fee` and `sigma_TV` with a forward-looking benchmark and test their forecasting content for realized volatility.
- **Publication/venue status:** preprint only; no peer review; no journal; no DOI beyond the DataCite arXiv DOI.

### Negative evidence

1. **The identification result itself is negative for the CEX-analogue reading:** from pool observables alone, `g/g_eff` and `V_N/V_A` are unobservable because classifying a swap as arbitrage requires knowing where the external price was when it executed; `sigma` is therefore identified only up to those factors (Section 7.4). The source's own v1 abstract went further and concluded the quantity is "a fee implied activity index, not a Black-Scholes consistent implied volatility".
2. **Headline inconsistency across the pinned version:** landing page/API/PDF-metadata title and abstract (v1 text, negative conclusion) contradict the v2 PDF body title and abstract (positive conclusion, Corr 0.87, median 1.18). Anyone citing this paper from its landing page will quote the opposite conclusion from the one in the PDF — recorded in frontmatter `contradictions`.
3. **Correlation in levels is admitted to be weak evidence:** the source states "Correlation in levels between two persistent volatility series is weak evidence on its own, and part of the comovement is mechanical, since same-day arbitrage volume is generated by same-day price moves". The informative tests (correlation of changes; forecasting beyond own lags) are **not run** — `data gap`, not a result.
4. **Single pool, single period, single fee tier** — the source says so: "this is a single pool over a single period. A systematic study across pools and fee tiers, with swaps classified against a reference price, is the natural next step."
5. **Zero statistical inference:** no t-statistic, p-value, confidence interval, bootstrap, significance test, seed or error bar appears anywhere in the pinned PDF (word scan counts all zero). Every number in Table 1 is a point summary.
6. **`RV` is an imperfect benchmark:** "Realized volatility is a noisy estimate of the depth weighted variance in (27), so daily values of `alpha_hat` are noisy estimates of `alpha`" — so the 68.26% share and the 1.18 median are noisy proxies for the economic claim.
7. **Mechanical spike contamination:** `alpha_hat` is a squared ratio (bounded below at 0, unbounded above) and any fee-window/RV-window timing mismatch spikes it upward precisely during volatility jumps; the trimmed mean (1.30 vs 1.33) mitigates but does not remove this.
8. **No cost model for actually being an LP:** gas for repositioning, priority fees, MEV/JIT adverse selection, impermanent-loss realization on exit and opportunity cost are absent; the paper's `Pi` is explicitly "up to corrections that vanish with the fee". A median gross `alpha_hat` of 1.18 therefore has no published net-of-cost counterpart.
9. **No strategy, no backtest, no turnover, no drawdown, no capacity:** word scan finds `backtest` 0 and `Sharpe` 0. Nothing in the source evidences that H1 or H2 is tradable.
10. **Block/friction correction is large where it matters most:** ~15% downward bias in the 5 bps tier and the source's own warning that "the continuous benchmark ... should not be applied to the 5 bps tier without this correction"; the ~6% `alpha` reduction at 12-second blocks is not included in the reported 1.18/1.33/68.26% figures.
11. **Fee-tier and pool differences are not volatility differences:** cross-tier `sigma_fee` differences "measure differences in `V_N/V_A` and `g_eff` rather than differences in `sigma`" (Section 9.5) — a direct obstacle to cross-pool or cross-chain signal construction.
12. **Units trap:** reading tick liquidity as raw Uniswap `L` is explicitly inadmissible (Remark 4) — an implementation that gets the numeraire/token-decimal convention wrong produces a silently wrong `sigma_fee`.
13. **No reproducibility artifacts:** no code, no data, no vendor, no pool address, no `RV` construction method, no data-availability statement (word scan `github` 0). Independent replication must be rebuilt from chain data from scratch.
14. **Cross-record contrary/adjacent evidence in this repository:** `crypto-amm-loss-versus-rebalancing-lvr-toxic-arbitrage-2026-08-31.md` frames LVR as adverse selection from toxic arbitrage (a cost that must be paid, not a premium received); `defi-amm-impermanent-gain-zone-fee-floor-lp-arbitrageur-symbiosis-2026-09-18.md` derives ex-ante fee-floor profitability zones for LPs on CPMMs; `defi-concentrated-liquidity-stochastic-impulse-control-tail-risk-2026-09-02.md` and `defi-amm-continuous-installment-options-lvr-delta-hedge-2026-09-01.md` both treat LVR as a quantity to be minimized or replicated rather than a leg that fees demonstrably cover; `fmz-robinhood-chain-v3v4-active-pool-lp-fee-cost-coverage-549380-2026-09-19.md` is a heuristic fee-vs-cost coverage screen with `confidence: low` and no evidence that coverage survives costs.
15. **Metadata staleness is not cosmetic:** the arXiv comment still claims 20 pages while the pinned v2 has 23, so checksum-level verification and prose-level description of this source disagree on basic bibliographic facts.

## Falsification plan

Every threshold below is a `research-defined` acceptance/failure cutoff and every rule is `research-proposed`; none is printed by the source. Failure action in each case: downgrade the hypothesis to rejected-for-now and record the failed cell as negative evidence (no unconstrained retuning).

- **F1 — Multi-pool point-in-time replication (`research-defined`).** Rebuild `sigma_fee` (Equation 23) and `sigma_TV` (Equation 34) from raw chain data for ETH/USDC at 5 bps and 30 bps, WBTC/USDC 30 bps and one 1% tier pool, over ≥3 non-overlapping 12-month windows covering at least two volatility regimes. **Fail** if level correlation with reference RV is < 0.70 on any pool, or if the reported sign/magnitude of the fee-to-LVR median does not replicate (median `alpha_hat` ≤ 1.00 on ≥2 of 3 pools).
- **F2 — The test the source says is informative (`research-defined`).** Daily first differences of `sigma_fee` vs RV must show positive correlation ≥ 0.30, and `sigma_fee` at `t-1` must add positive `Delta R^2` over an AR(1)/HAR-RV baseline for RV over horizons h ∈ {1, 7, 30} days in ≥2 of 3 horizons. **Fail** if differenced correlation ≤ 0 or if `Delta R^2 ≤ 0` at h = 1 and h = 7.
- **F3 — Net-of-cost LP accounting (`research-defined`).** Recompute `alpha` after charging repositioning gas (p50 and p90 from chain data), a `research-proposed` 20%-of-gas priority tip, and MEV/JIT leakage estimated as the difference between quoted and realized entry price. **Fail** if median net `alpha` ≤ 1.00 on ≥2 of 3 pools, or if the strategy's net result is within ±0.05 of the same-period passive full-range LP.
- **F4 — Friction-corrected estimator (`research-defined`).** Implement Section 10.3 direction 1: classify swaps against a reference price, estimate `V_N/V_A` and `g_eff`, form `sigma_hat`. **Fail** (i.e., the raw estimator is not usable as external vol) if `sigma_hat` does not reduce median absolute error versus RV by ≥30% relative to raw `sigma_fee`, or if corrected `alpha` falls ≤ 1.00.
- **F5 — Fee-tier invariance (`research-defined`, source's own research direction 2).** After the Equation (31) block correction, ETH/USDC `sigma_fee` at 5 bps vs 30 bps must agree within 10% median relative difference over overlapping days. **Fail** if the gap stays > 10%, which would confirm the estimator is measuring flow composition rather than `sigma`.
- **F6 — Placebo / mechanical-comovement control (`research-defined`).** Circularly shift the fee window against the RV window in 1,000 random draws. **Fail** if the observed levels correlation of 0.87 lies inside the placebo 95% band, or if the observed median `alpha_hat` lies inside the placebo band — that would show the reported comovement is window alignment, not information.
- **F7 — Window-mismatch robustness (`research-defined`).** Recompute `alpha_hat` on aligned UTC windows at Δ ∈ {1, 3, 7, 30} days. **Fail** if the median exceeds 1.00 only at Δ = 1 day, or if the 1-day median exceeds the 30-day median by more than 0.20 (spike artefact).
- **F8 — Regime breakdown (`research-defined`).** Split the sample into RV terciles. **Fail** H1 if median `alpha_hat > 1` holds in fewer than 2 of 3 terciles — the premium would then be regime-bound rather than structural.
- **F9 — Statistical floor (`research-defined`).** 20-day moving-block bootstrap (1,000 draws) on the daily `alpha_hat` series: the 95% CI of the median must exclude 1.00, and Newey-West t ≥ 2.0 on the mean of `(alpha_hat − 1)`. **Fail** otherwise; report Benjamini-Hochberg q across all printed cells (F1–F8) with q < 0.10 required for any "confirmed" label.
- **F10 — Competing-explanation horse race (`research-defined`).** Regress `alpha_hat` on (i) window-mismatch magnitude, (ii) `V_N/V_A` proxy from swap-direction imbalance, (iii) RV level. **Fail** if (i) alone explains > 50% of `alpha_hat` variance, or if after partialling out (i) and (iii) the residual median is ≤ 1.00.
- **F11 — Liquidity-free variant agreement (`research-defined`).** `sigma_TV` and `sigma_fee` must agree within 10% median relative difference; **fail** if they diverge more, since then the depth data (and its units conventions) are driving the result.
- **F12 — Capacity and breadth (`research-defined`).** Extend to ≥10 pools across ≥2 chains and require the fee-to-LVR median > 1.00 in ≥7 of 10 pools, plus a `research-proposed` cap that no test LP position exceeds 20% of pool TVL; **fail** H1 if fewer than 7 pools qualify or if the top-3 pools by TVL all show median ≤ 1.00.
- **F13 — Out-of-sample freeze (`research-defined`).** Freeze the rule (window, threshold 1.05/1.00, 7–30 day holding) before 2026-10-01 and evaluate only on data after that date for ≥6 months. **Fail** if cumulative net `alpha ≤ 1.00` or if the out-of-sample median `alpha_hat` is below 1.00.

## Crypto portability

**direct.** The source is itself crypto-native: Uniswap v3 concentrated liquidity on Ethereum mainnet, ETH/USDC, 365-day annualization, block-level state, and an ETH spot realized-volatility benchmark. Nothing needs to be ported from traditional assets.

Crypto-specific risks that must still be handled explicitly:

- **24/7 sessions and clock conventions:** the source uses a 365-day year and block timestamps; candle boundaries, UTC-day alignment of the fee window versus the RV window, and out-of-order/reorged blocks are `underspecified` (F7 exists precisely for this).
- **Fee tier, tick spacing and token ordering:** `sigma_fee` depends on the numeraire convention and token decimals (Remark 4, Section 9.1); the 5 bps tier carries a ~15% downward bias; Uniswap v4 hook-based dynamic fees require replacing `gamma*V` with realized fee income `F` (Section 6.1).
- **No funding/liquidation leg unless we add one:** pure LP exposure has no funding and no liquidation, but a delta-neutral wrapper or perpetual hedge (`research-proposed`) would add funding every 8 hours, mark-price basis and liquidation risk — none of which the source models.
- **Venue fragmentation:** `sigma_fee` is pool-specific by construction (Section 9.5); cross-pool/cross-chain comparisons measure flow composition and frictions, not volatility, so "the" `sigma_fee` for ETH does not exist as a single number.
- **Liquidity, MEV and JIT:** entry into a concentrated range is exposed to just-in-time liquidity and sandwich activity (see the repository's JIT record); capacity is `data gap`.
- **Point-in-time and survivorship:** pool selection must be point-in-time (pools are created and abandoned constantly); the source studies one long-lived pool only.
- Crypto portability being `direct` is not authorization to trade; it only means the source's own evidence is crypto evidence.

## Limitations

- `underspecified` — no entry, exit, holding, sizing, rebalance or execution rule exists in the source; all such content in this record is `research-proposed`.
- `underspecified` — the `RV` construction (source venue, sampling frequency, close convention) and the pool address/data vendor are not stated.
- `data gap` — no code, no data, no data-availability statement, no data vendor; independent replication starts from raw chain data.
- `data gap` — no slippage, funding, market-impact, capacity, borrow, fill or leverage model; gas and priority fees are named but unquantified; LVR is modeled but the LP's realized exit economics are only approximated "up to corrections that vanish with the fee".
- `not independently reproduced` — every number is third-party, single-pool, point-summary evidence with zero inferential statistics.
- `unproven` — H1 (tradable fee-to-LVR premium) and H2 (forecast content of `sigma_fee`) are hypotheses; the source reports association on one pool, not a tested rule.
- Source-quality limitation: preprint with no peer review, a single author, self-cited prior construction (ref [11]) and internally inconsistent title/abstract metadata across versions.
- Publication-bias/selection concern: one pool, chosen presumably because the data were available; the 11.33 maximum of `alpha_hat` and the spike structure warn against reading the median as a robust central parameter.
- Identification limitation: `sigma_fee` cannot separate uninformed flow from friction without an external reference price, so any "DEX implied vol vs CEX implied vol" spread mixes variance risk premium with measurement error (Section 10.2).
- Scope limitation: the estimator is `realized`, not forward-looking; using it as a forward vol input is out of scope of the source's own claims.
