## Economic mechanism

### Source-reported

Trend-continuation pullback-reclaim with a momentum-confirmation gate, by the page's own condition list: the Coral line (a six-stage smoothed-price cascade) must first print a whole candle on the trend side (C2 — proof the leg is real, not the cross itself), then price must pull back across the line while the line never flips colour (C3+C4 — a counter-trend excursion inside a stable regime, not a regime change), then price must reclaim the line (C5 — continuation trigger), confirmed by rising ADX above 20 with the trend-side DI on top (C6 — the move has expanding directional energy). Risk is a fixed-geometry box: the stop is the 5-bar swing extreme, the target is 1.5× that distance — every trade risks the local structure and asks one-and-a-half structures back. The script ships no regime theory beyond the C1–C6 list, and no performance figure is adopted (the "80% win rate" video title is a third-party claim, never a record fact).

### Research interpretation

Unlike pool slope-flip records (bare smoothed-line flips — this machine never trades the flip itself; the flip only *vetoes* stale pullbacks via C4), unlike pool band-reclaim records (band edges as the reference — here a single adaptive cascade line is both the excursion meter and the trigger, with the C2 whole-candle precondition no pool record carries), and unlike pool ADX-gated cross records (ADX gating a moving-average cross — here ADX gates a *reclaim after a validated pullback*, a strictly narrower event). The edge object is leg × excursion × reclaim × energy: C2 proves the leg, C3+C4 isolate a same-regime excursion, C5 times the continuation, C6.1 requires measurable directional expansion. Live-window signature is SL-leaning by construction: 131 entries / 131 exits across 62,399 post-warmup 1h bars (71 SL / 59 TP / 1 reversal — the 5-bar extreme sits closer than the 1.5× target in 1h noise, disclosed, not tuned). Signals are rare by design (50 long / 87 short bars in 62,699 bars — roughly one setup per 18 days, disclosed, never presented as a activity claim).

- **Predicates (actionable only t ≥ 300, at bar `t`'s close, Pine `barssince`-including-`t` semantics with the script's own `[1]` lag on the pre-pullback distance):** `bull[t] = bfr[t] > bfr[t−1]`, `bear[t] = bfr[t] < bfr[t−1]`; `co[t]/cu[t]` = strict close-vs-line crossovers; `wholeAbove[t] = L[t] > bfr[t] and H[t] > bfr[t]` (mirror below); `preOK_long[t] = since(wholeAbove)[t] < since(co)[t−1]`; `valid_long[t] = since(cu)[t] < since(slope-flip-up)[t]`; `entry_long[t] = co[t]`; `conf_long[t] = DI+[t] > DI−[t] and ADX[t] > 20 and ADX[t] > ADX[t−1]`; `goLong[t]` = conjunction of all six with `bull[t]` (mirror for short with `crossunder`, below-leg, `DIMinus > DIPlus`). - **Dynamic box (levels frozen at the signal bar, never moved inside the test bar):** long `SL = lowest low over t−4..t`, `TP = C[t] + 1.5·(C[t] − SL)`; short mirrored on the 5-bar highest high.
