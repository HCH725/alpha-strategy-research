## Hypothesis and exact signal

**Falsifiable mechanism:** RCI quantifies the ordinal relationship of recent closes to time. Crossing above its own five-observation mean is hypothesized to mark positive rank-momentum acceleration; crossing below marks its reversal. This is a hypothesis, not a measured return, independent backtest, historical Survivor or profitability claim.

**Data and clock:** Only actual completed Binance BTC-USDT spot UTC **1h** OHLCV bars `t=0,1,...`. Exchange timestamp `T[t]` is the **opening** time; the decision availability time is `E[t]=T[t]+3600 seconds`. Validate consecutive strictly increasing 3600-second UTC opening timestamps, finite positive O/H/L/C, `high>=max(open,close)`, `low<=min(open,close)`, finite nonnegative volume, and actual completion before feature construction. Reject duplicates, gaps, stale/unclosed candles, invalid values or synthetic/imputed prices. No other symbol, order book, higher timeframe, fitted model or external data.

1. For `t>=8`, use the nine closes `C[t-8],...,C[t]` ordered oldest to newest. Let time ranks be `x_i=i` for `i=1..9`. Let `y_i` be **ascending price ranks** of those nine closes (smallest close rank 1, largest rank 9), with **average/midrank for equal closes**. Calculate `RCI9[t] = 100 * sum((x_i-5)*(y_i-5)) / sqrt(sum((x_i-5)^2)*sum((y_i-5)^2))`. If the denominator is zero, reject the affected research data/run rather than substitute a fabricated signal. This fully specified Pearson correlation of ranks is a **researcher-chosen Spearman implementation**, not a claim of exact Pine `ta` or author implementation.
2. For `t>=12`, `SMA5[t]=(RCI9[t-4]+...+RCI9[t])/5`. Arithmetic uses deterministic chronological IEEE-754 float64, fixed ranking/midrank tie rule and no fitted coefficients. No adaptive thresholds, stochastic components, future extrema or full-sample normalization.
3. `UP[t]=(RCI9[t-1]<=SMA5[t-1]) AND (RCI9[t]>SMA5[t])`; `DOWN[t]=(RCI9[t-1]>=SMA5[t-1]) AND (RCI9[t]<SMA5[t])`. Current-bar equality is **not** a cross; previous-bar equality is permitted. These strict edge conventions are researcher-defined. At `t>=32`, confirmed FLAT with no pending order and `UP[t]` emits one BUY; confirmed LONG with no pending order and `DOWN[t]` emits one SELL. A persistent `RCI9>SMA5` does not re-enter; an isolated change in RCI sign or ±80 thresholds does nothing. The source's ±80 guides are **not** entry/exit gates in this derivative.
4. On a completed signal bar `s`, queue one side only. **Do not fill on `s`.** At the **next actually completed bar** `b=s+1`, submit one MARKET `OrderExecutorConfig` and use its actual `C[b]` as the bounded historical fill quote. Economic fill availability is `E[b]`, **not** raw `T[b]`. No real successor bar means **no fill**, no invented terminal price or end-of-sample forced liquidation. Never assume next-open, intrabar trigger or source-native Pine fill equivalence.
5. **Priority and state:** Start FLAT, no pending, 10,000 USDT cash, zero BTC. A queued order sets PENDING, not inventory. On the next completed fill bar, reconcile the full returned executor fill and fee first, update cash/base and state, and **skip all new signal decisions on that fill bar**. On any later completed bar, while LONG evaluate only DOWN/SELL; while FLAT evaluate only UP/BUY. After a SELL, a fresh **later** UP edge is required for re-entry. One position/pending order maximum, no simultaneous opposite actions. Failed, partial, duplicate, missing, stale, invalid or unresolvable fills **abort** the run instead of being treated as successful; no hidden retry or borrowed cash.
6. **Explicitly disabled:** shorts/borrow, reversals, pyramiding, scaling, DCA/safety orders, cooldown beyond the no-same-fill-bar rule, SL, TP, trailing/break-even, time/session exits, liquidation, leverage, margin, perpetual funding, shared-capital multi-asset portfolio, same-bar round trip and discretionary exits. An unprotected spot long can lose its invested principal; the absent stop is not a guarantee of bounded drawdown.

**Warmup/causality:** Require **32 fully completed consecutive valid candles** `t=0..31` before the first possible decision at `t=32`. The first RCI is defined at `t=8`, first SMA at `t=12`, and both current/previous crossover operands exist by `t=32`. Use only completed historical/current candles at `E[t]`; no future rank, negative shift, repaint, unfinished candle or pre-close knowledge. Validate raw continuity **before** pinned-engine merge/dropna; an unexpected dropped/misaligned bar is a hard failure.

Starting `cash=Decimal("10000") USDT`, `BTC=Decimal("0")`, fixed `Q=Decimal("0.001") BTC`, fee rate `f=Decimal("0.001")`. On confirmed full BUY at actual `P_b`, require `cash>=Q*P_b*(1+f)`, then `cash-=Q*P_b*(1+f)`, `BTC+=Q`. On confirmed full SELL at actual `P_s`, `cash+=Q*P_s*(1-f)`, `BTC-=Q`. Matched net round-trip PnL `=Q*(P_s-P_b)-f*Q*(P_s+P_b)`. Open inventory is marked only at the last **real** observed close, without inventing a terminal sell or exit fee. `BacktestPositionHold` gross PnL is **not** fee-inclusive net cash accounting.

Abort if price/fee/quantity missing, inconsistent, partial, failed, cash-negative, precision mismatch or stale; never use `BacktestingDataProvider.get_price_by_type`'s missing-price `Decimal("1")` fallback. **Additional modeled slippage=0 is an explicit idealization**, not measured zero spread, queue delay or impact.
