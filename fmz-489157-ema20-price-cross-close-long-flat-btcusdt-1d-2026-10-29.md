## Economic mechanism

### Source-reported

The page prose presents a single-indicator trend-following system: the 20-day EMA names the trend, a close crossing above it is read as bullish ignition worth buying, and a close crossing back below it ends the trade. No stop-loss, take-profit, trailing, or time exit ships anywhere — the opposite crossunder close is the entire risk control. No source performance metric is adopted (the page's win-rate label is a chart display computed from its own demo fills, not a claimed number).

### Research interpretation

This is a **single-line trend filter with a reversal-only exit**: the traded thesis is that a close pushing through its own 20-day average marks acceptance of a new price level, and staying long until acceptance fails (cross back below) harvests the drift between the two edges while paying whipsaw costs in ranges. With no bracket and no time stop, expectancy hinges on trends covering the fee-paying round trips that chop prints — winners are held only while acceptance persists, so a slow fade that never crosses back below round-trips gains, while a sharp reversal exits one bar late by construction. The failure mode is plain: in a range, price oscillates across a flat EMA20 and every edge prints a fee-paying entry-to-close cycle; a gap across 1d closes jumps clean past the line with no slippage accounting. No profitability is asserted.

## Signal

**Gate 1 — exact researcher-pinned arithmetic.** Index completed UTC 1-day bars by `t=0,1,...`. Use real Binance BTC-USDT spot `O[t],H[t],L[t],C[t]`; volume is not read. A bar with missing/nonfinite/non-positive close, or with `H<max(O,C)` / `L>min(O,C)`, is invalid; never interpolate.

1. Pinned input: `EMALEN=20` fixed (plain assignment exactly as coded). No other parameter exists.
2. `EMA20[t]` is undefined (`na`) for `t < 19`; `EMA20[19]=mean(C[0..19])` (SMA seed, Pine `ta.ema` convention); for later bars `EMA20[t]=(2/21)*C[t]+(19/21)*EMA20[t-1]`. Any `na` comparison is false, so no edge can fire pre-seed — coded behavior, disclosed.
3. On simulator bar `t` **only after its close**, evaluate with the already-completed **previous** bar `u = t-1`. `longEdge[u] = (C[u-1] <= EMA20[u-1]) and (C[u] > EMA20[u])` (`ta.crossover(close,ema20)`), requiring `C[u-1],C[u],EMA20[u-1],EMA20[u]` all defined; `exitEdge[u] = (C[u-1] >= EMA20[u-1]) and (C[u] < EMA20[u])` (`ta.crossunder(close,ema20)`), same definedness requirement. This mirrors confirmed-bar semantics: no current-bar `t` indicator may influence the action; `C[t]` is solely modeled fill-price/cash-check data, never a signal input.
4. The two edges are **provably mutually exclusive on every bar**: a single scalar comparison admits no bar with both `C[u] > EMA20[u]` and `C[u] < EMA20[u]`; equality on the current bar fires neither edge. No priority rule is needed and none is invented.
5. If confirmed FLAT and not pending, `longEdge[u]` queues one BUY of 0.001 BTC at simulated `C[t]` subject to warmup/affordability/guards below. No exit predicate is consulted while FLAT.
6. If confirmed LONG and not pending, `exitEdge[u]` queues one SELL of the confirmed 0.001 BTC at simulated `C[t]` (plain close of the long, Pine `strategy.close("Long")` semantics ported to a MARKET close). The entry edge is not consulted while LONG. Never re-enter on the same bar a SELL is queued.
7. There is no stop-loss, no take-profit, no trailing order, no time exit, no scale-out, no cooldown, no pyramiding, no opposite-signal reversal (no short leg exists), and no end-of-sample forced liquidation. An unclosed final position remains open, marked at the last observed valid price only. The `plot(close)` visual and the entire win-rate/label block (`wins`/`losses`/`totalTrades`/`winRate`/`label`/`labelText`/`label_pos`) have no decision function and are pinned as display-only.

**Gate 2:** LONG/FLAT on Binance BTC-USDT **spot**, leverage 1, no short leg in source and none added. **Gate 3:** complete cross-above entry, complete crossunder close exit, and intentionally disabled further risk legs as specified above (verified 1 `strategy.entry` + 1 `strategy.close`, zero `strategy.exit`/`strategy.order` in source).

**Gate 4:** starting research cash `10,000 USDT`, initial inventory `0 BTC`, fixed single-unit order quantity `Decimal("0.001") BTC`, maximum one open position and one pending action. State is `FLAT -> BUY_PENDING -> LONG -> SELL_PENDING -> FLAT` **only after an observed filled executor**, not on signal submission. Pyramiding, DCA, simultaneous strategies and safety orders are disabled.

## Required data and causality

**Gate 6:** require at least **100 consecutive fully completed** UTC 1-day OHLCV bars before the first signal/fill; the structural minimum is 22 (EMA-20 seed clears at index 19, plus the one-bar signal lag plus the one-bar signal-to-fill lag). A Binance 1-day bar opening at `T[t]` is not complete before `T[t]+86400 seconds`. Predicates computed on completed bar `u=t-1` are known before bar `t` completes (confirmed-bar semantics); the modeled fill at `C[t]` is assigned **effective time** `T[t]+86400`, never the raw Kline opening label `T[t]`. A completed-close MARKET simulator fill is a bounded historical modeling convention, **not** a claim that a live order can execute at a known historical close. No future prices, higher-timeframe partial bars, repainting pivots, full-sample fitted values, negative shifts, hindsight optimization or imputed candles. The header's `basePeriod: 1d` equals the single decision frame; no second timeframe is read (zero `request.`/`security(`), so the derivative pins a single 1d frame despite the page prose's multi-timeframe optimization suggestion. No volume or external feed is read.

Every bar must have a strictly consecutive UTC 1-day timestamp and valid OHLC. On a missing/invalid/out-of-sequence bar, generate no new signal or fill and **retain** any confirmed existing spot position; do not synthesize an exit. After continuity is restored, resume decisions only after a fresh 100-bar warmup. The controller must never use the current bar's still-forming close to construct a signal. Undefined EMA20, non-positive closes and degenerate bars deterministically yield no signal — no invented tie-break.
