## Economic mechanism

**Source-reported:** price deviation from a dual-moving-average baseline marks liquidity-exhaustion extremes; three long-side extreme prints arm a reversal entry; a technical rebound back toward the baseline, or an opposing-side extreme, ends participation. The prose additionally claims a trailing stop, which the pinned code does not functionally contain (see Evidence).

**Research interpretation (hypothesis only):** a close printing its third trailing-89-bar deviation extreme below a slow dual-MA baseline may indicate seller exhaustion, with the first rebound to within 1% below the baseline (or the first opposing upside extreme) as the fully specified exit. This is a testable hypothesis, not a return, profitability, source-backtest, Survivor, or Qlib-parity claim. After the third historical extreme the entry gate stays armed, so post-exit bars re-enter immediately unless an exit condition still holds; losing streaks can therefore compound with no stop beyond the signal exits, and this derivative has no stop/target beyond them.

## Signal and fully pinned state

**Market/data:** one real Binance spot `BTC-USDT` `1h` OHLCV series; no synthetic/Heikin-Ashi candles, higher timeframe, multi-market data, fitted model, news, order book or funding feed. Let `T[t]` be the Binance **opening** Unix timestamp of bar `t`; bar `t` is usable only after `T[t]+3600 seconds`. All bar timestamps must be exact consecutive 3600-second increments; every O/H/L/C must be finite and positive, `H>=max(O,C)`, `L<=min(O,C)`, volume finite and nonnegative. Missing, duplicated, malformed or incomplete bars **abort the research run**, not forward-fill, drop, or substitute a price.

**Pinned indicator semantics (Pine v5 built-ins, constants frozen — source ships zero `input.*`):** `src[t]=close[t]`; `ma1[t]=mean(close[t-14..t])` (SMA15, `na` until 15 closes); `ma2[t]` = Pine v5 `ta.ema(close,30)` under its documented SMA-seeded equivalence (`alpha=2/31`, seeded by `mean(close[0..29])` once 30 closes exist, `na` before — pre-warmup seeding differences never trade by the 150-bar flat rule below); `avgLine[t]=(ma1[t]+ma2[t])/2`, requiring `avgLine[t]` finite and strictly positive (aborts otherwise — no division by zero or sign-flip gauge); `distVal[t]=((close[t]-avgLine[t])/avgLine[t])*100`. `ph[t]`/`pl[t]` = maximum/minimum of distVal over bars `max(0,t-88)..t` (Pine partial-window behavior before 89 defined values: extremes over whatever defined prints exist). Persistent latches: on a bar with defined `distVal[t]==ph[t]` and `ph[t]>0`, `lastHigh:=distVal[t]`; on a bar with `distVal[t]==pl[t]` and `pl[t]<0`, `lastLow:=distVal[t]` (float64 exact-recomputation equality, same op order as pinned). `shortLiq[t] = lastHigh defined AND lastHigh==distVal[t] AND distVal[t]>0`; `longLiq[t] = lastLow defined AND lastLow==distVal[t] AND distVal[t]<0` (verified mutually exclusive: one needs `distVal>0`, the other `distVal<0`). `buy[t] = occ0 AND occ1 AND occ2` where `occ_k` is `ta.valuewhen(longLiq,longLiq,k)` (the value of longLiq at its k-th most recent true bar, `na` — hence falsy — until k+1 occurrences exist). Consequence pinned and verified: `buy[t]` is false until the third historical `longLiq`, true on every bar thereafter — the prose "3 consecutive" reads in code as three cumulative occurrences, disclosed, and the code governs. `entryPrice`, `lastPriceHigh`, `lastPriceLow` are assigned but never read by any order line (dead, excluded). `ta.atr(14)` is computed but its sole consumer is the provably dead `sellCondition3` (see Evidence) — ATR seeding can never change a traded event and is fenced as inert.

**Warmup/initial state:** require **150 consecutive fully completed valid hourly bars** flat by record rule (covers the 89-bar extreme window, the 30-bar EMA seed, and the valuewhen occurrence history; the three-occurrence precondition itself remains signal state, not warmup — a quiet tape simply never arms). Start with `FLAT`, no orders, no position, 10,000 USDT cash, empty latches, and no inferred earlier trade.

**Entry:** on each fully completed bar `t` after warmup, with state FLAT and no pending order, BUY if and only if `buy[t]` is true. Issue one MARKET BUY of exactly `Q=Decimal("0.001") BTC` at the same row's observed completed `close[t]` under the pinned **historical simulator convention**. Require `cash >= Q*close[t]*(1+f)`, `f=0.001`; otherwise stay flat. On **confirmed full fill**, record `entryPrice=close[t]` and `LONG`, cash minus `Q*entryPrice*(1+f)`. Do not infer a fill from an order intent. A missing/failed/partial fill, invalid price or undefined fee aborts this bounded research run instead of inventing a trade or retry rule.

**Exits and book priority:** on each **subsequent** fully completed bar after a confirmed BUY, SELL the entire confirmed Q at that bar's actual completed close if `sell1[t] = distVal[t] > -1` OR `sell2[t] = shortLiq[t]` (either leg suffices; `sell3` is dead by arithmetic proof, never a third leg). Source block order is entry-block-first: entry and exit legs can co-fire on one close (armed `buy[t]` plus `distVal[t] > -1`). Resolution is by book, never by engine fill ordering: a FLAT book ENTERS on a co-fire bar (the exit leg is a no-op with no position to cut — matching the source, whose `strategy.close("Buy")` with no open position does nothing); an already-LONG book EXITS in full (the entry leg is a no-op under the source's default no-pyramiding construction). No same-entry-bar exit of a just-opened position, stop/limit resting order, next-open price assumption, time exit, trailing stop, or fill at a threshold. A full confirmed SELL credits `Q*close[t]*(1-f)` and returns to FLAT. An unresolved SELL or partial fill aborts; no fabricated flat book.

**At most one position.** Source opens only Long with default no-pyramiding; while LONG, new long signals are ignored, and no short leg exists to block or reverse. No pyramiding, averaging, BO/SO/DCA, concurrent positions, cooldown, short, leverage, borrowing, compounding, stop/target, time exit, discretionary override or hidden controller risk default. On the last sample bar, mark any still-open BTC to the **actual last observed close**; do not fabricate a terminal liquidation. All signals are ignored while an order is pending.

## Required data and causality

Exactly one contiguous Binance BTC-USDT **spot 1h** historical OHLCV feed and the opening timestamp of each candle. Averages, deviation gauge, rolling extremes, latches and occurrence counters use only already completed candles; no value is available before its bar's close. At decision time `T[t]+3600`, only `t` and earlier bars are consulted. 4. **Position/re-entry:** one full-size 0.001 BTC unit, one pending order, re-entry whenever the armed gate holds after a confirmed FLAT (no cooldown specified — explicitly none, not invented), no DCA/concurrency/pyramiding, no same-bar entry/exit, abort on unsupported failed/partial fills.

6. **Point-in-time:** averages, gauge, extremes, latches and counters available only at bar close, later decisions only after their 1h close, no incomplete candles/future leakage; historical simulator's raw open-time labels explicitly mapped to close-effective times.
