## Hypothesis and complete signals (Gates 1–4)

**Research hypothesis:** a confirmed swing-high breakout may briefly persist, while a deterioration in the recent *sequence of confirmed swing highs* or a short fixed hold caps exposure. This is an untested hypothesis, not source-reported alpha, HB survivor status, or independently reproduced backtest result.

**One decision feed:** Binance BTC-USDT **spot 1d** consecutive UTC daily candles, raw exchange bar-**open** timestamps `T[t]` at 00:00 UTC and exact 86400-second cadence. Candle t is decision-available only at `E[t]=T[t]+86400`. Use O/H/L/C/V as finite decimal-input values; require positive O/H/L/C, `H>=max(O,C)`, `L<=min(O,C)`, `H>=L`, finite V>=0, no duplicates, missing bars, synthetic prices, resampling or interpolation. Reject an invalid/gapped input run before signals, rather than letting a dataframe `dropna` silently alter the time series. All indicators are calculated in chronological float64 with explicit strict comparisons and no fitted parameters.

1. **Confirmed high pivot event:** for each completed decision bar t>=4, center `c=t-2`. `PIVOT[t]` exists iff `H[c] > H[c-2]`, `H[c] > H[c-1]`, `H[c] > H[c+1]`, and `H[c] > H[c+2]`. Tied highs invalidate the pivot (strict >). A candidate high at c is **never available before** E[c+2]; no retroactive order at c. A single new event per confirmed center, at t; event stores its price `H[c]` and confirmation time E[t]. A missing pivot is not a zero price.
2. **Persistent resistance rail:** `R[t]` equals the price of the **most recently confirmed** pivot event at or before t; if none, it is undefined. It remains unchanged until another confirmed event. A completed-close upside **edge** `UP[t]` is true only when `R[t]` is defined, `C[t-1] <= R[t]` AND `C[t] > R[t]`. Equality on current close does not trigger. This uses the **same currently available rail** for both comparisons; a level change cannot be treated as an earlier, unobserved breakout. A breakout on a bar with no new pivot is allowed. No intrabar wick breakout.
3. **Last-three-confirmed-high mean:** after the kth confirmed pivot event (`k` is 1-based), if k>=3, `M3[k]=(P[k-2]+P[k-1]+P[k])/3`, where P is the **event sequence**, not the last three bars. On a newly confirmed event k>=4 only, `FALL[t]=(M3[k] < M3[k-1])`; otherwise FALL=false. Equality is not falling; on non-event bars no new mean comparison is invented. All event values are frozen at their actual confirmation timestamps; no lookahead.
4. **Warmup and first entry:** start FLAT with zero BTC and 10,000 modeled USDT. Bars 0..199 are fully valid **warmup only** (pivot events and rails may accumulate, but no trade orders). Earliest decision t=200. If no confirmed pivot, stay FLAT. While FLAT with no pending action, `UP[t]` emits one pending BUY. An UP while LONG or pending is ignored, never queued for later.
5. **LONG exit:** a LONG position may decide an exit only on a completed bar **strictly after its confirmed BUY fill bar f**. `EXIT[t] = FALL[t] OR (t-f>=3)`. If both, reason `FRACTAL_MEAN_FALL` has logging priority, then `THREE_COMPLETED_BARS`; emit exactly one full SELL. Time counting: first eligible post-fill completed bar f+1 counts 1, f+2 counts 2, f+3 counts 3; if no earlier FALL, time-exit decision is E[f+3] and historical fill is E[f+4]. This deliberately differs from ChartArt's stated time anchor at the **entry condition**. A new pivot can cause a FALL before the time exit, but an unconfirmed center cannot.
6. **Order and re-entry state:** `FLAT -> PENDING_BUY -> LONG -> PENDING_SELL -> FLAT`, with changes into/out of LONG **only after validated full fill**. A signal on completed t is submitted on the **following** bar t+1 and the simulator uses that bar's actual close for a MARKET historical fill; on fill bars suppress new signal decisions, but continue updating pivot/mean history. Only one pending action and one position maximum. A failed, rejected, partial, missing-price or mismatched fill **aborts** the bounded simulation; never silently mark it filled, resize or fill at a synthetic price. A final-bar pending order with no next real bar is unfilled and not counted. Re-entry requires a **new** UP edge on a later eligible bar after SELL fill; no latch of an ignored crossing, no same-fill-bar re-entry.
7. **Explicitly disabled:** SHORT, borrowing, margin, leverage, futures, funding, DCA, safety orders, pyramiding, scale-in/out, multi-pair concurrency, compounding, extra cooldown, intrabar stop/TP, trailing, breakeven, stop-limit/maker, profit target, opposite-side reversal, and forced end-of-sample liquidation. There is **no protective price stop**: a large adverse gap can occur before the mean/time exit. Time exit is not a guarantee of maximum loss or exact three-bar market exposure. If still LONG at sample end, report unrealized mark-to-last-real-close only.
