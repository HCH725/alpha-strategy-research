## Economic mechanism

### Source-reported

Four-momentum-leg confluence filter (sibling README: `combines four powerful momentum indicators (RSI, MACD, Stochastic RSI, and Rate of Change) to generate high-probability signals through confluence. Trades are only taken when multiple indicators agree, significantly reducing false signals`): each completed bar evaluates an RSI oversold/overbought leg, a MACD cross/slope leg, a StochRSI cross/drift leg, and a 10-bar momentum sign/slope leg. A long leg fires when at least two of the four print bullish with no position open; a short leg mirrors it on at least two bearish. The position closes on the opposite two-agreement print. There is deliberately no stop, no target, and no flat state past seeding — the order block ends with the two `strategy.close` guards and no risk leg ships anywhere, by design gap rather than by hidden leg. The README's `2-3 indicators` prose range is narrowed to the coded default exactly-`>= 2`; the `3`- and `1`-confirmation README tunings are disclosed-but-not-adopted. The file ships no backtest numbers of any kind — this record claims no source-reported performance (see Evidence).

### Research interpretation

Vote-count momentum-confluence reversal, two-sided with opposite-agreement exits — the first vote-count record in the pool. Unlike fixed-AND combo records (every named leg must agree, so one disagreeing leg vetoes the trade), the `>= 2` count lets any two legs outvote the other two, so the system fires through single-leg whipsaws but still blocks lone-leg noise; unlike single-oscillator records, no single quantity's cross can ever place an order by itself (each leg contributes at most one vote — proved mutually exclusive within-leg in Negative evidence — so a lone cross prints count 1 and waits). Unlike level/threshold oscillators, two of the four legs are cross-or-drift hybrids (MACD cross-or-rising-hist, StochRSI cross-or-drifting) and one is a pure sign-and-slope leg (MOM > 0 and rising), so the vote aggregates trend-cross, band, and velocity information rather than four copies of one signal shape. The bet is on multi-shape momentum agreement at frozen `(14, 12/26/9, 14/3/3, 10)` geometry with a frozen 2-vote quorum, not on any volatility extreme, level break, calendar effect, or single-quantity regime.
