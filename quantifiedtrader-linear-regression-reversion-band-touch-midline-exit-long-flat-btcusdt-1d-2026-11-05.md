## Economic mechanism

### Source-reported

Detrended cheapness: the regression midline tracks the trailing drift, so distance below the lower band measures statistically cheap closes relative to local trend rather than raw level. Falling to or through the lower band aligns long on the bet that stretched-below-trend closes snap back toward the drift line; returning to the midline banks the snap and stands flat. The design bets that regression-detrended entry timing on the daily frame beats raw level or average-cross timing, and that exiting at the drift line (rather than riding to the upper band) harvests the reversion leg while refusing trend-continuation exposure.

### Research interpretation

Band-touch mean-reversion system, long side only — the only pool machine that enters on a touch of an OLS-residual band and exits at the regression endpoint. Unlike the pool Bollinger touch-reversion record (SMA-centered bands with the exit at the **upper** touch — entries there ride the full band width and the between-band interval commands nothing in either state), this machine exits at the **midline**: any post-warmup bar at or above the drift line closes the book, so the holding interval is only `[lower, mid)` and the edge being timed is the snap-back leg alone. Unlike the pool Linear-Regression-Slope record (which trades the **sign of the slope** — direction of drift — with no bands, no cheapness read, and no midline), this machine never reads the slope sign anywhere: a steep rally and a steep selloff print the same bands shape up to drift removal, and entries fire in both. Unlike stop/trailing-stop machines, the exit carries no memory and no ratchet: `C[t] ≥ mid[t]` is recomputed each bar from the live window. Unlike two-sided reversal systems there is no short leg and no always-in-market grind — above-midline regimes are flat, so extended rich regimes cost only opportunity, never adverse exposure. The stack is recursion-free and seed-free (every print from index 19 is defined by the trailing 20 closes alone), so unlike seeded smoothers there is no seed-convention residual. The equity-index universe and the unmodeled-costs stance are source facts, not this record's venue or economics. No performance figure is carried anywhere.
