## Economic mechanism

### Source-reported

Next-day abnormal-return drift with asset-dependent sign: after a one-day abnormal return, prices `tend to move in the direction of the abnormal returns` into the next day (`H3 cannot be rejected`), but the effect is short-lived (`Usually, it takes a few hours for the price to peak`) with asset-specific timing parameters; in two cases (BTCUSD positive, ETHUSD negative) the next-day move reverses into a contrarian effect instead. For ETHUSD specifically both next-day legs point long: momentum after positive abnormal days (held `till 21:00`), contrarian reversal after negative abnormal days (held `till the end of the day`). The authors present both as developable timed strategies with significant trading-simulation profits. No behavioural, structural, or risk-premium mechanism ships beyond the documented drift/reversal finding itself.

### Research interpretation

Timed next-day long-only event trade, two complementary legs, never overlapping. Unlike the admitted Strategy-1 record (same-day momentum held into the midnight close, two-sided, BTC, k=2), this machine never holds the abnormal day itself: it sleeps through day D, buys the D+1 open only when D printed abnormal, and is always flat by the paper's leg-specific guillotine (21:00 for post-positive drift, midnight for post-negative reversal). Entry is rare (~115 events over the paper's ~3-year ETH sample, roughly three per month); holding is hours, never overnight beyond the leg window. The disclosed failure mode: the trailing band is backward-looking, so a volatility-regime jump prints late labels; a mid-hold reversal after entry pays the full excursion into the timed exit with no guardrail — the timed close is an exit guillotine, not risk control. No return, Sharpe, win-rate, or survivor claim is asserted beyond the paper's own printed simulation rows.

## Signal

**Input and clock:** exactly one Binance **spot** ETH-USDT UTC **1h** candle stream, `(open_time,O,H,L,C,V)`, strictly contiguous 3600-second opening timestamps. `open_time` is the exchange candle-open Unix timestamp; a completed candle becomes available only at `open_time+3600`. Compute and act only after that completion. Require finite positive O/H/L/C, H >= max(O,C), L <= min(O,C), finite nonnegative V, and valid strictly increasing timestamps. No forward fill, synthetic prints, higher-timeframe peeking, or missing-price substitution.

**Researcher-pinned rule mathematics (source k=1.5, frozen n=30, next-day legs):**

1. UTC day `D`: `O[D]` = open of the `00:00–01:00 UTC` bar, `C[D]` = close of the `23:00–00:00 UTC` bar, daily return `R[d] = (C[d]/O[d] − 1) × 100%` (source eq. (1)). Over the 30 completed UTC days `D−30 … D−1`: `R̄[D] = (1/30)·ΣR[d]` (source eq. (4)), `δ[D] = sqrt((1/30)·Σ(R[d] − R̄[D])²)` (source eq. (5), population divisor by paper rule). Upper `U[D] = R̄[D] + 1.5·δ[D]`, lower `L[D] = R̄[D] − 1.5·δ[D]` (`k = 1.5` source-pinned for ETHUSD). If `δ[D] = 0` exactly, day `D` is NORMAL (pinned convention, disclosed).
2. Day label (completed `1h` bars only, chronological, all 24 bars of `D`): running return `r(D,t) = (close[t]/O[D] − 1) × 100%`. The FIRST bar with `r(D,t) > U[D]` locks label POS; with `r(D,t) < L[D]` locks NEG (strict inequalities by paper rule; single scalar, first-breach lock — no tie-break needed beyond chronology). No breach in any of the 24 bars → NORMAL.
3. Maintain `position in {FLAT,LONG}`; initialize `FLAT`. After **30 contiguous valid completed UTC days**, evaluate only at day opens: at the completion of the `23:00–00:00 UTC` bar ending day `D` (the `D+1` open print), if `FLAT`: label(D)=POS → one `BUY` of 0.1 ETH (**Leg A**, only if affordable), label(D)=NEG → one `BUY` of 0.1 ETH (**Leg B**, only if affordable), NORMAL → nothing. Signals while LONG are deterministic no-ops.
4. Exits: Leg A sells the full 0.1 ETH at the close of the `20:00–21:00 UTC` bar of day `D+1` (paper `till 21:00`). Leg B sells the full 0.1 ETH at the close of the `23:00–00:00 UTC` bar of day `D+1` (paper `till the end of the day`). At a print carrying both a Leg-B exit and a fresh entry evaluation (day `D+1` itself abnormal), process the **exit first, then the entry** — pinned ordering, both fills at that print with a fee leg each.
5. **Explicitly disabled:** any short leg (no short exists in the ported ETH legs — never an omission); stop-loss / take-profit / trailing / early time exits (the paper's algorithm contains no risk leg — carried over exactly); pyramiding; BO/SO/DCA; averaging; compounding; concurrent positions; cross-pair capital sharing; maker/limit/next-open/intrabar execution; discretionary exits; any hidden Hummingbot default risk order. No stop is a deliberate research risk. Re-entry re-arms at the next qualifying day-open when FLAT (no cooldown specified — explicitly none, not invented).
6. **Invalid/missing candles:** stop the run as **data-incomplete** if any candle is missing, duplicated, malformed, out-of-order, non-finite, non-contiguous (open_time step != 3600), or any required print is undefined after warmup. This guard is load-bearing here: a data outage would corrupt both `R[d]` formation values and the trailing estimator, fabricating abnormal days. Do not fabricate a fill, silently drop a row, reset position state mid-run, or sell an existing LONG at an invented price. A restarted independent run must establish 30 new contiguous valid completed UTC days and a fresh FLAT state.
