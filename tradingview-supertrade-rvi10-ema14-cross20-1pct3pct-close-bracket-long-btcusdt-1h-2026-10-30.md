## Exact signal and data (Gates 1, 2, 6)

**Market/data:** actual Binance spot BTC-USDT **1h** historical OHLCV, UTC-aligned contiguous candle-open timestamps T[t] spaced exactly 3600 seconds. Each row must contain finite positive O/H/L/C, H >= max(O,C), L <= min(O,C), H >= L, finite nonnegative base volume and strictly increasing timestamps; reject duplicate, missing, incomplete, out-of-order or malformed rows **before** Hummingbot feature alignment/dropna. Only close is used by this signal. An observed completed bar t becomes decision-available at E[t] = T[t] + 3600 seconds; never use its close at T[t]. No synthetic prices, forward fill, full-sample fit, external data, security/request higher timeframe, or future bars.

**Researcher-frozen Relative Volatility Index (RVI, not Relative Vigor):** index candles t=0,1,...; for t>=9, mu[t] = (sum of C[i], i=t-9..t)/10 and sigma[t] = sqrt(sum((C[i]-mu[t])^2, i=t-9..t)/10). This is **population** standard deviation (divide by 10), not sample stdev. For t>=9 define u[t]=sigma[t] if C[t]>C[t-1], otherwise 0; d[t]=sigma[t] if C[t]<C[t-1], otherwise 0. Equal consecutive closes set **both** legs to zero. At t=9 seed EU[9]=u[9], ED[9]=d[9]. For t>=10 use EMA14 alpha=2/15: EU[t]=(2/15)*u[t]+(13/15)*EU[t-1], ED[t]=(2/15)*d[t]+(13/15)*ED[t-1]. If EU+ED>0, RVI[t]=100*EU[t]/(EU[t]+ED[t]); if both are zero, define RVI[t]=50 (researcher-pinned neutral zero-volatility case). EU/ED nonnegative. No additional EMA of the final RVI ratio, no 50-state latch and no lookahead. All math in chronological float64 without interim rounding; fail closed on nonfinite results.

**Entry trigger:** on completed bar t, CROSS20[t] = (RVI[t-1] <= 20 AND RVI[t] > 20), strict current-side comparison. A level already >20 does not repeatedly re-enter; equality at current 20 fails, equality at prior 20 qualifies. Only a **fresh** CROSS20 while FLAT, no pending order, and after warmup may signal BUY. There is no EMA200, RSI, volume gate, oscillator zero-cross, short entry or additional filter. The cross can occur during an otherwise falling price bar; this is a volatility-direction signal, not a bullish candle requirement.

**Warmup and causality:** first **120** fully completed contiguous 1h bars (indices 0..119) are indicator-only with FLAT initial state and no inherited inventory. First eligible decision is t=120, requiring RVI[t] and RVI[t-1]. The STD10 window and EMA14 recursion use only <=t closes. No intrabar extrema used to trigger orders. No incomplete higher timeframe, repaint, negative shift, future extrema, hindsight data partition, or ML fit. A cross that occurred during warmup is not replayed as an entry.

## Entry, exit, position and error state (Gates 2–4)

- **Scope:** Binance BTC-USDT unleveraged spot **LONG/FLAT**; initial cash **10,000 USDT**, inventory **0 BTC**, fixed per-position quantity Q=Decimal("0.001") BTC. One position, one pending order and one pair only. No shared capital across strategies, shorting, margin, perpetual futures or leverage.
- **FLAT -> PENDING_BUY:** on eligible CROSS20[t] at E[t], latch a single MARKET BUY action for the **next actually observed completed candle** t+1. Dispatch only on that row and use its **actual close C[t+1]** for an idealized historical full-fill event economically effective at E[t+1], not at its raw exchange-open timestamp T[t+1]. Check cash >= Q*C[t+1]*(1+0.001). A missing next bar leaves the action unfilled at sample end, not a fabricated fill. On insufficient cash, missing valid price, failed/partial fill or unreconciled fee/quantity, **abort the research run** rather than assume an execution.
- **PENDING_BUY -> LONG:** only after confirmed full BUY at price P=C[j], debit quote Q*P*(1+0.001), credit Q BTC, record fill_index=j and freeze **SL=0.99*P**, **TP=1.03*P**. SL/TP are **completed-close signal thresholds**, not exchange stop/limit prices. Ignore entry and exit evaluation on the BUY fill bar j.
- **LONG -> PENDING_SELL:** for each later completed t>j, trigger EXIT[t] when **C[t] <= SL OR C[t] >= TP**. If SL/TP both somehow report true, abort as an invariant failure (SL<TP makes this impossible for finite valid prices). These are frozen from the **actual modeled entry fill**, never recalculated using the prior signal close. A trigger queues a full Q MARKET SELL for next completed t+1; the SELL fills at actual C[t+1] and is economically effective at E[t+1], potentially far beyond the threshold. No opposite RVI signal exit.
- **PENDING_SELL -> FLAT:** on confirmed full SELL, debit Q BTC and credit Q*C[t+1]*(1-0.001) USDT, reconcile fees and realized quote PnL. Ignore all entry/exit signals on the SELL fill bar. A fresh CROSS20 on a **later** eligible bar is required for re-entry; a stale cross or a level that remains above 20 never causes entry. If a required SELL fill, fee or position confirmation is missing, **abort**; never silently retain an invented flat state.
- **Explicitly disabled:** DCA/BO/SO, pyramiding, averaging, partial entry/exit, simultaneous positions, shorts, reversal, trailing stop, time exit, opposite-signal exit, maker/limit/stop-market/resting brackets, intrabar SL/TP, next-open fills, forced final liquidation, compounding, discretionary trades, hidden cooldown and synthetic terminal fills. No extra cooldown beyond pending/fill-bar suppression. Open LONG at sample end remains marked at last **actual** close, not closed by assumption.
- **Risk semantics:** 1% and 3% are **trigger distances only**, not realized loss/profit guarantees. Completed-close detection followed by next-completed-close execution can produce losses much greater than 1% and does not protect against gaps.
