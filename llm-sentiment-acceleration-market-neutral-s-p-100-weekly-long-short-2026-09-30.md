## Economic mechanism

### Source-reported

The source poses one question (README.md section 1): `does an LLM-extracted signal, run through standard quant-finance machinery, produce a defensible alpha against a market benchmark?` The claimed channel is a **textual-sentiment momentum reversal in liquid large-cap equities**:

1. **Extraction layer.** Claude Haiku 4.5 (Batch API, stated cost `Total extraction cost: $11.84`) reads EDGAR 8-K filings (Items 1.01-9.01) and GDELT 2.0 financial-news articles and emits structured fields per (article, ticker): `polarity in [-1, 1]`, `magnitude in {minor, moderate, major}`, `event_type` (13 categories), `tense`, `specificity`, `novelty`, `industry_relevance`, `summary`. 11,367 (article, ticker) pairs survived JSON-validity filtering (README.md section 2).
2. **Signal layer.** The primary signal `sent_accel` is described in README.md section 3.2 as a WorldQuant Alpha 101-style stationary construction: `raw_pol = polarity * magnitude` per article, a 5-day rolling mean and a 20-day rolling mean of that score, `accel = short_ma - long_ma`, `score = -1 * (cs_rank(accel) - 0.5)`, then a 30-day rolling mean of `score`. The negation is justified by the source as the Engelberg-McLean-Pontiff (2018) mean-reversion convention: `long firms whose sentiment momentum decelerates, short firms whose sentiment momentum accelerates`. The shipped class `SentimentAcceleration` (src/alpha/strategies.py) matches this: `params = dict(L=1, lam=1.0, min_news=1, short_w=5, long_w=20, smooth_window=30)`, `a["score"] = a.polarity * a.magnitude_num`, `accel = short - long_`, `score = -1.0 * (self.cs_rank(accel) - 0.5)`, then `score.rolling(30, min_periods=1).mean()`.
3. **Portfolio layer.** README.md section 3.3: cross-sectional percentile rank of the smoothed signal, centring (`rank - 0.5`), normalisation `w = centered / sum(|centered|)` giving `sum(w) = 0, sum(|w|) = 1`, then GICS sector neutralisation (subtract the sector mean, renormalise gross to 1). The source's economic story is therefore **dollar-neutral, sector-neutral news mean-reversion**, not a directional call.
4. **Why it could pay, in the source's framing.** The README anchors on Tetlock (2007), Da-Engelberg-Gao (2011), Fang-Peress (2009), Engelberg-McLean-Pontiff (2018) and Kakushadze (2016) `101 Formulaic Alphas`, i.e. attention and news-driven mispricing plus a documented anomaly-versus-news reversal in liquid names. The mechanism claim is a **trading frictions / overreaction** channel: mispriced news impulses decay over days to weeks, so a bounded, rank-stationary, mean-reversion signal on the *change* in news sentiment should earn a small negative-autocorrelation spread within a 5-day horizon.
5. **What the source explicitly does not claim.** There is no model fitting and no ML training in the traded path (`Path A - pure factor portfolio. No model fitting, no ML training. Hyperparameters are tuned once on a 2021 dev set and frozen`, README.md section 1); the README's own TL;DR headline is a **risk-profile** claim (`Headline value is the risk profile - Sharpe comparable to passive equity, with one-eighth the drawdown and zero market beta`) and it discloses that the alpha t-statistic is below 1.96.

### Research interpretation

- **Mechanism class (research interpretation):** news-based mean-reversion / overreaction cross-sectional signal with a momentum-in-news-sentiment construction. The tradable object is the *acceleration* of a bounded news-score, i.e. a second-difference of attention-weighted tone, so the hypothesis is that the second difference of tone mean-reverts faster than the level of tone predicts direction.
- **Component roles (research interpretation, not source claims about each component's contribution):**
  - Regime / universe filter: point-in-time top 100 US equities by market capitalisation from `crsp.dsp500list` at each rebalance (no valuation or macro regime filter exists in the source).
  - Primary signal: `sent_accel` = negated cross-sectional rank of (5-day mean minus 20-day mean) of LLM polarity times magnitude, smoothed 30 days.
  - Confirmation filter: `min_news_count = 1` only; there is no volume, breadth, volatility or liquidity confirmation.
  - Sizing / portfolio: rank-weighted dollar-neutral weights, GICS sector-neutralised, gross 1.
  - Exit / holding: weekly re-formation on the first XNYS day of each ISO week, held flat for 5 trading days; no stop, no take-profit, no drawdown control anywhere in the source.
- **Ablation is required, not optional:** the same repository also ships `vader` (rule-based), `momentum` (12-1), `random`, `llm` / `llm_v3` / `llm_v4` (z-score LLM variants) and `bh_capwt`, so the hypothesis can be decomposed into (a) LLM polarity quality versus VADER, (b) acceleration versus level, (c) rank-weighting versus decile weighting. The source reports most of these rows but does not perform the decomposition argument (see Evidence and Negative evidence 2, 12).
- **Evidentiary status:** everything below is a third-party backtest on a single self-attested repository. It is a *falsifiable alpha hypothesis with a published construction*, not a validated alpha.

## Signal

All of the following is source-reported unless explicitly marked `research-proposed` or `research-defined`.

- **Formation timestamp and availability convention.** Per src/signal/score_articles.py: `EDGAR : event_date = accepted_datetime (or filed_date if missing)`, `GDELT : event_date = ts_utc`, and `Available date = next XNYS trading day strictly after event_date (calendar-day shift). This is the day the article first becomes usable for trading`. `available_idx` maps that date onto the panel index. The engine docstring (src/backtest/engine.py) states `The +1-day available_idx shift is already baked into the signal weights upstream ... so weight[t] is information at t and return[t] is realized return on day t -- no further lag needed`. Timezone of the XNYS calendar and the treatment of post-close or pre-open timestamps are **not stated** (data gap; see Execution assumptions and Negative evidence 13).
- **Lookback.** Article aggregation window `L = 1` trading day with decay `lam = 1.0` inside the shipped `SentimentAcceleration` class (the frozen config's `lam = 0.5` is documented as degenerate at `L = 1`: `lam value is degenerate at L=1 (decay never applied) -- ties broken by alphabetical pick`); per src/signal/aggregate.py the raw signal is `mean over articles k in window [t-L+1, t] of score * lam^offset`, and cells with fewer than `min_news_count = 1` articles become NaN. Sentiment-momentum windows: `short_w = 5`, `long_w = 20` trading days, both with `min_periods = 1` so sparse cells survive. Signal smoothing: 30 trading days with `min_periods = 1`. Warm-up: 2020-07 to 2020-12 feature buffer (README section 3.5). Endpoints are rolling inclusive windows over trading days.
- **Long entry.** Highest cross-sectional rank of the smoothed negated acceleration score (README section 3.3 rank-weighted rule): firms whose news-sentiment momentum is *decelerating* relative to peers.
- **Short entry.** Lowest rank of the same score: firms whose news-sentiment momentum is *accelerating* relative to peers. Weights are dollar-neutral (`sum w = 0`) with gross 1, then GICS sector-neutralised.
- **Exit / re-entry.** No explicit exit rule: weights are re-formed every week on the first XNYS day of the ISO week and the previous week's book is replaced. The engine's `v2_block30` scheme samples the (smoothed) sector-neutral weight at that first XNYS day and holds it flat Monday-Friday (`actual_w[t] = ma_w[last_rebalance_day] for t in [last_rebal, next_rebal)`), with all turnover concentrated on the rebalance day.
- **Holding period.** 5 trading days (Mon-Fri block), stated in README section 3.4 and locked-config. No overlapping positions.
- **Parameters (all fixed, source-reported).** `L = 1`, `lam` degenerate at `L = 1`, `min_news_count = 1`, `smooth_window = 30`, `short_w = 5`, `long_w = 20`, rank centring at 0.5, negation factor `-1`, gross exposure 1, GICS sector neutralisation, weekly XNYS rebalance, 5 bps round-trip cost. The frozen hyperparameter file additionally records `engine_scheme = bh_block` and `pipeline_version = v5`, which is contradicted by the run script (see frontmatter `contradictions`).
- **Frozen multipliers used in the wider suite (locked-config.md, `frozen priors - NOT tuned on dev/OOS data`):** `industry_relevance` company_specific 1.0, industry_wide 0.5, macro 0.0; `tense` past 0.5, present 1.0, forward 1.5, future 1.5; `event_type` earnings_beat / earnings_miss / guidance_raise / guidance_cut 1.5, mna_target 1.3, product_launch 1.2, mna_acquirer / regulatory / litigation / restructuring 1.0, exec_change 0.7, partnership 0.5, other 0.3. The shipped `SentimentAcceleration` class uses only `polarity * magnitude_num`, i.e. it does **not** apply this five-field product (README section 3.2 matches the class, not the lock file).
- **Hyperparameter selection (source-reported).** `config/hyperparameters.json`: search space `L in [1,3,5,10]`, `lam in [0.5,0.8,0.95,1.0]`, `min_news_count in [1,3,5]` = **48 configurations**, `smooth_window` locked at 30; `selection_metric = Information Ratio (annualized, gross of cost)`; `tuned_on 2021-H1` (124 days), `validated_on 2021-H2` (128 days); auto-pick `(L=1, lam=0.5, min=1)` with `h1_ir 1.785950`, `h2_ir -1.495646`; `frozen_at 2026-05-08T16:30:00+00:00`; `override.applied = true` with reason `H2 validation gate FAILED (H2 IR=-1.496 < 0). Khai authorized override on 2026-05-08 to use the auto H1-best config anyway`.
- **Underspecified signal items:** intraday publication-time handling (post-close filings), the GICS classification source and its point-in-time property, the market-cap data and lag used for the `dsp500list` top-100 selection inside the signal path, tie-breaking among equal ranks, treatment of delisted names in the signal panel, and whether the 30-day smoothing window resets at each fold boundary (src/signal/aggregate.py says smoothing runs on the full panel before fold windowing: `keeps the smoothing-window boundary clean at test_start (same convention`).
- Anything in this section not quoted from the source (for example a minimum article count above 1, a rank cutoff, a stop, a volatility filter) would be `research-proposed`; **none is proposed here**, because this record reports the source rule as published.

## Execution assumptions

Source-reported unless marked otherwise:

- **Signal-to-order timing.** Weights are formed from the signal panel and, under the shipped `v2_block30` scheme, sampled on the first XNYS day of each ISO week and held flat for the rest of the week; under the frozen config's declared `bh_block` scheme the same weekly sampling applies with no weight smoothing. Daily PnL is `gross_ret[t] = sum_i actual_w[i,t] * r[i,t]` with `r` the CRSP daily return for day `t` (src/backtest/engine.py).
- **Execution price / fill model.** **Not stated.** The engine charges no explicit fill price and models no partial fills, no queue position and no order types; the implied convention is that a weight known on day `t` earns the full close-to-close return of day `t`, which corresponds to executing at the previous close. That convention is stated by the source only as `weight[t] is information at t and return[t] is realized return on day t -- no further lag needed`. Whether the position is entered at the open, at the close, or at a VWAP is **underspecified** (data gap), and the repository publishes no intraday timestamps with which to check it.
- **Costs.** `COST_BPS_RT = 5.0`, `COST_PER_UNIT_DELTA_W = 0.00025`, `cost[t] = (round_trip_bps / 2) * delta_w[t]`, `net_ret[t] = gross_ret[t] - cost[t]`, where `delta_w[t] = sum_i |actual_w[i,t] - actual_w[i,t-1]|` (src/backtest/engine.py). README section 3.4 writes the same rule as `5 bps round-trip = 0.00025 x sum |delta w| per rebalance`. Audit test 5 claims `net_ret = gross_ret - 0.00025 * turnover exactly recovered on every daily PnL file`.
- **Fees, spread, slippage, impact, funding, borrow, margin:** flat 5 bps round-trip only. No spread model, no slippage model, no market-impact model, no borrow cost or locate constraint, no margin or leverage (gross 1, dollar-neutral), no funding, no failure handling. The README's own liquidity caveat: `The 5 bps cost reflects mid-spread execution on US large-cap stocks; intraday slippage on actual fills could be larger`.
- **Capacity / participation:** no participation cap, no ADV constraint, no capacity analysis (census `capacity` = 0).
- **Latency:** not modelled; the weekly cadence makes intra-day latency irrelevant to the published rule, but nothing in the source bounds the delay between an 8-K acceptance timestamp and the next rebalance.
- **Turnover consequence.** Annually scaled turnover is 11.305856 (= 1131 percent) for the headline row, i.e. the book is fully turned over roughly 11 times a year; the source's own engine comment says the 30-day weight smoothing was `introduced to bring annual two-way turnover into the [100%, 800%] sanity range`, yet the published figure is still above that range (Negative evidence 7).
- **`research-proposed` execution assumptions of this record (not from the source):** none are adopted as facts; realistic alternatives are only posed as falsification gates F3, F8, F9 and F13.

## Falsification plan

All thresholds below are **research-defined falsification thresholds** chosen by this scout; none comes from the source. Each gate states data, sample or regime, metric, threshold, decision rule and action. A single failure is sufficient to weaken or reject the hypothesis as stated.

- **F1 Printed-value reproduction.** Data: the pinned commit's `results/pooled_summary.csv`, `results/per_quarter_full.csv` and `results/folds.csv` plus the (currently unpublished) daily PnL and the WRDS inputs. Recompute the 12 quarterly rows and the pooled row from raw data. *Fail* if the pooled `sent_accel` Sharpe is not 0.783843 within 0.01, if any quarterly Sharpe differs by more than 0.05, or if the 753-day sum is not reproduced. *Action:* mark the published numbers unreproducible and stop.
- **F2 Random-baseline gate.** Data: same window plus the fresh window of F4. Metric: pooled net Sharpe and alpha t of `sent_accel` versus `random, continuous`. *Fail* if the random control equals or beats the strategy on either metric in the fresh window (it already does in-sample: 0.876068 vs 0.783843, 1.507720 vs 1.344953). *Action:* classify the strategy as indistinguishable from the control and reject the alpha claim.
- **F3 Cost ladder.** Replace the flat 5 bps round-trip with 5, 10, 16 and 25 bps and add a per-side spread of 0, 1 and 2 bps. *Fail* if net pooled Sharpe falls to 0 or below at a 16 bps round-trip (the research-computed break-even) or at 10 bps if turnover stays above 1100 percent. *Action:* mark cost-fragile; any live claim must then be restated gross.
- **F4 Frozen forward window.** From 2026-10-01, at least 12 months of new trading days with every parameter frozen (lock list below). *Fail* if pooled net Sharpe <= 0, if alpha t <= 0, or if more than one third of months have negative net return. *Action:* reject.
- **F5 Direction-lock gate.** Pre-register `SIGNAL_DIRECTION = -1` and, as a control, `+1`, and evaluate both on a window that played no part in the v3 amendment (i.e. strictly after 2026-10-01). *Fail* if `+1` matches or beats `-1`, or if the sign that worked in 2022-2024 does not hold in the fresh window. *Action:* treat the mean-reversion sign as fitted to the old window and reject the mechanism as stated.
- **F6 Gate-reinstatement check.** Re-apply the source's own validation rule (`annualised gross IR > 0` on a held-back half-year) to the frozen config and to the three printed `alternative_gate_passing_configs`. *Fail* if the frozen config again fails the gate while an alternative passes, or if the frozen config's fresh-window IR is <= 0. *Action:* the selection procedure is broken; re-open only as a new record with a new source identity.
- **F7 Selection-luck gate.** Evaluate all 48 printed hyperparameter configurations on the fresh window and compute the Deflated Sharpe Ratio with `N = 48 * 3 variants * 4 pipeline versions`. *Fail* if DSR < 0.95 for the chosen configuration, or if the chosen configuration is below the grid median on the fresh window. *Action:* treat the headline as search luck.
- **F8 Turnover and capacity gate.** Metric: realised annual two-way turnover, plus ADV participation of the weekly rebalance in the smallest names of the top-100 universe. *Fail* if turnover stays above 800 percent (the source's own upper bound) at unchanged rules, or if the rebalance requires more than 10 percent of a security's 21-day median dollar volume. *Action:* restrict to research-only and re-derive the cost model before any performance claim.
- **F9 Borrow and short-leg realism.** Source the actual borrow fee and locate availability for the short leg of the same 100 names. *Fail* if borrow plus the F3 spread ladder pushes round-trip cost above 16.01 bps, i.e. net alpha <= 0. *Action:* mechanism fails under real shorting costs.
- **F10 Execution-timing gate.** Re-run with execution shifted so that a weight formed from information dated `d` first earns returns from the open of `d+1` (and separately from the close of `d+1`), i.e. remove the same-close convention described in Negative evidence 13. *Fail* if pooled net Sharpe drops by more than 0.25 in absolute value or if alpha t falls to <= 0. *Action:* the reported edge was an artifact of execution timing.
- **F11 Regime gate.** Metric: quarterly net Sharpe and drawdown over at least 12 quarters of the fresh window. *Fail* if any quarter prints a net Sharpe below -2.0 (2024Q1 printed -4.454084), if any quarter's drawdown exceeds 10 percent, or if negative-alpha quarters exceed 40 percent of quarters. *Action:* the bear-market-hedging claim is withdrawn.
- **F12 Benchmark gate.** Compare against (a) `random, continuous`, (b) `momentum, continuous`, (c) `vader, decile_equal`, (d) the cap-weighted top-100 and (e) an equal-weight top-100, all net and all cost-matched. *Fail* if the strategy does not beat (a), (b) and (d) on net Sharpe, or if its FF5 alpha t <= 1.96. *Action:* no alpha claim survives.
- **F13 Sparse-news ablation gate.** Re-run with `min_news_count` in {1, 3, 5} and with the 30-day smoothing disabled, holding everything else fixed. *Fail* if the strategy's edge exists only at `min_news_count = 1` with smoothing on, i.e. if it is a carried-forward artefact rather than a news response. *Action:* mechanism downgraded to a smoothing artifact.
- **F14 Independent-build replication gate.** A third party rebuilds the pipeline from the README and lock file alone (WRDS access assumed), without reading the shipped results, and compares. *Fail* if they cannot reproduce the published rule - in particular if the ambiguity in `contradictions` (smoothing on signal vs weight panel; primary variant) leads them to a materially different turnover or Sharpe. *Action:* the source is not reproducible as documented; record stays research-only permanently.

**Global no-retuning rule.** Once any gate is attempted, freeze: `L = 1`; the degenerate `lam`; `min_news_count = 1`; `smooth_window = 30`; `short_w = 5`; `long_w = 20`; the rank centring at 0.5 and the `-1` negation; the rank-weighted dollar-normalisation and GICS sector neutralisation; weekly first-XNYS-day rebalance with 5-day holding; the engine scheme as actually shipped (`v2_block30`, weight-panel MA-30, `DEFAULT_SCHEME` in `src/backtest/engine.py`) and, separately, the frozen-config declaration (`bh_block`) - both readings must be reported rather than silently chosen; the flat 5 bps round-trip cost formula `cost = 0.00025 * delta_w`; the point-in-time top-100-from-`dsp500list` universe; the 2020-07 buffer, 2021 development year and 12 quarterly folds with purge 5 / embargo 5; the Claude Haiku 4.5 extraction schema and the three frozen multiplier maps; the `available_date = next XNYS day strictly after event_date` rule; and FF5-with-NW(lags=5) inference on net returns. A failed gate may not be rescued by retuning any of these; only a new record with a new source identity may re-open them.

## Crypto portability

**adapted** (the construction ports mechanically; performance is unproven and no crypto evidence exists in the source).

- What ports: the pipeline shape - event/filing/news extraction into structured polarity features, a bounded cross-sectional rank signal, a weekly dollar-neutral rebalance and a flat round-trip cost model - is asset-class agnostic, and crypto venues do publish news and on-chain/social event streams that could be scored the same way.
- What does not port without re-derivation: there is no GICS sector map for crypto, so sector neutralisation would have to be replaced (correlation-cluster or beta neutralisation would be `research-proposed`); the point-in-time `dsp500list` universe rule has no crypto analogue and would need a point-in-time liquidity/cap listing rule; `pandas_market_calendars` XNYS does not apply to 24/7 venues, so the `first XNYS day of each ISO week` rebalance and the 5-day block holding are undefined there.
- Market-structure differences the source never addresses (census `crypto`, `bitcoin`, `ethereum`, `perpetual`, `24/7` all 0): 24/7 sessions and candle boundaries, perpetual funding and mark/index price, liquidation and ADL, venue fragmentation and per-venue listing/survivorship, borrow and short mechanics on perpetuals, and much thinner top-of-book depth in all but a handful of pairs - which interacts directly with the 1131 percent turnover and the 16.01 bps research-computed cost break-even.
- Evidence status: **unproven** for crypto. This record is a porting hypothesis at most; nothing here is crypto empirical evidence, and any crypto deployment would need the full F1-F14 programme re-run on crypto data with the lock list re-derived rather than copied.
