## Economic mechanism

### Source-reported

The example controller holds a long whenever the close prints below the 100-bar 2σ lower band (`%B < 0`, extreme downside stretch) and a short whenever the close prints above the 100-bar 2σ upper band (`%B > 1`, extreme upside stretch) — a symmetric outer-band fade with no stop, no target, and no second indicator. No profitability claim ships with the example file.

### Research interpretation

This derivative buys a *completed* downside-extreme bar (`%B < 0` on a 100/2.0 band) and holds until a *completed* upside-extreme bar (`%B > 1`) — a level-based outer-band round trip, not an edge-triggered crossover and not a midline exit. It uses no MACD, RSI, volume, multi-timeframe, or order-book input. Because the entire executor layer (stops/targets/time-limits/cooldown/DCA) is disabled as outside the pinned close-simulation primitive, a position that never sees an opposite-extreme bar stays exposed indefinitely; the only risk control is the opposite-extreme close exit. Signals fire only outside the trailing 2σ envelope by construction, so events are sparse by design.

## Signal

Let `O[t], H[t], L[t], C[t]` be a **fully completed** UTC daily OHLC bar with positive finite `C[t]` and `H[t]>=max(O[t],C[t])>=min(O[t],C[t])>=L[t]>0`. All features below read closes only.

1. **Pinned percent-band arithmetic (researcher textbook choice, disclosed):** for `t>=99`, `SMA[t]=mean(C[t-99..t])`, `SD[t]=sqrt(sum((C-SMA[t])^2)/99)` (sample stdev, ddof=1), `U[t]=SMA[t]+2.0*SD[t]`, `L[t]=SMA[t]-2.0*SD[t]`. This is the derivative's pinned construction; it is not a claim of bit-equality with the pandas_ta `bbands` implementation.
2. **Percent-band:** if `U[t]==L[t]` (zero-width degenerate tape), `%B[t]` is **undefined** and the bar's signal is `0` (hold) — never a divide-by-zero, never an invented value. Otherwise `%B[t]=(C[t]-L[t])/(U[t]-L[t])`.
3. **Entry predicate `E[t]`** on a completed bar `t`: `%B[t]<0.0` (strict; `%B==0.0` fires nothing).
4. **Exit predicate `X[t]`** on a completed bar `t`: `%B[t]>1.0` (strict; `%B==1.0` fires nothing; the source `short_condition` state, reused as the LONG exit only — never a short entry).
5. `E[t]` and `X[t]` are **mutually exclusive by construction**: they require `C[t]<L[t]` and `C[t]>U[t]` respectively on the same scalar close with `L[t]<=U[t]` always (SD>=0); the degenerate `U==L` case is undefined-signal by rule 2. Fuzz-verified over 5,000 seeded random walks with zero joint triggers. On a valid completed bar, process **EXIT before ENTRY** as a standing rule (moot under exclusivity, binding if inputs ever change).
6. **Warmup:** the first **100** fully completed, contiguous, validated bars (indices 0–99, matching the source's `max_records = 100`) are warmup-only and cannot trigger orders. The first decidable bar is `t=100`. Warmup bars are used **only** to build indicator state (SMA/SD seed — legitimate indicator-state warmup); no signal decision is evaluated before `t=100`, and no pre-warmup signal is carried forward — every signal is a pure function of closes `<=t` evaluated at `t>=100`.
7. Direction and state: only `FLAT` or `LONG(0.001 BTC)`, initially FLAT. If LONG and `X[t]`, issue one full-quantity SELL at that completed close. Else if FLAT and `E[t]`, issue one BUY of 0.001 BTC at that completed close. Otherwise hold. Never short; no pyramiding, scaling, averaging, BO/SO, DCA, reverse orders, hedges, or simultaneous position/executor exposure. An entry predicate while LONG is a no-op; an exit while FLAT is a no-op. Re-entry is allowed only on a **future completed bar** with a freshly evaluated `E` after SELL fill confirmation — same-bar re-entry is impossible by predicate exclusivity and is additionally prohibited. No cooldown, no pending limit orders. At most one outstanding MARKET action per bar, resolved within that bar's simulation (see Execution assumptions).
8. No stop-loss, take-profit, trailing stop, maximum holding period, forced end-of-sample liquidation, equity-based sizing, leverage, margin, funding payment, or session/date restriction. These are **explicitly disabled researcher decisions** (the source family's executor-layer risk/time exits need fill/state primitives the pinned close-simulation path does not offer for this record), not asserted source defaults. Unclosed positions at the predetermined research end remain marked open at the last close and must not be represented as realized PnL.

## Required data

- One Binance **spot** market `BTC-USDT`, interval `1d`, UTC day boundary, `open/high/low/close` and original candle-open timestamp. No volume, external security, order book, trades, futures funding, mark/index price, higher/lower timeframe, ML model, shared capital, or portfolio synchronization is used.
- Each exchange candle's timestamp labels its **OPEN**, not its completed-close availability. Define `decision_ts[t] = candle_open_ts[t] + 86400` seconds; use the completed candle only when all its OHLC values are available. Feed prepared engine rows with the effective **completed-close timestamp**, never the original open timestamp for decision or simulated fill. Check monotonicity and exactly contiguous 86400-second UTC daily opens.
- A missing, nonfinite, nonpositive or inconsistent OHLC, absent completed close, timestamp gap, or unsorted bar blocks that bar's signal decisions **and fill**. Do not synthesize candles or substitute a prior price. Resume decisions only after 100 new contiguous valid completed bars (which exactly rebuilds the SMA/SD seed); an existing LONG stays open during the gap, with no fictitious stop/exit or fee. A missing/failed fill or position-book mismatch **aborts the run** rather than inventing an order, cash balance, or missing price. Such gaps can prolong unprotected exposure. This rule is strictly causal: an early bar is never retroactively voided by a later gap.

## Limitations

- An exit requires the opposite 2σ extreme; adverse moves that stay inside the envelope print no exit, and without SL/TP/time stop this strategy can hold through substantial adverse moves — the source family's executor-layer risk management was deliberately removed as outside the pinned primitive.
- Any stop/target/trailing/cooldown construction and live order semantics are not reproduced; entries/exits happen at the simulated signal-bar close, which changes events versus any live-managed trade.
- Exact pandas_ta bit-equality, source-native fill clock, original sizing/cost settings and profitability are unknown (upstream unavailable for line comparison this cycle). The simulated completed-close fill is a **backtest convention**, not a live execution guarantee.
- Signals are sparse (4 round trips per ~1000 daily bars in the availability check; both legs proven reachable in fuzz and on real data). The 100-bar trailing envelope lags fast reversals. Near-boundary float comparisons are exact and deterministic but platform-float-dependent at the dust level.
