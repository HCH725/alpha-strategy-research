## Economic mechanism

**Source-reported:** a trading-signal system where four classic oscillators must agree at their extremes before either side prints — oversold RSI under the lower Bollinger rail with depleted intraday bodies (IMI) and drained money flow (MFI) for the buy side, mirrored at overbought for the sell side. The design bets that multi-lens agreement filters single-indicator whipsaw; the page ships no performance numbers.

**Research interpretation (hypothesis only):** a completed hourly close printing a four-way extreme agreement (close-to-close exhaustion + rail position + open-to-close body share + volume-weighted flow) may indicate short-horizon capitulation acceptance, with the mirrored four-way agreement as the fully specified exit. This is a testable hypothesis, not a return, profitability, source-backtest, Survivor, or Qlib-parity claim. Confirmed exits can realize substantially worse prices than any gate line during gaps, and this derivative has no stop/target beyond the signal exit.

## Signal and fully pinned state

**Market/data:** one real Binance spot `BTC-USDT` `1h` OHLCV series with an explicit `volume` column (required by MFI — the source reads it implicitly via `ta.mfi`; this derivative requires it explicitly). No synthetic/Heikin-Ashi candles, higher timeframe, multi-market data, fitted model, news, order book or funding feed. Let `T[t]` be the Binance **opening** Unix timestamp of bar `t`; bar `t` is usable only after `T[t]+3600 seconds`. All bar timestamps must be exact consecutive 3600-second increments; every O/H/L/C must be finite and positive, `H>=max(O,C)`, `L<=min(O,C)`, volume finite and nonnegative. Missing, duplicated, malformed or incomplete bars **abort the research run**, not forward-fill, drop, or substitute a price.

**Pinned indicator semantics (defaults frozen):** `d[i]=close[i]-close[i-1]` (`i>=1`); `g=max(d,0)`, `l=max(-d,0)`; researcher Wilder RSI: seed `avgG/avgL` = SMA of `g/l` over `i=1..14`, iterate `avg=(avg*13+leg)/14` from `i=15`; `RSI[i]=100-100/(1+avgG/avgL)`, with `avgL==0 and avgG>0 → 100`, `avgG==0 and avgL==0 → undefined` (na; never signals). This seed is a researcher choice and is NOT claimed equal to Pine `ta.rsi` first-value seeding. `BB`: `mid/SMA20`, `sd` = population stdev (`ta.stdev` default `biased=true`) over the 20 closes ending at `t`, `bbUpper/mid/lower=mid±2·sd` (defined `i>=19`); The source variable `bbMiddle` actually holds the upper rail and never enters its traded predicates; its `bbUpper` holds the basis. This derivative independently uses the actual upper rail in its sell/flat predicate as explicitly defined above, not the source basis test. `IMI` (exact source arithmetic): `up[t]/dn[t]` = 14-bar sums of `max(C-O,0)`/`max(O-C,0)` over bars `t-13..t`; both zero → undefined (na; never signals); else `100*up/(up+dn)` (defined `i>=13`). `MFI` (researcher textbook pin): `tp=(H+L+C)/3`, `rawMF=tp*volume`; over the 14-bar window ending at `t`, `posMF` sums `rawMF[j]` where `tp[j]>tp[j-1]`, `negMF` where `tp[j]<tp[j-1]` (`j=0` of the whole series contributes to neither — pinned); both zero → undefined; `negMF==0 → 100`; `posMF==0 → 0`; else `100-100/(1+posMF/negMF)` (defined `i>=13`). All gates are strict inequalities — equality never triggers. `strongBuy ⇒ buy` and `strongSell ⇒ sell` hold term-by-term (20<30, 10<20, 80>70, 90>80 with the same rail clause) so the effective traded pair is exactly `buy[t]`/`sell[t]`; the strong variants add no independent events (verified analytically and over 24,000 fuzz bars with full set inclusion). No `request.security`, `time(`, `timestamp(`, or intrabar evaluation exists in the traded path.

**Warmup/initial state:** require **200 consecutive fully completed valid hourly bars** (first decidable signal needs 20 bars; 200 is the independently chosen warmup that additionally pushes the Wilder seed transient down by `(13/14)^186`), start with `FLAT`, no orders, no position, 10,000 USDT cash and no inferred earlier trade. The warmup is a single-spec determinism fence, not a proof of cross-implementation equality with Pine — none is claimed.

**Entry:** on each fully completed bar `t` after warmup, with state FLAT and no pending order, BUY if and only if `rsi[t]<30 and close[t]<bbLower[t] and imi[t]<30 and mfi[t]<20` (any undefined leg never satisfies the gate). A flat/degenerate window can never satisfy strict `<` against its own rail/level construction (verified: flat market is signal-silent). Issue one MARKET BUY of exactly `Q=Decimal("0.001") BTC` at the same row's observed completed `close[t]` under the pinned **historical simulator convention**. Require `cash >= Q*close[t]*(1+f)`, `f=0.001`; otherwise stay flat. On **confirmed full fill**, record `entryPrice=close[t]` and `LONG`, cash minus `Q*entryPrice*(1+f)`. Do not infer a fill from an order intent. A missing/failed/partial fill, invalid price or undefined fee aborts this bounded research run instead of inventing a trade or retry rule.

**Exit priority:** on each fully completed bar `t` while LONG, SELL the entire confirmed Q at that bar's actual completed close if and only if `rsi[t]>70 and close[t]>bbUpper[t] and imi[t]>70 and mfi[t]>80`. Entry and exit predicates are mutually exclusive on one close (`rsi<30` vs `rsi>70`, rail sides, IMI/MFI bands — verified analytically and over 24,000 fuzz bars with zero co-triggers), so no priority ordering is needed and no same-bar entry+exit can occur. The source fills such orders at the next bar open (no `process_orders_on_close` ships in source); this derivative's same-completed-close fill is a disclosed researcher execution choice under the pinned simulator convention, predeclared here. The declared flat gate uses the actual upper Bollinger rail, whereas source `bbUpper` is the basis due to its tuple assignment; this is a material disclosed derivative difference, with neither rule changed by this clarification. No stop/limit resting order, next-open price assumption, time exit, trailing stop, or fill at a gate line. A full confirmed SELL credits `Q*close[t]*(1-f)` and returns to FLAT. An unresolved SELL or partial fill aborts; no fabricated flat book.

**At most one position.** Source defaults to `pyramiding=0` (no setting ships); while LONG, new buy signals are ignored, and the dropped short leg never blocks or reverses. No pyramiding, averaging, BO/SO/DCA, concurrent positions, cooldown, short, leverage, borrowing, compounding, stop/target, time exit, discretionary override or hidden controller risk default. On the last sample bar, mark any still-open BTC to the **actual last observed close**; do not fabricate a terminal liquidation. All signals are ignored while an order is pending.
