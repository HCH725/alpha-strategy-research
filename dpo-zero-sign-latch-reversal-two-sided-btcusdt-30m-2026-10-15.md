## Economic mechanism

### Source-reported

Detrended-cycle acceptance with zero-sign reversal discipline (mirror bilingual prose plus pinned Pine, paper-trading notice fenced above): each bar's close is compared against its own 14-bar simple moving average — the average drawn as a horizontal straight line with prices placed along it by their relation to it — and the signed residue `nRes` prints positive when price stands above its own recent consensus (cycle upswing) and negative when it stands below (cycle downswing). Crossing to the positive side accepts the long side; crossing to the negative side accepts the short side; the print holds its side through exact-zero touches. There is no stop, no target, no flat state by design after the first signal — the opposite-sign print is the exit. The design bets that a close persistently above (below) its own short-horizon average identifies an accepted cycle leg better than any fixed-price breakout, and that the highest-expectancy posture is to always hold the side of the last sign.

### Research interpretation

Single-line level-latch system, two-sided, always-in-position after the first signal, with a sticky hold on exact-zero prints. Unlike two-line cross systems that trade the moment one derived line passes another (the pool's DPO-EMA record crosses DPO against its own EMA4; MACD/PMO/PVO cross an oscillator against its signal EMA), this rule has no second line at all: the 14-bar average is consumed inside the residue, so the trigger is a fixed level (zero), not a cross. Unlike band systems that need a tolerance zone (CMO ±80, Mass Index 26.5 latch, VHF/RAVI thresholds), the trigger sits at the exact balance point where price equals its own average, so every holding period is exactly one cycle-leg migration. Unlike the sibling zero-latch CFO record, which measures a least-squares endpoint forecast deviation in percent, this rule measures a plain equal-weighted average deviation in price units — a different fit (SMA vs linear-regression endpoint) with different lag and different perimeter behavior. The 14-bar window makes the reference slow but smooth: entries arrive after the residue has confirmed, exits arrive after it has decayed through zero. The price of the always-in posture is indefinite adverse rides: a leg whose opposite sign never prints is held forever. The bet is on mean-deviation persistence, not on any momentum extreme, volume print, second series, or calendar effect.
