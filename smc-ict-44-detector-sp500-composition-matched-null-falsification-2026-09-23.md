## Economic mechanism

### Source-reported

- **The paper proposes no economic mechanism**, and says so: §8 limitation 10 — *"No economic mechanism is proposed; this is an evaluation of indicator logic, not a theory paper."*
- The claim under test is the retail SMC/ICT premise: institutional order flow leaves identifiable footprints in price — order blocks, fair value gaps, liquidity sweeps, breaks of market structure — that a rule-based detector can read (§1).
- Two rival readings of a null result are held open by the source and it states its design cannot separate them (§1, §2, §7, §8 lim. 11): (a) **efficient markets** (Fama 1970) — a publicly taught price pattern is competed away; SMC/ICT has been taught openly since roughly the mid-2010s; (b) **adaptive markets / post-publication decay** (Lo 2004; McLean & Pontiff 2016, cited for 97 return-predicting variables with portfolio returns 26% lower OOS and 58% lower post-publication) — the edge existed and decayed. The 2010 sample start is *after* informal SMC/ICT popularisation, so `not stated in source` whether the patterns ever worked.
- **Where the gross return actually comes from (§7 Interpretation):** the long concepts' average gross return at h = 10 is **62 bp**, while entering the same stocks on randomly chosen dates earns **71 bp** (`source-reported`, §7). The source's own reading: the detectors do fire and the positions made money, but that is compensation for being long a rising market — *"The framework's error is not that it identifies nothing; it is that it takes credit for market drift."*
- On the negative point estimates (37/44 or 36/44 negative — see *Evidence*): §7 notes the ten concepts whose bounds exclude a cost-covering edge are break-of-structure, displacement, gap and order-block-formation detectors — continuation-after-a-sharp-move bets — a pattern **consistent with** short-term reversal (Jegadeesh 1990; Lehmann 1990). The source explicitly declines to claim it, because no negative excess survives the two-sided family.

### Research interpretation

- **Falsifiable hypothesis actually tested (`source-reported` test, `research interpretation` framing):** for each detector `k` and horizon `h`, `E[signed forward return | detector fires] − E[signed forward return | same ticker, same count, random dates] > 0`. Decision rule (`source-reported`, §4.3): one-sided, excess positive, survives Benjamini–Hochberg FDR at α = 0.05 applied **once** across all 352 concept × horizon hypotheses, and ≥ 30 trades. Result: **rejected for 0 of 44 — i.e. no detector beats composition-matched random entry** (Table 3).
- **Mechanism of the *methodological* finding (this is the record's transferable content):** three layers each decide the conclusion on identical data — (i) *benchmark choice* (zero-return null admits 38/44; composition-matched null admits 0/44), (ii) *dependence structure* (calendar clustering of events inflates an i.i.d./SRS variance test to 28–41% false rejection at nominal 5%), (iii) *test direction* (a two-sided Welch flag behind a directional claim had reported 27 losers as winners in the prior version). `research interpretation:` a reported technical-analysis edge is a joint product of null definition, variance formula and test direction, so any re-claim of SMC/ICT edge must restate all three.
- **Role of each component (this is a test harness, not an executable strategy):**
  - *Signal layer:* 44 prospective boolean OHLCV detectors with direction fixed in advance (institutional-footprint reading).
  - *Benchmark layer:* composition-matched random-entry null holding ticker mix and per-ticker trade count fixed (removes market drift and composition).
  - *Inference layer:* calendar-time Newey–West collapsed to business-day entry dates (handles overlapping h-day returns and same-day cross-sectional clustering); exact rotation test as secondary; a six-design, 1,000-replication zero-edge calibration to choose among three candidate tests.
  - *Validation layer:* 13-fold walk-forward selection, parameter sweeps, membership-aware survivorship re-test, equivalence/UCB power analysis.
  - `research interpretation:` do not assume each layer contributes equally; an ablation is exactly what Table 3 is.

## Signal

Everything in this section is `source-reported` unless marked otherwise; locations are the SSRN PDF sections cited.

- **Detector registry:** **46 signals declared, 2 never fire** (the New-Day Opening Gap pair, honouring the source script's `ndog_enabled = False` default) → **44 detectors fire**; **2,043,630 events** across 496 tickers, each with direction `+1` or `−1` fixed in advance; **22 long, 22 short** (§3.4). Event frequency ranges from **1,245** (`smc_swing_bos_bearish`) to **212,461** (`ict_displacement_bullish`) (§3.4).
- **Formalisation:** each detector is published as a YAML spec (`docs/specs/*.yaml` in the companion repo) giving a boolean expression over OHLCV and derived indicators, a direction, parameters with defaults taken from the source scripts' `input.*` values and allowed ranges, an expected minimum occurrence count, and an explicit no-future-bar argument; a disambiguation register (`docs/disambiguation.md`) records **16 entries** where the Pine source admits more than one reading (§3.2). **40 of the 44** signals had exactly one configuration ever run; the four exceptions (both liquidity-sweep directions, both new-week opening-gap directions) required a definition choice by the author (§3.2, §3.3).
- **Prospective detection vs confirmation labels:** detection fires on the bar where all conditions are observable; confirmation logic is carried as a *label* and excluded from the tradeable event set (§3.3). Named examples (§3.3, Appendix B):
  - **Liquidity sweep:** penetration of the most recent *confirmed* swing level by `≥ X × ATR`, followed by a close back past that level within `Z` bars; event stamped on the **reclaim bar**. Defaults `X = 0.25 ATR`, `Z = 3 bars`, `Y = 0`, `ATR length 14`, `swing lookback 10` (Appendix B.1). The source script's own sweep (fires when price merely enters a pool, no rejection leg) is kept alongside under its original name.
  - **Opening gap:** `|open − previous close| ≥ 0.10 × ATR(14)`, split by direction (§3.3).
  - **Fair value gap:** `bullish_i ← displacement_bullish[i−1] AND low[i] > high[i−2]`, gap region `[high[i−2], low[i]]`; `filled` within `N = 60` bars is an **outcome label, not an entry signal** (Appendix B.2).
  - **Order block:** follows `LuxAlgo's storeOrdeBlock` exactly — for a bullish block the bar with the lowest low between swing pivot and break, removed once mitigated; a *failing* bullish order block is scored as a **bearish** event (§3.3, §3.4, §6.5).
- **Trade construction (as tested, not as a recommendation):** for an event on bar `p` and `h ∈ {1, 2, 3, 5, 10, 20, 40, 60}`, entry at `close[p]`, exit at `close[p+h]`, MFE/MAE over `high/low[p+1 … p+h]`, returns signed by the detector's direction (§4.1). Primary horizon `h = 10` with **2,037,750 trades**; total **16,272,598 trades** over the eight horizons (§4.1). *(The abstract states **17.6 million** simulated trades — an unreconciled source-internal discrepancy; see Evidence.)*
- **No-look-ahead enforcement:** two mechanical checks — an index-position audit asserting every exit/excursion bar index is strictly greater than the entry bar, and a truncation-invariance test recomputing each detector on data cut at bar `i`; a deliberately leaky canary (the FVG-fill label) proves the probe is not vacuous (§4.1).
- **Not reconstructable from the paper alone:** the **complete list of 44 detector names and their individual boolean definitions** is not printed in the PDF (only the four reformulated detectors above plus scattered examples such as `smc_swing_choch_bearish`, `ict_mss_bearish`, `ict_nwog_gap_up`, `ict_liquidity_buyside_pool_formed`, `smc_internal_ob_bullish_mitigated`); full per-concept results live in `results/statistics_master.csv` / `results/rotation_null_all.csv` (Appendix C), which are repo artefacts, not PDF content → **`data gap`**. Reconstruction therefore requires the two source Pine scripts plus `docs/specs/*.yaml` at pinned commit `69f210c…`.
- **Position sizing, concurrency caps, portfolio construction, re-entry and stop rules:** **`not stated in source`** — the study is a per-event cross-sectional event study, not a portfolio spec. Stop/take-profit: **none** (fixed-horizon exit only).

## Required data

- **Instrument / universe:** S&P 500 constituents as a **2026 snapshot** applied to 2010–2026; of **503** symbols, **498** return data (five permanently unavailable at the snapshot: `AVB`, `EA`, `EQR`, `HONA`, `SATS`), and **496** have ≥ 300 bars and enter the study (§3.1).
- **Venue / market type:** US listed cash equities, daily bars, Yahoo Finance, split- and dividend-adjusted (§3.1, Declarations). No derivatives, no crypto (`source-reported`).
- **Timeframe / calendar:** daily OHLCV; **4,289 business days** in span vs median coverage **4,136 bars** (shortfall = US market-holiday calendar); the real trading-day calendar matters — a walk-forward purge computed on a generic Mon–Fri calendar was a found-and-fixed bug (§3.1, §6.5).
- **Fields:** OHLCV; **ATR(14)** (Wilder RMA); confirmed swing pivots (lookback 10); **GICS sector** labels from a committed constituent snapshot (11 sectors, all tickers classified; an earlier hand-curated map left 54 unclassified and mis-classified six); **index-entry dates** for the membership-aware re-test (§3.1, §5.7).
- **Point-in-time / survivorship:** the constituent list is a 2026 snapshot applied backwards — of 498 constituents with data, **265** were members at sample start and **233** joined later, so **≥ 235 of the 500 start slots (47%)** were held by firms since removed and absent from the sample (§3.1). The membership-aware re-test drops pre-entry trades and restricts the null pools identically; the absent removed firms **cannot be recovered from free sources** (`data gap`, §3.1, §5.7).
- **Data quality:** one material OHLC-invariant violation (`HUBB` 2021-05-05, a back-adjustment artefact of 0.12–0.38% of close) repaired by clamping high/low to enclose open/close; **1,358** bars with violations below `10⁻⁶` of close treated as float noise (§3.1). Imputation is otherwise not used (`not stated in source` beyond the above).
- **Missing-data handling for the five unavailable symbols:** excluded permanently, verified as permanent rather than transient (§3.1).

## Execution assumptions

- **Costs — read in the Methods-equivalent section (§4.6 `Costs`), not inferred:** a round trip is charged **1 bp fixed + 5 bp spread + slippage drawn from `Uniform(5, 20)` bp → 11–26 bp total**, with each draw keyed to the trade's identity so a re-sorted table is charged identically. Per concept the paper reports the **break-even cost** against the gross mean return *and* against the excess over the matched null, and states only the second is decision-relevant (`source-reported`).
- **Signal-to-order timing / fill model:** entry at the **close of the event bar** and exit at the close `h` bars later (§4.1) — i.e. same-bar-close fill at the detection bar's close; **no latency model, no order-type specification, no partial-fill or failure handling** → **`underspecified`**.
- **Slippage:** modelled as the `Uniform(5,20)` bp draw above (a stylised per-trade draw, not an impact model). **Market impact / participation caps / capacity:** **`not stated in source`**.
- **Shorting:** *"Short signals are the negation of the forward return, with no borrow cost or availability constraint"* (§8 limitation 5) → the 22 short detectors' economics are **`underspecified`** for real execution.
- **Leverage / margin / funding:** not applicable to cash equities as tested; **`not stated in source`**.
- **Portfolio construction:** none specified (per-event event study; overlapping positions across events and tickers are not constrained) → **`data gap`** for any tradable implementation.
- **Path-dependent metrics caveat (`source-reported`, §8 limitation 6):** Sharpe, Calmar and maximum drawdown in the paper are computed over an overlapping, cross-sectional trade sequence that is **not an attainable equity curve**; inference uses the calendar-time estimator instead.

### Negative evidence

- **The record itself is negative evidence:** **0 of 44** detectors beat a composition-matched random-entry null at any of eight horizons after BH-FDR across 352 hypotheses, and **0 of 44** are significantly worse (Table 3); the smallest unadjusted one-sided p anywhere is **0.077** (§5.1).
- **Selection does not transfer:** top-5-by-training-excess concepts earn **−31.0 bp** OOS against **+64.7 bp** in-sample, positive in only **5/13** folds, rank correlation **0.32** (Table 6).
- **Costs:** even the modelled 11–26 bp band is cleared on *gross* returns only by the 22 long concepts (market drift); on the matched excess, no concept clears the band significantly (§5.5).
- **Methodological negative evidence against prior positive claims of the same family:** the same data yields 38/44 or 0/44 depending only on the benchmark, and 4/44 or 0/44 depending only on the variance formula (Table 3); the superseded SRS variance rejects 28–41% of zero-edge clustered signals (Table 1); a two-sided flag reversed the sign of the finding for 27 of 28 concepts in the earlier version (§6.2).
- **Power caveat cutting against over-reading the null:** median MDE **47 bp**; for **17** concepts — including both liquidity-sweep detectors — the data cannot distinguish no edge from a **30–50 bp** edge (§7, §8 lim. 1); **19** concepts' bounds exclude nothing of economic interest (Table 5).
- **Survivorship:** ≥ **235 / 500 (47%)** of start-of-sample index slots are held by firms absent from the sample and unrecoverable from free sources (§3.1); the membership-aware re-test only removes pre-entry trades (§5.7).
- **Statistical caveat the source flags itself:** BH's FDR guarantee needs independence/positive regression dependence; the 352 hypotheses are correlated by shared tickers and overlapping windows — a *positive* headline would need Benjamini–Yekutieli, while the reported zero satisfies the bound trivially (§4.3).
- **Adjacent negative evidence in this repository (different sources, not merged here):** `crypto-smc-fvg-bpr-liquidity-retracement-random-walk-falsification-2026-09-12.md` (GitHub `haoyueOuo`, crypto, random-walk geometry control); `cme-futures-ifvg-cisd-opening-breakout-disproof-2026-09-16.md` (GitHub `aleks-drozy`, CME index futures 1-min, pre-registered walk-forward cluster bootstrap); `retail-signal-three-gate-falsification-oscillator-volume-calendar-trend-2026-09-04.md`, `trend-fib-extension-mechanical-pivot-null-edge-falsification-2026-09-14.md`, `retail-crypto-microstructure-signal-falsification-order-flow-cvd-funding-patterns-2026-09-11.md` (other retail-indicator families). Together they are three independent negative results on retail technical-analysis families across crypto, index futures and now large-cap US equity daily bars.

## Falsification plan

Items are labelled `source-reported` (the paper already ran them), `research-proposed` (our proposed test design) or `research-defined falsification threshold` (our pre-declared cutoffs).

2. **Companion-repo ↔ SSRN numeric reconciliation (`research-defined`).** Data: `results/manuscript_facts.json` + `results/statistics_master.csv` vs PDF Tables 3/5/6. 3. **Different-implementation holdout (`research-proposed`).** Replace the two LuxAlgo scripts with a disjoint implementation pair (e.g. `joshyattridge/smartmoneyconcepts`, `smtlab/smartmoneyconcepts`), same 44-concept *family* definitions, same null and inference. Threshold (`research-defined`): **≥ 2 detectors** surviving BH-FDR α = 0.05 across 352 on the holdout → the "no edge" conclusion narrows to *this implementation*. 5. **Cost stress (`research-proposed`).** Recompute break-even costs at **5 bp** and **30 bp** round trip (band re-centred, `research-defined` around the source's 11–26 bp). Threshold: at 30 bp the number of concepts whose excess clears the band must be **≤ 1** (source reports 1 at 26 bp); at 5 bp any count **> 4** flags cost-model dependence of the negative conclusion. ## Crypto portability

**`unproven`**

- The source tests **US listed cash equities on daily bars, single market, single period** (§8 lim. 3, 7) and contains **zero crypto evidence**; it must not be cited as crypto evidence.
- Mechanism transfer is *plausible in principle but untested*: the institutional-footprint claim (order blocks / sweeps / FVG) is venue-agnostic, so the honest label is a **ported hypothesis**, not a result.
- Crypto-specific risks if someone ports it anyway (`research-proposed` list):
  - **24/7 session:** the calendar-time Newey–West estimator is built by collapsing trades onto a **business-day entry calendar** (§4.3) — a crypto port needs a redefined time base (research-proposed: UTC day buckets) and a re-run calibration study, because clustering structure will differ.
  - **Constituent pool:** the composition-matched null needs a stable per-ticker trade-count-matched pool; crypto listing/delisting survivorship is far worse than the already-47%-biased S&P snapshot, so the null itself is harder to build honestly.
  - **Shorts / costs:** the 22 short detectors ignore borrow (§8 lim. 5); in crypto, perp funding, borrow and liquidation economics differ, and the 11–26 bp cost band (1 bp fixed + 5 bp spread + U(5,20) bp slippage) is an **equity-spread calibration that does not transfer** — taker/maker fees and funding must be re-derived per venue (`data gap`).
  - **Data:** no Yahoo-style split/dividend adjustment exists for crypto; venue fragmentation, index/mark price vs last price, and candle-boundary/timezone conventions all change detector inputs (daily bars defined on which 24 h?).
  - **Horizon:** the framework is mostly taught on intraday FX/futures (§7); daily-bar crypto is at least as far from the taught use case as daily-bar equities.

## Limitations

- `not independently reproduced`; single-author, independent-researcher, **non-peer-reviewed SSRN preprint**; SSRN page showed **41 downloads / 109 abstract views** at capture (2026-09-23); the author discloses that code, statistical analysis and prose were produced **with LLM (Claude, Anthropic) assistance** under his direction, with machine-checking claimed via `tools/check_manuscript_numbers.py` and `tests/` (`source-reported`, Declarations). **Zero external funding; no financial position in either indicator publisher** (`source-reported`).
- **Two implementations only, not SMC/ICT as discretionary practice** — context/confluence/judgement are out of scope (§8 lim. 4).
- **Daily bars only** — kill zones and all intraday structure out of scope (§8 lim. 3).
- **Survivorship only partly corrected** — ≥ 235 removed members absent and unrecoverable from free sources (§8 lim. 2).
- **Power:** median MDE 47 bp; edges of 10–30 bp cannot be excluded for most concepts; the primary test is conservative for dispersed entries (size 0.0% in the independent-entry designs, Table 1) (§8 lim. 1).
- **Short mechanics are symmetric negation with no borrow cost or availability** (§8 lim. 5).
- **Path-dependent Sharpe/Calmar/MDD are not an attainable equity curve** (§8 lim. 6).
- **Single market, single period** (§8 lim. 7); sector/regime results descriptive only, current GICS labels applied retroactively (§8 lim. 8); targeted sweep uses 30 tickers so its significance statements are weaker (§8 lim. 9); no economic mechanism proposed (§8 lim. 10); the sample **cannot separate "never worked" from "worked then decayed"** (§8 lim. 11).
- **Complete 44-detector list and per-concept full-horizon tables are `data gap` in the PDF** (they live in repo artefacts `docs/specs/*.yaml`, `results/statistics_master.csv`, `results/rotation_null_all.csv`).
- **The paper never prints its own repository URL** (only "the accompanying repository (Appendix C)"); the GitHub link in *Provenance* was recovered by web search and identity-matched — `data gap` in the primary source text.
- **Three source-internal numeric inconsistencies** and a **README-vs-PDF version skew** are documented in Evidence and remain unresolved (`data gap`).
- `underspecified`: latency, order type, fills, market impact, capacity, portfolio construction, sizing — the paper is an event study, not a tradable specification.
- `unproven` outside large-cap US equity daily bars.
