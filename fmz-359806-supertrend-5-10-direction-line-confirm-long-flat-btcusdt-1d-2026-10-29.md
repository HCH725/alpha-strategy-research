## Economic mechanism

### Source-reported

The 25-line program is a SuperTrend trend system with a confirmation overlay: the SuperTrend direction names the regime (bullish `direction<0` / bearish `direction>0` under `ta.supertrend(5, 10)`), a long is opened only when the ST line itself has risen over the last 2 bars, a short only when it has fallen over the last 3 bars, and an already-open position is flattened as soon as the regime flips against it without the opposite confirmation (otherwise it reverses). No source performance metric is adopted.

### Research interpretation

This is a **regime-plus-confirmation machine with an asymmetric trigger**: direction alone never trades — the ST line must additionally be moving with the regime, and the long leg demands a faster confirmation (2 bars) than the short leg (3 bars), so longs trigger on sharper line upturns while shorts wait for a more established downturn. In the long-flat derivative the asymmetry survives only on the entry side; the exit side is uniform (any bearish bar flattens), which makes exits more trigger-happy than the source's long leg (the source holds a long through a bearish bar when the line is still falling fast — it reverses instead — while the derivative is already flat). Expectancy therefore hinges on confirmed line upturns extending into multi-day trends; choppy direction flickers print fee-paying round trips. A gap across daily closes jumps clean past the band with no slippage accounting. The cash-admission rule adds a deterministic skip path: late-sample BUYs the 10,000 USDT balance cannot cover are cancelled rather than filled. No profitability is asserted.

## Signal

ST line and direction follow the canonical `ta.supertrend` flip recurrence: while `DIR[t-1]<0` (bullish), `ST[t]=FLB[t]` unless `C[t]<FLB[t]` (flip: `ST[t]=FUB[t]`, `DIR[t]=+1`); while `DIR[t-1]>0`, `ST[t]=FUB[t]` unless `C[t]>FUB[t]` (flip: `ST[t]=FLB[t]`, `DIR[t]=-1`); initial bar `t=0` starts bullish-unevaluated (`DIR[0]=-1`, `ST[0]=FLB[0]` — deterministic seed, never evaluated pre-warmup).

   - **Queue (valid completed bar `u`, post-warmup, pending `NONE`):** if confirmed FLAT and `ENTRY[u]`, set pending `BUY` for `f=u+1` (exit predicates are not consulted while FLAT). If confirmed LONG and `EXIT[u]`, set pending `SELL` for `f=u+1` (entry predicates are not consulted while LONG — Pine's same-direction `strategy.entry` under default no-pyramiding is rejected, preserved as the single-position gate). No other queue path exists.
   - **Resolve at bar `f`:** while pending is set, no signal is evaluated on `f` (never evaluate on a fill bar; never reverse on one bar). If `f` is a valid bar: a pending `SELL` fills the full `Q` at `C[f]`; a pending `BUY` fills `Q` at `C[f]` **iff** the cash-admission test `cash >= Q*C[f]*(1+FEE)` holds at that known close — otherwise the pending `BUY` is **cancelled** (skipped: no position, no fee, cash unchanged, confirmed state stays FLAT). ## Limitations

This is an independent derived hypothesis, not a faithful reproduction of the FMZ Pine source. Dropping the short leg and collapsing reversals to flat exits makes the derivative hold cash through bear regimes the source would trade short — in a sustained downtrend the book sits flat collecting nothing while the source shorts, and in a V-reversal the derivative re-enters only on a fresh 2-bar line upturn, potentially late.
