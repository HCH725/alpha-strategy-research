## Economic mechanism

### Source-reported

The source claims only what its code prints: hold long exposure entered when the 14-bar RSI crosses back up through 40 while the close stands above both its 50-bar and 200-bar exponential averages with ADX clearing its floor, stand back to flat when the RSI crosses back down through 60. The ADX floor at pinned default `0` certifies rather than vetoes (every non-negative ADX print clears it); the trend veto is carried entirely by the dual-EMA price gate. No stop, no target, no trailing, no time exit, no sizing, no cost basis ships anywhere — this record adopts no source-reported performance (see Evidence).

### Research interpretation

Oversold-bounce holder with a slow-trend chaperone, long-only, edge-triggered, zero-stop. The RSI leg fires only on fresh crosses (a dip below 40 followed by a reclaim — a level hold above 40 is silence), and the EMA leg demands the reclaim print above two slow averages at once, so entries are rare by construction: shallow pullbacks that never push RSI under 40 never arm, and deep washes that recover beneath the EMA200 never qualify. Verified this cycle on an 800-bar synthetic tape (pinned seed 99): 2 entries against 29 exits — the exit leg, a bare RSI-60 rollover with no price gate, fires an order of magnitude more often and usually from flat (absorbed as no-ops by the position pin). The failure mode is plain: a V-recovery that reclaims 40 below the EMA200 is watched from flat all the way up, and a slow bleed that holds RSI above 60 keeps a stale long with no trend-break exit (the exit reads RSI alone — price can sit under both EMAs and the book still holds until RSI rolls over).

## Signal

Exact rule as pinned (any other RSI length, threshold pair, EMA span, ADX floor, frame, fill, book, or risk posture is a different, unpinned rule). `RSI(14)` is the facade Wilder-style series; `EMA50/EMA200` are `adjust=False` exponential means:

```text
prev_rsi_t  = RSI14_{t-1}                       // .shift(1), causal prior-bar
trend_ok_t  = (close_t > EMA50_t) & (close_t > EMA200_t)
adx_ok_t    = (ADX14_t >= 0)                    // pinned floor, always true ex-NaN
entry_t     = (prev_rsi_t < 40) & (RSI14_t >= 40) & trend_ok_t & adx_ok_t   // EDGE
exit_t      = (prev_rsi_t >= 60) & (RSI14_t < 60)                           // EDGE
```

- Trade events: `entry_signal` on a fresh RSI-40 reclaim bar with the dual-EMA gate green; `direction` long-only; `exit_signal` on a fresh RSI-60 rollover bar with no price gate. A flat book enters one full unit (`0.001` BTC) on an entry bar; a long book exits the full unit on the first exit-leg bar.
