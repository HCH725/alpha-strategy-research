## Economic mechanism

### Source-reported

The paper's stated framing (Section 1): scheduled announcements have a **two-stage** influence - first the uncertainty inherent in the announcement induces a premium because investors bear undiversifiable risk, then the revealed signal moves prices. The paper studies only the **first stage, the pre-announcement premium**, for `monetary policy decisions announced by the Federal Open Market Committee (FOMC) and their impact on the U.S. options market, specifically on options written on the S&P 500 index and the induced implied volatility (IV) surface`. The justification for looking at derivatives is that `uncertainty and changes in market expectations are often reflected first in derivatives markets, which are inherently forward-looking`, and the event is chosen because prior empirical evidence documents `the particularly strong magnitude of the pre-FOMC effect in equity markets, as shown, for example, in (Lucca and Moench 2015)`.

The economic story the source tells for the observed IV rise (Section 3.1): as the conference approaches, `the uncertainty accumulates which cannot be diversified away`; invoking `Standard asset pricing theory in the spirit of (Sharpe 1964)`, instruments bearing higher systematic risk carry higher premia, so `options become more attractive and hence more expensive which raises the ATM IV`, while `the spot market only reacts to the news when they happen which raises the realised volatility later`. The source also notes the anomaly it inherits from Lucca and Moench: `the flow of information only spikes at the announcement, while the premiums in the underlying are earned before the announcement is made`, and reads the pre-FOMC options flow as evidence that `it is the fact the conference will be held and not the signal received what increases uncertainty, systematic risk and therefore, induces higher premia`. The decline of the effect with maturity is attributed to crowding out: longer maturities mix many other events and `cover more than one FOMC meeting over their lifespan`.

The forecasting claim is separate and explicitly modest (Sections 1, 3.2, 4): a 2D convolutional LSTM forecast of the whole IV surface, augmented with an exogenous FOMC dummy, should capture the pre-announcement shock better than a random walk and better than the same network without the dummy. The source concedes that `the edge of the ML framework can be limited due to the noisy characteristics of the IV surface`.

### Research interpretation

Normalized as a falsifiable, unsigned, event-window claim:

- **H-event (level, not direction):** the at-the-money implied volatility of **short-dated S&P 500 options** is systematically higher in the two sessions preceding an FOMC meeting than on matched non-event sessions, with the elevation decaying monotonically in time to expiry, and this is an **uncertainty-insurance-demand** effect rather than a directional view on equities. Candidate mechanism: a scheduled, fully anticipated information event makes short-horizon tail insurance bid up before the event while realized volatility only moves after it, so the tradeable residue (if any) is long-volatility timing, not direction.
- **Component roles (hybrid structure as decoded from the source):**
  - Regime / event calendar: the published FOMC schedule (known in advance; about `8 scheduled meetings per year`, two days Tuesday to Wednesday, and `press conference led by FED chair at 2pm EST`).
  - Signal: a categorical dummy `X` = `1` on the conference day, `-1` on the day after, `0` otherwise, fed through a dense side-branch and added to the ConvLSTM surface branch.
  - Forecast layer: two `ConvLSTM2D` layers (32 filters, 3x3, tanh) plus a `Conv2D` head over a fixed 41 x 21 grid with a 5-day lookback; benchmark is the persistence `random walk` at the same grid point.
  - Outcome: the **IV surface level itself** (RMSE / MPE, Diebold-Mariano against the benchmark) and, in the empirical half, the distributional shift of ATM IV - never a return, a PnL or a portfolio.
- **What is not claimed by us or by the source:** the source reports no trading rule, no entry, no exit and no performance figure, and its own conclusion is that the ML advantage `is not uniform across IV surface`. Do not read the IV rise as proven alpha: gates F3, F7 and F11 exist precisely because statistical elevation in IV is not the same as a long-volatility position that survives bid-ask, fees and the post-event IV collapse.

**Empirical half (Section 3.1, Tables 2, 3, 13-22, Figures 4-6):**

- Focus measure: ATM IV is the IV whose log-moneyness `k` is closest to 0 for each fixed maturity, evaluated on the original (uninterpolated) grid, `We also stick to call options, since put options feature a very similar reaction`; put-side tests are moved to Appendix C. Realized volatility is `rolling annualised standard deviation of returns on the underlying (S&P 500) with the window of size one hour`.
- Event windows: `U-test across 3 days preceding the FOMC conference in 2 hour intervals, as well as on the day of the conference itself`, i.e. 10:00, 12:00, 14:00 and 16:00 rows for T-3, T-2, T-1 and T-0, at the nine log-moneyness points printed in every table header, -0.02, -0.015, -0.01, -0.005, 0, 0.005, 0.01, 0.015 and 0.02 (research rendering of the header row). `The FOMC conference starts at 2pm at 0 DTE.`
- Test: `Mann-Whitney U-test which only requires the independence of both samples`, with the one-sided alternative that the event-day IV distribution is `shifted to the right`; significance stars at 5, 1 and 0.1 percent. Appendix C repeats the family for other maturities and for puts under a differently worded (two-sided) alternative - see contradiction 2.
- Reported shape (Figures 4-6): for 0 DTE `the implied volatility starts to rise in comparison to regular days approximately two days before the announcement`, peaks `even up to 50%` on the announcement day before 2pm, then declines `due to possibly the resolution of uncertainty`, while realized volatility `only starts rising after the start of the FOMC conference`; for 2 DTE the effect is `much less pronounced`; for 10 DTE `There is little to no effect` and the source concludes `a very sharply declining term structure of the pre-announcement effect`.

**Forecasting half (Sections 2.2, 2.3, 2.4, 2.5, Tables 1, 4-12):**

- Surface construction: three interchangeable methods, chosen so that `a specific interpolation or extrapolation does not biases the results` - linear interpolation of total variance with linear wing extrapolation using edge points `spaced out by 2`, the Dumas et al. For 0 DTE the SVI formula would divide by zero, so `we treat such options as having0.5DTE`.

## Falsification plan

Each gate states a **Threshold** (a `research-defined falsification threshold`, not a source value) and an **Action** that follows failure. Nothing in this section is claimed by the source.

- **F1 - Printed-value reproduction.** *Threshold:* from an independent options dataset, rebuild Tables 2, 3, 5, 7, 9 and 12 to their printed rounding (p-values to `0.001`, RMSE to `0.001`, MPE to `0.001`). **Action:** any mismatch beyond one printed rounding unit means the Source-reported block is treated as unverifiable, `contested` stays true, and status remains `research-only` with `not independently reproduced` retained.
- **F2 - Alternative-hypothesis reconciliation gate (attacks contradiction 2).** *Threshold:* a single rerun in which the one-sided right-shift and two-sided difference Mann-Whitney alternatives are reported separately and the two-sided p-values are consistent with the one-sided ones (two-sided approximately `2 x min(p, 1-p)` up to continuity correction). **Action:** until that exists, no p-value from Tables 2, 3, 13-22 may be quoted as confirmed, and `confidence` may not rise above `medium`.
- **F3 - Economic-utility gate for a pre-FOMC long-volatility rule (`research-proposed`).** *Threshold:* an event rule specified before data access - for example long ATM 0-2 DTE straddle entered at the T-1 close and exited at the T+0 16:00 close - must produce a mean net-of-cost expectancy whose 95 percent bootstrap interval excludes zero after charging half the quoted spread plus a fee ladder of 0 / 1 / 2 / 5 ticks per leg, with assignment and early-exercise cash flows modeled. **Action:** failure records the effect as statistically real but economically null, and no trading construction may cite this record.
- **F4 - Term-structure gate.** *Threshold:* the event-day ATM IV elevation must be monotone decreasing in DTE - 0 DTE > 2 DTE > 10 DTE - with the 10 DTE elevation statistically indistinguishable from zero. **Action:** if 10 DTE shows an equal or larger elevation, the short-horizon uncertainty reading of the mechanism is falsified and the claim must be re-framed as a generic volatility-level shift.
- **F5 - Moneyness / wing gate.** *Threshold:* after restricting to moneyness bins with at least 50 quotes per event-day cell, the OTM-wing elevation must be separately significant at `p < 0.05` before any statement about wings is repeated. **Action:** failure keeps the finding ATM-only and voids the abstract's OTM clause as unsupported.
- **F6 - Event-snapshot boundary gate (attacks the 14:00 ambiguity).** *Threshold:* re-estimating the event effect with snapshots stamped at 13:30 ET and at 14:30 ET must show a significantly larger elevation at 13:30 than at 14:30 for the **pre-announcement** reading to hold. **Action:** if the elevation appears only after 14:00, the artifact measures a post-statement reaction, not a pre-announcement premium, and every claim in this record must be re-labeled.
- **F7 - Regime gate (attacks contradiction 1).** *Threshold:* a pre-registered split of events by the VIX tercile at T-2 must show a strictly larger ATM IV elevation in the top tercile, one-sided `p < 0.05`. **Action:** failure marks the abstract's `high volatility regimes` clause as unsupported rather than merely untested, and it may not be repeated downstream.
- **F8 - Forecast-value gate.** *Threshold:* the dummy-augmented ConvLSTM must beat the random walk on announcement days with Diebold-Mariano `p < 0.05` **and** the win must hold in at least 5 of the 6 seeds. **Action:** failure downgrades the feature-engineering claim to noise and removes the forecasting half from any downstream summary.
- **F9 - Dummy-ablation gate.** *Threshold:* across the full test year, the dummy model must improve RMSE by at least `2.0 percent` relative to the same model without the dummy, not only on event days. **Action:** below that, the dummy is an event-day patch rather than a modeling improvement and must be labeled as such.
- **F10 - Multiplicity gate.** *Threshold:* Benjamini-Hochberg at `q = 0.10` over the full displayed family (9 moneyness x 4 times x DTE families x option types) must leave at least one T-1 and one T-0 cell significant for 0 DTE calls. **Action:** failure means the star pattern is a multiple-testing artifact and the headline elevation claim is void.
- **F11 - Cost-and-assignment stress gate.** *Threshold:* the event-rule expectancy in F3 must remain positive under a doubled fee ladder (0 / 2 / 4 / 10 ticks) and under the worst observed one-day spread widening on event days. **Action:** failure moves the finding to `economically fragile` and blocks any capacity or sizing discussion.
- **F12 - Independent-data replication gate.** *Threshold:* a replication on data from a vendor other than CBOE DataShop (different tape, same instrument definition) must reproduce the sign and the 0 > 2 > 10 DTE ordering of the elevation. **Action:** failure classifies the effect as vendor- or filter-specific and the record must be re-opened.
- **F13 - Sample-size and coverage gate.** *Threshold:* at least `80` FOMC events (2016 through the end of the forward window) with Wednesday expiries present, and at least `500` event-day option quotes per DTE bin after filters. **Action:** failure marks the event evidence as underpowered and forbids any significance language in downstream summaries.
- **F14 - Frozen forward window.** *Threshold:* events from `2026-10-03` through `2027-09-30`, with no retuning of grid, lookback, dummy coding, snapshot time, moneyness bins, DTE bands or any F1-F13 threshold, must reproduce a rejecting T-1 elevation at 0 DTE with the monotone DTE ordering. **Action:** failure forces this record to be re-opened, `contested` re-evaluated and `confidence` re-set before any further use.

**Global no-retuning rule (`research-defined`):** if any gate fails, none of the following may be changed before re-running it: the January-2016-to-2025 sample window and the 2016-2023 / 2024 / 2025 split; the six data filters and the open-interest > 0 rule; the 41 x 21 fixed grid and its `[-0.2, 0.2]` moneyness span and maturity set `{0..20}`; the total-variance linear, AHBS and SVI constructions and the 0-DTE-as-0.5-DTE rule; the 5-day lookback, the horizons `{1, 2, 5, 10}` and the autoregressive rollout; the dummy coding `1 / 0 / -1` and its dense side-branch; the ConvLSTM depth, 32 filters, 3x3 kernel, learning rate 0.00005, batch 32, dropout 0.1, Adam with MSE, and the 6-seed mean reporting; the random-walk benchmark; RMSE, MPE and Diebold-Mariano-with-HAC as metrics; the ATM definition (k closest to 0), the 2-hour event rows, the nine moneyness points and the Mann-Whitney alternative; the `2pm ET` snapshot and the ET/EST convention; and every threshold printed in F1-F14. **`adapted` (mechanism plausibly portable, sign and economic value `unproven`).**

The source contains **no crypto analysis**: the only crypto-adjacent strings are one literature-review sentence (`ML models have become a standard tool in financial forecasting, especially in crypto-currency markets`), two reference titles about cryptocurrency trading systems and one reference title about Bitcoin volatility drivers - census `crypto` 3, `bitcoin` 1, `ethereum` 0, `perpetual` 0, `funding` 0, `liquidation` 0, `deribit` 0. Under the repository rule that a traditional-asset mechanism may not be labeled `direct` unless the source itself demonstrates it in crypto markets, this record is `adapted`, and any crypto version is a `research-proposed` ported hypothesis rather than crypto empirical evidence.

Portability risks that must be resolved before any port:

- **Event analogue.** FOMC is a US equity-index-specific scheduled event; crypto has its own scheduled macro events (CPI, FOMC itself still moves crypto, exchange listings, token unlocks) but no FOMC-linked expiry cycle. Which event calendar to use, and whether the pre-event uncertainty premium transfers to a 24/7 asset, is `research-proposed`.
- **Expiry and session structure.** The paper's whole term-structure result depends on `0 DTE`, `2 DTE` and Wednesday-maturing contracts introduced in 2016, on a `9:30am to 4:00pm ET` session and on a `2pm` snapshot. Crypto options (e.g. Deribit BTC/ETH) settle on a 24/7 clock with daily and quarterly expiries, no 14:00 statement boundary and no ET close, so DTE bands, the event snapshot and the `0.5DTE` stabilisation all need `research-proposed` redefinitions.
- **Surface construction.** The SVI / AHBS / linear menu, the -0.2 to 0.2 moneyness span and the open-interest filter were tuned on CBOE index options with deep, dense two-sided quotes. Crypto option chains are far thinner, skew is steeper, and the source's own warning that `the data in the wings, of especially short-maturity options is of poor quality due to low liquidity and arbitrage violations` applies at least as strongly - `data gap`.
- **Frictions.** Quoted spread, taker fees, funding on the hedge leg, mark/index divergence between the option underlying and the perp used to delta-hedge, venue fragmentation, listing churn and 24/7 gap risk are all unaddressed by the source (`data gap`). The source has no cost model to port in the first place.
- **What could port.** The calendar-dummy discipline (exogenous, pre-scheduled, known-in-advance event feature), the three-way interpolation robustness check, the random-walk benchmark with Diebold-Mariano/HAC, the multi-seed reporting, and the explicit admission that a statistical IV effect is not an economic result - these are `research-proposed` as a crypto evaluation contract even while the alpha sign stays `unproven`.
