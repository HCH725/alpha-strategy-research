## Economic mechanism

### Source-reported

Volatility-trailing trend riding with the stop as the trigger (strategy README: `Volatility-based trailing stop system developed by Chuck LeBeau. "Hangs" from the highest high (for longs) or lowest low (for shorts) like a chandelier, using ATR to set the distance. Excellent for riding trends while protecting profits`; `Trading Style: Trend riding with automatic trailing`): in an uptrend the long never chases a fresh-range extreme — it waits for price to cross back above a line hung 3 ATRs beneath the 22-bar highest high; the short mirrors against a line hung 3 ATRs above the 22-bar lowest low in a downtrend. Either leg exits on the first cross back through its own line — the trailing line is the entry trigger and the stop in one object. There is deliberately no stop, no target, no trailing order leg, no time exit, and no always-in-market claim beyond post-signal holds — the order block ends with the two `strategy.close` guards and no risk leg ships anywhere, by design gap rather than by hidden leg.

### Research interpretation

Regime-gated volatility-line reclaim with the trail as the exit — the first dual-line Chandelier record in the pool. Unlike single-stop systems that watch one line for both sides, this rule keeps two independent lines: a breakdown far under the long line while the short line was never crossed fires no short — each side's trigger is its own extremum's trail, so a ranging market that sags without printing a fresh 22-bar lowest low simply holds or sits flat instead of flipping. Note the two pinned asymmetries, stated plainly: first, the entry needs the trend regime on its side (longs only above the 50 EMA, shorts only below) while the exit needs only its own line cross — a long entered in an uptrend that later sees price sink under the 50 EMA is NOT exited by the regime loss itself, only by the long-line crossunder, so a regime flip without a trail cross is held, disclosed; second, each exit is a single edge cross of its own line, not a hold-beyond state — a bar that gaps far through the line in one move still exits exactly once at that close with no trailing guard for the further slide, and a bar that merely kisses the line without crossing holds, disclosed. The bet is on EMA-regime trend continuation at frozen `22/3.0/50` geometry with the ATR trail banking the move, not on any breakout extreme, volume print, momentum sign, calendar effect, or second timeframe.
