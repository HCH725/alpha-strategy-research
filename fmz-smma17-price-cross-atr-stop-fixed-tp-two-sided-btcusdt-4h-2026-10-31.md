## Economic mechanism

### Source-reported

Smooth-trend breakout with a volatility-anchored leash, both directions. The 17-period Wilder SMMA sketches a slow consensus price; a close printing through it marks a regime handoff. The long rides while closes hold above the line, guarded by a stop that floats 0.75 ATR beneath the same line and a fixed 1150-point profit cap; the short is the exact mirror with a 1500-point cap. The first opposite cross closes the trade and opens the other side. The source ships no sample statistics, trade count, fee basis, win rate, or performance figure of any kind — no source-reported performance is adopted or restated.

### Research interpretation

Single-line trend machine with a triple exit, event-driven, two-sided, effectively always-in-market after warmup. Unlike stack records (alligator-smma-stack — three displaced hl2-SMMA containment with a mouth-state gate, long-flat, Jaw-break exit), the trigger here is one close crossing one unshifted close-based SMMA — no displacement, no containment, no gate. Unlike average-cross records (ema-20-50-cross, hull-ema-crossover, dpo-ema-crossover — two averages of closes crossing each other), the cross is price-against-average, so every extreme print is tested against the slow line rather than one smoothing against another. Unlike bracket-only records (the merged PowerZone 1.5R/1.0R family — pattern-anchored risk boxes with no cross exit), the reverse cross adds a third exit that can cut winners short of the cap and flip the book without any bracket touch — the record's principal whipsaw channel, disclosed. The edge, if any, lives in the documented intuition that closes holding one side of a slow Wilder average mark a rideable 4h drift, with the floating SMMA stop giving trends room while the fixed caps bank them — a bet on multi-hour drift persistence, not on any level, streak, volume flow, calendar effect, or second timeframe.

## Signal — Gate 1, fully pinned derivative

Index completed UTC 4-hour bars `t = 0, 1, ...` with real Binance BTC-USDT futures `O[t], H[t], L[t], C[t]` (volume not read). A bar with missing/nonfinite/non-positive prices, or with `H < max(O,C)` / `L < min(O,C)`, is invalid; never interpolate. All inputs pinned at source defaults: `SMMALEN = 17`, `ATRLEN = 14`, `STOPMULT = 0.75`, `TPLONG = 1150`, `TPSHORT = 1500` price points (any other values are a different, unpinned rule).

- **SMMA `S[t]`:** Wilder recursion `S[t] = SMA(C,17)` at the first seedable bar, else `(S[t−1] × 16 + C[t]) / 17` (Pine `na(smma[1]) ? ta.sma(...) : ...` semantics; undefined before 17 valid bars).
- **ATR `A[t]`:** RMA of true range over 14 bars (`ta.rma(ta.tr(true), 14)` semantics; `TR[0]` uses the bar's own range only, never a fabricated prior close; undefined before 14 valid bars; `A[t] ≥ 0` always).
- **Long signal `EL[t]`:** `C[t−1] ≤ S[t−1] AND C[t] > S[t]` (`ta.crossover(close, smma)`). **Short signal `ES[t]`:** `C[t−1] ≥ S[t−1] AND C[t] < S[t]` (`ta.crossunder(close, smma)`).
- **Mutual exclusivity (proven, no invented priority):** `EL[t]` needs `C[t] > S[t]` while `ES[t]` needs `C[t] < S[t]` (close-equality fires neither); the `t−1` legs are likewise incompatible — verified by 20,000-case exclusivity fuzz plus equality-edge cases in scratch this cycle: `SELF-CHECK OK`. A signal bar arriving while positioned on the same side is discarded unseen (explicitly no queue, no add — Pine `pyramiding = 0` default behavior pinned as an explicit gate).
- **Bracket legs (floating stop, fixed cap):** on entry bar `e` (fill bar, see Execution assumptions), long limit `LTP = C[e] + 1150` latched once and never recomputed; long stop recomputed every held bar `t ≥ e` as `LST[t] = S[t] − 0.75 × A[t]` (floats with SMMA — can move adversely, carried over exactly). Short mirror: `STP = C[e] − 1500` latched; `SST[t] = S[t] + 0.75 × A[t]`.
- **Exit precedence (explicit researcher pin):** from the fill bar onward, each held bar resolves in this order: (1) bracket first-touch against the bar's `H/L` with the **stop-first** same-bar rule (both touched → stop price wins); (2) only if the bracket is untouched, the cross-close/reversal signal at the bar close. A bar touching the bracket AND printing the opposite signal closes the old unit at the bracket price and still opens the reversed unit at the next bar's open (matches Pine intrabar-exit-before-next-open-entry sequencing; disclosed, never a file literal).

**Direction (Gate 2):** BUY-to-open / SELL-to-close on the long leg; SELL-to-open / BUY-to-close on the short leg. The short leg requires margin-short permission, realistic on the pinned perpetual venue (same two-sided posture as the merged PowerZone and Super-Scalper records). No hedge, no cross-market trade.

**Risk (Gate 3, explicit):** bracket-plus-reverse-cross book. Floating SMMA-anchored stop (0.75 ATR), fixed 1150/1500-point caps, opposite-cross close-and-reverse. No trailing ratchet, no breakeven move, no time stop, no partial exit, no averaging, no forced end-of-data liquidation, no silent executor default. An end-of-sample open position is marked at the last real valid close, not closed with an invented fill.

**State (Gate 4):** `FLAT`, `BUY_PENDING`, `LONG`, `SELL_PENDING`, `SHORT`, `COVER_PENDING`. Initialize `FLAT` after 40 valid completed bars. Entries fire only from confirmed `FLAT` or as one-step reversals from the opposite confirmed state; a missing/failed/unconfirmed fill **halts** this research run, never synthesizes inventory. While `LONG`, further `EL` bars are no-ops and `ES` reverses (bracket canceled on reversal — Pine `from_entry` semantics); symmetrically while `SHORT`. A pending order prohibits additional actions. **No DCA/safety orders (explicitly disabled — the downstream 6%+6%+6% house overlay is not active in this admission and never silently overrides single-unit semantics)**, no pyramiding, no concurrency, no compounding. No cooldown is specified (explicitly none, not invented) — re-entry needs a fresh cross print.
