## Economic mechanism

### Source-reported

The example controller buys a state where price has penetrated below the 100-window 2σ lower band (`%B < 0`) while the 21/42/9 MACD histogram is already positive although the MACD line itself is still negative — i.e. a deeply oversold print whose downside momentum is turning — and mirrors it for the short side (`%B > 1` with histogram negative while the line is still positive). The band penetration shows dislocation; the histogram-vs-line split shows the turn starting before the slower line confirms it. No profitability claim ships with the example file.

### Research interpretation

This derivative buys a *completed* long-triple bar and holds until a *completed* exit-triple bar. It is neither a MACD line/signal cross nor a band-midline reclaim, and it uses no RSI, volume, volatility, multi-timeframe, or order-book input. Because the entire executor layer (stops/targets/time-limits/cooldown/DCA) is disabled as outside the pinned close-simulation primitive, entries may fire on dislocations that keep falling, and a continued decline after entry may leave the long position exposed indefinitely; the only risk control is the opposite-triple close exit. Signals are sparse by construction.

## Signal

Let `O[t], H[t], L[t], C[t]` be a **fully completed** UTC daily OHLC bar with positive finite `C[t]` and `H[t]>=max(O[t],C[t])>=min(O[t],C[t])>=L[t]>0`. All features below read closes only.

1. **Bollinger %B(100, 2.0):** for `t>=99`, `SMA100[t]` is the exact arithmetic mean of closes `C[t-99..t]`; `SD100[t]` is the sample standard deviation (ddof=1) of the same 100 closes; `UB[t]=SMA100[t]+2*SD100[t]`, `LB[t]=SMA100[t]-2*SD100[t]`; `%B[t]=(C[t]-LB[t])/(UB[t]-LB[t])`. If `UB[t]==LB[t]` (zero-range window), `%B[t]` is undefined and both predicates are false. This is the derivative's pinned computation (sample-std guards the pandas_ta `bbands` convention); it is not a claim of bit-equality with the pandas_ta/TA-Lib binaries.
2. **MACD(21, 42, 9):** classic recursive EMA with `k=2/(n+1)`, seeded deterministically: `EMA21` seeded with `SMA(C[0..20])` at index 20, `EMA42` seeded with `SMA(C[0..41])` at index 41; `MACD[t]=EMA21[t]-EMA42[t]` for `t>=41`; `SIG[t]` is the 9-EMA of the MACD line seeded with `SMA(MACD[41..49])` at index 49; `MACDh[t]=MACD[t]-SIG[t]` for `t>=49`. This is the derivative's pinned definition (researcher seeding, disclosed); it is not a claim of bit-equality with the pandas_ta `macd` implementation.
3. **Entry predicate `E[t]`** on a completed bar `t`: `%B[t]<0.0` AND `MACDh[t]>0.0` AND `MACD[t]<0.0`.
4. **Exit predicate `X[t]`** on a completed bar `t`: `%B[t]>1.0` AND `MACDh[t]<0.0` AND `MACD[t]>0.0` (the source `short_condition` triple, reused as the LONG exit only — never a short entry).
5. All six comparisons are strict; equality on any conjunct (`%B==0.0/1.0`, `MACD==0.0`, `MACDh==0.0`) fires nothing. `E[t]` and `X[t]` are **mutually exclusive by construction**: they require opposite strict signs of the shared `MACD[t]` (`<0.0` vs `>0.0`); a `MACD[t]==0.0` bar satisfies neither. Fuzz-verified over 20,000 seeded random walks with zero joint triggers. On a valid completed bar, process **EXIT before ENTRY** as a standing rule (moot under exclusivity, binding if inputs ever change).
6. **Warmup:** the first **120** fully completed, contiguous, validated bars (indices 0–119, matching the source's `max_records = 120`) are warmup-only and cannot trigger orders. The first decidable bar is `t=120`. Warmup bars are used **only** to build indicator state (BB window + EMA seeds — legitimate indicator-state warmup); no signal decision is evaluated before `t=120`, and no pre-warmup signal is carried forward — every signal is a pure function of closes `<=t` evaluated at `t>=120`.
7. Direction and state: only `FLAT` or `LONG(0.001 BTC)`, initially FLAT. If LONG and `X[t]`, issue one full-quantity SELL at that completed close. Else if FLAT and `E[t]`, issue one BUY of 0.001 BTC at that completed close. Otherwise hold. Never short; no pyramiding, scaling, averaging, BO/SO, DCA, reverse orders, hedges, or simultaneous position/executor exposure. An entry predicate while LONG is a no-op; an exit while FLAT is a no-op. Re-entry is allowed only on a **future completed bar** with a freshly evaluated `E` after SELL fill confirmation — same-bar re-entry is impossible by predicate exclusivity and is additionally prohibited. No cooldown, no pending limit orders. At most one outstanding MARKET action per bar, resolved within that bar's simulation (see Execution assumptions).
8. No stop-loss, take-profit, trailing stop, maximum holding period, forced end-of-sample liquidation, equity-based sizing, leverage, margin, funding payment, or session/date restriction. These are **explicitly disabled researcher decisions** (the source family's executor-layer risk/time exits need fill/state primitives the pinned close-simulation path does not offer for this record), not asserted source defaults. Unclosed positions at the predetermined research end remain marked open at the last close and must not be represented as realized PnL.

## Required data

- One Binance **spot** market `BTC-USDT`, interval `1d`, UTC day boundary, `open/high/low/close` and original candle-open timestamp. No volume, external security, order book, trades, futures funding, mark/index price, higher/lower timeframe, ML model, shared capital, or portfolio synchronization is used.
- Each exchange candle's timestamp labels its **OPEN**, not its completed-close availability. Define `decision_ts[t] = candle_open_ts[t] + 86400` seconds; use the completed candle only when all its OHLC values are available. Feed prepared engine rows with the effective **completed-close timestamp**, never the original open timestamp for decision or simulated fill. Check monotonicity and exactly contiguous 86400-second UTC daily opens.
- A missing, nonfinite, nonpositive or inconsistent OHLC, absent completed close, zero-range `%B` window, timestamp gap, or unsorted bar blocks that bar's signal decisions **and fill**. Do not synthesize candles or substitute a prior price. Resume decisions only after 120 new contiguous valid completed bars (which exactly rebuilds the BB window and EMA seeds); an existing LONG stays open during the gap, with no fictitious stop/exit or fee. A missing/failed fill or position-book mismatch **aborts the run** rather than inventing an order, cash balance, or missing price. Such gaps can prolong unprotected exposure. This rule is strictly causal: an early bar is never retroactively voided by a later gap.

- `hummingbot/strategy_v2/backtesting/backtesting_engine_base.py` — `simulate_execution` loops row-wise (`for i, row in processed_features.iterrows()`), calls `update_state(row)` then `controller.determine_executor_actions()`, and routes each `CreateExecutorAction` to the executor simulator with the forward slice **starting at the current row** (`processed_features.loc[i:max_ts]`). `update_state` sets the engine clock from the prepared row, converts the previous tick's stopped POSITION_HOLDs into position holds, then refreshes executor info and positions held. `prepare_market_data` merges onto the backtest clock and resets the row timestamps to the backtest clock. - `hummingbot/strategy_v2/backtesting/executors_simulator/order_executor_simulator.py` — `OrderExecutorConfig` with `ExecutionStrategy.MARKET`, BUY/SELL, base amount 0.001 fills at the **first supplied row's close** (`fill_timestamp = df['timestamp'].iloc[0]`, `entry_price = close` at that row), records `filled_amount_quote = amount * close` and `cum_fees_quote = trade_cost * filled_amount_quote`, then reports `CloseType.POSITION_HOLD`. With the current-row forward slice, a completed-bar signal at `decision_ts` produces a same-prepared-row MARKET-close **simulation**. This is the actual pinned close-effective **backtest** primitive, not an exchange close auction, a next-open fill, a stop-order break, or an intrabar fill. The LIMIT_MAKER/LIMIT RESTING/EXPIRED paths in the same file exist but are **never used** by this MARKET-only derivative. The researcher-defined controller would be a strategy-specific `DirectionalTradingController` emitting these actions; **it is not implemented or deployed**.
- **Terminal settlement (pinned closing-tick evidence, not a researcher promise):** after the row loop, `simulate_execution` runs `update_executors_info(last_index)` ("Give the engine one last look so those executors are terminated, booked into the running totals and … routed to the position-hold ledger, exactly as on any other tick"), then `_update_positions_from_stopped_executors()`, and returns `collect_executors_ledger(last_index)` — a complete run ledger built from both live and stopped executors. Consequently a signal evaluated on the **last** prepared row still executes at that row's close inside the loop and is terminated, booked, and reported by the closing tick; no cross-bar PENDING remains by construction (MARKET fills synchronously; no next-tick confirmation is required for state). An open LONG at the end remains a POSITION_HOLD marked at the last close — never auto-liquidated, never represented as realized PnL.
- **Fill/position-state setting:** instantiate `BacktestingEngineBase(terminated_executors_window=None)` so the controller's terminated-executor view is the whole ledger; the pinned default is only 3600 seconds (`DEFAULT_TERMINATED_EXECUTORS_WINDOW = 60*60`) and could hide a previous daily fill from `controller.executors_info` (the `None`-means-whole-ledger branch is pinned in `_reset_simulation_state`). Maintain explicit controller-side fill-confirmed quantity, pending-action suppression, and position cost/fee ledger; never equate `POSITION_HOLD` with a fresh unfilled order or assume an order was filled when submitted. This model convention does **not** establish causal executable live close fills. A future execution path that cannot honor the same effective-timestamp model must be revalidated as a distinct derivative.
- **Cost and capital:** research `initial_quote_cash=10000 USDT`, fixed `amount=0.001 BTC`, `trade_cost=0.001` of quote notional **per simulated BUY and SELL fill**, modeled slippage `0` as an expressly artificial simulation assumption. A BUY at price `p` reduces exact Decimal research cash by `0.001*p*(1+0.001)`; a SELL increases it by `0.001*p*(1-0.001)`. Do not describe this 0.1% as a measured exchange fee or the zero modeled slippage as realized slippage. Require sufficient simulated cash including modeled BUY fee; if not, no BUY. SELL only confirmed available base quantity. Unleveraged spot has no perpetual-futures funding contract; **no claim of zero realized futures funding** is made. Actual maker/taker schedules, lot/tick rounding, min notional, spread, latency and partial fills are not verified and are not authorized for Paper/Testnet/Live. Reconcile engine net base with exact research inventory and engine gross PnL/fees with the Decimal ledger on every fill, allowing at most **1e-10 BTC** base residual and **1e-6 USDT** quote rounding discrepancy (the simulator's float quote math versus the research Decimal ledger); larger deviations, negative modeled cash, or an unaccounted fill **abort**, not silently reclassify exposure. No fabricated prices or fills. Float rounding residuals must not create a second position.
- **Capability boundary:** this path models completed 1d OHLCV, one spot pair, historical MARKET-close fills and quote-fee accounting only. It does **not** verify exchange tick/lot/min-notional rules, real order acceptance, market liquidity, actual commission tiers, slippage, partial fills, or live signal-triggered execution. Simulator modeled price impact/slippage is absent; that is **not** evidence that actual slippage is zero. ## Limitations

- An entry triple can print repeatedly through a crash; without SL/TP/time stop this strategy can hold through substantial adverse moves, and the source family's executor-layer risk management was deliberately removed as outside the pinned primitive.
- Any stop/target/trailing/cooldown construction and live order semantics are not reproduced; entries/exits happen at the simulated signal-bar close, which changes events versus any live-managed trade.
- Exact pandas_ta/TA-Lib bit-equality, source-native fill clock, original sizing/cost settings and profitability are unknown. The simulated completed-close fill is a **backtest convention**, not a live execution guarantee.
- Signals are sparse (2 entries and 0 exits per ~1000 daily bars in the availability check; the exit leg is proven reachable only in fuzz). The 100-bar band gate lags fast reversals. Near-boundary float comparisons are exact and deterministic but platform-float-dependent at the dust level.
- The modeled 0.1%-per-side research cost, zero modeled slippage, fixed base size and absence of futures funding do not prove actual Binance trade economics. A long position can remain exposed during data outages. No Qlib parity, survivor status or trading approval follows from HB_READY.
