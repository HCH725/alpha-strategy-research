## Economic mechanism

### Source-reported

Three nested Bollinger envelopes (20/120/240 closes, ±2.0 population-σ) share one close series; a long fires only when a single completed close prints strictly below all three lower rails at once, and the position closes only when a later close prints strictly above all three upper rails. Between the two triple extremes the book holds without acting. No protective stop, target, trailing, or time exit exists anywhere in the program.

### Research interpretation

This derivative is long only inside multi-scale washout episodes: the 20-bar rail demands an immediate stretch, while the 120- and 240-bar rails demand that the stretch also be extreme against the intermediate and long-horizon dispersions — a triple-scale confirmation, not a single-band touch, not a cross edge, and not a midline reclaim. The exit is the mirror image (triple overheat), so a round trip spans the full triple channel by construction and positions rest exposed through the entire hold zone. Because the sole risk mechanism is the mirror signal, a market that drifts sideways mid-channel, or keeps sliding without ever printing a triple-upper bar, keeps the book exposed; the longest observed hold is 1,333 hourly bars (~55 days, duration only, not a loss bound).

## Signal

1. **Input and availability:** one chronological, contiguous sequence of actual Binance BTCUSDT spot UTC **1h** candles indexed t=0,1,... . Native opening time T[t] must be UTC hour-aligned and T[t+1]-T[t]=3600 seconds. Require finite positive O,H,L,C, H>=max(O,C), L<=min(O,C), finite nonnegative V. Reject duplicates, gaps, out-of-order bars, missing/zero/nonfinite observed prices or incomplete candles; never interpolate or forward-fill. Completed bar t is available at E[t]=T[t]+3600, not T[t]. Float64 chronological arithmetic, no intermediate rounding. Only the **close** series is consumed by the decision.
2. **Constants (researcher-pinned to payload defaults):** `length1=20`, `mult1=2.0`, `length2=120`, `mult2=2.0`, `length3=240`, `mult3=2.0` pinned (the payload's six `input()` defaults; the source menu is fenced, not adopted).
3. **Pinned indicator semantics (Pine v5 built-ins, warmup-fenced):** for each (L, mult) in {(20,2.0),(120,2.0),(240,2.0)}: `basis[t]=mean(C[t-L+1..t])`, `sd[t]=sqrt(mean((C-basis[t])^2))` over the same L closes (population form, Pine `ta.stdev` default `biased=true`), `upper[t]=basis[t]+mult*sd[t]`, `lower[t]=basis[t]-mult*sd[t]`. Pine yields `na` until L bars exist; this derivative fences bars 0..239 as seed-only and never decides before t=240, so only full-window values are ever consumed (first decidable bar t=240; disclosed researcher pin, same fencing shape as the admitted FMZ-521670 record). No division by `sd` exists anywhere, so a zero-variance window simply collapses its rails onto `basis==close` and both predicates below evaluate false — a defined hold, never NaN, never an invented value.
4. **Entry latch (verbatim, strict inequalities):** at t>=240 while FLAT with no pending action and t strictly later than the last SELL-fill bar: `C[t]<lower1[t] AND C[t]<lower2[t] AND C[t]<lower3[t]`. Equality on any leg blocks entry. Queue exactly one BUY. Dispatch on row t+1 only, independently of that row's signal, and model a full 0.001 BTC MARKET fill at its **actual observed completed close** C[t+1], effective E[t+1]. No t+1 price or indicator is used to decide at t. If row t+1 does not exist, leave unfilled. Require cash >=Decimal("0.001")*Decimal(str(C[t+1]))*(1+Decimal("0.001")); insufficient funds or ambiguous/missing fill aborts rather than assuming borrowing.
5. **Exit latch (verbatim, strict inequalities):** when confirmed LONG and no pending action, on the first completed bar u after the BUY-fill bar with `C[u]>upper1[u] AND C[u]>upper2[u] AND C[u]>upper3[u]`, queue a full 0.001 BTC SELL for row u+1 at actual observed C[u+1], effective E[u+1]. No stop/target/trailing/time order exists in the payload; all are expressly disabled, not hidden defaults. The mirror triple signal itself is the sole risk mechanism.
6. **State and priority:** exactly FLAT, BUY_PENDING, LONG, SELL_PENDING; one position and one pending action maximum. Entry and exit predicates are **mutually exclusive by construction** (they require `C<lower_i` and `C>upper_i` on the same scalar close with `lower_i<=upper_i` always since `sd>=0`; the degenerate zero-variance case is hold by rule 3) — verified zero co-fires over the full 59,787-bar replay, so no tie-break is needed. Single unit with no adds (source default `pyramiding=0` gives a single unit; repeated entry legs while LONG and close legs while FLAT are Pine no-ops, mirrored here as explicit state-gate no-ops — same convergence shape as the admitted FMZ-521670 record). On a pending/fill bar ignore new order signals but keep bands updating. On confirmed SELL, return to FLAT using the independent confirmed-fill inventory ledger and record last SELL-fill bar. Re-entry requires FLAT plus a future triple-breach latch; no cooldown exists in the payload (explicitly none, not invented). If a fill fails, is partial, duplicated, stale or cannot be reconciled, abort the segment. At sample end, an unfilled pending signal remains unfilled; open inventory is marked only to the last actual completed close and not fictitiously liquidated.
7. **Disabled:** Pine next-bar default order semantics (adapted to the declared t+1 completed-close MARKET fill), default quantity semantics (no source sizing is adopted), the BNB_USDT demo venue/period/window, the source menu beyond pinned defaults, short/borrow leg, stops/targets/trailing/time exits, pyramiding, DCA/safety orders, simultaneous markets/shared capital, partial exits, dynamic sizing/compounding, limit/maker fills, intrabar triggers, discretionary overrides, hidden executor risk defaults and forced end-of-sample close.
