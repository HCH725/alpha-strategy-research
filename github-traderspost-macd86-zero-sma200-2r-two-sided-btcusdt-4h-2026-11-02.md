## Economic mechanism

### Source-reported
Sub-zero momentum-rotation capture with a moving-average guillotine and a fixed 2R box, two-sided: only MACD-signal crosses printed wholly below (longs) or above (shorts) the zero line may fire; a live SMA200 close-cross guillotines any open leg; each flat-originated entry latches a 2×-risk target from the entry bar's SMA distance. The `"86% Win Rate"` title ships no table, figure, window, or settings trace anywhere in the file — adopted as no performance claim of any kind.

### Research interpretation
Unlike the pool naked-MACD sibling (`macd-signal-cross-reversal-btcusdt-1d` — unfettered cross-to-cross reversal, no gate, no stop, no target, always-in-market), this machine trades only zero-sided crosses, rests flat under the SMA guillotine and the 2R box, and reverses only on opposite gated crosses. Unlike the pool FMZ MACD-zero-cross sibling (`fmz-macd-zero-cross-sma50-atr-bracket` — MACD-zero-cross trigger, SMA50 trend filter, ATR stop/target box), the trigger here is the MACD-*signal* cross (not the zero cross), the gate is the both-lines zero side (not a price-vs-SMA trend filter), the stop is a live SMA200 close-cross (not an ATR multiple), and the target is latched 2R off SMA distance (not an ATR box). Five-axis distinction holds on signal (signal-cross × zero-side vs naked cross vs zero-cross), gates (both-lines-side vs none vs SMA50), exits (live-SMA guillotine + latched 2R vs pure reversal vs ATR box), state (flat-capable with stale-TP reversals vs always-in-market), and source identity (TradersPost GH pin `ebc382f` vs all others).

## Signal — Gate 1, fully pinned derivative

Index completed UTC 4-hour bars `t = 0, 1, ...` with real Binance BTC-USDT futures `C[t]` for signals/stops, `H[t]`/`L[t]` for TP-touch legs, `O[t]` for fills. A bar with missing/nonfinite/non-positive OHLC, or `H < max(O,C)` / `L < min(O,C)`, is invalid for signals. All constants are source-default literals.

- **Oscillator (causal, per-bar):** `E12[t]=EMA12(C)`, `E26[t]=EMA26(C)`, `M[t]=E12-E26`, `S[t]=EMA9(M)`; `up[t]` = `M[t-1]<=S[t-1] and M[t]>S[t]`, `dn[t]` = `M[t-1]>=S[t-1] and M[t]<S[t]` (strict `ta.crossover/under` semantics); `below[t]` = `M[t]<0 and S[t]<0`, `above[t]` = `M[t]>0 and S[t]>0` (strict — zero-touch fires neither).
- **Predicates (actionable only t ≥ 300, at bar `t`'s close):** `longSig[t] = up[t] and below[t]`, `shortSig[t] = dn[t] and above[t]`. Pre-warmup bars flat by record rule; every na/degenerate bar is an explicit neither-fire hold.
- **Latched target (fixed at flat signal bar `s`, never moved):** long `TP=C[s]+2.0*(C[s]-SMA200[s])`; short mirrored `TP=C[s]-2.0*(SMA200[s]-C[s])`. Latched only if flat at `s`; reversal-signal bars do not relatch (source-faithful). `longSL/shortSL` floats are computed but unread (inert, disclosed).
- **Resolution (explicit researcher pin):** entries fill next-bar-open MARKET; each held bar `v` (fill bar onward) evaluates at its close in code order — opposite entry first (reversal dominates, no relatch), else live stop-cross (`C` under/over SMA200), else latched TP-touch (`H` over TP / `L` under TP); stop+TP co-fire is one flat close. Opposite confluence while flat is impossible by construction (`up` and `dn` need opposite strict inequalities — verified property).
