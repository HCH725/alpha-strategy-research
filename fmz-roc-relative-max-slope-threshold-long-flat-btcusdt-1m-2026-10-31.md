## Economic mechanism

### Source-reported

Bottoming-slope long with topping-slope exit, long-only. The machine normalizes the 9-bar rate-of-change against its own 200-bar maximum (relative momentum level), smooths that level with a 10-bar SMA, and trades the smoothed curve's slope: a 3-bar rise while the level sits below −80% is read as decline-deceleration (go long); a 3-bar fall while the level sits above +80% is read as ascent-deceleration (close the long). The prose explicitly frames the three devices as invalid-signal filters (max-relative comparison, SMA smoothing, ±80 thresholds). No short leg, no stop-loss, no target, no trailing, no partial, no time exit, and no performance figure of any kind ships anywhere (a full-token census for Sharpe/胜率/win-rate/profit-factor/drawdown/annualized returns zero performance tokens — no source-reported performance is adopted or restated; the prose lists missing stop-loss and trend-filter as future work that ships nowhere).

Deceleration-gated dip-long with a single market close, event-driven, long-only, flat except after an admitted slope-plus-threshold print. Unlike clock-only records (`monday-drift` — UTC day-of-week + hour predicates with time exits), there is no clock predicate anywhere here: chop prints nothing without a 3-bar slope plus an extreme relative level. - **Smoothed level `S[t]` (t ≥ 217):** `S[t] = SMA(P[t−9..t])` (exact 10-value mean; `na` until 10 valid levels exist).
- **Slope predicates (Pine v5 `ta.rising`/`ta.falling` documented semantics, pinned with explicit tie rule):** `rise[t] = S[t] > max(S[t−1], S[t−2], S[t−3])` (strictly greater than EACH of the prior 3 — ties fail, `na` in window fails); `fall[t] = S[t] < min(S[t−1], S[t−2], S[t−3])` (strictly less than EACH of the prior 3 — ties fail, `na` in window fails). - **Full predicates (actionable only t ≥ 280):** long entry `LE[t] = rise[t] AND S[t] < −80`; long close `LX[t] = fall[t] AND S[t] > +80` (no trend gate on either leg — file-literal: the `if`s test only slope-plus-threshold).
