### Research interpretation

Strength-slope trend-hold with regime discipline, event-persistent, two-sided. Unlike DI-cross records (`adx-di-cross-strength-gated` — `ta.crossover` event entries with opposite-cross-or-0.7-decay exits, 1d; `freqtrade AdxSmas` open PR — SMA3/6 cross gated by ADX, long-only, 1h), this machine never computes a cross — entries are level conjunctions that stay true across consecutive bars (first such bar trades while flat; the position then blocks the rest), and the exit is pure slope-down with no opposite-signal leg. - **Wilder DMI triple (Pine v4 `dmi(dilen,adxlen)` seeding, disclosed):** `up[t]=H[t]-H[t-1]`; `dn[t]=L[t-1]-L[t]`; `plusDM[t]=(up>dn AND up>0)?up:0`; `minusDM[t]=(dn>up AND dn>0)?dn:0`; `TR[t]=max(H-L,|H-C[t-1]|,|L-C[t-1]|)`; `diplus[t]=100*RMA(plusDM,14)[t]/RMA(TR,14)[t]`; `diminus[t]=100*RMA(minusDM,14)[t]/RMA(TR,14)[t]`; `adx[t]=100*RMA(|diplus-diminus|/((diplus+diminus)==0?1:(diplus+diminus)),14)[t]`, where `RMA(x,n)` uses `alpha=1/n` seeded by the SMA of the first `n` values (`na` until the seed completes — any `na` leg fails its predicate).
