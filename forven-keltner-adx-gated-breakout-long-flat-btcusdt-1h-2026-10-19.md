### Research interpretation

Volatility-normalized expansion timing with a strength veto, long-only, state-holding. The envelope breathes with realized range (2.0 ewm-ATRs around the 20-bar EMA), so identical price moves trigger entries in calm regimes and are ignored as noise in wild ones; the ADX ≥ 20 conjunction vetoes breakouts printed in directionless markets where the envelope itself is flat and crossable by drift. The midline exit is the entire risk control — a failed breakout that falls back through the EMA is closed with no stop order and no target. Unlike Donchian records (raw highest-high/lowest-low extremes with no volatility normalization and no gate), the trigger level is range-scaled and strength-confirmed; unlike the pool's FMZ Keltner sibling (momentum-sign gate, symmetric two-sided reversal), this machine exits to flat and sits out downtrends entirely — flat is a first-class state and the short side is explicitly disabled under the pinned position, never merely untriggered. The failure mode is plain: a slow grind that rides above the midline but never re-crosses the upper band is never entered; chop that pierces the upper band on ADX flickers around 20 whipsaws the book with no cooldown; a vertical advance is held with no profit-taking leg; a grinding decline is watched from flat with no short participation by construction.
