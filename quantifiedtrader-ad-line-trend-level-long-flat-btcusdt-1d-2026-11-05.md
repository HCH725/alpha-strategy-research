## Economic mechanism

### Source-reported

Cumulative money-flow accumulation: the A/D line cumulates range-position-weighted volume, distilling whether closes cluster near highs (accumulation) or lows (distribution). When the line rises above its own 20-bar average it signals institutional accumulation and aligns long; when it drops below, accumulation has failed and the book stands flat. The design bets that the A/D line's position against its own trailing average on the daily frame times durable money-flow regimes better than raw price crosses, and that sidestepping below-average regimes harvests more drift than it surrenders to cumsum lag at regime births.

### Research interpretation

Level-held money-flow regime system, long side only — the only pool machine that trades the level of a Chaikin A/D line against its own SMA20. Unlike the pool Forven ADL record (ADL-vs-EMA20 cross *edges* plus a close-momentum gate — entries fire only on the crossing bar), this machine holds a persistent *level*: any post-warmup bar with the line above its average enters from flat, including the first evaluated bar when pre-warmup accumulation already sits high (live probe: entry decision t=60, fill t=61 — disclosed, not patched). Unlike the pool OBV/CMF oscillators, the signal is a raw cumulative level, not a bounded oscillator — there is no overbought rail and no normalization, so a slow-grind accumulator holds LONG indefinitely while a steady distributor never enters (planted sustained-accumulation tape: 1/0 ending LONG; sustained-distribution tape: 0/0 FLAT — disclosed hold-by-construction, not patched). Unlike stop/trailing-stop machines, the exit carries no memory and no ratchet: `AD[t] < MA[t]` is recomputed each bar from the live window. Unlike two-sided reversal systems there is no short leg and no always-in-market grind — below-average regimes are flat, so distribution costs only opportunity, never adverse exposure. The cumsum seed is algebraically event-neutral (an additive constant cancels in every `AD[t] − MA[t]` comparison — proven numerically this cycle: a +1,234,567.89 offset prints the identical event set), so unlike seeded smoothers there is no seed-convention residual beyond the disclosed flat-bar guard. The equity-index universe and the unmodeled-costs stance are source facts, not this record's venue or economics. No performance figure is carried anywhere.
