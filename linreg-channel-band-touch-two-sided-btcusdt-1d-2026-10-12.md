## Economic mechanism

### Source-reported

Statistical trend-following with mean-reversion entries inside a regression channel (strategy README: `best-fit line through recent price action with standard deviation bands`; `Entries occur on mean-reversion touches within the regression channel while following the overall trend slope`): in an uptrend the long leg buys the bounce off the lower band; in a downtrend the short leg sells the rejection off the upper band; either leg exits when price crosses back through the regression midline (the README's `fair value — price gravitates toward it`). There is deliberately no stop, no target, no trailing exit, and no flat state beyond pre-signal bars — the order block ends with the two `strategy.close` guards and no risk leg ships anywhere, by design gap rather than by hidden leg.

### Research interpretation

Slope-gated channel-touch trend-continuation, two-sided with midline exits — the first linear-regression-channel record in the pool. Unlike the pool's Bollinger/Keltner/Donchian breakout records, entries here fade the extreme *toward* the trend rather than chase the break *away* from it: the touch must print while the regression slope already points the trade's way and price already sits on the trade's side of the 50-bar EMA, so the system buys dips inside uptrends and sells rallies inside downtrends instead of entering breakouts. Unlike the SQZMOM record, the regression line is the trade's spine (entries at its ±2σ rails, exits at the line itself) rather than a detrended-momentum histogram leg inside a squeeze regime. Note the two pinned asymmetries, stated plainly: first, a band touch without the slope-plus-EMA trend alignment fires nothing — counter-trend touches are ignored by construction, so ranging markets with a flat slope produce long flat stretches; second, the exit needs price to travel all the way back through the midline — a touch that reverses only partway and re-extends never banks, and a leg whose price first crosses the midline the wrong way out (straight through to the far rail) is still held until a midline cross prints back. The bet is on channel-respecting trends at frozen `100/2.0/50` geometry, not on any breakout extreme, volume print, calendar effect, or second timeframe.

The only `high`/`low` reads anywhere are the single-bar touch confirmations below (census-verified).
- Trend gates: `uptrend = close > emaFilter and linReg > linReg[5]`; `downtrend = close < emaFilter and linReg < linReg[5]` — strict inequalities on the decision bar's own prints.
- Entry long: `strategy.entry("Long", strategy.long)` under `if uptrend and ta.crossover(close, lowerBand) and low <= lowerBand` — fires on completed bars with same-bar-close execution under the derived declaration. The touch needs both the upward close-cross of the lower rail and the bar's own low at-or-through the rail.
- Entry short: `strategy.entry("Short", strategy.short)` under `if downtrend and ta.crossunder(close, upperBand) and high >= upperBand` — mirrored.
- Exits (coded, the sole position-closing path): `strategy.close("Long")` under `if ta.crossunder(close, linReg)`; `strategy.close("Short")` under `if ta.crossover(close, linReg)`. No stop, no target, no trailing exit leg, no time exit — pinned as written, explicitly none, not invented.
