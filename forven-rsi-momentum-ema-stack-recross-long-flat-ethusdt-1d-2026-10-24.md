## Economic mechanism

### Source-reported

The source claims only what its code and `describe()` print: buy the bar whose RSI(14) bounces back up through 40 while the close already sits above both its 50- and 200-bar exponential averages with ADX(14) clearing a zero floor; sell when RSI(14) drops back below 60. No stop, no target, no trailing, no time exit, no sizing, no cost basis ships anywhere — this record adopts no source-reported performance (see Evidence).

### Research interpretation

Dip-reclaim timer inside a structurally intact uptrend, long-only, edge-triggered entry with a one-leg overheating exit. The dual-EMA stack is a slow-trend leash, not a timing device: both averages must already sit beneath the close, so entries print only on pullbacks that never broke trend position — a dip that closes under either average is left alone, however deeply oversold its RSI. The ADX floor (`adx_min = 0`) is a trivially satisfied gate (ADX is non-negative by construction; NaN only before seeding) — disclosed as a pass-through, never as a regime filter. Unlike the pool's washout sibling (S00139: SMA20 gate, RSI must print under 25, exit on overbought-cross-or-SMA-loss), this system buys shallower reclaim turns (40, not 25) and exits purely on momentum cooling (cross-down through 60) with no trend-loss leg — a melt-up that never prints a sub-60 prior bar is ridden until RSI finally cools. Unlike pullback-reclaim records (RSI reclaims a 45 midline inside a fast stack), both legs here are edges, never levels: parked RSI holds no state. The failure mode is plain: chop that oscillates RSI across 40/60 while the EMAs stay beneath prints repeated enter-then-exit round trips with no cooldown; a breakdown that gaps from an entry straight through trend support exits only when RSI cools below 60, with no stop to front-run it.
