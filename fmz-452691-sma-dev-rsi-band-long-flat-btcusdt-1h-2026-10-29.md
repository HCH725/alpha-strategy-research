## Economic mechanism

### Source-reported

The page prose presents a volatility-adaptive band system: an SMA centre with standard-deviation bands that widen in volatility and narrow in calm, plus RSI as an auxiliary filter. A long fires when price crosses back above the lower (−1.5σ) band while RSI reads oversold (<30) — framed as a washed-out dip reclaiming its volatility envelope. A long closes when price crosses back below the narrower exit-lower (−0.5σ) band or RSI reaches overbought (>70). No stop-loss, take-profit, trailing, or time exit ships anywhere. No source performance metric is adopted.

### Research interpretation

This is a **washed-out-reclaim entry with a two-trigger exit**: the thesis is that a close pushing back above its −1.5σ envelope while momentum is still oversold marks capitulation exhaustion, and holding until the envelope is lost again (−0.5σ crossunder) or momentum overheats (RSI>70) harvests the snap-back while cutting both slow fades and parabolic overextensions. The asymmetric bands (wide 1.5σ gate in, narrow 0.5σ gate out) make exits structurally quicker than entries — winners must survive a tighter leash than the one that admitted them, so expectancy hinges on snap-backs travelling from −1.5σ to above −0.5σ (or to RSI 70) before fees. The failure mode is plain: in a grinding downtrend the −1.5σ band slides with price and every dead-cat reclaim prints a fee-paying entry-to-close cycle; in a flat range σ compresses, both bands hug the SMA, and noise crosses print whipsaw round trips. No profitability is asserted.

## Signal

**Gate 1 — exact researcher-pinned arithmetic.** Index completed UTC 1-hour bars by `t=0,1,...`. Use real Binance BTC-USDT spot `O[t],H[t],L[t],C[t]`; volume is not read. A bar with missing/nonfinite/non-positive close, or with `H<max(O,C)` / `L>min(O,C)`, is invalid; never interpolate.

1. Pinned inputs (all fixed at coded defaults, no sweep): `LEN=20`, `TH_ENTRY=1.5`, `TH_EXIT=0.5`, `RSILEN=14`, `RSI_OB=70`, `RSI_OS=30`.
2. `SMA[t]` is undefined (`na`) for `t < 19`; `SMA[t]=mean(C[t-19..t])` for `t >= 19`. `DEV[t]` is undefined for `t < 19`; for `t >= 19` it is the **population** standard deviation of `C[t-19..t]` (divide by 20, Pine `ta.stdev` convention, pinned as this derivative's own rule — Pine float bit-identity not claimed). `upper[t]=SMA[t]+1.5*DEV[t]`, `lower[t]=SMA[t]-1.5*DEV[t]`, `exitUpper[t]=SMA[t]+0.5*DEV[t]`, `exitLower[t]=SMA[t]-0.5*DEV[t]`; all `na` while `SMA/DEV` are `na`. A zero-σ bar collapses all four bands onto `SMA[t]`; comparisons remain defined, cross predicates below still apply.
3. `RSI[t]` is the Wilder-RMA RSI(14) on closes (Pine `ta.rsi` convention: average gain/loss via RMA with alpha 1/14, seeded on the first 14 closes, pinned as this derivative's own rule). `RSI[t]` is `na` before the seed clears; any `na` comparison is false, so no predicate can fire pre-seed — coded behavior, disclosed.
4. On simulator bar `t` **only after its close**, evaluate with the already-completed **previous** bar `u = t-1`. `longEdge[u] = crossover(C,lower)[u] and (RSI[u] < 30)`, where `crossover(C,lower)[u] = (C[u-1] <= lower[u-1]) and (C[u] > lower[u])`, requiring `C[u-1],C[u],lower[u-1],lower[u],RSI[u]` all defined; `exitEdge[u] = crossunder(C,exitLower)[u] or (RSI[u] > 70)`, where `crossunder(C,exitLower)[u] = (C[u-1] >= exitLower[u-1]) and (C[u] < exitLower[u])`, requiring the crossunder side's four prices defined when that leg fires (the RSI leg requires only `RSI[u]` defined). This mirrors confirmed-bar semantics: no current-bar `t` indicator may influence the action; `C[t]` is solely modeled fill-price/cash-check data, never a signal input.
5. Entry and exit predicates are **state-gated and need no priority rule**: if confirmed FLAT and not pending, only `longEdge[u]` is consulted (queues one BUY of 0.001 BTC at simulated `C[t]` subject to warmup/affordability/guards below); the exit predicate is not consulted while FLAT. If confirmed LONG and not pending, only `exitEdge[u]` is consulted (queues one SELL of the confirmed 0.001 BTC at simulated `C[t]`); the entry edge is not consulted while LONG. Never re-enter on the same bar a SELL is queued. (For the record: with `lower < exitLower` whenever σ>0, a single bar cannot simultaneously cross up through `lower` and cross down through `exitLower`, and RSI cannot be both <30 and >70 — the two edges are additionally mutually exclusive on every non-degenerate bar; the state gate alone already removes any ordering ambiguity, including the degenerate σ=0 collapse.)
6. The short leg is **explicitly disabled**: `shortCondition` (`crossunder(C,upper) and RSI>70`) never queues an entry and `exitShortCondition` is never consulted; no short inventory, no short close, no reversal. There is no stop-loss, no take-profit, no trailing order, no time exit, no scale-out, no cooldown, no pyramiding, no opposite-signal reversal, and no end-of-sample forced liquidation. An unclosed final position remains open, marked at the last observed valid price only. All six `plot(` visuals and both `hline(` levels have no decision function and are pinned as display-only; `margin_long/margin_short=100` are Pine account-margin declarations with no signal function.
