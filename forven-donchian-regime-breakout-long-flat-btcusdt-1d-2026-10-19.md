### Research interpretation

Durable-trend breakout timing with a health veto, long-only, state-holding. The 55-bar high is a slow Turtle-style trigger that only fires on genuine range expansion; the EMA200-plus-ADX25 conjunction vetoes breakouts printed in directionless or counter-trend markets where the channel itself is flat and crossable by drift. The dual exit (20-bar low breakdown OR close back under the EMA200) is the entire risk control — a failed breakout that sags through either rail is closed with no stop order and no target. Unlike the pool's naked Donchian sibling (20/10 extremes with no gate), the trigger is slower and strength-confirmed, and flat is a first-class state that includes every veto-failed breakout bar; unlike Keltner/Bollinger channel cousins (volatility-scaled envelopes), the trigger levels are raw prior extremes with no normalization; unlike the pool's Donchian fade (opposite-side bet on the same geometry), this machine buys the break and never fades it. The failure mode is plain: a V-turn that recovers without first printing a veto-passing 55-bar piercing is entered strictly after the recovery is underway; chop that pierces the 55-bar high on ADX flickers around 25 whipsaws the book with no cooldown; a vertical advance is held with no profit-taking leg; a grinding decline is watched from flat with no short participation by construction.

## Signal

All identifiers below are the program's own; `donchian_bands` (donchian.py lines 21–27) is the single band construction both twins share:

```text
inputs (defaults, frozen): period = 55, exit_period = 20, ema_period = 200, adx_period = 14, adx_min = 25, _asset = "BTC"
bands:   upper_t = max(high_{t-55..t-1})      // rolling(55).max().shift(1), causal prior-bar
         exit_lower_t = min(low_{t-20..t-1})  // rolling(20).min().shift(1), causal prior-bar
gate:    ema_t = close.ewm(span=200, adjust=False).mean()

         trend_ok_t = (close_t > ema_t) AND (adx_t >= 25)
ready:   positional index >= 202  (per-bar guard: len(df) < max(55,20,200,14)+2 → neutral)
entry_t  = (close_{t-1} <= upper_t) AND (close_t > upper_t) AND trend_ok_t AND ready
exit_t   = ((close_{t-1} >= exit_lower_t) AND (close_t < exit_lower_t)) OR (close_t < ema_t)   // AND ready
```
