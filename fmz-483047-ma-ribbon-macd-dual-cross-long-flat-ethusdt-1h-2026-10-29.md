## Economic mechanism

### Source-reported

The page prose presents an MA-ribbon plus MACD-signal trend system: a short/long MA ribbon names the trend (default EMA 9/21), the MACD 12/26/9 crossover confirms momentum, both must fire together for a long entry, either one flipping shuts the position, and an intraday-reset veto suppresses fresh signals on each new day's first bar to avoid overnight-style holding. No source performance metric is adopted (the page carries prose discussion only, no claimed backtest figure adopted here).

### Research interpretation

This is a **coincidence machine with a same-bar AND gate and a daily veto**: the traded thesis is that an EMA9/21 trend turn and a MACD momentum turn landing on the *same* hourly bar marks an accepted regime shift, while either indicator flipping back cuts the trade mechanically. With the AND gate, entries are rare (two independent crossovers must coincide — most single-crossover bars print nothing), so expectancy hinges on coincident turns extending rather than immediately reversing; every whipsaw prints a fee-paying round trip. The day-boundary veto deterministically skips whatever signal the first bar of each UTC day would otherwise carry, so backtest-day edges always start flat-or-held, never freshly entered. A gap across 1h closes jumps clean past every line with no slippage accounting. The cash-admission rule adds a second, deterministic skip path: late-sample BUYs that the 10,000 USDT balance cannot cover are cancelled rather than filled. No profitability is asserted.

## Signal

**Gate 1 — exact researcher-pinned arithmetic.** Index completed UTC 1-hour bars by `t=0,1,...`. Use real Binance ETH-USDT spot `O[t],H[t],L[t],C[t]`; volume is not read. A bar with missing/nonfinite/non-positive close, or with `H<max(O,C)` / `L>min(O,C)`, is invalid; never interpolate.

1. Pinned inputs (all source defaults, fixed): `MA1LEN=9`, `MA2LEN=21` (both EMA of close), `FAST=12`, `SLOW=26`, `SIGLEN=9` (MACD oscillator and signal line both EMA), `Q=0.1` ETH, `FEE=0.001`, `CASH0=10,000` USDT.
2. `A[t]`/`B[t]`: first-bar-seeded EMAs of close with alpha `2/10` / `2/22` (`A[0]=B[0]=C[0]`; researcher-pinned first-bar seed; Pine `ta.ema` seed bit-identity not claimed).
3. `F[t]`/`S[t]`: first-bar-seeded EMAs of close with alpha `2/13` / `2/27`. `M[t]=F[t]-S[t]`. `G[t]`: first-bar-seeded EMA of the `M` series (`G[0]=M[0]`, alpha `2/10`).
4. Cross helpers on completed bar `u` (all values required defined, else the predicate is false): `crossUp(X,Y,u)=(X[u-1]<=Y[u-1] AND X[u]>Y[u])`; `crossDn(X,Y,u)=(X[u-1]>=Y[u-1] AND X[u]<Y[u])`.
5. Day veto: `VETO[u]` is true iff completed bar `u` is the first bar of a new UTC calendar day (derivative expression of the source `newDay := bool(ta.change(time("D")))` rule; well-defined for every `u>=1`, hence for every post-warmup bar).
6. On completed bar `u` (post-warmup, `NOT VETO[u]`): `ENTRY[u]=(crossUp(A,B,u) AND crossUp(M,G,u))`; `EXIT[u]=(crossDn(A,B,u) OR crossDn(M,G,u))`. On a veto bar both predicates are false. ENTRY and EXIT are mutually exclusive on every bar with no invented priority: ENTRY needs `crossUp(M,G,u)` while EXIT's MACD leg needs `crossDn(M,G,u)` (mutually exclusive), and ENTRY needs `crossUp(A,B,u)` while EXIT's MA leg needs `crossDn(A,B,u)` (mutually exclusive) — no bar can fire both.
7. **Position/pending/cash state machine (Gate 4, researcher-pinned).** Confirmed position is `FLAT` or `LONG` (LONG always holds exactly `Q=0.1` ETH; no other size exists — no DCA, no safety orders, no pyramiding, no averaging; the downstream 6%/6%/6% campaign overlay is explicitly **not** part of this derivative's position events). A single-slot pending is `NONE`, `BUY` or `SELL`, always bound to exactly one expected fill bar `f=u+1`. Quote cash starts at `CASH0=10,000` USDT and is never negative.
   - **Queue (valid completed bar `u`, post-warmup, pending `NONE`):** if confirmed FLAT and `ENTRY[u]`, set pending `BUY` for `f=u+1` (exit predicates are not consulted while FLAT — Pine's flat `strategy.close` is a no-op, preserved). If confirmed LONG and `EXIT[u]`, set pending `SELL` for `f=u+1` (entry predicates are not consulted while LONG — Pine's same-direction `strategy.entry` under default no-pyramiding is rejected, preserved as the single-position gate). No other queue path exists.
   - **Resolve at bar `f`:** while pending is set, no signal is evaluated on `f` (never evaluate on a fill bar; never reverse on one bar). If `f` is a valid bar: a pending `SELL` fills the full `Q` at `C[f]`; a pending `BUY` fills `Q` at `C[f]` **iff** the cash-admission test `cash >= Q*C[f]*(1+FEE)` holds at that known close — otherwise the pending `BUY` is **cancelled** (skipped: no position, no fee, cash unchanged, confirmed state stays FLAT). If `f` is missing/invalid/out-of-sequence: the pending order (BUY or SELL) is **cancelled, never retained or deferred** — no fill, no fee, no new signal on `f`; a confirmed LONG keeps its base holding; continuity warmup restarts (see Required data) and the machine resumes afterwards from the confirmed position with pending `NONE`.
   - **After resolution** (fill or cancel), pending returns to `NONE` and the position is confirmed (`FLAT` or `LONG`). A pending therefore never survives past its single expected bar and never blocks more than that bar.
   - No stop-loss, no take-profit, no trailing, no time exit, no scale-out, no cooldown, no pyramiding, no short leg, no borrowing, no partial fills, no end-of-sample forced liquidation. An unclosed final position remains open, marked at the last observed valid price only.

**Gate 2:** LONG/FLAT on Binance ETH-USDT **spot**, leverage 1, no short leg in source and none added. **Gate 3:** complete same-bar-AND entry, complete either-leg MARKET close exit with day veto, cash-admitted fills, and intentionally disabled further risk legs as specified above (verified 1 `strategy.entry` + 1 `strategy.close`, zero `strategy.exit`/`strategy.order`/`strategy.risk` in source).

## Required data
