## Economic mechanism

### Source-reported

Triple-gated trend capture with an envelope stop, long-only at defaults. The machine hangs two supertrend-style ATR trailing stops (a slow 5×ATR(5) trail and a fast 1×ATR(1) trail) beside a Tillson T3 line wrapped in a ±5% envelope, enters long only when the close stands above ALL THREE levels on the same bar, and closes the long only when the bar midpoint sinks 5% below the T3 line. The prose claims steady trend-following character with qualitative flexibility language and lists future work (extra filters, dynamic stops, Kelly sizing) that ships nowhere. No numeric performance figure of any kind ships anywhere (stanza window 2023-09-09→2023-09-16 is a seven-day 5m sample — cited as provenance, never as performance evidence).

### Research interpretation

Level-gated trend riding with a distant memoryless stop, event-persistent, long-flat. Unlike edge-triggered records, there is no cross anywhere: flat bars print entries whenever the triple conjunction holds (streak-holding level, not a one-bar event), and a still-true conjunction re-enters on the first flat bar after any close — re-entry needs no fresh cross, no cooldown (explicitly none). Unlike latched records (`t3-tillson-channel-breakout` — slope-and-position conjunction flips a direction latch with flip-only entries, always-in-market, 1d), this machine holds no latch: every bar re-evaluates three strict level comparisons, flat is a first-class resting state, and the close leg is a price-vs-envelope level, not an opposite flip. Unlike raw-extreme records (`donchian-20-10-breakout` — rolling highest/lowest breakout entries; 117 Donchian-family records), no rolling extreme exists anywhere here: the widest object is a 5% T3 envelope, and extremes never trigger anything. Unlike MA-cross records (dual-SMA/EMA crosses), no average ever crosses another average — the close only ever compares against trailing/envelope levels. Unlike oscillator records, no averaged-gain/loss or stochastic computation exists in the signal path. Five-axis distinction from the pool T3 cousin holds on mechanism (level conjunction + envelope close vs slope-position latch + reversal-only exits), signal construction (dual ATR trails + T3 + envelope vs T3 midline + T3-smoothed range channel), horizon (5m vs 1d), risk (envelope stop vs no-stop), and source identity (CryptoJoncis 427067 vs Trader_7ye 426786). Against the eight pool supertrend records (none of which computes a Tillson T3, most of which add volume/ROI-ladder/Keltner/EMA100 machinery), the T3 conjunction plus hl2-vs-envelope close is the unique construction.
