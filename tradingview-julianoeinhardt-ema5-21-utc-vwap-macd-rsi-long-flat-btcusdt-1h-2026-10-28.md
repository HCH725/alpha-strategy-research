## Economic mechanism

The original indicator combines a fast/slow EMA direction change, volume-weighted price context and MACD/RSI confirmation. This independently specified derivative enters **only on a fresh EMA bullish cross** if price is above that day's causal VWAP and MACD/RSI agree; it exits when either trend or MACD confirmation fails. Unlike rolling-VWAP mean-reversion, the UTC-session VWAP is a within-day participation filter, while the EMA cross supplies the entry edge. It can miss early trends, whipsaw at daily VWAP resets, and suffer unbounded adverse excursions without an intrabar stop. No performance or suitability claim.

## Signal

A single uninterrupted sequence of **actual Binance spot BTCUSDT 1h completed** candles, zero-based index `t`, UTC opening time `T[t]`. Float64 indicator arithmetic in chronological order, no fitted model, no optimization and no future information.

1. **Input validity:** require `T[t]` UTC hour-aligned, strictly increasing in exact 3600-second increments, no duplicates/gaps; finite positive O/H/L/C, `high>=max(open,close)`, `low<=min(open,close)`, `high>=low`, and finite nonnegative base volume. No fabricated/missing price, forward fill, incomplete candle, data-window cherry-pick or synthetic bar. A bar is available only at `E[t]=T[t]+3600 seconds`; exchange timestamps represent OPEN, not completion.
2. **EMA seeds:** for each `n in {5,12,21,26}`, `EMA_n[n-1]=mean(close[0..n-1])`; for later t, `EMA_n[t]=2/(n+1)*close[t]+(1-2/(n+1))*EMA_n[t-1]`; undefined before seed. `MACD[t]=EMA12[t]-EMA26[t]` first valid t=25; `SIG9[33]=mean(MACD[25..33])`, then `SIG9[t]=(2/10)*MACD[t]+(8/10)*SIG9[t-1]`. MACD positive-state confirmation is `MACD[t]>SIG9[t]` (strict); exit failure is `MACD[t]<=SIG9[t]`.
3. **Wilder RSI14:** `delta[t]=close[t]-close[t-1]` for t>=1; gains `max(delta,0)`, losses `max(-delta,0)`. Seed mean gain/loss over deltas 1..14 at t=14; thereafter `avg[t]=(13*avg[t-1]+new[t])/14`. `RSI=100-100/(1+avg_gain/avg_loss)`; both averages zero => RSI50, zero loss with positive gain => RSI100, zero gain with positive loss => RSI0. RSI entry confirmation is `RSI14[t]>40` (strict; equality fails).
4. **UTC-day VWAP:** `typ[t]=(high[t]+low[t]+close[t])/3`; `D[t]=UTC calendar date of T[t]`. Reset cumulative `V=0, PV=0` at every new UTC opening-date; for each completed bar of that date add `volume[t]` and `typ[t]*volume[t]`. `VWAP[t]=PV/V` if V>0, otherwise undefined. This is **not** a rolling 20/32-bar VWAP and not a future full-day VWAP. A decision requiring an undefined VWAP, including a full zero-volume UTC day, fails closed rather than assuming zero or carrying yesterday's VWAP. Daily reset and accumulation occur even during warmup, pending or LONG.
5. **Fresh EMA entry edge:** `CROSS_UP[t]=(EMA5[t-1]<=EMA21[t-1]) AND (EMA5[t]>EMA21[t])`; equality on current bar does not trigger. On completed decision bar `s>=240`, if FLAT, no pending action, `CROSS_UP[s]`, `close[s]>VWAP[s]`, `MACD[s]>SIG9[s]`, `RSI14[s]>40`, queue one BUY for next completed bar. This MACD **state**, not source's MACD same-bar cross, is an explicit researcher-designed modification.
6. **Exit:** on the first eligible completed decision bar `u` strictly after confirmed BUY-fill bar, while LONG and no pending action, queue one SELL if `EMA5[u]<=EMA21[u]` **OR** `MACD[u]<=SIG9[u]`. Both conditions simultaneously yield only one SELL. Source's crossing-only exit is deliberately replaced by state invalidation; VWAP and RSI do **not** force exits. No intrabar stop, take-profit, trailing stop, partial exit, time stop or forced sample-end liquidation.
7. **Warmup and state:** bars t=0..239 (240 fully completed bars) are FLAT/no orders; all indicators and daily VWAP still update. First decision t=240, never retrospectively act on warmup crosses. State machine `FLAT -> BUY_PENDING -> LONG -> SELL_PENDING -> FLAT`, with at most one pending action and one position. A pending/fill bar cannot produce another action, though features update; an exit can first be signaled on the following completed bar. After SELL fill, require a later **new** EMA5/21 bullish crossover, not a persistent bullish level. No cooldown beyond pending/fill-bar exclusion, no pyramiding, DCA, safety orders, averaging, rebalancing, compounding, simultaneous pairs or shared capital. Ignore short signals. At end of sample, an unfilled pending order stays unfilled and an open LONG is marked only to last observed completed close.
8. **Execution order:** a signal at completed s is committed without seeing s+1. Dispatch the one MARKET executor **only on row s+1**; simulate full 0.001 BTC at that row's **actual completed close** `P=close[s+1]` with economic time `E[s+1]`. This is an idealized historical close-fill convention, **not** an achievable guaranteed post-close trade or a same-signal-bar fill. Missing next row, invalid price, insufficient cash, unmatched/partial/duplicate fill or ledger mismatch => no invented fill and fail closed. Entry and exit cannot occur on the same bar.
