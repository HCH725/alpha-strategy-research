## Economic mechanism

**Source-reported:** a close holding above the 95th-percentile close minus one standard deviation marks statistically supported trend participation; losing that level for the confirmation span ends participation. Power-law return bands and the remaining bands provide context only.

**Research interpretation (hypothesis only):** a completed hourly close printing a fresh 1-bar-confirmed hold above the trailing 20-bar 95th-percentile-minus-stdev line may indicate short-horizon trend acceptance, with the confirmed loss of that line as the fully specified exit. This is a testable hypothesis, not a return, profitability, source-backtest, Survivor, or Qlib-parity claim. Confirmed exits can realize substantially worse prices than the trigger line during gaps, and this derivative has no stop/target beyond the signal exit.

## Signal and fully pinned state

**Market/data:** one real Binance spot `BTC-USDT` `1h` OHLCV series; no synthetic/Heikin-Ashi candles, higher timeframe, multi-market data, fitted model, news, order book or funding feed. Let `T[t]` be the Binance **opening** Unix timestamp of bar `t`; bar `t` is usable only after `T[t]+3600 seconds`. All bar timestamps must be exact consecutive 3600-second increments; every O/H/L/C must be finite and positive, `H>=max(O,C)`, `L<=min(O,C)`, volume finite and nonnegative. Missing, duplicated, malformed or incomplete bars **abort the research run**, not forward-fill, drop, or substitute a price.

**Pinned indicator semantics (Pine v6 built-ins, defaults frozen):** `data[t]` = last up-to-20 closes ending at `close[t]` (push/shift); `rets[t]` = last up-to-20 one-bar returns `close[i]/close[i-1]-1`. `sma[t]=mean(data[t])` over 20 closes; `sd[t]=sqrt(mean((close-mean)^2))` over the same 20 closes (population form, Pine `ta.stdev` default `biased=true`, unchanged in v6). `qu95[t]` = ascending-sorted `data[t]` at index `round(19*0.95)=18`; `ql05[t]` = index `round(19*0.05)=1` (Pine `math.round`, no half-case at frozen defaults). `trigger[t] = close[t] > qu95[t]-sd[t]` (strict `>`; equality never triggers), or undefined (`na`) until 20 closes exist with all-finite returns. `entryCounter[t] = trigger[t] ? entryCounter[t-1]+1 : 0`; `exitCounter[t] = !trigger[t] ? exitCounter[t-1]+1 : 0`; an `na` trigger resets both counters (verified: no signal can form before bar 20). Power-law bands (`close*(1+return-percentile)`), `ql05`, Bollinger lower band, Bollinger-cross colors and the EMA(50) reference are computed-or-displayed in source but **never enter the traded predicates** in either source or derivative. No `request.security`, `time(`, `timestamp(`, volume, or intrabar evaluation exists in the traded path.

**Warmup/initial state:** require **100 consecutive fully completed valid hourly bars** (first decidable signal needs 20 bars: 20 closes for arrays/stdev plus finite returns; 100 is the independently chosen warmup), start with `FLAT`, no orders, no position, 10,000 USDT cash and no inferred earlier trade.

**Entry:** on each fully completed bar `t` after warmup, with state FLAT and no pending order, compute the trigger over closes ending at `t` as above. BUY if and only if `entryCounter[t] >= 1` with a finite trigger (i.e. `close[t] > qu95[t]-sd[t]` on a fully defined window; at defaults exactly one confirmed bar). A flat/degenerate 20-bar window (`sd[t]==0`, `qu95[t]==close[t]`) can never satisfy strict `>`; an undefined trigger never satisfies the gate (both verified). Issue one MARKET BUY of exactly `Q=Decimal("0.001") BTC` at the same row's observed completed `close[t]` under the pinned **historical simulator convention**. Require `cash >= Q*close[t]*(1+f)`, `f=0.001`; otherwise stay flat. On **confirmed full fill**, record `entryPrice=close[t]` and `LONG`, cash minus `Q*entryPrice*(1+f)`. Do not infer a fill from an order intent. A missing/failed/partial fill, invalid price or undefined fee aborts this bounded research run instead of inventing a trade or retry rule.

**Exit priority:** on each **subsequent** fully completed bar after a confirmed BUY, SELL the entire confirmed Q at that bar's actual completed close if `exitCounter[t] >= 1` (i.e. `close[t] <= qu95[t]-sd[t]` on a fully defined window). Entry and exit predicates are mutually exclusive on one close (trigger true vs false — verified analytically and over 24,000 fuzz bars with zero co-triggers), so no priority ordering is needed. No same-entry-bar exit, stop/limit resting order, next-open price assumption, time exit, trailing stop, or fill at the threshold. A full confirmed SELL credits `Q*close[t]*(1-f)` and returns to FLAT. An unresolved SELL or partial fill aborts; no fabricated flat book.

**At most one position.** Source opens only Long and defaults to `pyramiding=0`; while LONG, new long signals are ignored, and no short leg exists to block or reverse. No pyramiding, averaging, BO/SO/DCA, concurrent positions, cooldown, short, leverage, borrowing, compounding, stop/target, time exit, discretionary override or hidden controller risk default. On the last sample bar, mark any still-open BTC to the **actual last observed close**; do not fabricate a terminal liquidation. All signals are ignored while an order is pending.

Arrays, SMA/stdev, sorted percentiles and counters use only already completed candles; no value is available before its bar's close. 4. **Position/re-entry:** one full-size confirmed 0.001 BTC unit, one pending order, re-entry only after confirmed FLAT, no DCA/concurrency/pyramiding, no same-bar entry/exit, abort on unsupported failed/partial fills.

6. **Point-in-time:** arrays, percentiles and counters available only at bar close, later decisions only after their 1h close, no incomplete candles/future leakage; historical simulator's raw open-time labels explicitly mapped to close-effective times.
