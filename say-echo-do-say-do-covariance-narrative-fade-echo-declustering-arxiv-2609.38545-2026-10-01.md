## Economic mechanism

### Source-reported

The paper's claim is a conditional-sign claim about text, plus a measurement kit; the author states its boundary explicitly in Section 1 (`What we do not claim`): `We do not test the theory on market data; our experiments are simulated so that the truth is known`, and `A "strategic" reading means only that words and money moved in opposite directions, in a particular order. It is not evidence of deception.`

1. **Three observable voices (Figure 1, Section 1).** `Say` = what institutions state (ratings, notes, management tone); `Echo` = how the media repeat and amplify it; `Do` = where capital is revealed to have gone (holdings, insider trades, futures positioning, order flow). The stated asymmetry: `Words are cheap and easy to observe, repetition is mostly redundant, and positions are costly, slow to be disclosed and hard to fake.`
2. **Follow-or-fade rule (Theorem 1, Corollary 2, Section 3.1).** With `E[r|y] = kappa (w* - phi)'y`, a channel `k` should be faded iff `phi_k > w*_k` and followed iff `phi_k < w*_k`. An echo channel that is a garbling of the others has `w*_e = 0` and loading `-kappa*phi_e`, so `any crowd weight on echo is reversed`; independent news has `w*_n in (0,1)` and returns drift in its direction whenever the crowd underweights it. This is the paper's `fade the echo, follow the news` result.
3. **Strategic speech and the false-alarm wedge (Theorem 3, Section 3.2).** An informed institution that maximises `x(v - phi*m - lambda*x) - k/2 (m-v)^2` sets `m* = psi*v`, `x* = chi*v` with `psi = (a-phi)/(a-phi^2)`, `chi = k(1-phi)/(a-phi^2)`, `a = 2*lambda*k`; for `v > 0` the regimes are truth (`phi = 1`), shading (`phi < 1, a > phi`), **false alarm** (`phi^2 < a < phi < 1`: `psi < 0 < chi`, talk it down and buy) and exaggeration (`phi > 1`: `psi > 1, chi < 0`, talk it up and sell).
4. **The Say-Do covariance identity (Theorem 4, Section 3.2) - the central, distribution-free object.** Pathwise `r = lambda*x - phi*epsilon`, hence `Cov(r, m_tilde) = lambda*Cov(x, m_tilde) - phi*sigma_epsilon^2`, and in the Theorem-3 equilibrium `Cov(x, m_tilde) = psi*chi*sigma_v^2 < 0` exactly in the false-alarm and exaggeration regimes. The source's operational reading: `A negative rolling dCov(Say,Do) says that an institution's words should be faded; following them requires lambda*Cov(Do,Say) > phi*sigma_epsilon^2. Deeds are always followed.`
5. **Say-Do gap and absorption (Theorems 13, 14, Appendices A.4-A.5).** With a noisy positioning proxy the optimal forecast depends on words and deeds only through `G_gamma = gamma*m - x_hat` entering with coefficient `-c_x < 0` when the Say channel is overweighted; absorption `a = p - phi'y` predicts continuation iff `beta*sigma^2_{v|y} > lambda*sigma_u^2`, and Lemma 15 makes that information content invariant to a misspecified crowd multiplier.
6. **Measurement kit (Section 4).** (i) Theorem 6 semantic declustering: a Hawkes cluster representation in which each article is immigrant (news) or offspring (echo), with factorised posterior `P(pi(j)=0) = mu*f0(z_j)/Lambda_j` and `P(pi(j)=l) = g(tau_j-tau_l) f(z_j|z_l)/Lambda_j`, so `s_new = sum_j p_j s_j` is the MMSE news sentiment and Remark 22 states the split is `causal and free of look-ahead`. (ii) Theorems 7-8 return-aligned contrastive objective with an attainment characterisation and a neighbour-ranking certificate. (iii) Theorem 9 Levy area identifies lead-lag, `long-run area rate has sign sgn(gamma*ell)`.
7. **The composite decision rule (equation (1), Section 4.4, Algorithm 1).** `D = w1*s_new - w2*s_echo + w3*Do + dsgn*w4*Say + w5*A + w6*A_plus`, where `dsgn` is `+1` if the rolling Say-Do correlation is at or above `-c` and `-1` otherwise, `A` is absorption and `A_plus` the direction-signed Levy area. The source says `The weights should be fitted on past data, and a fitted weight whose sign contradicts the theory is a diagnostic that an assumption fails.`
8. **Virality and instability.** Assumption 4 maps echo amplification to effective credulity `phi = phi0/(1-n)`; Proposition 18 makes false alarms reachable only in deep markets (`a < 1`) and exaggeration only in shallow ones (`a > 1`) inside explicit echo-share windows. Theorem 5 shows adaptive trust can settle into a 2-cycle (`5/6 < lambda*k < 1`, born at `a = 2`, lost at `a = 5/3`) and simulations `suggest chaos`; Remark 19 proposes a testable prediction that `the rolling Say-Do covariance should be negatively autocorrelated in markets where the product of price impact and misreporting cost is moderate`.

### Research interpretation

- **Mechanism class:** strategic-communication / credulous-crowd market-structure channel - informed cheap talk plus redundant repetition feeding a narrative-sensitive price, not a risk premium and not a fundamentals forecast. The falsifiable hypothesis in one line: *in a market where part of the price response to institutional text is the crowd's credulity rather than the text's information content, the conditional sign of the cross-sectional return response to statement sentiment flips with the sign of the rolling Say-Do covariance, echo articles carry negative predictive content, and independent news carries positive predictive content.*
- **Component roles (kept separate so later work can ablate them):**
  - Regime / switch: sign of the rolling correlation of `Say` and `Do` over the firm's previous `W` events - `source-reported`, `W = 20` past events (at least 5) with switch threshold `-0.2`, `None of these values was tuned on test data` (Table 6, Section F Hyperparameters).
  - Primary signal: semantic declustering posterior splitting sentiment into `s_new` (follow, `+`) and `s_echo` (fade, `-`) - `source-reported` (Theorem 6, Corollary 2, Proposition 24).
  - Secondary signals: `Do` (always followed, `+`), absorption `A` (sign must be estimated, never assumed - Section G), signed Levy area `A_plus` (who moved first, `+`).
  - Confirmation / risk / exit: **none in the source's experiments.** No stop, no exit, no holding rule, no sizing rule and no cost term is wired to any reported number. Appendix D supplies a causal three-state regime filter (Propositions 32-33), adaptive conformal intervals (Theorem 34), a classical Kelly-shrinkage result (Theorem 35) and the cost-aware existence programme (Proposition 36); none of them is used in the experiments.
- **What this record does NOT claim:** no real-market evidence of any kind (the source says so itself); no cost-aware or net-of-fee result; no printed Sharpe value; no executable trading rule; no independent reproduction by us. The record is captured because it contributes a *new signal construction* and *new negative evidence* (the theory features add at most about 0.005 IC to a plain linear fit in the source's own simulation), which is what the incremental-write threshold in the canonical specification allows.

## Crypto portability

`adapted` - the mathematics (Hawkes semantic declustering, the rolling Say-Do covariance switch, the composite sign pattern) is universe-agnostic, but **every piece of evidence in the source is a simulation of listed-equity-style firms**, and the pinned text contains `crypto` 0, `bitcoin` 0, `perpetual` 0 occurrences, so nothing here is crypto empirical evidence. This is a ported hypothesis, not a validated crypto signal, and the sign of the crypto effect is `unproven`.

What ports mechanically: the declustering posterior, the `W = 20` / `-0.2` switch, the `+news / -echo` sign pattern, and the causal feature construction.

What must be rebuilt for crypto, each a `research-proposed` adaptation rather than a source specification:

- **`Say` channel:** crypto has no analyst-rating or research-note ecosystem of the equity kind; the natural statements are exchange/project announcements, X and Telegram posts, and governance or listing notices, with a very different credulity structure and much cheaper creation of "institutional" voice.
- **`Do` channel:** no 13F, no Form 4, no quarterly holdings. Positioning proxies would have to be funding rate, open interest, exchange netflow, whale transfers or on-chain flow - observed continuously rather than disclosed on a lag, which changes the very asymmetry (`positions are costly, slow to be disclosed`) that the theory leans on.
- **Session and timestamps:** 24/7 markets make the source's `ten days before / ten days after` windows and its business-day event spacing ambiguous, and candle-boundary, exchange-clock and timezone conventions must be declared explicitly (`data gap` in the source).
- **Frictions:** maker/taker fees, quoted spread and depth, participation, venue fragmentation, mark-versus-index divergence, funding payments, leverage, liquidation cascades and stablecoin numeraire all have to be introduced from scratch, because the source models none of them (see the Methods-level census in Provenance).
- **Echo structure:** crypto narrative cascades are dominated by a few social platforms with different virality than newswires, so the Hawkes branching ratio and the von Mises-Fisher concentration are not transferable parameters - they must be re-estimated, not copied.
- **Survivorship:** token listing and delisting churn is far more severe than equity reconstitution, so any point-in-time universe must be built with an explicit listing/delisting rule (`data gap` in the source).

Crypto portability is not authorization to trade.
