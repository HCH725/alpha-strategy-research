## Economic mechanism

### Source-reported
Plain dual-rail trend tracking: the 14-bar average and the 20-bar OLS fitted endpoint form two rails; the long sleeve is held while close sits between them on the prescribed sides, closed when close sits between them on the mirror sides. The prose frames the rails as dual breakout filters that cut false breaks, admits parameter sensitivity, chop-weakness and breakout-failure loss, and suggests dynamic lengths, elastic stops and extra filters (all unported future work — disclosed). The prose stacks the rails (above-both in, below-both out) while the code crosses them (contradiction recorded; crossed rails govern). The stanza window 2024-01-04→2024-02-03 is a one-month 1d sample — cited as provenance, never as performance evidence.

### Research interpretation
Crossed-rail level holder, event-persistent, long-flat. Unlike breakout-edge linreg machines (`forven-linreg-channel-breakout-long-flat-btcusdt-1h` — 50-bar OLS spine with a 2σ channel, edge-triggered breakout chase above the upper rail, midline exit, 1h; `fmz-hull-pac-linreg-swing-two-sided-btcusdt-1m` — Hull377 regime plus PAC extension plus linreg endpoints, two-sided, 1m), this machine never computes a channel, a σ band, a Hull average or a cross — entries and exits are close-between-rails LEVEL states that stay true across consecutive bars (first such bar trades while flat; the position then blocks the rest), and the tradable region flips with the rail order (sma-below-trendline bars can only enter; sma-above-trendline bars can only exit). Unlike cross-event average machines (`fmz-tbma-sma20-cross-low7-stop-2r-bracket-long-btcusdt-1d` — SMA20 cross edge with a Low7/2R stop-bracket box, 1d; the MA-cross family generally), there is no cross event and no stop/limit box anywhere here: direction comes from rail-between levels, the averages only bound the tradable band, and the posture is long-flat on 1d. Unlike oscillator-gated machines (RSI/CCI/Stoch/CMO kin), no oscillator ships anywhere — the only objects are one plain SMA and one hand-rolled OLS endpoint. Five-axis distinction from the nearest pool cousins holds on mechanism (crossed-rail level hold vs breakout-edge chase vs cross-plus-box), signal construction (SMA14 plus hand-rolled OLS-20 endpoint vs 50-bar-OLS-plus-2σ vs Hull-plus-PAC), horizon (1d source-native vs 1h/1m), exits (mirror rail-between id-specific close with no edge/box vs midline/PAC-extension exits), and source identity (FMZ 440986 SMA-Trendline vs Forven linear_regression.py vs Hull-PAC lineages).
