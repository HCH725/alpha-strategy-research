## Economic mechanism

### Source-reported

A two-state (price + velocity) Kalman filter estimates the "true" price by separating trend from noise; symmetric bands at ±2.6× the 200-bar mean absolute error define the volatility envelope. A close crossing above the upper band is read as a volatility breakout (go long); a close crossing below the lower band as breakdown (go short). The declared posture is always-in-the-market: the position flips whenever the opposite band breaks, so the full swing is held with no premature exit. No independently reproduced return, win rate, profit factor, or market advantage is established; the author's tuning/backtest prose claims are noted above and adopted nowhere.

### Research interpretation

Edge-triggered two-sided reversal system with no price level, stop, target, or confirmation leg beyond the two band-break edges. Because entries require genuine edges (not band-above states), closes riding along outside a band without re-crossing hold the position indefinitely, including through adverse excursions with no guardrail; overlapping-range whipsaws that alternately break both bands flip repeatedly with both legs paying fees. Unlike cooldown-gated or confirmation-gated reversal systems, there is no time latch and no second gate — the cross edge alone fires. The bet, kept on the source's own ETH 15m closes, is on state-space-filtered breakout persistence at fixed 200-bar MAE scale.

## Signal

Exact rule as pinned (all five active inputs frozen at source defaults; any other value is a different, unpinned rule). **Single arithmetic declaration (applies to this whole section): all indicator and signal arithmetic is IEEE-754 binary64 (`float64`) — the same width as Pine `float`. No `Decimal` appears anywhere in the signal path; no indicator float is ever converted to `Decimal` and no `Decimal` is ever compared against an indicator value.** The sole `Decimal` in this record is the fixed base-quantity plumbing constant in Execution assumptions, which is never derived from, and never touches, any indicator computation.

- Declaration (source-native): `//@version=6` Pine semantics. `calc_on_every_tick` unset defaults false: exactly one evaluation per completed bar. No `max_bars_back` ships; the derived 300-bar warmup below governs seeding.
- Kalman state (source-native, per-bar recursions on completed closes, index t=0,1,...): on the first bar only, `x_p = close`, `x_v = 0`, `P = [[1,0],[0,1]]`. Every bar: predict `p̂ = x_p + x_v`, `v̂ = x_v`, `P' = F·P·Fᵀ + Q` with `F = [[1,1],[0,1]]`, `Q = diag(0.05, 0.0001)` (i.e. `p00 += 0.05`, `p11 += 0.0001` after the transition multiply, exactly as the code's `a00/a01/a10/a11` then `p00_/p01_/p10_/p11_` lines); update with `z = close`, `y = z − p̂`, `S = p00' + 250`, `K0 = p00'/S`, `K1 = p10'/S`, `x_p = p̂ + K0·y`, `x_v = v̂ + K1·y`, `P = (I−K)·P'` per the code's `i00/i01/i10/i11` and `pp00/pp01/pp10/pp11` lines. `kalmanPrice = x_p`. Seeding is tied to the sample start by source construction; traded signals start only after the 300-bar warmup (below), keeping seeding transients out of every order leg.
- Bands (source-native): `absDiff[t] = |close[t] − kalmanPrice[t]|`; `mae[t] = ta.sma(absDiff, 200)` (arithmetic mean of the last 200 values including bar t; `na` before 200 values exist); `upper[t] = kalmanPrice[t] + 2.6·mae[t]`; `lower[t] = kalmanPrice[t] − 2.6·mae[t]`. The dead `m = 7.88` input computes nothing and gates nothing.
- Triggers (source-native edge semantics): `BULL[t]` iff `close[t] > upper[t]` and `close[t−1] <= upper[t−1]`; `BEAR[t]` iff `close[t] < lower[t]` and `close[t−1] >= lower[t−1]`. Either predicate with an `na` input is false (hold). **Band-value trap, pinned:** the band at bar t embeds `close[t]` itself (via the Kalman update and the inclusive MAE window), so a re-implementation MUST evaluate the trigger against the same-bar band; substituting a lagged band value specifies a different, unpinned rule.
- `BULL[t]` and `BEAR[t]` are mutually exclusive on every bar by construction: with `mae[t] > 0`, `upper[t] > lower[t]` strictly and one close cannot sit strictly above the upper band while strictly below the lower band; in the degenerate `mae[t] == 0` case both bands equal `kalmanPrice[t]` and the two predicates demand strict opposite inequalities against that single value — still exclusive. No same-bar ordering ambiguity exists anywhere in this record. Exact equality (`close == band` on either leg) fails the strict predicate and the position holds — stated, not patched.
- Orders: `BULL[t]` while FLAT or SHORT → queue LONG leg; `BEAR[t]` while FLAT or LONG → queue SHORT leg. While already LONG, a repeat `BULL` is a no-op (single-unit pin, consistent with `pyramiding=1` under either documented reading); while already SHORT, a repeat `BEAR` is a no-op. Before the first signal the system is FLAT by construction.
- Risk legs (pinned absence, not invented): 0 `strategy.close`, 0 `strategy.exit`, 0 `strategy.order` ship anywhere — the posture is explicitly no stop, no target, no trailing, no time exit. The only exit from a position is the opposite band-break reversal.
- Direction: two-sided long/short with symmetric full reversal. Futures venue required for the short leg (realistic on the pinned perpetual venue).
- Display isolation: every `plot` leg renders only and gates no order.

## Execution assumptions

**Market/size:** Binance ETH-USDT **perpetual**, LONG/SHORT single-unit reversal only; initial 10,000 USDT cash and zero ETH; fixed `Q = Decimal("0.3")` ETH per full leg (mirrors the source's $1,000 cash order scale at ETH prices without claiming equivalence, since engine `amount` is base-denominated); one pair, one position side at a time, no shared-capital portfolio, `leverage = 1` (matches the source's `margin_long = margin_short = 100`). No compounding: every leg trades exactly Q.

**Signal → queued action:** an eligible `BULL[t]`/`BEAR[t]` at completed `E[t]` queues its leg(s) for the **next actually observed completed** candle t+1. Dispatch only on row t+1; simulator full-fill price is that row's **actual `C[t+1]`**, economically labeled `E[t+1]`, not its raw candle-open timestamp. Same-side repeat while positioned is a no-op and queues nothing. A reversal queues two legs on the same t+1 row: full close of the held side, then full open of the opposite side (each a full Q fill with its own fee). Check buying power covers `Q·C[t+1]·(1+0.0005)` for the opening leg. Confirm complete fills, fees, and quantities before updating position state. Ledger conversions are explicit and single-sourced: observed fill/mark prices enter the Decimal base/quote cash ledger only via `Decimal(str(P))` (shortest round-trip, deterministic); no indicator float is ever converted, and no `Decimal` is ever compared against an indicator value. No same-signal-bar fill and no next-open assumption.

**Position transitions:** FLAT --BULL--> LONG (one MARKET BUY Q); FLAT --BEAR--> SHORT (one MARKET SELL Q); LONG --BEAR--> SHORT (close full Q, open full Q, same t+1 row); SHORT --BULL--> LONG (mirror). Ignore all triggers on any fill bar j; after a fill, only a strictly later opposite edge may queue the next action. If t+1 is absent at sample end, leave the action queued-unfilled and never invent a fill; if a position remains open, mark only to the last observed actual close.

**Failure/disabled behaviors:** missing price, nonfinite indicator, fee/quantity discrepancy, partial/failed fill, insufficient buying power, or irreconcilable position => **abort** rather than assume success. No DCA, safety orders, BO/SO, partial exits, hedges, simultaneous two-sided holdings, discretionary orders, maker/limit/resting orders, intrabar stop/target, forced terminal liquidation, extra cooldown, or additional filters. Cooldown consists only of fill-bar suppression.

**Costs and risk:** fixed hypothetical `trade_cost = 0.0005` (0.05% each filled side, taken from the source's own commission declaration), **not verified live Binance taker fees**; fee-net per leg `±Q·ΔP − 0.0005·Q·(P_open + P_close)` with both reversal legs fee-bearing. No separate slippage model in this historical simulator path; this is **not** evidence of zero real spread, slippage, latency, impact, or partial fills. The delayed completed-close fill is a retrospective engine convention, **not** an achievable guarantee of obtaining a close already observed. Perpetual funding exists in reality and is an **unsupported economic term** in this historical simulator path: this record makes **no claim** about zero funding, realized funding, margin interest, or liquidation behavior; downstream screening must supply its own verified funding treatment rather than inherit a zero.
