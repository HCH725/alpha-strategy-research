## Economic mechanism

### Source-reported

Detrended-cycle timing, long side only. Subtracting a backward-shifted slow average removes the long drift so the residual exposes where the current close stands against its own settled consensus; the position opens when the residual reclaims positive ground from below (cross above zero — shorter-cycle upturn) and ends it when the residual falls back below zero (the reverse). The equity-index universe and the unmodeled-costs stance are source facts, not this record's venue or economics. No performance figure is carried anywhere.

### Research interpretation

Backward-displaced baseline oscillator — the patient cousin of the pool's raw-momentum records (which read trailing returns against zero with no detrending) and the fixed-level sibling of the pool's DPO-EMA record (which races the same residual family against a self-carved signal line instead of the zero line). Unlike zero-line oscillators without displacement (ROC, CMO, Force Index), the trigger reads a stale-consensus-relative residual, so a slow grinding climb that keeps printing above its 11-bar-old average holds the long without re-firing, and the entry edge keys off cycle-turn timing rather than absolute momentum sign. Unlike signal-line cross systems (DPO-EMA, MACD, gyroscopic signal pairs) the trigger never moves — the zero line is fixed, so entries fire on residual polarity flips, not on adaptive-threshold races. Unlike level-state holds (ROC-sign, SMA-position) the entry is a one-bar cross edge — consecutive positive-residual bars enter exactly once on the crossing bar and hold silently after. The failure mode is plain: smooth geometric ramps never fire (on a sustained ±1%/day tape the residual keeps its sign from the first finite print, so no zero cross ever arms post-warmup — planted sustained-up and sustained-down tapes both print 0 entries / 0 exits ending FLAT, disclosed non-participation by construction, not patched); chop that whips the residual around zero prints repeated cross/sub-zero round trips with no stop and no profit-taking leg (the planted flat/dip/recover/parabolic/decay trip exercises exactly one such round trip: 1 entry / 1 exit ending FLAT, first entry t=117, first exit t=269 — disclosed); an exactly flat tape pins the residual at exact zero forever, so the strict edge never arms (planted dead-flat tape: 0/0 ending FLAT — disclosed, not patched).
