## Economic mechanism

### Source-reported

Asymmetric volatility around the open. The article's thesis sentence: "Market take the stairs up and the elevator down" — volatility distribution is not symmetric, so upside and downside fluctuation must be separated. With the opening price as benchmark, fluctuation above the open is upward volatility, below it downward volatility; "the upward volatility is greater than the downward volatility when the market is in the upward trend, and vice versa in the downward trend". The per-bar gap `diff_t = (High_t + Low_t) / Open_t - 2` measures exactly that: it is positive when the day's high-plus-low mass sits above twice the open (upside reach dominates), negative when it sags below. A 60-day moving average smooths short-term fluctuation; a positive smoothed print reads uptrend, a negative print downtrend. The article ships no stop, target, or risk leg of any kind.

### Research interpretation

Open-anchored range-asymmetry sign as a slow trend read, two-sided overall. Write `A[t] = (H[t] + L[t]) / O[t] - 2` and `S[t] = mean(A[t-59..t])`. `S[t] > 0` means the trailing sixty days' ranges leaned, on average, above their opens — the book goes long the next open; `S[t] < 0` means they leaned below — the book goes short. Unlike sibling AR-popularity (which divides *summed* upside reach by *summed* downside reach into a level oscillator with 100/120 contrarian bands — a ratio object), this rule *subtracts* a fixed open-benchmark (2.0) bar-locally and trades only the *sign of the mean* — a sign object with no band, no ratio, no contrarian leg. Unlike dual-thrust (which hangs breakout rails off the open scaled by recent range — a breakout object), this rule never breaks out of anything: it averages. Unlike Ehlers CG (which weights a midpoint price by recency into an oscillator and crosses it against its own lag — a cross object), this rule crosses nothing against itself: zero is the sole reference. Unlike Twiggs (which multiplies a typical-price location by volume — a flow object), this rule reads zero volume. The failure mode is plain: the live window holds each side for months (90 entries / 90 exits across 2,512 post-warmup bars) with no guardrail, and the 60-bar mean lags regime turns by construction — disclosed, not upgraded.
