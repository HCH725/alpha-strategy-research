## Signal — exact researcher-frozen arithmetic

**Input/clock:** One real Binance `BTC-USDT` **spot** UTC `1h` OHLCV feed. Bar `t` has exchange **open** timestamp `T[t]`; `O[t],H[t],L[t],C[t],V[t]` are knowable for decisions **only at `T[t]+3600 seconds`**, never at the raw opening timestamp. Require exactly consecutive UTC-aligned 3600-second bars, finite strictly positive O/H/L/C, `H>=max(O,C)`, `L<=min(O,C)`, finite `V>=0`, and a real observed close. No synthetic bars, interpolation, dropped missing candles or default/fabricated prices. Reject duplicate/out-of-order bars and invalid data. The initial history must contain at least **64 consecutive complete valid candles** before the first decision; a later gap/invalid bar aborts the run instead of silently bridging it.

**Williams %R14:** for `t>=13`, `HH14[t]=max(H[t-13..t])`, `LL14[t]=min(L[t-13..t])`, `W[t]=-100*(HH14[t]-C[t])/(HH14[t]-LL14[t])`. Undefined if the denominator is zero; no trade while unavailable, and an undefined required value after warmup is a data-incomplete abort. A **fresh recovery edge** is `W[t-1] <= -80 AND W[t] > -80` (strict current crossing, previous equality allowed). The source's prose says previous below -80; this boundary equality convention is **researcher-defined**, not a Pine-equivalence claim.

**EMA21 and EMA55:** define `EMA_n[n-1] = mean(C[0..n-1])` and for `t>=n`, `EMA_n[t] = (2/(n+1))*C[t] + (1-2/(n+1))*EMA_n[t-1]`, with `n=21,55`; earlier EMA values undefined. This SMA-seeded EMA convention is researcher-defined and may differ from Pine `ta.ema` initialization. After warmup both EMAs and current/previous W are defined. A long setup requires **all**: `FLAT`, no pending order, fresh recovery edge, `EMA21[t]>EMA55[t]`, and `C[t]>EMA21[t]`; all inequalities strict except the explicitly specified previous W boundary. No opposite-side signal, fitted model, higher timeframe, calendar/session or lookahead is consulted.

**Frozen risk at entry:** on a qualifying completed signal bar, `S=min(L[t-1],L[t])`, `P=C[t]`, `R=P-S`. Require `R>0`, finite values and a valid positive target; otherwise ignore that signal without a fill. On confirmed full entry at actual modeled close `P`, freeze `stop=S` and `target=P+2*R`. The stop/target **do not trail or reprice**. These are close-decision thresholds, **not** resting exchange stop/limit orders. No author backtest statistic or native Pine entry/exit is represented as reproduced.

## Position, entry/exit priority and failure state

Start `FLAT` with `cash=10,000 USDT`, `base=0 BTC`, no inherited inventory and no pending orders. At most **one** 0.001 BTC long, no short, borrowing, leverage, margin, hedging, pyramiding, BO/SO/DCA, averaging, concurrent positions, partial take profit or compounding. Fixed quantity never scales with equity. A confirmed BUY subtracts its actual modeled quote fill and fee from cash; a confirmed SELL adds quote proceeds net of its modeled fee. Require `cash >= 0.001*P*(1+0.001)` before BUY; insufficient cash skips the edge without placing an order, with no automatic retry on that same edge.

**Per fully completed bar, one decision:** (1) If an order is pending, issue no other action until its outcome is reconciled. (2) If confirmed LONG, do not evaluate entry; beginning with the **first later completed bar**, SELL all confirmed BTC at that bar's **actual close** if `C[t]<=stop`, else if `C[t]>=target`. Stop check has priority, although with `R>0` the two close predicates cannot both hold at one price. A gap past a threshold fills at the **observed close**, never at the threshold or an invented intrabar price. (3) If confirmed FLAT, evaluate the long setup above and submit a single MARKET BUY at that completed bar's observed close. No same-bar entry/exit or exit/re-entry; the next entry requires a **new later W%R recovery edge**. Cooldown is zero completed bars beyond the no-same-bar rule; no entry is triggered merely by staying above -80.

A submitted action is `PENDING`, **not** an inventory update. A full confirmed fill updates cash and position; no partial, failed, rejected or ambiguous fill may be silently treated as full. The bounded research run **aborts** on an unexpected partial/failed/unresolved submitted order, invalid price/fee, data gap or inconsistent fill ledger; it does not invent a retry, borrow capital, synthesize a price, or assume a zero fee. At the final sample bar, any still-open BTC is marked to the **real last observed close** with its already-incurred entry fee; do not invent terminal SELL/exit fee. No time-stop, signal-flip exit, trailing stop, intrabar SL/TP, ATR buffer, discretionary override or Hummingbot hidden stop default is active. Exposure while a close threshold is not hit is an explicit research risk.
