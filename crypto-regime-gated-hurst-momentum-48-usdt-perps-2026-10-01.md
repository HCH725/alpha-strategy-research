## Economic mechanism

### Source-reported

The author frames the problem as a null-hypothesis question rather than an indicator question: if hourly crypto-perpetual returns are a random walk, no price-based rule has positive expectation before costs; if the process departs from a random walk in identifiable, temporally clustered windows, then the class of departure dictates the tradeable class (Section 1, "This is the question that motivates this paper"). The claimed chain has three links, each stated by the source:

1. **Persistence exists but is weak.** The rescaled-range Hurst exponent on 1-hour bars averages 0.568 in TRAIN and 0.584 in HOLDOUT (Section 6.2), classifying 70-81 percent of windows as trending (H > 0.55), 19-29 percent as random walk (0.45 <= H <= 0.55) and under 0.2 percent as mean-reverting (H < 0.45) (Abstract and Section 6.1), while the Lo-MacKinlay heteroskedasticity-robust variance ratio fails to reject the random walk for any individual asset at any of lags 2, 6, 12, 24 hours (Section 6.3).
2. **Observable conditions predict return magnitude, not direction.** A 30-condition screen finds all 30 significant for absolute 4-hour forward return after Benjamini-Hochberg FDR correction (Section 13.2 restates this as 29 of 30 without naming the drop-out - recorded as contradiction 4), effect sizes Cohen's d from 0.464 (C10, volatility expansion) down to 0.007 (C21, buyer/seller imbalance), yet the correlation between the composite condition signal and signed forward 4-hour return is 0.004 (Sections 10.2, 11.1, 13.2).
3. **The Hurst exponent supplies direction and the condition count supplies timing.** The source's own summary is "the gate IS the alpha": gate2_long_only (long only, no short leg) reaches TRAIN Sharpe 4.85 against directional gate2 4.25, and in HOLDOUT the ordering reverses to 3.04 against 5.36 (Section 11.3.1, image-transcribed and partly in prose), so the source attributes most value to knowing when to be in the market rather than which way to trade.

Two architectures are reported side by side. The **cross-sectional condition-ranking architecture (V4)** ranks assets by a composite condition signal, goes long the top 6 and short the bottom 6, rebalances every 24 hours, and is described by the source as fragile: TRAIN Sharpe 0.54, HOLDOUT Sharpe 0.59, 13,061 trades per year, 4 of 9 validation tests passed, breakeven fee 6.6 bps per side, single asset (1INCH) contributing 122 percent of PnL (Abstract, Sections 11.1, 13.3). The **regime-gated architecture** is flat by default and enters only when at least G of five screened conditions are simultaneously ON, using the rolling Hurst exponent for direction; the source reports gate3 (G = 3) at TRAIN Sharpe 3.62 / HOLDOUT Sharpe 4.89 / HOLDOUT CAGR 63.8 percent / HOLDOUT MaxDD -4.9 percent while in market 9-20 percent of the time, and hurst_only (no gate) at TRAIN Sharpe 8.83 / HOLDOUT Sharpe 12.08 / CAGR 78.1 percent / 118.0 percent while in market 97 percent of the time (Abstract, Sections 11.3.3, 11.4, image-transcribed). The source then states these headline numbers are "likely inflated" and gives a conservative true-Sharpe estimate of **1.0-2.0** (Section 13.8).

### Research interpretation

- **Mechanism class:** regime-conditional momentum plus selective market timing, not a new premium. The testable hypothesis is: *the joint event "at least G of five cheap OHLCV conditions are ON and the rolling R/S Hurst exponent is outside its ambiguous band" marks windows in which hourly crypto-perpetual cross-sectional momentum has positive expectation after a 10 bps round-trip fee, while the complement of that event has expectation indistinguishable from zero or negative.*
- **Component roles (each kept separate for later ablation):**
  - Regime / gate: count of ON conditions among {C10 volatility expansion, C20 volume climax, C03 post-cascade, C14 short-term momentum, C04 high cross-asset correlation} with threshold G in {2, 3} (Section 11.2) - the source does not claim this component is alpha, and its own gate2 anti-predictive failure says otherwise.
  - Primary signal: rolling Hurst direction, momentum when H > 0.65, fade when H < 0.50, flat in between (Section 11.2).
  - Confirmation: none beyond the condition count; there is no volume, order-flow or funding confirmation layer.
  - Risk / exit: flatten when the active-condition count falls below G (Section 11.2), plus equal-weight top-6 sizing at 1x leverage and a 5 bps/side fee model.
- **Alternative explanations the source itself supplies, which any acceptance must defeat:** (a) in-market exposure to a bull-market beta, since gate2_long_only beats directional gate2 in TRAIN and the flipped gate2 signal still returns +0.81 Sharpe (Sections 11.3.1, 13.8); (b) diversification across 31 low-correlation positions, which the source offers as the reason hurst_only Sharpe 8.83/12.08 "warrant skepticism" (Sections 11.4, 13.7); (c) volatility-target amplification interacting with volatility-expansion conditions (Section 13.8); (d) survivorship in a 32-asset full-coverage universe (Section 13.7); (e) condition-selection data mining over C(30,5) = 142,506 possible five-condition subsets with no combinatorial correction (Section 13.8). None of these has been ruled out by the source.
- **What this record does NOT claim:** no claim that the mechanism is an alpha, that the reported Sharpes are reproducible, or that the strategy has been implemented anywhere in our stack.

3. ## Falsification plan

Every threshold below is `research-defined falsification threshold` (Scout-chosen, not the source's), and every reproduction step is `research-proposed`. Data: Binance USDT-margined perpetual 1-hour OHLCV for the same 48 contracts plus point-in-time listing/delisting, Bybit open interest where needed, and observed funding rates.

- **F1 Printed-value reproduction.** Recompute gate3 and hurst_only TRAIN/HOLDOUT Sharpe, CAGR, MaxDD and in-market fraction from the pinned specification. **Threshold:** any headline Sharpe reproduced within +/-0.05 and any CAGR within +/-1.0 percentage point, with the image-transcribed cells matching to the printed precision. **Action:** on failure, mark every numeric claim in Section Evidence "not reproducible from the published description" and stop all downstream use of those numbers.
- **F2 Full-friction net gate.** Re-run with funding cash flows, observed bid-ask spread, depth-based slippage and a 1-tick adverse selection, on top of the 5 bps/side taker fee. **Threshold:** gate3 HOLDOUT Sharpe > 0 and hurst_only HOLDOUT Sharpe > 0 after all frictions. **Action:** fail -> classify the result as a fee-model artifact; no further work.
- **F3 Source-debiasing gate.** Apply the source's five declared inflation corrections (condition re-selection on a fresh window, parameter perturbation, variant-count deflation, beta-neutralisation of the long leg, vol-target ablation). **Threshold:** debiased HOLDOUT Sharpe >= 1.0, the lower bound of the source's own 1.0-2.0 band. **Action:** fail -> record the edge as below the source's own conservative estimate and reject as tradable.
- **F4 Gate-versus-exposure ablation.** Compare gate3 against (a) 1,000 random gates matched on in-market time (9-20 percent) and (b) a pure "high-volatility-hours" exposure control with identical holding times. **Threshold:** real gate3 Sharpe above the 95th percentile of both null distributions in TRAIN and HOLDOUT. **Action:** fail -> the gate is market-timing beta, not non-random-walk detection; downgrade the mechanism claim.
- **F5 Anti-predictive replication for gate3.** Flip the direction signal inside gate3's active windows. **Threshold:** flipped Sharpe < 0 in both TRAIN and HOLDOUT (the abstract claims this; the source prints no gate3 number). **Action:** fail -> contradiction 1 stands unresolved and gate3's directional claim is withdrawn.
- **F6 Block-bootstrap significance.** Stationary block bootstrap, block length 5 days, 1,000 resamples, on gate3 and hurst_only daily-equivalent returns. **Threshold:** 95 percent CI excludes zero in both periods (V4's CI [-0.40, 1.39] already fails this). **Action:** fail -> report "not distinguishable from zero" and reject.
- **F7 Multiplicity control.** Benjamini-Hochberg at q <= 0.10 across the 30 conditions x 4 horizons x 5 variants x all reported metrics, plus a Bailey-Lopez de Prado deflated Sharpe ratio for the selected variant. **Threshold:** DSR p < 0.05 and no more than 10 percent of reported cells lose significance. **Action:** fail -> attribute the headline to selection.
- **F8 Concentration and sub-period stability.** **Threshold:** at least 3 of 4 equal sub-periods positive, no single asset above 50 percent of total PnL, and leave-one-out Sharpe degradation below 30 percent. **Action:** fail -> the result is one name or one episode (the V4 122 percent single-asset case).
- **F9 Frozen forward condition window.** Freeze the seven surviving conditions and the five gate inputs as of 2026-10-01 and evaluate on 2026-10-01 through 2027-09-30 with zero re-screening. **Threshold:** each gate condition keeps the same sign with |d| >= 0.05 in the new window, and gate3 in-market share stays within 9-20 percent +/- 5 points. **Action:** fail -> condition instability, mirroring the Section 9 sign flip in C4; abandon the gate.
- **F10 Point-in-time universe rebuild.** Rebuild the panel with listing and delisting dates, including contracts delisted before 2026-02 and launched after 2022-01. **Threshold:** HOLDOUT Sharpe within 20 percent of the reported value. **Action:** fail -> survivorship artifact; reject.
- **F11 Frequency robustness.** Repeat at 4-hour bars with the adaptive half-life re-derived and the Hurst window scaled by 4, and at 15-minute bars. **Threshold:** sign of HOLDOUT Sharpe unchanged at all three frequencies. **Action:** fail -> frequency-specific artifact.
- **F12 Cross-venue replication.** Re-run unchanged on Bybit and OKX perpetuals with venue-matched fee schedules. **Threshold:** same sign and |Sharpe| >= 1.0 on at least 2 of 3 venues. **Action:** fail -> Binance-specific microstructure or data artifact.
- **F13 Synthetic-null test.** 100 i.i.d. paths per asset matched to marginal moments (the OV-08 test the source specified but never ran), plus a Markov regime-sequence placebo (OV-11). **Threshold:** real Sharpe above the 95th percentile of synthetic and placebo Sharpe. **Action:** fail -> distributional artifact; reject.
- **F14 Random-walk null on the label itself.** Re-run the regime labeller on 1,000 white-noise paths of the same length and tails. **Threshold:** the 70-81 percent "trending" share must fall inside the null's 95 percent band or the kurtosis-matched null must be shown to explain it entirely. **Action:** if the null reproduces the label share, the regime variable carries no information and the whole architecture collapses.

## Crypto portability

`direct` - the source is crypto-native end to end: 48 USDT-margined perpetual futures on Binance, hourly bars, crypto-specific mechanisms (liquidation cascades feeding the C03 post-cascade condition, funding settlement windows at 00:00/08:00/16:00 UTC as calendar structure, leverage-driven volatility clustering). Nothing needs to be ported from traditional assets to apply the hypothesis in crypto.

Portability caveats that remain even inside crypto: single-venue dependence on Binance with no cross-venue test; USDT-margin funding payments are structurally part of any real perpetual position but are absent from the model; 24/7 sessions make the "session" conditioning variables venue-clock conventions rather than exchange hours; venue fragmentation, mark/index price divergence, delisting and custody are untouched. Porting the mechanism to equities or futures would be `adapted` at best, since the author motivates the whole construction from crypto microstructure and reports no non-crypto evidence. Crypto portability is not authorization to trade.
