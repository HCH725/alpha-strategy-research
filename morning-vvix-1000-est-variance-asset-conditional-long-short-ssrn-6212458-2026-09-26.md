## Economic mechanism

### Source-reported

- The variance risk premium is persistently negative, yet "volatility claims appear initially too cheap precisely when uncertainty is highest" (Section 1, PDF page 2) — a puzzle risk-based explanations struggle with. The paper's move is to locate **when** the volatility-uncertainty signal arrives rather than whether it arrives.
- **Signal arrives in the US-European overlap**: volatility-of-volatility measured at **10:00 EST**, "during the U.S.-European market overlap", strongly predicts **next-day** variance-asset returns, and the predictive content "dissipates by midday" (Abstract; Section 3.1). The source argues 10:00 is "the first comprehensive assessment of volatility uncertainty, synthesizing information accumulated overnight" (Section 5).
- **Why 10:00 specifically**: Figure 1 (Section 2.2) shows VIX-option volume peaks at 10:00 with narrower spreads, so 10:00 proxies an opening time "not contaminated by opening illiquidity"; Table IA.1 shows cross-time correlations among hourly VVIX measures of 0.78-0.99 with the **10:00 measure the least correlated and least persistent**; Figure 2 shows the VVIX/VIX correlation at 10:00 is roughly 10% lower than at other times.
- **Direction and interpretation**: elevated morning volatility uncertainty forecasts **higher** next-day variance-asset returns, which the source calls "suggestive of investor underreaction and slow-moving beliefs (Lochstoer and Muir, 2022)" (Section 3.1), consistent with underreaction under information uncertainty (Zhang, 2006) — episodes are "structurally novel and informationally complex, generating conservative belief updating and gradual price adjustment rather than immediate incorporation" (Abstract).
- **Direct test of delay** (Section 3.4.2, PDF pages 22-24): if investors adjust slowly, the relationship should be flat for **same-day 10:00→16:00 returns** and strong for **close-to-close returns**. That is what Figures 7-9 show; the source reads this as "direct evidence of delayed price adjustment" (Section 5).
- **Benchmark / baseline mechanism already in the data**: the unconditional short position in variance assets "captures the negative variance risk premium" (Table 3 note), so part of the strategy's raw edge is ordinary premium harvesting, with the conditional signal on top.
- **Competing explanations the source itself rules out** (Section 4): risk factors (4.1, Table 4: VRP, VIX level, slope, skew, VIX², noise, long term-volume, crash index, VSTOXX), overnight information (4.2, Tables 5, IA.10), order-imbalance inventory effects (4.3, Tables 6, IA.11), liquidity premium (4.4, Table 7), the CFE 24-hour VIX futures rule change (4.5, Table 8), day-of-week (4.6, IA.13), and FOMC/ECB announcements, daylight-saving transitions and leading-earnings days (4.7, IA.14).

### Research interpretation

- The missing link between the evidence and the mechanism: **attention is never measured.** The US-Europe overlap is assumed to be an attention/processing window; no order-flow, headline-count, search or institutional-participation proxy is used to test it, so the channel stays an interpretation, not an identification.
- A cheaper reading also fits: 10:00 EST VVIX is simply the **freshest** VVIX of the day (lowest autocorrelation, lowest VIX correlation — Table IA.1, Figure 2), so it may carry a **stale-quote/price-discovery** component rather than a belief-updating component. The source does not separate freshness from attention.
- Endogeneity: VVIX is an option-implied quantity measured at the same timestamp as the options that later form the SPX/VIX straddle legs. If mid-morning option quotes are noisy, the "long variance when VVIX is high" rule is partly a **same-instrument contrarian** trade; the source's controls (Section 4) are all end-of-day or daily aggregates, so this is not ruled out.
- Why it could pay after costs: the position flips only when VVIX crosses a percentile and the contracts are held to maturity (Table 3 note), so the strategy's economics rest on a **slow signal** rather than on turnover — which is plausible, but it also means the result is hostage to the threshold rule.

## Signal

Everything in this section is **source-reported unless marked research-proposed**. The source does not print a rule card; the reconstruction below is assembled from Section 2.2, Section 3.4 and the Table 3 / IA.5 / IA.6 notes.

- **Formation timestamp**: VVIX is recomputed from **1-minute VIX option quotes** (CBOE LiveVol, 09:31-16:15) using the VIX model-free formula on OTM VIX options, Equation (1): `VVIX_t²` equals a VIX-style prefactor `2 e^{rT} / T` multiplying `( ∫_0^{F_t} K⁻² P_t(K) dK + ∫_{F_t}^{∞} K⁻² C_t(K) dK )`, with `F_t` the VIX forward (a theoretical 30-day VIX futures) and `T` in minutes. The rate symbol inside the prefactor renders ambiguously in text extraction, so we assert the structure of the formula, not its exact subscript. The source builds VVIX measures at **hourly intervals 10:00, 11:00, ..., 16:00 EST** (Section 2.2); the trading signal uses the **10:00 EST** value (`VVIX_M`), standardized to mean zero / unit variance in the regression tables.
- **Tradable time**: **underspecified.** Returns are defined as **close-to-close excess returns** `r_{i,t+1} - r_{f,t+1}` (Sections 2.3, 3.1; Table 1 note) so the predictability window runs from day-`t` close to day-`t+1` close, but the source never prints the clock time at which the traded-contract position is opened. Reading "enter at the day-`t` close after observing 10:00 EST" is a **research-proposed** operationalization, not a source rule.
- **Lookback / threshold**: `VVIX_M` is compared with a threshold. Panel A of Table 3: a **static threshold equal to the in-sample 75th percentile** of `VVIX_M`. Panels B and C: the **75th percentile computed over the trailing 126 and 252 trading days**, respectively. Table IA.5 repeats the grid at the **50th, 60th, 70th, 80th and 90th percentiles** (its Panel A note calls the static threshold "the in-sample percentile of VVIX"). Warm-up for the rolling percentiles: not stated → **data gap**.
- **Entry (direction)**: "The strategy takes a long position in the variance asset on days when `VVIX_M` exceeds the threshold and a short position otherwise" (Table 3 note). No explicit entry price/time is printed beyond the cost convention below → entry price `underspecified`.
- **Instrument construction at entry**: "SPX and VIX straddles are constructed by selecting, at the time of entry, contracts with **moneyness between 0.95 to 1.05** and **maturities between 15 and 50 days**, retaining the **contract closest to 30 days**" (Table 3 note). VIX straddles are explicitly the instrument "to examine how VVIX signals are priced on the VVIX itself" (Section 2.3).
- **Exit / holding period**: "Once selected, contracts are **held to maturity unless the `VVIX_M` signal reverses**" (Table 3 note). VIX futures: "**front-month contract ... rolled four trading days prior to expiration; intermediate rebalancing occurs only when the signal reverses or the contract expires**". No stop, no take-profit, no maximum holding period other than contract maturity. What fill/reference price applies at a signal-reversal exit is not stated → `underspecified`.
- **Parameters and their source**: 10:00 EST (the source's headline timing choice); 75th percentile (headline threshold); 126- and 252-trading-day windows for time-varying thresholds; 0.95-1.05 moneyness; 15-50 day maturity band with selection nearest 30 days; four-trading-day futures roll; hourly VVIX grid 10:00-16:00. All are **fixed by the source**, not fitted by this record. **Whether the 75th percentile was chosen after looking at the results is not disclosed** → selection risk (see Negative evidence).
- **Reconstruction gaps** (so the signal is **not** presented as reproducible): exact entry timestamp; tie-break when two contracts are equidistant from 30 days; whether moneyness is measured on forward or spot; spread/quote-mid convention used to mark the held contract between entry and maturity; rolling-percentile warm-up; how `VVIX_M` standardization is applied inside the trading rule (regression-only vs rule); number of contracts and sizing (equal weight vs per-instrument standalone legs); treatment of contracts that fall outside the 15-50 day band after entry.

Constant-maturity horizons `T = 1, 2, 3, 6` months (Section 2.3).

Commissions, exchange/CFTC fees, clearing fees, margin/financing cost, borrow, market impact, participation and latency are **0 occurrences in the pinned PDF → `data gap`, not 0**.

## Execution assumptions

- **Source-stated**: for SPX and VIX straddles "long positions are opened at the ask and closed at the bid, while short positions are opened at the bid and closed at the ask, embedding the bid-ask spread directly in the realized returns"; for VIX futures "a conservative round-trip cost of 0.45 VIX index points" (Table 3 note, repeated verbatim in IA.4/IA.5/IA.6). ## Falsification

Every threshold below is **research-defined** by this record; none is a source claim. Data assumed for all tests: a point-in-time VIX/SPX option quote history with the 10:00 EST VVIX recomputable from the paper's Equation (1), plus tradable quotes for SPX straddles, VIX straddles and VIX front-month futures.

- **F1 — Frozen forward window (walk-forward).** Recompute `VVIX_M` and run the source's Panel-B/C rule on data **after 2022-06-30 for ≥6 months, with zero re-tuning**. Metric: annualized net Sharpe per instrument. **Fail** if the VIX-futures leg (the only leg clearing the source's own 0.70 bar) has net Sharpe < 0.30, or if a Newey-West t < 1.96 on the conditional-minus-unconditional spread.
- **F2 — All-in cost ladder (stress).** Recompute Table 3 with (a) options legs crossed as the source does **plus** 0/1/2.5/5 USD per contract round trip, (b) futures cost 0.10/0.25/0.45/1.00 VIX points, (c) one extra tick of adverse fill on 50% of option fills. **Fail** if the 252-day SPX-straddle net Sharpe (source: 0.571) drops below **0.30** under source-plus-commissions, or if any instrument's net Sharpe ≤ 0 at the source's own 0.45-point assumption.
- **F3 — Signal-timing placebo.** Repeat the whole trading table at 09:35, 10:00, 11:00, 13:00 and 16:00 EST. **Fail** if the 10:00 leg's net Sharpe is not in the **top 1 of 5** timings for the VIX-futures instrument, or if 16:00 is within 0.10 Sharpe of 10:00 (the source's own specificity claim).
- **F4 — Circular-shift null.** Randomly circular-shift the `VVIX_M` series (≥1000 draws, blocks of 5 trading days) and re-run the Panel-B rule. **Fail** if the observed conditional-minus-unconditional net Sharpe does not exceed the **95th percentile** of the null distribution.
- **F5 — Threshold-selection discipline (decisive test of the in-sample 75th).** Fix the threshold using an expanding window only (first 252 days), never revisiting history. **Fail** if the out-of-sample net Sharpe is < **50%** of the in-sample net Sharpe, or if the optimal percentile chosen ex post differs from the ex-ante choice by more than 10 percentile points in >50% of years.
- **F6 — Subperiod breakdown.** Net Sharpe by non-overlapping windows: 2006-2011, 2012-2016, 2017-2022, post-2022. **Fail** if ≤2 of 4 windows have net Sharpe > 0, or if the post-2022 window is negative for the VIX-futures leg.
- **F7 — Competing-explanation regression, out-of-sample.** Re-estimate `r_{i,t+1} ~ VVIX_M + VIX level + VIX slope + skew + VIX futures basis + short-term VIX momentum` on the forward window with HAC errors. **Fail** if `VVIX_M` t < 1.96 or its sign flips — this directly re-tests the freshness-vs-attention interpretation flagged under Research interpretation.
- **F8 — Freshness/attention disambiguation (mechanism test).** Compare 10:00 `VVIX_M` against (a) the **stale 09:30-10:00 tick carry-forward** VVIX and (b) an **overnight-only** VVIX jump measure. **Fail** if the carried-forward stale measure predicts as well as the genuine 10:00 print (|Δt| < 1.96), i.e. the effect is stale-quote freshness rather than the overlap window.
- **F9 — Turnover / flip stress.** Measure daily signal flips and `0.5·Σ|Δw|`. **Fail** if the strategy flips position on >33% of trading days (research-defined) or annualized one-way turnover exceeds 500% (research-defined) while the source's cost model is applied, because then the static bid/ask-plus-0.45 model understates realized cost.
- **F10 — Alternative data dependency.** Rebuild the signal from **end-of-day CBOE VIX options only** (no 1-minute LiveVol data) using a 10:00-reconstructable or EOD proxy. **Fail** if the proxy's coefficient is insignificant (|t| < 1.96) — this checks whether the result is an artifact of a proprietary 1-minute feed.
- **F11 — Placebo day calendar.** Exclude FOMC, ECB, DST-transition and LEAD earnings days as the source does (Table IA.14), plus all Mondays. **Fail** if the conditional spread loses >50% of its magnitude or falls below t = 1.96.
- **F12 — Capacity / fillability.** For each instrument, size the position at ≤10% of the instrument's daily volume (options: the selected contract; futures: front month). **Fail** if the rule cannot be filled at or better than the quoted ask/bid at 16:00 EST on >20% of entry days (research-defined), since the source's net table assumes exactly those fills.
- **F13 — Reproducibility gate.** Seek code/data from the authors; if no replication package exists within **12 months**, the strategy is permanently recorded as **`unverifiable`** (only its published numbers stand).
- **F14 — Crypto port test (only if portability is ever claimed).** No crypto claim is made here. If attempted later, it must use a 24/7 option-implied vol-of-vol series (e.g. Deribit DVOL options) with an explicitly chosen timing anchor, ≥3 months of forward data, Newey-West t ≥ 1.96 and **net-of-taker-fee Sharpe > 0** after spread crossing; otherwise portability stays `unproven`.

## Crypto portability

**`unproven`** — and structurally awkward.

- **Zero crypto evidence in the source**: the pinned PDF contains no crypto instrument, venue or data (universe is CBOE SPX/VIX options and futures only).
- **The mechanism is clock-dependent**: the signal is defined by the **09:31-16:15 US session** and the **US-European overlap at 10:00 EST** (Sections 2.1, 2.2). Crypto trades 24/7 with no open/close and no recurring 10:00 EST overlap event, so the *timing* mechanism has **no natural counterpart**; any port must invent its own anchor (research-proposed: a US-session-hours window or a fixed UTC hour), which is a different hypothesis, not the source's.
- **Observable exists, mapping untested**: an option-implied vol-of-vol observable does exist for BTC/ETH (this repository's `btc-option-implied-vov-predicts-excess-returns-risk-premia-2026-09-19.md` and `crypto-deribit-options-volatility-of-volatility-vov-realized-quarticity-2026-09-01.md` show it is measurable), but **neither that evidence nor anything in the source demonstrates the morning-timing effect in crypto**.
- **Instrument mapping**: SPX/VIX straddles → Deribit/OKX BTC and ETH option straddles (far wider spreads, fewer strikes, quarterly expiry conventions); VIX futures → perpetual funding or BTC volatility-linked products (no VIX-equity equivalent, funding/borrow enters, exchange fragmentation applies). The source's **bid-ask-crossing** cost model is the only part that ports directly — and in crypto the spread is typically an order of magnitude wider relative to the instrument, so the thin net margins in Table 3 (0.389-0.571 SPX) would not be expected to survive unchanged.
- Conclusion: **`unproven`**, not `direct`, not `adapted`; portability would require F14 and is **not authorization to trade**.

## Limitations

- **Data**: requires licensed 1-minute CBOE LiveVol option history and Refinitiv futures; EOD OptionMetrics ranges are not printed; cleaning rules, stale-quote handling and bad-print handling are unstated (`data gap`).
- **Identification**: attention/overlap channel is narrative, not measured; freshness-vs-attention and same-instrument endogeneity are not separated (see Research interpretation); controls are daily/EOD aggregates.
- **Sample/regime**: ends 2022-06; no post-2022 window, no cross-market replication, single asset family, single country.
- **Execution**: no entry timestamp, no order type, no fill/latency model, no turnover, no capacity, no commissions/margin/financing; "net" means bid/ask crossing for options plus a flat 0.45 VIX-point futures cost only.
- **Model/statistics**: no confidence intervals or dispersion on traded results, no multiple-testing correction across the large reported grid, headline threshold chosen in-sample, no drawdown/risk metrics, no position counts.
- **Source quality**: unrefereed SSRN working paper with zero citations, no code/data, partially inconsistent internal notes (three recorded in `contradictions`), and an acknowledgement-by-footnote with no funding/conflict statement.

- **Reproducibility**: without the authors' replication package, exact reproduction of Tables 3/IA.4-IA.6 is blocked by the undisclosed contract tie-break, threshold warm-up and quote-marking convention (see F13).
- **Incremental-write check**: this record was written because the repository contained **no VVIX, no intraday signal-timing, and no conditional long/short on index variance instruments** (pre-write identity search above); it is not ordinary repeated RSI/MACD-family content.
