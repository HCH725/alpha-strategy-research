## Economic mechanism

### Source-reported

The page prose presents a Bollinger-breakout plus MACD-filter system: Band principally names the volatility regime (basis SMA-200, ±2σ), MACD-delta crossovers filter band-reclaim entries, and an EMA-20 line plus an entry-relative ~1.16% line provide the two closes. No source performance metric is adopted (the page carries prose discussion only, no claimed backtest figure adopted here).

### Research interpretation

This is a **band-reclaim entry machine with momentum-delta confirmation and two hard closes**: the traded thesis is that reclaiming the lower 200-band after weakness, especially when MACD-delta turns up, marks acceptance back inside the volatility envelope, while the EMA-20 crossunder close and the entry-relative fractional close cut losers mechanically. With four overlapping entry IFs the machine fires often in choppy tapes (E1 pure reclaim needs no momentum at all), so expectancy hinges on reclaimed bands extending rather than oscillating — every whipsaw prints a fee-paying round trip, and the fixed-fraction close guarantees small realized losses cluster near −1.16% minus fees. A gap across 1h closes jumps clean past every line with no slippage accounting. No profitability is asserted.

## Signal

**Gate 1 — exact researcher-pinned arithmetic.** Index completed UTC 1-hour bars by `t=0,1,...`. Use real Binance BTC-USDT spot `O[t],H[t],L[t],C[t]`; volume is not read. A bar with missing/nonfinite/non-positive close, or with `H<max(O,C)` / `L>min(O,C)`, is invalid; never interpolate.

1. Pinned inputs (all source defaults, fixed): `EMALEN20=20`, `BBLEN=200`, `BBMULT=2`, `FAST=12`, `SLOW=26`, `SIGLEN=9`, `DZERO=0.0`, `DPLUS=0.01`, `FRAC=1.01175734321249` (verbatim source constant).
2. `E20[t]`: `E20[0]=C[0]`; for later bars `E20[t]=(2/21)*C[t]+(19/21)*E20[t-1]` (researcher-pinned first-bar seed; Pine `ta.ema` seed bit-identity not claimed).
3. `BASIS[t]`: population SMA of `C[t-199..t]`, defined only for `t>=199`. `DEV[t]`: population standard deviation (`sqrt(mean((C-mean)^2))`, Pine `ta.stdev` biased convention) over the same window, defined only for `t>=199`. `UP[t]=BASIS[t]+2*DEV[t]`, `LO[t]=BASIS[t]-2*DEV[t]`. Comparisons stay well-defined when `DEV=0` (bands coincide with basis); **this spec contains no degenerate-veto guard anywhere, so no guard-vs-exit conflict of the kind that closed Scout PR #598 can arise**.
4. `M12[t]`/`M26[t]`: first-bar-seeded EMAs of close (alpha `2/13`, `2/27`). `MACD[t]=M12[t]-M26[t]`. `SIG[t]`: first-bar-seeded EMA of the MACD series (`SIG[0]=MACD[0]`, alpha `2/10`). `D[t]=MACD[t]-SIG[t]`.
5. Cross helpers on completed bar `u` (all values required defined, else the predicate is false): `crossUp(A,B,u)=(A[u-1]<=B[u-1] AND A[u]>B[u])`; `crossDn(A,B,u)=(A[u-1]>=B[u-1] AND A[u]<B[u])`; `crossUp0(D,u)=(D[u-1]<=0 AND D[u]>0)`; `crossUp001(D,u)=(D[u-1]<=0.01 AND D[u]>0.01)`.
6. On completed bar `u`, with predicates evaluated on already-closed data only: `E1[u]=(C[u-1]<LO[u-1] AND crossUp(C,LO,u))`; `E2[u]=(crossUp0(D,u) AND crossUp(C,LO,u))`; `E3[u]=(crossUp0(D,u) AND L[u]>E20[u] AND C[u-1]<L[u])`; `E4[u]=(crossUp001(D,u) AND H[u-1]<UP[u] AND NOT crossDn(C,E20,u))`. `ENTRY[u]=E1[u] OR E2[u] OR E3[u] OR E4[u]` (one action whatever the overlap — no priority needed or invented).
7. `X1[u]=(crossDn(L,E20,u) AND C[u]<E20[u])`; `X2[u]=(C[u]<P/FRAC)` where `P` is the confirmed entry-fill close of the open position. `EXIT[u]=X1[u] OR X2[u]` (same full-close action either way).
8. If confirmed FLAT and not pending, `ENTRY[u]` queues one BUY of 0.001 BTC at the next completed close (exit predicates are not consulted while FLAT — Pine's flat `strategy.close` is a no-op, preserved). If confirmed LONG and not pending, `EXIT[u]` queues one SELL of the full 0.001 BTC at the next completed close (entry predicates are not consulted while LONG — Pine's same-direction `strategy.entry` under no-pyramiding is rejected, preserved as the single-position gate). Never evaluate on a fill bar; never reverse on one bar. No stop-loss, no take-profit, no trailing, no time exit, no scale-out, no cooldown, no pyramiding, no short leg, no end-of-sample forced liquidation. An unclosed final position remains open, marked at the last observed valid price only.

**Gate 2:** LONG/FLAT on Binance BTC-USDT **spot**, leverage 1, no short leg in source and none added. **Gate 3:** complete four-edge entry disjunction, complete dual-close exit disjunction with the verbatim fractional constant, and intentionally disabled further risk legs as specified above (verified 4 `strategy.entry` + 2 `strategy.close`, zero `strategy.exit`/`strategy.order` in source).

## Required data

**Gate 6:** require at least **200 consecutive fully completed** UTC 1-hour OHLCV bars before the first signal/fill; the structural minimum is the BB-200 seed clearing at index 199 plus the one-bar signal lag plus the one-bar signal-to-fill lag (first decision `t=200` over completed `u=199`, first fill no earlier than `C[200]`). Predicates computed on completed bar `u=t-1` are known before bar `t` completes (confirmed-bar semantics); the modeled fill at `C[t]` is assigned **effective time** `T[t]+3600`, never the raw Kline opening label `T[t]`. A completed-close MARKET simulator fill is a bounded historical modeling convention, **not** a claim that a live order can execute at a known historical close. Every bar must have a strictly consecutive UTC 1-hour timestamp and valid OHLC. On a missing/invalid/out-of-sequence bar, generate no new signal or fill and **retain** any confirmed existing spot position; do not synthesize an exit. After continuity is restored, resume decisions only after a fresh 200-bar warmup. The controller must never use the current bar's still-forming close to construct a signal. Undefined indicators, non-positive closes and invalid bars deterministically yield no signal — one uniform rule, no invented tie-break and no conflicting veto.
