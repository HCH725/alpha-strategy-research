## Economic mechanism

### Source-reported

Counter-alignment cloud breakout with Donchian entry-bar stops and cloud-flip rail takeprofits, two-sided. The machine hangs a fast EMA high/low cloud (60) beside a slow EMA high/low cloud (240), enters against the standing alignment when price breaks back through the far slow rail (longs fire while the fast cloud sits fully below the slow cloud), stops a sleeve at the 240-bar extreme frozen on its entry bar, and takes profit when the clouds flip alignment and price breaks back through the near fast rail. The prose claims trend-plus-reversal character with qualitative win-rate gains and lists future work (volume/channel filters, trend judgment, adaptive cloud width) that ships nowhere. No numeric performance figure of any kind ships anywhere (stanza window 2023-12-14→2023-12-19 is a six-day 1m sample — cited as provenance, never as performance evidence).

### Research interpretation

Breakout-against-alignment with memory stops, event-driven, two-sided, reversing. Unlike clock-only records, there is no clock predicate anywhere: flat clouds print nothing without a counter-alignment plus rail-cross conjunction. Unlike single-cloud records (`ema-cloud-7-20-trend` — one fast/slow EMA-of-close cloud, entries *with* the alignment on 1d, exits on prior-close vs fast average), this machine builds TWO high/low-rail clouds, enters *against* the alignment on 1m, stops at an entry-bar-frozen Donchian extreme, and exits on cloud-flip rail breaks — five-axis distinction (mechanism, signal construction with `valuewhen` memory, timeframe, exit legs, source identity). Unlike raw-extreme records (`donchian-20-10-breakout` — rolling highest/lowest breakout with a time exit; `forven-naked-donchian-20` — rolling extremes, long-only), no rolling extreme ever triggers an entry here: extremes only freeze stops at entry bars. Unlike box-state records (`darvas-box-breakout` — formation-gated frozen rails, long-only 1d), the rails here are recursive EMA clouds, not confirmed-pivot boxes, and the stop level re-freezes on every entry rather than on formation bars. Unlike MA-cross records (`fmz-dual-sma50-100-atr-stop` — dual-SMA cross with an ATR stop box, 1m two-sided; `sma-crossover-5-200` family), there is no moving-average cross anywhere here and no volatility-scaled box. Unlike oscillator records, no averaged-gain/loss or stochastic computation exists in the signal path.
