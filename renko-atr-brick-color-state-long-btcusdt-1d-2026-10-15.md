## Economic mechanism

### Source-reported

ATR-adaptive noise-filtered trend following (page prose plus quoted Pine, no performance section ships): Renko bricks advance only when price moves a full ATR step, so sub-step chop leaves the brick state frozen and the color unchanged — the market must earn every state change. Trend is not the brick level but the recency of the brick-color inequality: whichever side printed most recently owns the state, with equality resolving to white (explicit no-bet). Entries fire only on the first green bar of a fresh state inside the backtest window (the coil-release bet — the first bar that re-certifies uptrend after any red or white interruption); exits fire on any red bar with no window gate and no stop (the discipline bet — a red brick-color print kills the thesis immediately, even outside the entry window, and nothing else ever closes the book). The prose's crypto-on-higher-timeframes framing and the porter's 1d-BTC stanza describe the venue affinity; the executable block itself names neither.

### Research interpretation

Brick-color-state edge machine, long-only, single-unit, flat-or-long (never short, never leveraged by rule). Unlike every cross record in the pool (MACD-cross, DSS, StochRSI, KDJ) this system never trades a line cross: its only comparisons are brick inequalities and a recency race, and order legs fire on color-change edges, so a bar where the bricks merely hold their color fires nothing. Unlike every breakout record (Donchian, Darvas, Turtle-55d, NR7, BB-breakout) there is no level to break — the brick state itself is the signal, and the ATR step is the only threshold in the system. Unlike stop-carrying records there is no protective leg: the book closes only on a red brick-color print, so a vertical-against move that never prints red is ridden in full, and a white grind holds the book without re-edging. Unlike always-in-market flip systems the book rests flat whenever no fresh green edge fires, whenever the window is closed, and after every red close. The edge, if any, lives in the documented intuition that ATR-stepped brick color on daily crypto bars marks push-persistence worth riding with the brick's own recency memory as chaperone — a bet on witnessed trend state, not on any cross level, volume print, funding regime, session, or second timeframe.
