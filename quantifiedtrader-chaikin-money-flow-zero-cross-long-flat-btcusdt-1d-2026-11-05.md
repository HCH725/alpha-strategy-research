## Economic mechanism

### Source-reported

Volume-weighted accumulation: summing money-flow volume over 20 days signs whether closes cluster toward highs on volume (accumulation, positive CMF) or toward lows on volume (distribution, negative CMF). Crossing up through zero aligns long on the bet that the weight of volume-tilted closes has turned toward accumulation; falling below zero banks nothing and stands flat on the bet that distribution has resumed. The design bets that volume-placement timing on the daily frame beats price-only momentum timing, and that refusing exposure through distribution regimes beats riding them out.

### Research interpretation

Accumulation-cross money-flow system, long side only — the only pool machine that trades the **level of the raw CMF ratio** crossing zero. Unlike the pool `cmf-velocity` record (which trades the **derivative** of CMF — the turn of the turn — gated by an EMA200 regime and exited by ATR brackets), this machine reads no derivative, no regime gate, and no bracket: any post-warmup bar with `CMF > 0` after a non-positive print opens the book at the next open, and any bar with `CMF < 0` closes it. Unlike the pool softkill21 record (where CMF20-sign is one of four simultaneous 1h conjuncts), here the CMF cross is the whole signal. Unlike the pool chaikin-oscillator records (EMA spread of the cumulative A/D line — unbounded, trend-shaped), CMF is a bounded `[-1, 1]` ratio that mean-flips with volume placement, so its zero events time placement rotation rather than trend persistence. Unlike price-only zero-cross machines (DPO, QStick, ROC-threshold), a flat-price tape with symmetric ranges prints exactly `0.0` and commands nothing — volume placement, not price motion, is the traded object. The stack is recursion-free and seed-free (every print from index 19 is defined by the trailing 20 bars alone), so unlike seeded smoothers there is no seed-convention residual. The equity-index universe and the unmodeled-costs stance are source facts, not this record's venue or economics. No performance figure is carried anywhere.
