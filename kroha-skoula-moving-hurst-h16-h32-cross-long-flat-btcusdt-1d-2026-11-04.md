## Economic mechanism

### Source-reported

Chaos-theoretic trend timing: market time series mix white-noise (trendless) and brown-noise (trending) components; the Moving Hurst (MH) indicator tracks the fractal dimension of trailing returns, and its fast/slow cross is hypothesized to move **before** prices reflect the clever-traders-start-selling regime shift, hence ahead of price-based MACD. MACD(26,12,9) is the explicit rival (12-day minus 26-day EMA line, 9-day signal line, cross triggers). Reported context only (fenced, never adopted): MH brings ~3–4× Buy&Hold theoretically; on DAX 4.5× Buy&Hold and 7.2× MACD; on NASDAQ 2.9× Buy&Hold and 16.8× MACD (paired t-tests, alpha 1%: DAX `t = −6.9046, df = 27, p = 1.014e-07, mean diff −160.3643`; NASDAQ `t = −8.8001, df = 87, p = 5.764e-14, mean diff −118.2739`). Practical warning (Section 5.2, fenced): profit is purely theoretical — stationarity unguaranteed, MH can mean a smaller loss, and MH generates a large number of transactions (2000-stake NASDAQ 1996–2014 simulation: MH terminal 7819.8 with 34497.3 in 1%-fees vs Buy&Hold 16648.1 with 187.7 fees; taxes further reduce). Future work is genetic-programming signal filtering (Kroha-Friedrich 2014 direction).

### Research interpretation

Persistence-premium flip, long-flat. When the 16-day Hurst sits above the 32-day Hurst, short-memory structure is hotter than background — the paper reads this as rising chaos and stands aside; when it crosses below, the short window has calmed relative to background — the paper reads this as decreasing chaos and takes exposure. Unlike the pool's century-trend cousin (a 1/3/12-month return-sign majority vote that never computes any Hurst number) this rule never looks at return signs or levels: it trades only the *relative position of two R/S memory estimates*. Unlike MACD cousins it never smooths prices: both legs are second-order statistics of returns. The failure mode is plain and measured: crossings alternate by construction, so a ranging market prints full open/close cycles at each flicker with median hold 2 bars on the live window — the paper's own fee warning, disclosed, not upgraded.

- R1 — Measure grid: completed `1d` bars of BTCUSDT spot only. Continuous calendar days; no session template, no holiday skip. Bar time is never read by any order-gating line.
- R2 — Source series: `C[t]` closes are the sole signal inputs (`O[t]` never read by any order-gating line — fills price at opens; notionals reference closes for cost sizing only). No `H[t]`/`L[t]` (extreme variants unused), no volume, no funding/OI/mark-index read gates on any order; no sentiment/macro/on-chain/cross-venue state. Single timeframe.
- R3 — Returns (frozen): `r[t] = C[t]/C[t−1] − 1` (simple one-day returns, `R1`); non-positive/non-finite closes force `r = NaN`. Log-return, excess-return, or multi-day-return re-readings are different, unpinned rules.
- R4 — Hurst legs (frozen R/S variant; `W ∈ {16, 32}` trailing returns ending at `t`): subperiod lengths `n ∈ {16,8,4}` for W=16, `{32,16,8,4}` for W=32, exact adjacent partition (`W/n` blocks); per block: mean, cumulated-deviation range `R = max−min`, population std `S` (`ddof=0`); skip block if any input non-finite or `S < 1e-12` (near-zero-variance floor — constant-return tapes carry only ~1e-16 FP dust, disclosed); per-`n` average over valid blocks only; `H[t]` = OLS slope of finite `(log n, log avgR/S)` points, requiring ≥2 points else NaN. `H16[t]` needs `r[t−15..t]`, `H32[t]` needs `r[t−31..t]`; `D[t] = H16[t] − H32[t]` when both finite else NaN.
- R5 — Events (strict cross polarity, pinned): `BUY[t] = D[t−1] > 0 ∧ D[t] < 0` (H16 crossing down through H32); `SELL[t] = D[t−1] < 0 ∧ D[t] > 0` (crossing up). Any `== 0`, any NaN, is hold — explicit hold, no event. At most one fill decision per bar by construction; crossings strictly alternate by arithmetic.
- R6 — Position (derived machine, predeclared): `LONG`/`FLAT` single-unit book. If FLAT and `BUY[t]` (post-warmup) → buy at the next open. If LONG and `SELL[t]` → close the whole book at the next open. While LONG, `BUY` bars are no-ops; while FLAT, `SELL` bars are no-ops (no add can fire — pyramiding 0).

- R8 — Position state (single deterministic path — no reset, no re-warmup): one 0.01-BTC unit max, long or flat. Safety orders: explicitly none. No cooldown: after any close, a fresh `BUY` may re-enter at the next signal. Fills are unconditional. Invalid bars (non-positive/non-finite prints) force NaN → hold (no signal, no close). Missing-bar stretches freeze evaluation at the prior target — book and ledger retained across the freeze; evaluation resumes on the next valid bar with no warmup repetition, never backfilled, never forced flat.
