## Hypothesis and six-gate deterministic specification

**Research hypothesis, not a backtest claim:** Requiring RSI>55 and ADX>20 at a fresh EMA9/21 bullish crossover may filter weak trend starts; the 2% close-triggered stop can incur substantially more loss than an exchange-native or intrabar stop. No return, Sharpe, win rate, profitability, Survivor, Qlib parity or live readiness is asserted.

**Gate 1 — inputs, indicators, deterministic conditions.** Single unbroken Binance Spot BTC-USDT **UTC 1h** OHLCV series indexed t=0,1,... by candle **opening** Unix timestamp T[t]; require exactly 3600-second increments. Require finite positive O/H/L/C, H>=max(O,C), L<=min(O,C), finite nonnegative volume, unique sorted bars and no incomplete bars, gaps, interpolation, synthetic closes or forward fills. Float64 feature calculation in chronological order, no fitting. Complete bar t becomes available at E[t]=T[t]+3600, never at T[t].

- EMA_n[0]=C[0], EMA_n[t]=a*C[t]+(1-a)*EMA_n[t-1], a=2/(n+1), for n=9 and 21. Bullish crossover on completed t iff EMA9[t]>EMA21[t] AND EMA9[t-1]<=EMA21[t-1]. Bearish crossover iff EMA9[t]<EMA21[t] AND EMA9[t-1]>=EMA21[t-1]. Equality at current t is **not** a crossing.
- Wilder RSI14 from close: delta_i=C[i]-C[i-1], gain=max(delta,0), loss=max(-delta,0), for i>=1. Seed G14=mean(gain_1..gain_14), L14=mean(loss_1..loss_14). For t>14, G_t=(13*G_(t-1)+gain_t)/14, L likewise. RSI=100-100/(1+G/L); if L=0,G>0 =>100; if G=0,L>0 =>0; if both zero =>50. Undefined for t<14.
- Wilder ADX14/DMI from t>=1: up=H[t]-H[t-1], down=L[t-1]-L[t]; +DM=up if up>down and up>0 else 0; -DM=down if down>up and down>0 else 0. TR=max(H-L,abs(H-Cprev),abs(L-Cprev)). At t=14 seed smoothed TR14=sum(TR[1..14]), +DM14=sum(+DM[1..14]), -DM14=sum(-DM[1..14]); for t>14 each smoothed sum S_t=S_(t-1)-S_(t-1)/14+raw_t. +DI=100*(+DMsum/TRsum), -DI analogous; if TRsum=0 both DI=0. DX=100*abs(+DI-(-DI))/(+DI+(-DI)); if denominator zero set DX=0. Seed ADX[27]=mean(DX[14..27]); for t>=28 ADX[t]=(13*ADX[t-1]+DX[t])/14. Undefined before t=27. Do not substitute an unpinned TA library variant.
- **Long entry edge:** at completed t>=100 while FLAT and no pending order, if bullish EMA9/21 crossover **and** RSI14[t]>55 **and** ADX14[t]>20, queue exactly one BUY for completed t+1. If any condition fails on that crossover, it is consumed; no late entry merely because indicators remain aligned. Strict >; equality fails.
- **Long exit decision:** only after an actual confirmed BUY fill and starting from the next completed candle after its fill candle, first check C[t]<=entry_fill_price*0.98 (STOP); otherwise check bearish EMA9/21 crossover (OPPOSITE). Queue one SELL for completed t+1; STOP wins if both true. RSI/ADX do **not** gate exits. No intrabar wick/high/low trigger or stop order is used. The threshold is fixed from the actual filled BUY close and never trails.
- **Fill convention:** decision after bar t closes at E[t], never execute on that same decision bar. On row t+1, after its full candle is available and actual finite C[t+1] is validated, a MARKET OrderExecutor simulates BUY/SELL at **C[t+1]**. This is a bounded historical row-close convention, **not** a claim that a live order submitted after E[t+1] can fill at that close. A fill is recognized only from confirmed nonzero complete executor fill, not from submitted action. The entry fill bar itself cannot trigger an exit. The exit fill bar cannot trigger re-entry.
- **Gate 2 — direction:** LONG/FLAT on unleveraged spot only; SHORT and auto-reverse explicitly disabled. No borrowing, futures or synthetic short.
- **Gates 3–4 — state/risk:** `FLAT -> BUY_PENDING -> LONG -> SELL_PENDING -> FLAT`, one market and one 0.001 BTC position maximum. No pyramiding, DCA/safety orders, simultaneous positions, partial planned exits, compounding, cooldown, extra stop/TP, breakeven, trailing, time limit or forced terminal liquidation. Re-entry only on a new bullish EMA crossover on a **later eligible bar** after full SELL confirmation. While pending, ignore other signals; do not stack or carry missed crossovers. If BUY cannot be fully confirmed, cancel intent and stay FLAT until a fresh crossover. If SELL cannot be confirmed, retain LONG and latched SELL intent; retry at the next real completed row, never mark FLAT without a confirmed fill. Failed/partial/duplicate fill, invalid market precision/min-notional, cash shortage or inconsistent ledger is a research **abort** rather than assumed execution. At tape end, no next bar means pending action is unfilled; an open LONG is marked at the last real close, never falsely sold.
- **Gate 6 — causality/warmup:** first 100 consecutive fully completed valid 1h bars are feature-only; first eligible signal is t=100 with t-1 valid. All EMA/RSI/ADX values use bars <=t; no future bars, negative shifts, repainting, incomplete HTF, pivots, fitting or lookahead. Validate continuity **before** the engine's `dropna` preparation, and abort the bounded run on any gap/invalid bar rather than silently bridging it. Signal decision timestamp E[t]; simulated fill timestamp E[t+1] despite raw Binance feed indexing by open T[t+1].

**Spot accounting and fee pin:** initial USDT=10,000, BTC=0, fixed Q=Decimal("0.001"), `trade_cost=0.001` on each **confirmed** buy and sell fill, a hypothetical 0.1% of quote notional per side (distinct from the author's 0.075% commission).
