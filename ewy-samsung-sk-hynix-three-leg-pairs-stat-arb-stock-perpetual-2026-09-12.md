## Economic mechanism

### Source-reported

The strategy exploits a structural relationship between the iShares MSCI South Korea ETF (EWY) and its two largest holdings — Samsung Electronics and SK Hynix — which together account for approximately 45% of EWY's portfolio weight (Samsung ≈ 21.72%, SK Hynix ≈ 23.17%, per iShares holdings disclosure 2026-07-17). When EWY deviates from the "implied price" constructed from these two constituents via log-log regression, the strategy bets on convergence of the residual back toward zero.

The author identifies the economic basis for the relationship: Samsung and SK Hynix are genuinely core holdings of the ETF, so deviations have an attribution (other constituents, FX timing mismatches, funding rate differences, liquidity noise) rather than being spurious correlation.

The strategy is explicitly framed as relative-value (not risk-free arbitrage). All three legs are USDT-margined perpetual contracts on Binance (EWY_USDT.swap, SAMSUNG_USDT.swap, SKHYNIX_USDT.swap), eliminating cross-market settlement, FX, and funding-channel frictions that plague direct "Korean cash ↔ crypto perpetual" basis trades.

### Research interpretation

This is a cointegration-based pairs trade adapted to the new Binance stock-perpetual instrument class. The hypothesis is that the ETF's price is cointegrated with a weighted combination of its two largest constituents, and short-term deviations from the cointegrating relationship are mean-reverting.

The mechanism requires that:
1. The perpetual contracts accurately track the underlying cash equities during Korean trading hours.
2. Overnight pricing deviations (when KRX is closed but perpetuals continue quoting) create temporary dislocations that revert at the next KRX open.
3. The residual captures genuine mispricing rather than information arrival (the author explicitly acknowledges this ambiguity cannot be resolved from the order book alone).

Key risk: The author notes that overnight basis deviations can arise from either (a) price discovery (perpetual pricing in overnight information, in which case convergence trade fights the trend) or (b) thin-liquidity dislocation (convergence trade is valid). These two cases are observationally identical in the order book.

## Signal

### Regression model

```
ln(EWY) = c + β₁·ln(Samsung Electronics) + β₂·ln(SK Hynix) + ε
```

- Rolling OLS on the most recent 480 fifteen-minute K-lines (approximately 5 trading days)
- β coefficients are elasticity coefficients: β₁ = 0.5 means "when Samsung rises 1%, EWY rises 0.5%"
- The residual ε represents the portion of EWY's price unexplained by Samsung and SK Hynix co-movement

### Z-score construction

```
z = (latest residual − mean residual over window) / residual standard deviation over window
```

**Important caveat (source-acknowledged)**: The regression is fitted on the full sample within the window, and the latest residual participates in that fit. This systematically compresses the z-score. The residual sequence is not a set of comparable residuals generated by one fixed model. Absolute z-scores cannot be interpreted as standard normal; thresholds must be calibrated empirically.

### Trading state machine

| State | Trigger | Direction |
|-------|---------|-----------|
| `FLAT` | Initial / `|z| < 0.5` / holding > 48h | No position |
| `SHORT_SPREAD` | `z > 1.0` (entry_z) | Short EWY, long Samsung, long SK Hynix |
| `LONG_SPREAD` | `z < −1.0` (entry_z) | Long EWY, short Samsung, short SK Hynix |

Entry and exit thresholds are asymmetric (1.0 vs 0.5) to prevent oscillation near the threshold.

### Position sizing

- Hedge ratios derived from β: notional ratio is 1 : β₁ : β₂
- Gross exposure normalized to GROSS_EXPOSURE = 0.7 (sum of absolute weights)
- Leverage: 3×
- Rebalancing triggered only when: (a) direction of any leg flips, or (b) absolute weight drifts > 20% from last rebalance
- Minimum rebalancing notional: 3 USDT (also serves as exit-closing switch)

### Parameters (all research-proposed, not source-calibrated)

| Parameter | Default | Note |
|-----------|---------|------|
| `K_PERIOD` | 15 min | K-line period |
| `LOOKBACK` | 480 bars | ~5 trading days |
| `ENTRY_Z` | 1.0 | research-proposed |
| `EXIT_Z` | 0.5 | research-proposed |
| `MAX_HOLD_HOURS` | 48 | research-proposed; author notes it should be derived from half-life |
| `DRIFT_THRESHOLD` | 0.20 | Re-hedge threshold |
| `LEVERAGE` | 3 | |

| `EMERGENCY_DROP` | 0.06 | Per-leg circuit breaker |

| `MIN_REF_LOGRANGE` | 1e-6 | Price-freeze detection |

### Data hygiene gates (source-specified)

1. **Price-freeze detection**: if a hedge leg's log-range < 1e-6 over the window, skip (market closed/frozen)
2. **Collinearity detection**: if normal equations are near-singular (Gaussian elimination pivot < 1e-12), skip
3. **Minimum residual standard deviation**: if residual sd < 5e-4, skip (z denominator too small)
4. **Z-value sanity cap**: if |z| > 15, treat as calculation fault and skip
5. **Minimum common K-lines**: requires at least 490 common timestamps across all three legs

### Risk controls

- **No z-based stop-loss**: if z widens against the position, no stop-out occurs; only 48h timeout or |z| < 0.5 exit
- **No position reversal**: if z crosses to the opposite extreme while holding a position, position is held until timeout or exit band
- **Per-leg emergency reduction**: if a single leg moves adversely by ≥ 6% between checks, that leg's position is halved (source acknowledges this can damage hedge structure)
- **48-hour forced exit**: prevents short-term dislocation from becoming long-term belief

- **Instruments**: EWY_USDT.swap, SAMSUNG_USDT.swap, SKHYNIX_USDT.swap (Binance perpetual contracts)
- **Market type**: USDT-margined linear perpetual contracts
- **Timeframe**: 15-minute OHLCV candles
- **Fields**: Close prices for all three instruments; timestamp alignment across series
- **Venue**: Binance Futures
- **Data as-of**: Binance listed SKHYNIXUSDT, SAMSUNGUSDT, and HYUNDAIUSDT on 2026-06-02 (source-reported)

## Execution assumptions

- **Signal-to-order timing**: Signal computed on completed 15-minute bars; orders placed at next bar open (research-proposed; not explicitly stated by source)
- **Fill model**: Source simulation assumes immediate full fill at latest price (source-acknowledged as unrealistic; no queue priority, partial fills, or slippage modeled)
- **Fees**: Not modeled in the simulation (source-acknowledged gap)
- **Slippage**: Not modeled (source-acknowledged gap)
- **Funding rates**: Not modeled; source acknowledges funding rates differ across the three legs and create carry bias
- **Leverage**: 3× (research-proposed)
- **Three-leg coordination**: No execution-coordination mechanism; source acknowledges leg risk when fills are not simultaneous
- **Order precision**: Not aligned to contract stepSize or minimum notional (source-acknowledged gap)
- **Capacity**: Not assessed; newly listed stock perpetuals may have limited order-book depth

### Negative evidence

- The author's own checklist of 10+ items that must be completed before live deployment constitutes significant negative evidence about readiness

- The strategy is in a new instrument class (stock perpetuals listed 2026-06-02) with very limited market history

## Falsification plan

- **Cointegration test**: Engle-Granger or Johansen test on the residual ε to confirm stationarity. If residual is non-stationary, the mean-reversion hypothesis fails. (research-defined falsification threshold)
- **Half-life estimation**: Fit ε to an OU process; if half-life > MAX_HOLD_HOURS, the holding period is insufficient for convergence. (research-defined)
- **Walk-forward out-of-sample test**: Rolling window with out-of-sample evaluation across at least 6 months of data covering multiple market regimes (bull, bear, sideways). (research-defined)
- **Cost sensitivity**: Re-evaluate convergence amplitude net of estimated taker fees, slippage, and funding-rate carry. If average convergence < 2× round-trip cost, strategy is not viable. (research-defined)
- **Funding-rate carry analysis**: Track funding rates for all three legs; if portfolio has persistent carry bias toward one side, the alpha is contaminated by carry exposure. (research-defined)
- **Parameter stability**: Test ENTRY_Z, LOOKBACK, and MAX_HOLD_HOURS across sub-periods; if optimal parameters change drastically, the relationship is unstable. (research-defined)
- **Market-structure change test**: If Binance delists or relists any of the three perpetuals, or if KRX changes trading hours, re-evaluate from scratch. (research-defined)
- **Action on failure**: If any of the above tests fails, the strategy should not proceed to paper/testnet/live.

## Limitations

- **Extremely short sample**: 2-day simulation only; no statistical significance possible
- **No peer review**: Published as a community post on FMZ, not a peer-reviewed paper
- **No named author**: Provenance is traceable to FMZ but author credentials are unknown
- **No cointegration verification**: The author explicitly states no ADF or Engle-Granger test was performed
- **No cost modeling**: Fees, slippage, funding rates, and execution delays are not modeled
- **New instrument class**: Stock perpetuals listed 2026-06-02; less than 3 months of market history at time of article
- **Liquidity risk**: Newly listed contracts may have thin order books; quoted spread ≠ executable spread
- **Overnight ambiguity**: The author explicitly identifies that overnight basis deviations cannot be distinguished between information arrival and liquidity dislocation from the order book alone
- **Collinearity risk**: Samsung and SK Hynix are both Korean semiconductor companies; high correlation may cause unstable β estimates
- **Per-leg circuit breaker flaw**: The emergency reduction evaluates each leg individually, not the portfolio; can damage hedge structure and create naked directional risk (source-acknowledged)
- **No trading calendar**: KRX holidays, earnings windows, and index rebalances are not handled
- **Simulation bias**: FMZ simulation assumes instant full fills with no costs; three-leg strategies are especially sensitive to execution quality
- **All thresholds and parameters are research-proposed**: No empirical calibration behind ENTRY_Z, EXIT_Z, LOOKBACK, or MAX_HOLD_HOURS
