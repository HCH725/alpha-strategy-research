## Economic mechanism

### Source-reported

Trend-gated intraday mean reversion (sibling README: `Institutional-grade mean reversion within a trend. Uses Volume Weighted Average Price (VWAP) as dynamic support/resistance. Enters when price bounces off VWAP bands in the direction of the main trend (determined by 50 EMA)`): each completed bar prints the session-anchored VWAP and symmetric ±0.5% deviation bands plus EMA-50. A long leg fires when price holds above EMA-50 and taps back up through the lower band with its wick touching the band — a with-trend dip into the volume-weighted mean that immediately reclaims it. A short leg mirrors it below EMA-50 at the upper band. The position closes when price crosses back through the VWAP center line. There is deliberately no stop, no target, and no flat state beyond pre-signal bars — the order block ends with the two `strategy.close` guards and no risk leg ships anywhere, by design gap rather than by hidden leg.

### Research interpretation

Session-anchored VWAP band-tap reversal, two-sided with center-line exits — the first VWAP record in the pool. Unlike Bollinger/Keltner breakout records (volatility-multiple envelopes traded on breaks with the break direction), this rule fades band tags back toward the volume-weighted center and only with the EMA-50 trend behind it, so counter-trend tags fire nothing; unlike oscillator level/cross rules, the reference line is volume-weighted and resets each session rather than oscillating in a fixed range; unlike price-level breakout records, both the entry bands and the exit line are recomputed every bar from the session accumulator, so there is no fixed level to defend. The bet is on with-trend reclamation of the session's volume-weighted mean at frozen `0.5%/50` geometry, not on any volatility extreme, calendar effect, fixed support/resistance level, or oscillator zone.

## Signal

- Indicators (pinned arithmetic — evaluated from the frozen inputs, not tuned): `vwapValue = ta.vwap(close)` — session-anchored volume-weighted mean of the close series, causal accumulation over completed bars of the current session; `ema50 = ta.ema(close, 50)` — causal recursion on completed-bar closes; `vwapUpper = vwapValue * (1 + 0.5 / 100)` / `vwapLower = vwapValue * (1 - 0.5 / 100)` — symmetric percentage deviation bands recomputed each bar.
- Regime gate: `uptrend = close > ema50` / `downtrend = close < ema50` (completed-bar close versus completed-bar EMA-50; equality is neither, disclosed).
- Entry long: `longCondition = uptrend and ta.crossover(close, vwapLower) and low <= vwapLower` then `strategy.entry("Long", strategy.long)` — completed bar holds above EMA-50, closes back above the lower band after being at/under it, with its wick touching the band. Fires on completed bars with same-bar-close execution under the derived declaration.
- Entry short: `shortCondition = downtrend and ta.crossunder(close, vwapUpper) and high >= vwapUpper` then `strategy.entry("Short", strategy.short)` — mirrored at the upper band.
- Exits (coded, the sole position-closing path): `exitLong = ta.crossunder(close, vwapValue)` then `strategy.close("Long")`; `exitShort = ta.crossover(close, vwapValue)` then `strategy.close("Short")`. No stop, no target, no trailing exit leg, no time exit — pinned as written, explicitly none, not invented.
- Direction: two-sided with no coded flat state past seeding (after the first signal the system is always in market unless an exit prints without a same-bar entry — disclosed, not enabled). Entry order in source (long `if` before short `if`, exits after entries): the two entries are mutually exclusive by construction (`close > ema50` and `close < ema50` cannot co-print), the two exits are mutually exclusive (`crossover` and `crossunder` of one pair cannot co-print), so at most one entry leg and at most one exit leg fire per bar; an entry and the opposite-side exit can co-print (e.g. a long tap bar that also crosses above center closes a coincidental short — a same-bar no-op for the short side since the short exit fires regardless of position, disclosed under Execution assumptions).
