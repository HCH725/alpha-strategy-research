## Economic mechanism

### Source-reported

SMA-cross trend ignition with a pattern-anchored fixed payoff, long-only. The 20-bar SMA is the slow consensus price; a close crossing from below to above it is read as trend ignition. Risk is anchored to the market's own recent footprint — the lowest low of the last 7 bars — and the take-profit is set at twice the stop distance (2:1). The LONG prose block restates exactly this (SMA20 cross, 7-candle lowest-low stop, 2:1 TP) plus an explicit risk admission: choppy-market false breakouts, execution slippage, potentially wide stops, fast post-breakout reversals, and parameter sensitivity. The source ships no sample statistics, trade count, fee basis, win rate, or performance figure of any kind — a full-token census for Sharpe/胜率/win-rate/profit-factor/drawdown/annualized returns zero performance tokens — no source-reported performance is adopted or restated.

### Research interpretation

Consensus-cross ignition machine with a footprint-anchored 2R bracket, event-driven, long-only, flat except when a fresh cross fires. Unlike clock-only records (`monday-drift` — UTC day-of-week + hour predicates with time exits), there is no clock predicate anywhere here: quiet bars print nothing without the cross. Unlike day-open threshold records (`dual-thrust`, `holmberg-orb` — displacement measured against the 00:00 UTC open with first-breach entries), the test is consensus-relative (each close against its own trailing SMA20, no open anchor, no breach direction vote). Unlike band-stretch records (`bollinger-band-breakout-long-midline-exit` — close crossing the SMA20 ± 1.5σ UPPER band with a midline-reclaim exit and no coded bracket), this trigger is the consensus line itself (not a dispersion-normalized stretch), and risk is a fixed 7-low-anchored 2R bracket (not a midline reclaim). Unlike color-sequence records (`1-3-1` — one red bar plus three rising green closes, red-low-anchored 1R bracket), the alphabet here is close-vs-average position (never open-vs-close colors), the pulse is a cross edge (not a 4-bar alternation), and the bracket is 7-low-anchored at 2R (not pattern-low-anchored at 1R). Unlike series-vs-average records (`tradingview-pponbin-donchianlow7-wma62` — the rolling 7-low SERIES crossing a 62-bar WMA with a nonrising-WMA exit gate), this compares the raw close against a plain 20-bar SMA and exits only on the fixed bracket. Unlike reversal-pattern records (`forven-three-bar-reversal` — three consecutive lower lows plus a higher-close confirmation with a cumulative-decline gate, new-low exit, no bracket), this machine buys consensus-cross ignition (continuation, not washout-reversal), needs no decline gate and no confirmation bar, and always brackets every trade. Unlike channel-reversion records (`tradingview-mateosandoval1025-channel-reversion-donchian50-sma200-atr14` — Donchian50/SMA200/ATR14 reversion system), there is no channel, no 200-bar leg, no ATR leg — only the 20-bar consensus line and the 7-bar footprint. Unlike volume-gated cross records (`alma-cross-volume-gate` — ALMA cross with a volume gate), no volume is read anywhere. The edge, if any, lives in the documented intuition that a fresh close-above-consensus after weakness marks a footed short-horizon trend worth riding 2R while risking the recent footprint — a bet on month-scale drift persistence after consensus reclaim, not on any level, oscillator, volume flow, calendar effect, or second timeframe.

## Signal — Gate 1, fully pinned derivative

Index completed UTC daily bars `t = 0, 1, ...` with real Binance BTC-USDT futures `O[t], H[t], L[t], C[t]` (volume not read). A bar with missing/nonfinite/non-positive prices, or with `H < max(O,C)` / `L < min(O,C)`, is invalid; never interpolate. All inputs pinned at source defaults: `trendLength = 20`, stop lookback `7`, TP multiple `2` (any other values are a different, unpinned rule; the dead `length = 25` input is not a parameter of this record).

- **Consensus `SMA20[t]` (t ≥ 19):** exact arithmetic mean of `C[t−19..t]` (Pine `ta.sma` — non-recursive, no seed state; `na` before index 19, and `na` comparisons never fire — disclosed).
- **Footprint `LOW7[t]` (t ≥ 6):** exact minimum of `L[t−6..t]` (Pine `ta.lowest` — non-recursive; `na` before index 6).
- **Long signal `EL[t]` (t ≥ 20):** `C[t] > SMA20[t] AND C[t−1] <= SMA20[t]` — strict break side, non-strict arming side, both against the CURRENT bar's SMA (file-literal placement — disclosed). Single pulse per bar by construction (one boolean per bar; no counter, no queue).
- **Bracket legs (latched at the signal bar, never re-anchored):** for a long signal on bar `s`, `SL = LOW7[s]`, `TP = SL + 2×(C[s] − SL)`, resting until filled (the two exit legs are issued only on signal bars). Levels never move after the signal bar (latched bracket, NOT a ratchet, NOT floating).
- **Degenerate pin (explicit):** `SL ≤ C[s]` always (the signal bar's own low is inside the 7-bar window). If `C[s] == SL` (seven doji-flat bars then a cross — vanishingly rare on BTC 1d), `TP == SL` and the stop-first same-bar rule resolves the tie to the stop price (self-check fuzz: zero `TP == SL` cases in 20,000 tapes; the pin governs the case analytically — disclosed).
- **Exit precedence (explicit researcher pin):** from the fill bar onward, each held bar resolves bracket first-touch against the bar's `H/L` with the **stop-first** same-bar rule (both touched → stop price wins). There is no opposite-signal exit (no short leg exists) — a signal bar arriving while long is discarded unseen (explicitly no queue, no add — Pine `pyramiding = 0` default behavior pinned as an explicit gate, same pin class as the merged Super-Scalper, SMMA17, PowerZone, candle-size and staircase records).
- **Gap/contiguity pin (explicit):** the 20-bar and 7-bar windows must be fully populated by consecutive valid calendar bars; a gap pauses signals until the windows refill (no interpolation, no carry-forward of stale windows — the stateless construction makes this the only consistent reading — disclosed).

**Direction (Gate 2):** BUY-to-open / SELL-to-close on the long leg only. No short leg ships anywhere (0 `strategy.short`). The long-only stance on the pinned futures venue is realistic and needs no margin-short permission. No hedge, no cross-market trade.

**Risk (Gate 3, explicit):** latched 7-low-anchored 2R bracket book. Fixed stop at the 7-bar lowest low and fixed 2×-risk take latched at each signal close. No trailing ratchet, no breakeven move, no time stop, no partial exit, no averaging, no forced end-of-data liquidation, no silent executor default. An end-of-sample open position is marked at the last real valid close, not closed with an invented fill.

**State (Gate 4):** `FLAT`, `BUY_PENDING`, `LONG` — plus no counters (stateless signal). Initialize `FLAT` after 30 valid completed bars. Entries fire only from confirmed `FLAT`; a missing/failed/unconfirmed fill **halts** this research run, never synthesizes inventory. While `LONG`, further `EL` bars are no-ops. A pending order prohibits additional actions. **No DCA/safety orders (explicitly disabled — the downstream 6%+6%+6% house overlay is not active in this admission and never silently overrides single-unit semantics)**, no pyramiding, no concurrency, no compounding. No cooldown is specified (explicitly none, not invented) — re-entry needs a fresh cross edge. A position whose bracket never touches is held indefinitely with no time exit (same indefinite-hold clause as the merged 1-3-1 record — disclosed as the principal holding risk alongside prose-admitted chop whipsaw).
