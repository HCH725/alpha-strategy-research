## Economic mechanism

### Source-reported

Cynthia Kase's Dev Stops seek the statistical balance between letting profits run and cutting losses, accounting for volatility (risk), the variance of volatility bar-to-bar, and volatility skew (occasional spurious volatility spikes). Stops sit where reversal-against-trend becomes statistically significant under the log-normal range curve: enough risk to stay in a good position, no more. When `Pk>0` the line is built from the high side (stop), when `Pk<0` from the low side (target); price touching the line triggers the opposing position. The mirror prose warns: stop computation lags and can miss the best point, the deviation multiple needs tuning, and **single-trade loss size cannot be capped** — large losing trades are possible. No profitability assertion ships with the file.

### Research interpretation

This derivative holds a long only while the completed close sits at or above the Level-4 DevStop line (`state +1`) and exits to flat when the close prints strictly below it (`state -1`) — a close-vs-volatility-band state hold, not an edge-triggered cross and not a midline exit. The signal construction is range-asymmetry, not a trailing multiple of ATR: `RWH/RWL` normalize the 30-bar high/low reach by `ATR·√Length`, `Pk` smooths the asymmetry with a 3-bar WMA to pick the branch, and the band is `AVTR ± 3·SD` (average true range plus three population deviations of the 2-bar range) extended by a 20-bar extremum. Because the source program contains no TP/SL-order leg, an adverse move that never prints a sub-line close leaves the position exposed indefinitely; the only risk control is the state-flip close exit.

## Signal

1. **Input and availability:** one chronological, contiguous sequence of actual Binance BTC-USDT spot UTC **1d** candles indexed t=0,1,... . Native opening time T[t] must be UTC midnight-aligned and T[t+1]-T[t]=86400 seconds. Require finite positive O,H,L,C, H>=max(O,C), L<=min(O,C); volume is unused and unchecked. Reject duplicates, gaps, out-of-order bars, missing/zero/nonfinite observed prices or incomplete candles; never interpolate or forward-fill. Completed bar t is available at E[t]=T[t]+86400, not T[t]. Float64 chronological arithmetic, no intermediate rounding. Only O/H/L/C are consumed by the decision.
2. **Constants (researcher-pinned to source defaults):** LEN=30, MULT=3, WMA=3, WIN=20, `Level=4` pinned (the `Val1/Val2/Val3` branches are code-present but unexplored and frozen out, not silently removed), `reverse=false` pinned (the source `reverse` input would swap sides; frozen OFF).
3. **Pine-faithful recursion (all bars, causal only):** `TR[0]=H[0]-L[0]`; `TR[t]=max(H[t]-L[t],|H[t]-C[t-1]|,|L[t]-C[t-1]|)` for t>=1. `ATR` is Wilder `rma(TR,30)` (seeded with the SMA of the first 30 TR values, first value at index 29). `RWH[t]=(H[t]-L[t-30])/(ATR[t]·√30)` for t>=30; `RWL[t]=(H[t-30]-L[t])/(ATR[t]·√30)` for t>=30. Nonpositive/zero ATR or an unavailable 30-bar lag yields Pine `na`, never an invented value or an exception. `Pk=wma(RWH-RWL,3)=(3·x+2·x[1]+x[2])/6`. `range2[t]=highest(H,2)-lowest(L,2)` for t>=1; `AVTR=sma(range2,20)`; `SD=stdev(range2,20)` with Pine v4 population semantics (biased, divided by N). `inner_up=H-AVTR-3·SD`; `inner_dn=L+AVTR+3·SD`. `Val4=Pk>0 ? highest(inner_up,20) : lowest(inner_dn,20)`, na-propagating. `ResPrice=Val4`. A flat tape drives ATR toward zero and holds `na` by the guard; this is fail-closed silence, not 0/0.
4. **State:** `ResPrice` na → hold the prior state (pre-validity: no signal, never an order). Else `C[t]<ResPrice[t]` → `-1`; otherwise → `+1`. Strict inequality: `C==ResPrice` routes to `+1`. `possig=pos` under pinned `reverse=false`. Once valid the state takes values in `{-1,+1}` only; `possig==0` (the `strategy.close_all()` guard) is unreachable in every parameter setting, including `reverse=true` — a dead branch, expressly fenced, not a hidden exit.
5. **Warmup:** bars t=0..89 are **90 completed-bar no-trade warmup** (researcher-declared, 3× Length; covers the 30-bar reach seed, the Wilder seed transient, the WMA chain and the 20-bar extremum windows — first valid `ResPrice` falls at index 39, verified on real data this cycle). First eligible decision is t=90.
6. **Entry:** when FLAT with no pending action, t>=90, t strictly later than the last SELL-fill bar, and `state[t]==+1` (level-based, faithful to the source `if (possig == 1) strategy.entry("Long")` under default pyramiding=0), queue exactly one BUY. Dispatch on row t+1 only, independently of that row's signal, and model a full 0.001 BTC MARKET fill at its **actual observed completed close** C[t+1], effective E[t+1]. No t+1 price or indicator is used to decide at t. If row t+1 does not exist, leave unfilled. Require cash >=Decimal("0.001")*Decimal(str(C[t+1]))*(1+Decimal("0.001")); insufficient funds or ambiguous/missing fill aborts rather than assuming borrowing.
7. **Exit:** when confirmed LONG and no pending action, on the first eligible completed bar u after the BUY-fill bar with `state[u]==-1` (the source short-state, reused as the LONG exit only — never a short entry), queue a full 0.001 BTC SELL for row u+1 at actual observed C[u+1], effective E[u+1]. No stop/target/trailing/time order exists in source; all are expressly disabled, not hidden defaults. The `ResPrice` flip itself is the sole risk mechanism, and the source warns it cannot cap single-trade loss.
8. **State and priority:** exactly FLAT, BUY_PENDING, LONG, SELL_PENDING; one position and one pending action maximum. The state is single-valued so entry and exit levels can never co-fire on one bar; no tie-break is needed. Source default pyramiding=0 gives a single unit with no adds. On a pending/fill bar ignore new order signals but keep the state updating. On confirmed SELL, return to FLAT using the independent confirmed-fill inventory ledger and record last SELL-fill bar. Re-entry requires FLAT plus `state==+1` on a future bar (no cooldown). Never BUY and SELL on the same bar. If a fill fails, is partial, duplicated, stale or cannot be reconciled, abort the segment. At sample end, an unfilled pending signal remains unfilled; open inventory is marked only to the last actual completed close and not fictitiously liquidated.
9. **Disabled:** Pine next-bar order semantics, futures leverage/margin, default quantity semantics, the `reverse=true` side-swap, `Level=1/2/3` branches, short/borrow leg (no short entry exists in this derivative), stops/targets/trailing/time exits, pyramiding, DCA/safety orders, simultaneous markets/shared capital, partial exits, dynamic sizing/compounding, limit/maker fills, intrabar triggers, the `basePeriod: 1h` stanza granularity, discretionary overrides, hidden executor risk defaults and forced end-of-sample close.
