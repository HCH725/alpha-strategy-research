## Economic mechanism

### Source-reported
Envelope-breakout trend capture with volume-force confirmation, two-sided: a 200-bar close-range midline ratchets by lagged-40-bar ATR only while price rides outside the band; the FIRST bar of each band transition is the trigger, admitted only when the Klinger volume oscillator agrees in sign and the candle closes in the trade direction; risk is the 5-bar swing extreme with a 1.5R target, latched for the life of the trade. - **Q-Trend state (causal, per-bar):** `qH[t]=max(C[t-199..t])`, `qL[t]=min(C[t-199..t])`; midline seeds `(qH+qL)/2` while `bar_index<=200`, then carries the prior close (`m[1]`); `eps[t]=1.0*ATR40[t-1]` with Pine `ta.atr` (RMA of previous-close TR, explicitly lagged one bar); Type-A `changeUp[t]` = cross-up of `C` over `m+eps` OR `C[t]>m[t]+eps[t]` (mirrored down); ratchet `m` by `±eps` only on transition bars, else carried; `ls` tracks `"B"/"S"`; `qBuy[t]` = `changeUp` on the first bar with prior `ls!="B"` (mirrored sell).
- **KVO gate (causal):** `mom[t]=hlc3[t]-hlc3[t-1]`, sign-persistent `trend`, `dm=H-L`, `cm` recursion, `vf=100*V*trend*|2*dm/cm-1|` (0 when `cm=0`), `kvo=EMA29(vf)-EMA55(vf)`; long needs strict `kvo>0`, short strict `kvo<0`.
- **Predicates (actionable only t ≥ 300, at bar `t`'s close):** `longSig = qBuy and kvo>0 and C[t]>O[t]`, `shortSig = qSell and kvo<0 and C[t]<O[t]`; `risk>0` required (`C[t]>lowest(L,5)[t]` / mirrored). Pre-warmup bars flat by record rule; every miss/na/degenerate/doji bar is an explicit neither-fire hold.
- **Latched bracket (fixed at signal bar `s`, never moved):** long `SL=lowest(L,5)[s]`, `TP=C[s]+1.5*(C[s]-SL)`; short mirrored off `highest(H,5)[s]` (levels tickSize-snapped at 0.10 — sole precision pin). Eligible from bar `s+1` onward.
- **Resolution (explicit researcher pin, sibling class):** each held bar resolves the box first-touch against `H/L` with the **stop-first** same-bar rule. Opposite confluence while holding is IGNORED (no reversal by record rule); same-side confluence while holding is a no-op; co-fire is structurally impossible (verified above).
