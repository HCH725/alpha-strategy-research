## Economic mechanism

### Source-reported

Trend-gated mean-reversion with band-touch exits, two-sided. A three-conjunct band-vs-SMA200 state classifies the regime (all three band lines above the SMA200 for longs, all below for shorts); a reclaim edge against the band (close crossing back OVER the lower band for longs, close crossing OVER the upper band for shorts) is the entry trigger; each leg closes on the opposite band touch (high crossing over the upper for longs, low crossing under the lower for shorts) or on losing the slow average (close under SMA300 for longs, close over SMA250 for shorts). No stop-loss, target, trailing, partial, time exit, or performance figure of any kind ships anywhere (a full-token census for Sharpe/胜率/win-rate/profit-factor/drawdown/annualized returns zero performance tokens — no source-reported performance is adopted or restated; the prose explicitly defers stop-loss to future work).

### Research interpretation

Regime-gated reclaim switch with dual-leg market closes, event-driven, two-sided, flat except after an admitted trend-plus-edge print. Unlike clock-only records (`monday-drift` — UTC day-of-week + hour predicates with time exits), there is no clock predicate anywhere here: chop prints nothing without a trend state plus a reclaim edge. Unlike joint-edge records (`chartart-bollinger-rsi-double-joint-cross` — RSI(6)-50 edge AND SMA200±2σ rail edge on the SAME bar, two-sided 1d, always-in reversal with no closes), this machine separates state from edge across bars (a persistent three-conjunct trend state enables single-series reclaim edges), exits by explicit market closes (not opposite-entry reversal), and runs 1m (not 1d) — different basis (EMA20 vs SMA200), multiplier (2.3 vs 2.0), gate doctrine, exit doctrine, and horizon. Unlike MACD-gated band records (`fmz-442643-nabz-bbmacd` — four-IF MACD-delta-gated lower-band entries with EMA20-crossunder plus entry-relative fractional closes, long-only 1h), this machine has no momentum oscillator anywhere, gates on a triple band-vs-average state (not MACD delta), closes on band touch plus slow-average cross (not EMA20/fractional), and runs two-sided 1m. ## Signal — Gate 1, fully pinned derivative
