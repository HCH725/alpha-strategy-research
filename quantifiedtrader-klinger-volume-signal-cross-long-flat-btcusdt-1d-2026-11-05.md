```text
class KlingerVolume(Strategy):
    n = 34; sig_n = 13
    def init(self):
        c, v = pd.Series(self.data.Close), pd.Series(self.data.Volume)
        kvo = (np.sign(c.diff()) * v).rolling(self.n).sum()
        self.kvo = self.I(lambda: kvo, name="KVO")
        self.signal = self.I(lambda: kvo.ewm(span=self.sig_n, adjust=False).mean(), name="Signal")
    def next(self):
        if crossover(self.kvo, self.signal) and not self.position: self.buy()
        elif crossover(self.signal, self.kvo) and self.position: self.position.close()
```

## Economic mechanism

### Source-reported

Long-horizon money-flow composite, long side only. Each bar's volume is signed by the direction of that bar's close change and accumulated over a 34-bar rolling window, so sustained accumulation (up closes on volume) builds a high KVO print and sustained distribution drives it negative; the KVO's own EMA-13 signal line defines the trigger — the position opens when signed-volume accumulation crosses above its recent self (money-flow upturn confirmed across the 34-bar window) and ends it on the reverse cross. The equity-index universe and the unmodeled-costs stance are source facts, not this record's venue or economics. No performance figure is carried anywhere.

### Research interpretation

Rolling signed-volume sum raced against its own signal line — the windowed cousin of cumulative OBV machines and the volume-side sibling of the lane's price-composite cross family (KST's quad-ROC-vs-EMA9). Unlike cumulative OBV-vs-MA machines (the pool's OBV-EMA-cross records, which never forget — every print since genesis feeds the level), this machine forgets on a fixed 34-bar schedule: only the last 34 signed-volume bars exist, so a years-old accumulation extreme cannot pin the trigger. Unlike raw volume-thrust machines, direction comes from the sign of close changes, not from volume magnitude alone — a huge doji-day volume prints `sign(0) = 0` and contributes nothing. Unlike zero-state level machines the entry is a one-bar cross edge — consecutive above-signal bars enter exactly once on the crossing bar and hold silently after. Unlike fixed-level oscillators the trigger line moves — it is the KVO's own EMA-13, so entries key off accumulation acceleration relative to its recent self, not off an absolute level. The failure mode is plain: a dead-constant tape pins every close difference at exact zero, so KVO and signal both print exact zero forever and the strict edge never arms (planted dead-flat tape: 0 entries / 0 exits ending FLAT — disclosed, not patched); dead-smooth geometric ramps on constant volume pin the KVO at a constant print the EMA signal is seeded onto, so no crossing can ever form (planted sustained ±1%/day tapes print 0 entries / 0 exits ending FLAT — disclosed never-trades degeneration by construction, deterministic, not patched); smooth volume-trended ramps likewise print no post-warmup cross (the cross ages out before the warmup ends — disclosed); chop that whips signed accumulation around its signal prints repeated cross/reverse round trips with no stop and no profit-taking leg (the planted chop tape exercises entry, exit, and re-entry: 4 entries / 3 exits ending LONG — disclosed).

## Limitations

- Never-trades degeneration by construction: an exactly flat tape pins every close difference at exact zero, so KVO and signal both print exact zero and the strict edge never arms (planted dead-flat tape: 0/0 ending FLAT — disclosed, not upgraded).
- Steady-trend silence by construction: smooth geometric ramps pin the KVO at a constant print the EMA signal is seeded onto (constant volume) or age the cross out before the warmup ends (trended volume) — planted sustained-up, sustained-down, and volume-trended ramps all print 0/0 ending FLAT (disclosed, not patched).
- Chop-whip by construction: signed accumulation whipping around its signal prints repeated cross/reverse round trips with no stop or target (planted chop tape: 4/3 ending LONG; live probes show the same edge-race texture) — disclosed, not patched.
- Single-venue port residual: backtesting.py pandas arithmetic vs Binance spot execution (prints, fees, open-definition across 24/7 sessions, volume-denomination comparability with the source's equity tapes) is unclaimed, disclosed.
- Next-open latency idealization: signal cadence on the decision close print while fills land on the next open; any live gap between the two differs by construction — disclosed, research-only (this convention is the source's own stated execution, preserved verbatim).
- Hold-band residual: the machine commands nothing while KVO stays above its signal — multi-week holds through above-signal chop that never prints a reverse cross are the normal case, not an anomaly, disclosed.
- No-authorship-page residual: the page prints no handle, license, or creation stamp; provenance rests on the QuantifiedTrader URL/slug, printed names, page hash, data stamp, and fetch date — disclosed.
- Performance evidence is absent: nothing here is calibrated, fitted, or tuned to any backtest (the `34` / `13` defaults are the source's, never re-run here; the page's own grid-search section, where available, is not adopted as a parameter source; the page's printed Sharpe/return/drawdown figures are fenced, never adopted).
- Author scope: the page ships exploratory research with an explicit not-investment-advice posture in the site family, not a vetted trading system — this record is research-only and claims no live suitability.
