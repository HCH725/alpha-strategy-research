## Economic mechanism

### Source-reported

Three differently parameterized supertrend direction filters must agree `up` before a long is taken (and agree `down` before a signal exit), so a single noisy band cannot trigger trades alone; the `volume > 0` gate keeps dead bars out; a four-leg ROI ladder banks winners early while letting calm trends run; a deep fixed stop plus optional trailing bounds catastrophic loss. This is a qualitative hypothesis, not independently validated profitability.

### Research interpretation

Test whether hourly triple-supertrend agreement (fast/medium/slow bands with hyperopt-frozen lengths) predicts BTC appreciation durable enough to reach an 8.7%-to-0% duration-gated ROI ladder — measured from the actual fill bar — before a 26.5% fixed stop or a triple-`down` reversal, with state-based entries (no cross edge, no cooldown) and at most one fixed-size position. The derivative measures only this frozen bracket; it cannot speak for the source's trailing leg, equity sizing, hyperopt variants, or Freqtrade fill timing.

## Signal

- Input: strictly chronological, **completed** UTC Binance Spot BTC-USDT `1h` candles indexed `t=0,1,...`; use `O/H/L/C[t]` and `V[t]`. Candle `t` carries exchange open timestamp `T[t]` and is decision-available only at `E[t] = T[t]+3600` seconds. Reject missing/duplicate/gapped hourly bars, nonfinite/nonpositive prices, `H<max(O,C)`, `L>min(O,C)`, `L>H`, or nonfinite/negative volume. Reject/abort invalid research segment; never synthesize prices, forward-fill signals, or silently skip a broken bar. No funding, order book, cross-market, alternate-timeframe, or private feed is read.
- **Researcher-pinned supertrend arithmetic (own executable rule; restates pinned `technical@00190d7f supertrend.py` loop-for-loop):** float64 throughout. `TR[0]=H[0]-L[0]`; `TR[t]=max(H[t]-L[t],|H[t]-C[t-1]|,|L[t]-C[t-1]|)` for `t>=1` (identical to TA-Lib `TRANGE` on validated finite inputs). `ATR` = rolling SMA of TR over period P: `ATR[P-1]=mean(TR[0..P-1])`, undefined for `t<P-1`. `HL2=(H+L)/2` (the only divisions are `/2` and the length-P mean — both over validated finite inputs with nonzero constant divisors, so no zero-division is reachable). Basic bands `bU=HL2+M*ATR`, `bL=HL2-M*ATR`. Final bands zero-initialized, loop `i=P..n-1`: `fU[i]=bU[i]` if (`bU[i]<fU[i-1]` or `C[i-1]>fU[i-1]`) else `fU[i-1]`; `fL[i]=bL[i]` if (`bL[i]>fL[i-1]` or `C[i-1]<fL[i-1]`) else `fL[i-1]`. Line `st` zero-initialized, same loop: if `st[i-1]==fU[i-1]` then `st[i]=fU[i]` if `C[i]<=fU[i]` else `fL[i]`; elif `st[i-1]==fL[i-1]` then `st[i]=fL[i]` if `C[i]>=fL[i]` else `fU[i]` (the `0==0` seed quirk at `i=P` is preserved exactly as in the pinned implementation). Direction `d[i]='up'` if (`st[i]>0` and `C[i]>=st[i]`), `'down'` if (`st[i]>0` and `C[i]<st[i]`), else undefined (mirrors the source `fillna('')`; strict inequalities — equality on the `up` side never fires `down`). Any undefined input makes the dependent value undefined; a gate on undefined is false, never imputed. Post-warmup (`t>=199`, max period 18) no undefined branch is reachable; fail-closed handling governs invalid candles only.
- **Entry conjunction (all on completed signal bar `t`, state-based — no cross edge required):** FLAT, no pending, `t>=199`, `d[t]=='up'` on all three buy legs `(4,8)`, `(7,9)`, `(1,8)`, and `V[t]>0`. On fire: queue exactly one BUY of 0.001 BTC for the **fill bar `f = t+1`**; do not use bar `f`'s data to decide at `t`. At `E[f]`, fill at the actual finite `C[f]` iff `cash >= Q*P*(1+fee)` in exact Decimal with `P = Decimal(str(C_float[f]))`; if insufficient cash or no valid `C[f]`, consume the signal, remain FLAT, and invent no partial fill or deferred order. A confirmed full fill sets LONG and records **`P_entry = C[f]`** (the actual fill-bar close) and `fill_index = f`. No signal or exit evaluation on the fill bar. Re-entry after flat needs only the same state on a later bar; there is no cooldown, no fresh-cross requirement, and no pyramiding.
- **Exits (LONG, no pending, evaluated only at completed bars `u` with `u > f`, in pinned order; at most one exit per bar):** (1) **fixed stop, checked first:** if `C[u]<=0.735*P_entry` queue one full-position SELL (division-free form of `(C-P_entry)/P_entry<=-0.265` for `P_entry>0`); (2) **else ROI ladder on fill-anchored duration `dur_min = (u-f)*60`** (elapsed minutes from fill availability `E[f]` to decision availability `E[u]`): required profit is the value of the **largest ROI key `<= dur_min`** in `{0:0.087, 372:0.058, 861:0.029, 2221:0}`, fire if `(C[u]-P_entry)>=req*P_entry`; (3) **else signal exit** if `d[u]=='down'` on all three sell legs `(1,16)`, `(3,18)`, `(6,18)` and `V[u]>0`. The winning exit queues exactly one MARKET SELL for the next completed bar `s=u+1` at real `C[s]`, sells the full recorded base inventory, sets FLAT. No exit executes if its required future bar is absent; the pending order stays unfilled at sample end. No evaluation on the SELL fill bar; earliest re-entry decision is a later eligible bar.
- **Worked clock example (normative, not illustration):** signal `t=199`, fill `f=200`. At `u=206`, `dur_min=360`, largest key `<=360` is `0` → require 8.7%. At `u=207`, `dur_min=420`, largest key `<=420` is `372` → require 5.8%. On BUY fill enter LONG; on SELL fill become FLAT. No short, hedge, borrowing, DCA, safety orders, partial exits, dynamic sizing, independent cooldown, or forced end-of-sample liquidation.
