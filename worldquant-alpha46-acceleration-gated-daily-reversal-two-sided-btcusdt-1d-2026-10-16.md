## Economic mechanism

### Source-reported

Coarse mean-reversion/momentum building blocks. The paper frames its universe coarsely: `one can think of alpha signals as based on mean-reversion or momentum`, where `a mean-reversion alpha has a sign opposite to the return on which it is based`. Alpha#46 is a three-regime acceleration block keyed by a 20/10-day second difference on closes: strongly positive acceleration takes a fixed contrarian short, negative acceleration takes a fixed long, and the narrow middle band takes the opposite side of the trailing 1-bar close change. The paper ships no behavioural, structural, or risk-premium mechanism beyond this measurement construction, and no stop, target, or risk leg of any kind.

### Research interpretation

Fixed-leg acceleration-contrarian book with a narrow reversal middle, two-sided overall. The gate (`trend = (close[t−20] − close[t−10])/10 − (close[t−10] − close[t])/10 = (close[t−20] − 2·close[t−10] + close[t])/10`) is a discrete acceleration (convexity) object over two adjacent 10-bar legs on the same single close series — rising-then-falling (positive acceleration) fires the fixed `-1` short leg, falling-then-rising (negative acceleration) fires the fixed `+1` long leg, and only the thin `0 <= trend <= 0.25` band fires the reversal payoff (`delay(close,1) − close[t]`). Unlike sibling worldquant-alpha24 (a 100-day slow momentum ratio spliced to a distance-vs-reversal payoff), this rule has no trailing extremum and no multi-day reversal leg — its memory is exactly 21 closes and its fallback is a raw 1-bar fade. Unlike sibling worldquant-alpha9 (a sign-purity streak gate, always-in-market), the regime here is an acceleration level with two fixed legs, not a streak count. Unlike sibling worldquant-alpha12 (a volume-signed 1-day product), this rule never reads volume. Unlike sibling worldquant-alpha23 (a high-level gate with a formula flat regime), this rule has no formula flat regime — every defined bar fires one of three legs, and undefined bars alone go flat by record rule. The failure mode is plain: a steadily accelerating trend pins the book to the fixed `-1` short (or fixed `+1` long) with no scaling and no guardrail, while the middle-band reversal fades every 1-bar push with no smoothing — disclosed, not upgraded. The absolute-dollar thresholds (`0.25`, `0`) are carried over exactly without normalization: on BTCUSDT scale (tens of thousands per coin) typical 10-bar acceleration magnitudes dwarf `0.25`, so the middle band fires only on near-zero acceleration prints and the book behaves near-purely as the sign of acceleration — disclosed as a single-venue port residual in Crypto portability and Limitations, never re-scaled into a source claim.

## Signal

Exact rule as pinned (any other window, series, comparison, threshold, fill, frame, book, or tie treatment is a different, unpinned rule):

- Inputs (paper A.2): `close[t]` on completed `1d` bars only. No `open`, no `high`, no `low`, no `volume`, no vwap/adv/returns/market-cap/sector, no cross-sectional rank, no second asset or timeframe is read by any order-gating line.
- Acceleration gate (paper Appendix A.1, pinned impl lines 427–428): `trend[t] = (delay(close, 20)[t] − delay(close, 10)[t]) / 10 − (delay(close, 10)[t] − close[t]) / 10` — full-window trailing construction, undefined before the 21st completed bar per the `delay` code (`delay(close, 20)` needs 21 closes), never seeded or backfilled. Constant divisors `10` only; no guarded division is involved.
- Fixed-short leg (paper A.1, pinned impl line 430): if `trend[t] > 0.25` (strict), `score[t] = −1`. Tie `trend[t] == 0.25` falls through to the fallback by the printed `0.25 < trend` form and the pinned `trend > 0.25` code — predeclared, never left ambiguous.
- Fixed-long leg (paper A.1, pinned impl line 429): else if `trend[t] < 0` (strict), `score[t] = +1`. Tie `trend[t] == 0` falls through to the reversal leg by the printed `trend < 0` form and the pinned `trend < 0` code — predeclared, never left ambiguous.
- Reversal leg (paper A.1, pinned impl lines 81–82, 429): else (`0 <= trend[t] <= 0.25`), `score[t] = −(close[t] − delay(close, 1)[t]) = delay(close, 1)[t] − close[t]`. Undefined before the 2nd completed bar by construction; genuinely two-sided; fires only inside the narrow middle band.
- Direction mapping (source-grounded, fenced in Negative evidence 1): `score[t] > 0` → long signal; `score[t] < 0` → short signal; `score[t] == 0` → flat signal (fires when the 1-bar change prints exactly zero inside the middle band). The entry fills at the next bar's open, one single unit, only if the book does not already carry that side (see position rule).
- Exit: the first opposite signal reverses the full unit in one step (close plus opposite entry at the same next-bar-open fill); a zero print flattens the full unit at the next open — reversal-or-flatten is the sole exit path by construction. No stop-loss, no take-profit, no trailing, no time exit (the paper ships no risk leg — carried over exactly, disclosed in Provenance adaptation (4), never upgraded into a source claim).
- Direction: two-sided overall (fixed short plus reversal shorts; fixed long plus reversal longs).
- Position rule (predeclared researcher pin): single-unit; an already-long book treats further long-signal bars as no-ops (no pyramiding, no averaging), and symmetrically for shorts; flat-regime bars while already flat are no-ops. Re-entry re-arms on the next opposite or flat-exit signal only (no cooldown specified — explicitly none, not invented). Pre-first-signal and any NaN-score bars are flat by record rule.

## Limitations

- No price stop, no time stop: a steadily positive-acceleration trend pins the book to the fixed `-1` short, and a steadily negative-acceleration trend pins it to the fixed `+1` long, while middle-band prints fade every 1-bar push — all with no guardrail before the next regime print, by the carried-over paper design — the record's principal risk.
- Gate-boundary churn: the `trend == 0.25` and `trend == 0` neighbourhoods flip the book between a fixed leg and the reversal leg on acceleration noise — the paper provides no buffer band and this record invents none.
- Absolute-threshold port residual: `0.25` dollars means something on $20–$200 equities and almost nothing on ~$100k BTC — the middle-band reversal leg is vestigial on this venue by construction, disclosed, not re-tuned.
- One-bar close noise: the fallback leg is a raw 1-bar close difference with no smoothing — single-print spikes move the book whenever the rare middle band fires, disclosed.
- 20-bar staleness: acceleration legs compare closes 20/10 bars ago — a flat old anchor shapes today's gate with no lookback decay, disclosed.
- Single-venue port residual: paper-regime U.S. equities vs Binance perpetual execution (prints, fees, funding, close-definition, margin mechanics beyond the house model) is unclaimed, disclosed.
- Performance evidence is absent: nothing here is calibrated, fitted, or tuned to any backtest.
- Author scope: the paper presents production formulaic alphas as research objects, not a vetted retail trading system — this record is research-only and claims no live suitability.
