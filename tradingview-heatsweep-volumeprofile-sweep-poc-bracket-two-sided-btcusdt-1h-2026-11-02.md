## Economic mechanism

### Source-reported
Stop-hunt reversal at volume-backed levels, two-sided: resting liquidity (last two confirmed swings per side) is scored by nearby traded volume as a share of the POC row; a wick through a hot level that closes back inside on above-average volume is read as a sweep, entered toward the range with a wick-plus-0.5×ATR stop and a 1.5R target; POC-reclaim crosses trade the same bracket off the 3-bar extreme. Consumed levels cannot refire. The page warns volumeless symbols do not work, delta is candle-estimated not order-flow, graphics repaint until bar close while signals stay confirmed-bar-only, and same-bar stop+limit touch order is unknown. No performance figure of any kind ships anywhere — none adopted.

### Research interpretation
Unlike the pool sweep cousins (`tradingview-pridarasx-turtle-soup` — 20-bar Donchian sweep with next-bar extreme confirmation, long-flat, no volume; `tradingview-jygirouard-asia-sweep` — session-low sweep plus bullish reclaim, once daily, 2R close exit; `turtle-soup-donchian-fade` — breakout fade with adds and 2.5× brackets), this machine gates every sweep on a **100-bar close-binned buy/sell volume profile** (heat ≥ 0.25 of the POC row) plus a **20-bar relative-volume > 1.0** gate, adds a second POC-reclaim setup, consumes levels so none refire, and exits ONLY through the latched wick/3-bar-plus-0.5×ATR first-touch bracket — never a signal close. Unlike the pool volume cousins (prior-day value-area prose in R-Breaker, HVN mentions in scans — all prose, none a profile implementation), this is the pool's first executable volume-profile/POC machine. Five-axis distinction holds on signal (profile-heat sweep + POC cross vs naked price/session sweeps), gates (heat + rvol vs none), exits (latched 0.5×/1.5R bracket vs close exits), state (consumed levels vs reusable levels), and source identity (TV FjletcWZ vs all others). Same-author closed PRs #606 (HVN fade, long-flat) and #625 (Volume Vault order-block, `ouvmvItR`) are different scripts and mechanisms, both closed unmerged — disclosed, never proof.

## Signal — Gate 1, fully pinned derivative

Index completed UTC 1-hour bars `t = 0, 1, ...` with real Binance BTC-USDT futures `C[t]`/`O[t]` for signals, `H[t]`/`L[t]` for post-hoc bracket resolution, `V[t]` for the profile and rvol gate. A bar with missing/nonfinite/non-positive OHLC, missing volume for the rvol gate, or `H < max(O,C)` / `L < min(O,C)` is invalid for signals; never interpolate. All constants are source-default literals.

- **Profile (causal, rebuilt each bar):** `hi[t]=max(H[t-99..t])`, `lo[t]=min(L[t-99..t])`, `step=(hi-lo)/30` (na-gated unless `t ≥ 100` and `step > 0`); each of the last 100 closes binned by close with `nz(volume)` split buy (`C≥O`) / sell; POC = first strictly-maximum row, `POC[t]=lo+step*(row+0.5)` (na unless `maxV>0`); `heat(p)[t]` = best of row ±1 volume share of POC row, 0 outside `[lo,hi]`.
- **Levels (causal confirmed pivots):** `ta.pivothigh/high ±3` pivots located at `t-3`, known from `t`; last two per side stored, shifted on each new confirmed pivot, consumed (→na) on any sweep-wick print on a completed bar.
- **Predicates (actionable only t ≥ 150, at bar `t`'s close):** `swL[t]` = wick-below-plus-close-back-inside a stored low AND `heat ≥ 0.25`; `swS[t]` mirrored; `pL[t]` = `C` crosses up `POC` with `C[t]>O[t]`; `pS[t]` mirrored; `volOK[t]` = `V[t]/SMA(V,20)[t] > 1.0` strict; `longSig = volOK and (swL or pL)`, `shortSig = volOK and (swS or pS)`. Pre-warmup bars flat by record rule; every miss/na/degenerate bar is an explicit neither-fire hold.
- **Latched bracket (fixed at signal bar `s`, never moved):** long `SL=(swL?L[s]:lowest(L,3)[s])-0.5·ATR14[s]`, `TP=C[s]+1.5·(C[s]-SL)`; short mirrored (levels tickSize-snapped at 0.10 — sole precision pin). Eligible from bar `s+1` onward.
- **Resolution (explicit researcher pin, sibling class):** each held bar resolves the box first-touch against `H/L` with the **stop-first** same-bar rule. Opposite signal while holding REVERSES at the next open (old pair cancelled, new pair armed); same-side signal while holding is a no-op; co-fire nets short per script order.

## Required data / Gate 6 causality

**Warmup:** minimum 150 contiguous valid completed 1h bars; covers structural first-defined index 100 (profile gate; pivots seed from bar 6, ATR ~14, vol-SMA 20) plus a 50-bar researcher margin. No warmup-bar trades; pre-warmup flat by record rule.

Binance Kline `timestamp` is bar-open `T[t]` (pinned perpetual-candles path parses row[0] open-time; `1h` is a carried `INTERVALS` entry `3600` — byte-verified at the pin). Bars become available no earlier than `T[t]+3600s`. All decisions use completed bars only (profile/pivots/heat/rvol/crosses on completed prints, `barstate.isconfirmed` identically true); fills at next-bar open; bracket touches resolve post-hoc on completed bars. No incomplete bar, future extremum, repainted graphic, MTF call, or same-bar intrabar signal. Volume comes from the SAME candle feed — no second data dependency. If this effective-time discipline cannot be enforced, Gates 5/6 fail in implementation.
