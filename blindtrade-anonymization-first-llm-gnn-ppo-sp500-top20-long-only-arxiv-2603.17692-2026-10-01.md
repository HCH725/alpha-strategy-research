## Economic mechanism

### Source-reported

The authors argue that an LLM trading agent cannot be said to "understand the market" unless memorization is excluded first, because pre-trained models carry `pre-trained preferences for technology stocks and large-cap stocks` and the backtest environment compounds this with survivorship and lookahead bias (Section 1). Their answer is an **anonymization-first** pipeline: `We map tickers to synthetic identifiers (e.g., "AAPL"-> "STOCK 0026"), and proper nouns like "Apple", "iPhone", "Tim Cook" in news are replaced using Google Knowledge Graph API. We do not claim this blocks all leakage, but it at least blocks the path where LLM sees a ticker and decides "it's Apple, so buy."` (Section 3.2).

Six stages are described (Section 3.1): (1) daily anonymization of point-in-time S&P 500 constituents; (2) four specialized LLM agents - Momentum, News-Event, Mean-Reversion, Risk-Regime - score each stock daily and emit reasoning; (3) IC validation of LLM outputs against RAW features before anything is used; (4) SemGAT encoding, a 2-layer GATv2 whose first layer sees only same-sector edges and whose second layer adds semantic edges where cosine similarity of reasoning embeddings exceeds `0.75` (top 10 neighbours per node) over an `11 GICS Sectors` prior graph; (5) a PPO policy with a Differential Sharpe Ratio reward from which `Transaction costs (10bps/turnover) are also subtracted`; (6) an out-of-sample backtest `with 10bps transaction costs`.

The policy keeps only the `top 20` masked stocks, allocates `weights only within the equity universe (no cash)`, and expresses `risk control ... via diversification and turnover`; a consensus Intent Head picks `defensive(0)/neutral(1)/aggressive(2)` from four cross-sectional global statistics plus the GNN's 64-dim market state, and an execution inertia parameter `eta` slows weight changes to cut turnover (Sections 3.6, 4.1). The authors state the intended division of labour explicitly: `The RL policy's primary role is cost-aware execution rather than signal generation - without RL, high turnover makes the strategy unprofitable.` (Section 4.3).

### Research interpretation

The falsifiable hypothesis is **not** a return premium; it is the conjunction of three separable claims, and the source's own ablations let each be named by component role:

- **Regime / posture**: consensus Intent Head decides a market posture (defensive / neutral / aggressive) from cross-sectional aggregates of the agents' outputs and the GNN market state; turnover by posture is reported as defensive `2.9%/day`, neutral `1.8%/day`, aggressive `0.4%/day`.
- **Primary signal**: four heterogeneous LLM agents read anonymized price-volume indicators and up to 5 anonymized headlines per stock over a strict `t-60 to t-1` window, emit bounded scores plus free-text reasoning; reasoning is embedded to 384 dims with `all-MiniLM-L6-v2`, concatenated with 7 numeric scores and 3 categorical states into a 394-dim node feature, and passed through SemGAT to a 128-dim embedding that predicts a 101-bin HL-Gauss next-day return distribution with a pairwise ranking term.
- **Confirmation filter**: IC screening - only features that stay informative on holdout (`positive IC or positive dIC`) are retained, with the stated caveat that `a positive dIC can reflect either (i) additional predictive signal, or (ii) removal of misleading inverse correlation`.
- **Risk / exit**: there is **none in the usual sense** - no cash leg, no stop, no take-profit, no market-timing exit. Risk is expressed only through diversification inside a top-20 long-only book and through the turnover penalty; the source states `there is no mechanism to move to cash or reduce risk during market crashes`.
- **Execution**: PPO-DSR with a 10 bps per unit turnover charge inside the reward and a `rebalance eta` smoothing step.

Three candidate channels could produce the reported edge and the source does not discriminate between them: (a) genuine cross-sectional signal from heterogeneous LLM reading of price, volume, news and risk; (b) inter-stock structure learned from reasoning-embedding similarity, which the no-GNN ablation (Sharpe `1.40 -> 0.62 +/- 0.50`) suggests carries more of the effect than the LLM features do (`1.40 -> 1.14 +/- 0.02`); (c) **cost control as the whole story** - the source's own RL-versus-Top-K comparison shows an identical GNN score ranking returning Sharpe `-1.17` at `139%/day` turnover and `1.08 +/- 0.31` at `1.7%/day`, which means the sign of net performance is set by turnover management rather than by forecast quality. Channel (c) is the only one the paper demonstrates causally, and it implies that the forecast only has to clear a very low bar once turnover is suppressed.

Not established by the source: any behavioural or risk-premium explanation; any microstructure identification; whether anonymization contributes anything at all (Appendix A.1 states no anonymization ablation was run); and any claim that the effect persists outside a 145-day or 397-day window.

## Signal

**Fully specified parts (source-reported).**

- **Formation timestamp / knowledge cutoff**: each agent receives prompt text enforcing `CRITICAL KNOWLEDGE LIMITATION` / `Your knowledge is strictly limited to information available up to {cutoff_date}`, with `The cutoff date is set to t-1 (one day before the prediction target)`; news is restricted to headlines from `t-60 to t-1` (Appendix C.1, Section 3.3). Timezone, session calendar and clock are **not stated** (data gap).
- **Lookback**: `only 60 business days of data prior to time t (from t-60 to t-1)`; `we limit input to 5 headlines per stock`; each agent `processes stocks in batches of 15 for cross-sectional context`; agents are run daily. Warm-up and endpoint inclusivity are not stated (underspecified).
- **Feature selection**: Spearman rank IC against returns `21 days later` (`h=21`) screens features; `Features evaluated at h=21 were verified to show positive (though weaker) IC at shorter horizons (h=1, h=5)`.
- **Graph**: sector edges fully connect the 11 GICS sectors; semantic edges added at cosine similarity `> 0.75`, `top 10 neighbors per node`; GATv2 `2 layers with 4 attention heads`, first layer sector-only, second layer full augmented graph.
- **Policy / sizing**: PPO with Dirichlet-distributed weights over the masked top-20 (`Top-K masking ... keep only the top 20`), no cash, so weight = capital fraction and the book is always fully invested. PPO `Learning rate 3e-4, gamma 0.99, GAE lambda 0.95, clip epsilon 0.2`; Optuna-tuned `reward cost scale (c) = 0.358, dirichlet alpha0 = 466.8` (main protocol), `eta = 0.08, cost scale = 0.40` (extended protocol). Reward `A_t = r_t - eta * SR_{t-1}` per Figure 2, described in text as Differential Sharpe Ratio minus the 10 bps turnover charge.
- **Entry / exit / holding period**: not stated as such. The source describes a daily scoring loop, an inertia-smoothed weight update and dictionary-based turnover that reflects S&P 500 additions and deletions; it never states the execution price, the rebalance clock, whether positions are closed or held across days, or the holding-period distribution (underspecified; see Execution assumptions).
- **Benchmarks**: passive SPY and EQWL; active `Momentum (top 20 by trailing 12-month returns, rebalanced monthly)`, `MCap Top-20`, `RAW Top-20 (top 20 by momentum-minus-volatility score using only technical indicators)`.
- **Metric**: `annualized Sharpe ratio as mean_d / sd_d * sqrt(252)`; volatility `sd_d * sqrt(252)`; `AnnRet` is **not defined anywhere** (underspecified - see Evidence).

**Research-proposed operationalisation (NOT source-reported; required only to make the rule testable, and labelled research-proposed here and nowhere presented as the paper's rule):** signal computed after day `t-1` close; orders sent at day `t` open; one rebalance per trading day; weights held until the next rebalance; turnover computed one-way as half the sum of absolute weight changes; no intraperiod trading; no cash; no shorting.

**Reconstructibility verdict:** the *signal* side is reconstructible in principle (the four system prompts are reproduced verbatim in Appendix C and the hyperparameters are listed); the *trading* side is **not** - execution price, rebalance clock, fill model, news vendor, OHLCV vendor, macro-context source and the score-to-weight timing are all unprinted, and **no code or data is released** (`repository` 0, `github` 0, `source code` 0, `data availability` 0 occurrences; the Reproducibility Statement says only that the authors `plan to release the LLM input/output datasets ... upon publication`).

## Falsification plan

All thresholds below are **research-defined falsification thresholds** unless explicitly attributed to the source; none is a source claim. Each gate has a failure condition and a fixed action. **No-retuning rule:** once gates F2-F13 are opened, no parameter, window, prompt, embedding model, hyperparameter, cost assumption or gate threshold may be changed to rescue a failure; a failed gate downgrades the record to untradable research and must not be re-tested with adjusted thresholds on the same data.

- **F1 - Printed-value reproduction (blocked on release).** If and only if the authors release code plus the LLM input/output dataset at an immutable commit, regenerate Tables 1, 2, 3, 8 and 9 from it. **Threshold:** any Table 1/3/8/9 cell differs by more than `0.01` Sharpe or `0.05` percentage points on a return, or any Table 2 mean differs by more than `0.02` Sharpe / `0.20` pp. **Action:** reject the entire evidence section.
- **F2 - Anonymization ablation (the source's own missing test).** Run the identical pipeline twice, once with `STOCK_XXXX` identifiers and once with raw tickers and company names, same seeds, same splits. **Threshold:** `|Sharpe_raw - Sharpe_anon| < 0.15`, i.e. anonymization changes nothing. **Action:** strike the paper's central claim and reclassify the system as an ordinary LLM feature stack; keep only the cost-aware-RL finding.
- **F3 - Feature-timestamp leakage gate.** Regenerate every agent feature with a model snapshot frozen strictly before the first evaluation date (or with a locally hosted pre-2020-cutoff model), keeping prompts, windows and seeds fixed. **Threshold:** out-of-sample net Sharpe `< 0.70` (half of the printed `1.40`). **Action:** treat `1.40` as parametric-knowledge contamination rather than market signal.
- **F4 - Negative-control strengthening.** Repeat the cross-sectional score shuffle `1,000` times instead of once, on the full system rather than the Top-K diagnostic. **Threshold:** the observed `|RankIC| 0.015` does not exceed the 95th percentile of the shuffled distribution, or the real net Sharpe does not exceed the shuffled median by `>= 0.50`. **Action:** mark the signal layer as noise and attribute any net performance to the turnover controller alone.
- **F5 - Frozen forward window.** Evaluate on `2026-10-01` to `2027-09-30` with no retraining, no re-tuning and the printed `eta`, `c`, `alpha0` frozen. **Threshold:** net Sharpe `< 0.50`, or net Sharpe `<= ` the SPY Sharpe over the same window. **Action:** mark the mechanism non-stationary.
- **F6 - Benchmark-consistency gate (currently FAILING).** Recompute SPY over `2025-01-02` to `2025-08-01` once and publish one canonical row. **Threshold:** Table 2 and Table 9 SPY CumRet still differ by more than `0.05` pp, or Table 8 SPY CumRet still differs from the sub-period product by more than `0.20` pp. **Action:** withdraw every benchmark-relative claim in the paper until reconciled; re-derive all relative numbers from one series.
- **F7 - Nested-drawdown consistency gate (currently FAILING).** For every seed, check `|MDD_full| >= |MDD_nested|` for all nested windows. **Threshold:** any seed violates the inequality (the printed means already do). **Action:** recompute and republish all drawdown cells; treat Table 8/9 risk numbers as unreliable.
- **F8 - Cost ladder.** Charge `0 / 1 / 5 / 10 / 20 / 50` bps per unit turnover and add an explicit per-side commission ladder `0 / 0.5 / 1 / 2` bps plus a quoted-spread term. **Threshold:** net Sharpe `< 0.50` at `2x` the source ladder (20 bps), or net Sharpe `<= 0` once a spread term is added. **Action:** reject as non-tradable.
- **F9 - Turnover disclosure and materiality gate.** Publish the headline configuration's daily turnover and annualise the source's own charge against it. **Threshold:** turnover not reported, or the charge at a realistic 20 bps ladder exceeds `2.0%/yr` while net Sharpe is unchanged by less than `0.20` (i.e. the cost model is doing no work). **Action:** keep cost materiality as `data gap` and re-run F8 before any performance claim is cited.
- **F10 - Executability gate.** Next-day-open fills, one-way turnover convention, a `5%` per-day participation cap on the top-20 book, no same-day fills. **Threshold:** net Sharpe falls by more than `0.40` from `1.40`. **Action:** mark the execution assumptions as not realizable.
- **F11 - Multiplicity gate.** Apply Benjamini-Hochberg at `q < 0.10` over the family `20 seeds x 3 ablations x 3 GNN objectives x 2 protocols` plus the Optuna search, and compute a deflated Sharpe for the backtest configuration search. **Threshold:** fewer than half of the claimed deltas (`-0.26`, `-0.78`, `+0.76`, `+0.37`, `+0.16`) survive. **Action:** downgrade all superiority wording to uncorrected exploratory results.

## Crypto portability

**`adapted`** - the mechanism is universe-agnostic in construction but has **zero crypto evidence**; the sign of the effect in crypto is `unproven`.

- **What ports mechanically**: ticker/entity anonymization, four heterogeneous LLM scorers with a strict cutoff, IC gating of features, a reasoning-embedding graph over a sector/industry prior, and a PPO policy whose reward nets out turnover cost are all instrument-agnostic. A crypto port would swap the point-in-time S&P 500 membership screen for a point-in-time listing screen and the 11 GICS sectors for an industry taxonomy or co-movement clustering.
- **What does not port**: every traded number in the source is a US cash-equity long-only top-20 book. The pinned text contains `crypto` once, inside a Related-Work sentence about third-party tests, and `bitcoin` 0 / `perpetual` 0 occurrences. There is no crypto backtest, no crypto forecast table and no crypto universe rule anywhere.
- **Crypto-specific friction that must be rebuilt rather than inherited**: taker/maker fee schedules and any maker rebate, quoted spread and depth at the intended participation, 24/7 session and candle boundaries versus the equity session calendar assumed by `daily` scoring and by the `t-60 to t-1` business-day window, venue fragmentation and per-venue listing/delisting churn (far higher turnover in the universe itself, which changes the dictionary-based turnover calculation materially), and - for any perpetual-based implementation - funding carry, mark-versus-index divergence, liquidation mechanics, leverage and stablecoin numeraire, all of which are `data gap` in the source.
- **Anonymization is harder in crypto**: tickers are the asset. Anonymizing `BTC`/`ETH` removes most of the signal an agent could use, and the source's limitation 1 means no test exists for whether anonymization helps or hurts even where it is easy.
- Crypto portability is **not** authorization to trade; it states only that the mechanism can be evaluated in crypto with new data, a new universe rule and a new cost model.
