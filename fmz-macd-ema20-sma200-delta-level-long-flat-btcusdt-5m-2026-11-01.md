## Economic mechanism

### Source-reported

Conservative long-only trend tracking: the 200-bar average decides the regime (close above = uptrend, close below = downtrend); in the uptrend leg the MACD-delta sign alone gates entries (delta>0 holds the long sleeve); in the downtrend leg a second price gate joins (delta>0 AND close above the 20-bar average — dual confirmation); the mirror-image delta<0 conjunction closes the sleeve. The prose frames the uptrend leg as aggressive and the downtrend leg as conservative dual confirmation, admits MACD lag, dual-confirmation misses, indicator dependence, event-risk blindness and stop-placement sensitivity, and suggests KDJ/Bollinger extensions (all unported future work — disclosed). The prose names 200-day/20-day averages while the stanza and code ship 5m bars (contradiction recorded; bars govern), and promises golden-cross/dead-cross edges while the code ships strict level tests with zero cross calls (contradiction recorded; levels govern). The stanza window 2023-10-15→2023-10-22 is a seven-day 5m sample — cited as provenance, never as performance evidence.

### Research interpretation

Regime-split delta-sign level holder, event-persistent, long-flat. Unlike cross-event MACD machines (`macd-signal-cross-reversal` — MACD/signal cross edges with reverse-cross exits, 1d; `fmz-483047-ma-ribbon-macd-dual-cross` — ribbon plus dual-cross edges, 1h; `freqtrade-universalmacd`/`freqtrade-awesome-macd` — cross-plus-band machines, 5m/1h), this machine never computes a cross — entries and exits are sign-holding LEVELS that stay true across consecutive bars (first such bar trades while flat; the position then blocks the rest), and the exit is the mirror sign level rather than a reverse cross. Unlike band-gated MACD cousins (`fmz-442643-nabz-bbmacd-ema20-gated-close` — Bollinger plus MACD plus EMA20, 1h), there is no band object anywhere here: the only gates are the SMA200 regime split, the EMA20 second leg and the delta sign itself. Unlike single-average reclaim records (`fmz-bollinger-ema20-sma200-reclaim-close` — band reclaim with EMA20/SMA200 rails, two-sided, 1m), this machine has no band, no short sleeve and no reclaim edge: direction comes from the delta sign, the averages only split and gate it, and the posture is long-flat on 5m. Five-axis distinction from the nearest pool cousins holds on mechanism (regime-split sign-level hold vs cross-edge events vs band-reclaim edges), signal construction (manual EMA12/26/9 delta with SMA200+EMA20 if-expression gates vs macd-triple cross vs BB-gated MACD), horizon (5m source-native vs 1h/1d), exits (mirror-sign id-specific close with no cross/box vs reverse-cross or band exits), and source identity (FMZ 429951 MACD/EMA-Long vs NABZ/ribbon/freqtrade lineages).
