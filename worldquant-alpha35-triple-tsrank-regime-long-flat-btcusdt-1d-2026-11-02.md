## Economic mechanism

### Source-reported

Coarse mean-reversion/momentum building blocks. The paper frames its universe coarsely: `one can think of alpha signals as based on mean-reversion or momentum`, where `a mean-reversion alpha has a sign opposite to the return on which it is based`. Alpha#35 is a triple time-rank regime product: high trailing volume-rank keeps the score up, while a trailing-max print in the close-plus-range level or in close-to-close returns zeroes it. The paper prints no behavioural mechanism for #35 itself and ships no stop, target, or risk leg of any kind.

### Research interpretation

Exhaustion-gated participation, long-flat overall. The three legs read as: `a[t] = ts_rank(volume, 32)` — volume participation regime (near `1` on the heaviest-volume day of the trailing 32, near `1/32` on the lightest, `0.5`-ish on ties); `b[t] = ts_rank(close + high - low, 16)` — close-plus-range level regime (`close + (high - low)`, i.e. close marked up by the daily range; near `1` when the level prints a trailing-16-bar top); `c[t] = ts_rank(returns, 32)` — return regime (near `1` on the strongest close-to-close gain of the trailing 32). The score `a·(1-b)·(1-c)` stays long while volume participates without exhaustion, and flattens exactly when the level or the daily gain prints a unique trailing top (`(1-b) = 0` iff the level is the unique 16-bar max; `(1-c) = 0` iff the gain is the unique 32-bar max — both under the pinned average-tie rank, so tied tops do NOT flatten). Unlike sibling worldquant-alpha26 (which correlates two 5-bar rank trajectories inside a trailing-max envelope — an agreement object), this rule never correlates anything: it multiplies three independent regime prints. Unlike sibling worldquant-alpha7 (a volume-gated time-rank contrarian that shorts by default), this rule never shorts and never negates: the product is always nonnegative. Unlike sibling worldquant-alpha12 (a 1-bar volume-sign × price-change product with no window), every leg here is a 16/32-bar windowed rank with nothing but the current print's position inside its own past. The failure mode is plain: the book is long ~90% of bars (2,313 of 2,572 post-warmup live bars) with no scaling and no guardrail, and exits arrive only as exact-zero exhaustion prints — disclosed, not upgraded. Live-window behaviour (2,612 completed 1d bars 2019-09-08→2026-11-01) prints zero negative scores and 259 exact-zero flats driving 195 entries / 194 exits ending LONG — the single-sided construction verified empirically, not assumed.

## Signal

Exact rule as pinned (any other series, window, operator, fill, frame, book, or tie treatment is a different, unpinned rule):

- Inputs (paper A.2): `high[t]`, `low[t]`, `volume[t]`, `close[t]` on completed `1d` bars only, plus `returns[t] = close[t] / close[t-1] - 1` (paper `daily close-to-close returns`; `returns[0]` NaN by definition). No `open`, no vwap/adv/market-cap/sector, no cross-sectional rank, no second asset or timeframe is read by any order-gating line.
- Level leg (paper Appendix A, pinned impl lines 379–384): `lvl[t] = close[t] + high[t] - low[t]` — close marked up by the daily range, computed bar-locally with no window.
- Regime prints (paper A.1 `ts_rank(x, d)`, pinned impl `ts_rank`/`_rank_1d`/`_rolling`): `a[t] = ts_rank(volume, 32)[t]`, `b[t] = ts_rank(lvl, 16)[t]`, `c[t] = ts_rank(returns, 32)[t]` — each the average-tie rank of the last print inside its trailing window normalized by the window length, NaN unless the full window is finite (pinned all-finite-block rule, never interpolated).
- Score: `score[t] = a[t] × (1 - b[t]) × (1 - c[t])`, defined (finite) only when all three prints are finite — first possible at the 33rd completed bar (`returns[0]` NaN pushes `ts_rank(returns, 32)` to index 32). The score lives in `[0, 1)` and can never print negative by construction.
- Direction mapping (source-grounded, fenced in Negative evidence 1): `score[t] > 0` → long signal; `score[t] == 0` → flat signal (fires exactly on unique-trailing-top exhaustion prints — 259 such bars across the full 2,612-bar live window, disclosed); short is explicitly disabled (no score can map to it). The entry fills at the next bar's open, one single unit, only if the book is flat (see position rule).
- Exit: the first exact-zero print flattens the full unit at the next-bar open — long-or-flat is the sole book by construction. No stop-loss, no take-profit, no trailing, no time exit (the paper ships no risk leg — carried over exactly, disclosed in Provenance adaptation (4), never upgraded into a source claim).
- Direction: long-flat overall (participation longs; exhaustion-print flats; never short).
- Position rule (predeclared researcher pin): single-unit long-flat; an already-long book treats further long-signal bars as no-ops (no pyramiding, no averaging); flat-regime bars while already flat are no-ops. Re-entry re-arms on the next long signal after a flat exit (no cooldown specified — explicitly none, not invented). Pre-first-signal, pre-warmup, and any NaN-score bars are flat by record rule.
