## Economic mechanism

### Source-reported

- The paper's object is a **four-layer gold timing system**, each layer independently switchable so contributions can be isolated (Section 3.2): **Layer 1** golden cross trend filter (MA50 > MA200 eligibility, death cross forces exit), **Layer 2** a 4-component Gaussian Mixture Model over 7 macro variables (real rate, VIX, yield curve, high-yield spread, CPI, dollar momentum, gold volatility) retraded every 63 trading days on expanding windows, **Layer 3** a four-model supervised ensemble (probability-weighted regime-specific Blend Ridge and Blend GBM, plus global Augmented Ridge and Augmented GBM with regime probabilities as features) whose simple average is the production signal `REGIME_ENS`, **Layer 4** volatility-targeted sizing (inverse to 60-day realised gold volatility, 15% annualised target, 95% cap, 20% floor, 5-day cooldown, 10 bp per-leg transaction costs).
- **Stated mechanism of the edge (Section 8, Abstract)**: "the system's alpha is structural (trend following) rather than predictive (ML forecasting)" — the golden cross keeps the system in gold during trending regimes and out during the 2012-2015 bear, while the ML layer contributes **noisy profit-taking through exit timing** rather than directional accuracy (Sections 4.2, 7.2).
- **Why the forecast supposedly helps at all (Sections 4.2-4.3, 7.2)**: the exit rule (`REGIME_ENS < -1.5%` exits, `> 0%` permits re-entry) fires during extended profitable positions, producing an **exit asymmetry** — 22 forecast-driven exits average **+10.4%** with a 68% win rate versus 8 death-cross exits averaging **-3.9%** with a 12% win rate (Table 3) — which the source itself then shows is not statistically distinguishable from random timing (Section 4.5).
- **The look-ahead story (Sections 5.1-5.4)**: the target `Fwd_1M(t) = Gold(t+21)/Gold(t) - 1` means that in an uncorrected expanding walk-forward the last H training rows reference prices **at or beyond the prediction date**; because those rows are the most recent and most informative, they create "a spurious contemporaneous signal that does not exist in real time". A **21-day training embargo** plus a **3-business-day CFTC COT release lag** (Tuesday positions published Friday) restores honest performance; "less than 2% of training data was leaked, but it produced 65% of the apparent forecast signal" (Figure 2 caption) and 10% of the apparent Sharpe.
- **Why the trend filter dominates (Sections 4.1, 7.1)**: the golden cross is "conventional and unoptimised", fixed before any OOS analysis, active ~66% of OOS days, and requires no data beyond gold prices; its contribution is "primarily defensive" (drawdown compression), consistent with Zhu and Zhou (2009) on moving-average timing and with trend-following attribution in Clare et al. (2016).
- **Macro rationale for the regime layer (Section 2.2)**: gold's dependence on real rates and inflation expectations (Erb and Harvey, 2013) and regime-dependent safe-haven behaviour (Baur and Lucey, 2010) motivate conditioning on macro states; GMM is chosen over HMM because it imposes no Markov transition structure — and, the source concedes, "given the forecast's ultimate insignificance, the regime model choice is likely immaterial".

### Research interpretation

- The falsifiable core we would test is **not** the ML layer: it is the hypothesis that **a pre-specified, unoptimised MA50/MA200 filter on a single trending asset captures most of the risk-adjusted improvement over buy-and-hold, and that anything layered on top adds an amount that is statistically indistinguishable from zero**. That is a trend-persistence + drawdown-avoidance mechanism with an explicit anti-complexity prior, and it is directly testable (see F3).
- The ML layer's "contribution" is best read as **regime-conditional profit-taking**, not prediction: the forecast IC is insignificant (0.083, p = 0.255) while Sharpe rises 0.70 → 0.91, and the source's own null models place that increment between p = 0.01 and p = 0.26 depending on how tightly the null's entries are constrained. The honest interpretation is a **range**, not a point.
- The label-leak result is a **methodology mechanism** with scope beyond gold: any walk-forward system whose forecast horizon H exceeds the distance between the last training row and the prediction date leaks, and tree ensembles leak harder (GBM IC degradation 81-105% vs Ridge 42-62%, Section 5.4). We record it as a reproducible falsifiable claim (F4), not as established fact.
- Cheaper readings the source does not eliminate: (a) the whole result could be **one lucky trade** (the May 2024-January 2026 position is +118.1% and 60% of PnL; excluding it takes Sharpe 0.91 → 0.24); (b) the second-half performance is inseparable from the 2024-2026 gold rally; (c) the system was developed iteratively **on the same OOS period it reports** (Section 7.4, sixth limitation), so the DSR at 30+ trials already fails in the source's own calculation.

## Signal

Everything in this section is **source-reported unless marked research-proposed** or `data gap` / `underspecified`.

- **Formation timestamp**: daily bars; the four layers re-evaluate on each trading day; models retrain every **63 trading days** on expanding windows from **2004** onward; prediction day `t` uses only backward-looking features (all 15 forecast features and 7 regime features, Appendix A Table A1). **Clock time of the daily bar and timezone of the XAU/USD close are `not stated in source`** (Investing.com daily bars) → `underspecified`.
- **Lookback**: Layer 1 MA50 / MA200 (explicitly "conventional industry standards, not parameters optimised for this study", fixed before OOS analysis). Layer 4 realised-volatility window **60 trading days**. Layer 2 GMM on 7 macro variables with **63-day refits**, regime labels anchored by sorting on real-rate centroids. Features in Table A1: 21-day and 63-day gold returns, 20/60-day volatility ratio, 50/200-day MA ratio, 20/60-day ATR ratio, 20-day mean range, 52-week-z-scored COT money-manager net, gold-minus-silver COT spread, top-4 concentration COT net, 21-day dollar return, plus FRED levels (DFII10, VIXCLS, 10Y-2Y, T10YIE, DFF, CPI_YoY forward-filled, DTWEXBGS 63-day return, BAMLH0A0HYM2, Gold_Vol_60). Target `Fwd_1M = Gold(t+21)/Gold(t) - 1`.
- **Long entry / eligibility**: "The golden cross (MA50 > MA200) determines position eligibility" and "A death cross (MA50 < MA200) forces exit regardless of all other signals" (Section 3.2). Entries occur at golden-cross activations or as re-entries after forecast-driven exits (Section 4.5: "the system enters at golden cross activations or re-enters after forecast-driven exits"). **Exact entry price/session is `underspecified`.**
- **Exit rules (two kinds, source-reported)**: (1) **forecast-driven exit** when `REGIME_ENS < -1.5%`, with re-entry permitted when the forecast rises **above 0%**; (2) **death cross** full exit regardless of other signals. The -1.5% threshold was "selected during development based on qualitative reasoning (roughly one standard deviation of monthly gold returns), not optimised on OOS performance" (Section 4.7). A **5-day cooldown** prevents whipsaw re-entries after forecast-driven exits.
- **Holding period / overlap**: single position in one asset; **30 trades over 16.1 years**, each with exactly one exit (22 forecast-driven, 8 death cross); average holding 65 days (forecast exits) and 95 days (death-cross exits) per Table 3; over the full OOS the filter is active ~66% of days, exposure 54.6% for the production configuration (Table 1).
- **Position sizing**: allocation scales inversely with 60-day realised gold volatility targeting **15% annualised portfolio volatility**, **capped at 95%, floored at 20%**.
- **Leakage controls (the paper's own correction)**: predict on day `t` with training stopped at `t-21` (Section 3.3) — or `t-H-1 = t-22` (Section 5.2), the two sections disagree by one day (see `contradictions`); plus a **3-business-day lag on all COT dates** before merging.
- **Parameters and their source**: MA50/MA200 (conventional, fixed pre-OOS); GMM components = 4; refit = 63 days; forecast horizon = 21 trading days; exit threshold = -1.5% (development-stage qualitative choice); re-entry = forecast > 0%; cooldown = 5 days; vol target = 15% (cap 95%, floor 20%); cost = 10 bp per leg; embargo = 21/22 days; COT lag = 3 business days. All are **fixed by the source**; none is fitted by this record. Whether -1.5% survived multiple comparisons during development is `not stated in source` beyond the DSR discussion (Section 4.4: at 15-20 trials DSR p = 0.96-0.97, at 30 trials 0.94, at 50 trials 0.91 — i.e. it fails at 30+).
- **Reconstruction gaps** (so the signal is **not** presented as reproducible): the traded instrument (spot XAU/USD vs a gold ETF vs futures — the returns are computed on Investing.com XAU/USD while the cost assumption is justified by a GLD remark); the daily bar's timestamp/timezone and weekend/holiday cash treatment (Section 4.4 says the daily analysis "includes non-trading days where the system earns the risk-free rate", but how those days are constructed is not stated); the exact entry/exit price within the bar; whether the re-entry rule requires forecast > 0% **and** still-golden-cross (implied but not formalised); the GMM convergence/seed handling beyond the reported seed table; treatment of missing FRED/COT dates; and the 21-vs-22-day embargo width.

## Execution assumptions

- **Scout assumptions (research-proposed, explicitly not the source's)**: enter at the daily close on the bar after the signal, exit at the next daily close, plus a commission ladder (research-proposed: 0 / 1 / 2.5 / 5 bp per leg), an explicit spread-crossing add-on (research-proposed: half-spread per side), and a next-bar-fill penalty of one day's adverse move on 25% of trades. Any number produced with these is a **research-proposed** re-computation, not a source-reported result.

### Negative evidence

1. **The forecast itself is statistically insignificant**: IC 0.083, p = 0.255, directional accuracy 56.0%, R² -0.236 on N = 191 non-overlapping observations, and **no model in Table 2 reaches significance** (one is worse than random) — the ML layer's stated input signal does not predict 21-day gold returns (Table 2).
2. **The ML increment is not statistically significant**: Ledoit-Wolf z = 1.27, **p = 0.20** for the 0.17 Sharpe difference, and the daily CIs [0.42, 1.40] vs [0.25, 1.23] overlap heavily (Section 4.4, Table 3b).
3. **Random-timing null**: under the uniform null the production system sits at the **74.1st percentile (p ≈ 0.26)** of 10,000 random-exit simulations whose mean Sharpe (0.726) is close to the golden-cross baseline — i.e. most performance comes from "being in gold during golden cross periods, not from the specific timing of entries and exits" (Table 3c). The entry-constrained nulls do favour the system (p = 0.01-0.02), but the source itself says "the truth likely lies between these bounds".
4. **Deflated Sharpe fails at 30+ trials** (DSR 0.94 at 30, 0.91 at 50, threshold 0.95), and the development process is admitted to be iterative on the same OOS window (Sections 4.4, 7.4) — an explicit self-reported multiple-comparisons problem.
5. **Extreme trade concentration**: top 3 trades = 82% of PnL, the single largest = 60%; excluding it drops Sharpe **0.91 → 0.24** and widens MaxDD to **-53%** (Table 3g).
6. **First-half Sharpe is 0.44** (2010-2017) vs 0.91 full period, and the source explicitly advises evaluating viability "against the first-half Sharpe of 0.44 rather than the full-period figure" (Sections 4.6, 4.9).
7. **A one-parameter 8% trailing stop captures 76% of the Sharpe (0.69 vs 0.91)** with no ML infrastructure; all fixed stops land in 0.683-0.692 (Table 3f) — the complexity premium is 0.22 Sharpe and is itself unproven.
8. **Vol targeting contributes +0.001 Sharpe** (0.698 → 0.699) and is flat across targets (CV 0.017) — a whole layer is inert (Tables 1, 6).
9. **The look-ahead problem was real and material in the source's own system**: uncorrected Sharpe 1.014 / IC 0.234 / p 0.001 collapsed to 0.910 / 0.083 / p 0.255; <2% of training rows produced 65% of the apparent signal (Table 4, Figure 2). ## Falsification

Every threshold below is **research-defined** by this record; none is a source claim. Data assumed for all tests: daily XAU/USD (or a named tradable gold instrument, which must itself be fixed — research-proposed), the FRED series of Appendix A Table A1, CFTC gold COT (088691), all point-in-time (ALFRED vintages, COT as published), plus a forward window after 2026-02-10.

- **F1 — Frozen forward window (walk-forward).** Run the frozen production configuration (MA50/MA200, 4-component GMM, four-model ensemble, -1.5% exit, 0% re-entry, 5-day cooldown, 15% vol target, 10 bp per leg) on data **after 2026-02-10 for ≥12 months with zero re-tuning**. **Fail** if forward net Sharpe < **0.30**, or if it does not beat buy-and-hold gold on net Sharpe over the same window.
- **F2 — All-in cost ladder (stress).** Recompute with 0 / 1 / 5 / 10 / 20 / 30 bp per leg **plus** a research-proposed commission (1 bp) and half-spread crossing on a named tradable instrument. **Fail** if the production net Sharpe falls below **0.50** at the source's own 10 bp + commission, or flips sign at 20 bp per leg.
- **F3 — Decisive layer-ablation test.** On the forward window, compare four pre-registered arms: buy-and-hold; golden-cross only; golden-cross + vol targeting; full production. **Fail the paper's structural-alpha claim** if golden-cross-only does not deliver at least half of the full system's risk-adjusted improvement over buy-and-hold (research-defined: Sharpe increment ratio < 0.5); **fail the ML-layer claim** if production minus (golden-cross + vol targeting) ≤ 0 with a Ledoit-Wolf/Newey-West p ≥ 0.05 — the source's own p = 0.20 already sits on this line.
- **F4 — Label-leak audit replication (mechanism test).** Re-run the pipeline three ways: (a) no embargo, (b) 21-day embargo, (c) 22-day embargo (the source's two conflicting widths), each with and without the 3-day COT lag, recording non-overlapping IC and Sharpe. **Fail the leak claim** if the no-embargo IC is not at least **1.5×** the embargoed IC (research-defined), or if the embargo changes IC by < 0.02 in absolute terms; report which width reproduces Table 4 (IC 0.234 → 0.083, Sharpe 1.014 → 0.910) — if neither does, mark the correction `unverifiable`.
- **F5 — Entry-constrained random-exit null, pre-registered.** Rebuild the Section 4.5 null with the entry pool fixed **before** looking at results (research-proposed: entries only within 10 trading days of a golden-cross activation or immediately after a forecast exit). **Fail the ML-timing claim** if the production system does not exceed the **95th percentile** of the null distribution (the source spans p = 0.01-0.26 depending on pool choice; the claim must survive one pre-declared pool).
- **F6 — Circular-shift permutation null.** Shift the `REGIME_ENS` forecast series circularly (≥1,000 draws, ≥21-day blocks) and re-run the exit rule inside the golden-cross regime. **Fail** if the observed production-minus-null Sharpe does not exceed the **95th percentile** of the shifted distribution (research-defined).
- **F7 — Threshold pre-registration.** Fix the exit threshold at -1.5% ex ante and sweep -0.5% to -5.0% only as a robustness report. **Fail** if the forward-window Sharpe at the pre-registered threshold is < 50% of the best swept threshold's Sharpe (research-defined), or if the sign of the ML increment flips anywhere inside the swept range.

## Crypto portability

**`adapted`** — mechanically portable in part, empirically unproven, and only Layers 1 and 4 port at all.

- **Zero crypto evidence in the source**: the pinned PDF contains no crypto instrument, venue or data (universe is XAU/USD spot plus FRED and CFTC).
- **What ports**: the golden-cross trend filter, the volatility-targeting layer, the cooldown and the 21-day-label-leak discipline are all instrument-agnostic rules; they apply to BTC/ETH series by substitution.
- **What does not port**: Layers 2-3 are built on **US rates, CPI, high-yield spreads, the trade-weighted dollar and CFTC gold positioning** — there is no crypto counterpart for DFII10, T10YIE, BAMLH0A0HYM2, DTWEXBGS or COT 088691. Replacing them with funding rates, open interest or on-chain flows would be a **different hypothesis**, not a port of the source's system.
- **Market-structure differences**: XAU/USD is a ~24/5 macro-driven spot market; crypto is 24/7 with perpetual funding, mark/index price divergence, exchange fragmentation, venue-specific candle boundaries and far larger gap risk around the death-cross/entry logic. The source's 10 bp cost rung maps to taker fees only — crypto round-trip costs, funding on any leveraged long, and liquidation mechanics are absent from the source (`data gap`).
- Conclusion: **`adapted`**, not `direct` (the source has no crypto evidence) and not `not applicable`; performance in crypto is **unproven** and would require F13. Portability is not authorization to trade.

## Limitations

- **Data**: single-vendor daily XAU/USD (Investing.com) with unstated cleaning/session rules; FRED latest-vintage rather than point-in-time (self-disclosed); COT features depend on a 3-day publication lag corrected post hoc; weekend/holiday return construction unstated (`data gap`).
- **Identification**: the source itself cannot separate ML timing from regime membership (null model p = 0.01-0.26 range); layer interactions are acknowledged (Section 7.4, fifth limitation); the label-leak correction width is contradictory (21 vs 22 days).
- **Sample/regime**: 16.1 years of OOS dominated by the 2024-2026 gold rally; ex-largest-trade Sharpe 0.24; first-half Sharpe 0.44; single asset, single country's macro inputs, no cross-asset replication.
- **Execution**: no named tradable instrument, no order type, no fill/latency model, no turnover, no capacity, no commissions/impact; "net" = 10 bp per leg only.
- **Model/statistics**: 30 trades, wide CIs, 34% power for Sharpe 0.5, DSR failing at 30+ trials, no multiplicity correction across the paper's own sweeps, iterative development on the reported OOS window.
- **Source quality**: unrefereed SSRN preprint by unaffiliated independent researchers, zero citations, no code, date-field contradictions, one off-by-one in the headline correction — presentation-level reliability is not high.
- **Publication bias**: a self-critical paper still reports a positive headline (0.91 vs 0.66 buy-and-hold); the counterfactual (a null result that never gets written) is unobservable; no pre-registration or analysis plan is `not stated in source`.
- **Reproducibility**: without code, exact replication of Tables 1-6 is blocked by the embargo-width ambiguity, the unnamed instrument, the unstated bar timestamp and the GMM seed/convergence details (see F4, F12).
- **Incremental-write check**: this record was written because the repository contained **no record of this source, no XAU/USD golden-cross timing system, and no training-label-leak (H-day embargo) mechanism** (pre-write identity search above); it is not ordinary repeated indicator content.
