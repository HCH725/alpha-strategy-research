## Economic mechanism

**Source-reported:** The indicator highlights statistically unusual *estimated* directional bar-volume imbalance. It does not establish genuine aggressor-side volume, order-book flow, entry rules or an edge.

**Research hypothesis only:** A new positive close-location delta anomaly, unusually high volume and EMA50>EMA200 may mark directional participation in an existing uptrend. Exit on opposite anomaly, trend loss, adverse close or finite holding horizon. This is **untested**, not a claim of expectancy or signal fidelity to the source's 1–4-star alerts.

## Signal

1. **Market and data:** One chronologically sorted, **real** Binance `BTC-USDT` spot UTC `1h` OHLCV feed. Raw `T[t]` is exchange candle **opening epoch seconds**. Require exact 3600-second UTC-aligned consecutive timestamps, no duplicates or gaps, finite positive O/H/L/C, `L<=min(O,C)<=max(O,C)<=H`, finite base-asset `V>=0`, and actual observed prices. A candle is complete/available only at `E[t]=T[t]+3600`. Reject missing/incomplete/invalid data before any engine `dropna`; no forward fill or synthetic candles.
2. **Researcher-pinned delta proxy:** For each completed bar, if `H[t]>L[t]`, `q[t]=(C[t]-L[t])/(H[t]-L[t])`, `BuyProxy[t]=V[t]*q[t]`, `SellProxy[t]=V[t]*(1-q[t])`, `Delta[t]=BuyProxy[t]-SellProxy[t]=V[t]*(2*q[t]-1)`. If `H[t]==L[t]`, set `Delta[t]=0` (explicit researcher convention). This is an OHLCV proxy, **not exchange-observed trade aggressor delta**. Do not substitute Binance taker-buy volume or order book.
3. **Previous-nine Z-score:** For `t>=9`, define `mu9[t]=sum(Delta[t-i],i=1..9)/9` and `sd9[t]=sqrt(sum((Delta[t-i]-mu9[t])^2,i=1..9)/9)` (population standard deviation of **nine prior completed bars**, excluding current t). If `sd9[t]==0`, `Z[t]` is **undefined**, so no anomaly signal. Otherwise `Z[t]=(Delta[t]-mu9[t])/sd9[t]`. Do not use full-sample statistics, future data, a current-inclusive rolling window or unobserved footprint input. Undefined Z cannot satisfy a comparison.
4. **Volume gate:** `AvgV9[t]=sum(V[t-i],i=1..9)/9` for t>=9. Require `AvgV9[t]>0` and `V[t]>=2*AvgV9[t]`. Equality at 2x passes. This hard gate is a **researcher interpretation** of the source's high-volume discussion, not a native alert assertion.
5. **EMA trend gate:** EMA50 and EMA200 of completed **close**, seeded with SMA of their first n closes at index n-1; for `t>=n`, `EMA_n[t]=(2/(n+1))*C[t]+(1-2/(n+1))*EMA_n[t-1]`. Undefined before seed. Require `EMA50[t]>EMA200[t]` strictly; equality fails. Both EMAs use no future data. Trend filter is **enabled here** despite the source's default disabled setting.
6. **Warmup and entry event:** Process at least **220 consecutive fully completed valid 1h bars** before the first eligible decision (`t>=220`, with `t=0` the first observed bar). Initialize FLAT, 0 BTC, 10,000 USDT cash, no inherited position or pending action. `NewPositiveAnomaly[t]=(Z[t]>2.5 AND Z[t-1]<=2.5)`; both values must be defined. `BullBar[t]=(C[t]>O[t])`. A BUY signal exists iff FLAT, no pending action, `NewPositiveAnomaly[t]`, `V[t]>=2*AvgV9[t]`, `AvgV9[t]>0`, `EMA50[t]>EMA200[t]`, and `BullBar[t]` all hold at completed E[t]. No intrabar updates, confidence-star checks, future-bar confirmations or stale queued anomalies.
7. **Entry scheduling and fill:** On signal at E[t], queue one BUY for the **next completed bar t+1**, with decision frozen at t. On E[t+1], if real valid C[t+1] exists and modeled cash `>=Q*C[t+1]*(1+f)`, issue exactly one `MARKET` BUY for `Q=Decimal("0.001") BTC` at that row's **actual close** (historical simulator idealization), `f=Decimal("0.001")`. Only confirmed full executor fill changes inventory; record entry fill index `e=t+1`, actual modeled price `P=C[e]`, `Stop=0.97*P`. Insufficient cash consumes the signal and stays FLAT; a missing price, unexpected partial fill, rejected executor or inconsistent ledger **aborts** rather than inventing a fill.
8. **Exit signals and priority:** On each **subsequent** completed bar `u>e` while LONG and no pending exit, evaluate in order: (a) `C[u]<=Stop` (completed-close 3% reference stop); (b) defined `Z[u]<=-2.5` (opposite signed anomaly); (c) `C[u]<EMA50[u]`; (d) `u-e>=12` (12 completed bars after entry). First true predicate records the sole exit reason and queues one full `Q` SELL for the **following completed bar u+1** at its **real C[u+1]**. No intrabar stop, limit bracket, threshold-price fill, or source-native star/follow-through exit. An adverse move between signal and next close can cause loss **greater than 3%**. If exit price missing, do not fabricate; mark run incomplete. No exit check on the entry-fill bar.
9. **Position/confirmation/re-entry:** States `FLAT`, `BUY_PENDING`, `LONG(Q)`, `SELL_PENDING`. At most one active/pending executor and one full spot position; no opposing exposure. A confirmed SELL credits `Q*C_exit*(1-f)` and returns FLAT. Unconfirmed SELL does not erase BTC; reconcile or abort on failure rather than count an unfilled close. No new BUY on a fill/exit bar. Fresh entry requires a **new** positive-Z upcross on a later completed bar after SELL confirmation; events while LONG/pending are discarded. No pyramiding, DCA, safety orders, rebalancing, compounding, cooldown beyond this fill-bar exclusion, re-entry on persistent Z>2.5, shorts, leverage, margin, borrowing, futures, shared capital, maker/limit orders, discretionary stop/TP/trailing, or hidden controller defaults.
10. **End of history:** Never assume a next bar exists. An entry/exit pending beyond the last complete bar remains unfilled and explicitly reported. Any still-held BTC is marked at the **last actual valid completed close**, separately from realized PnL; no synthetic final liquidation. Indicator arithmetic is chronological float64, position size and modeled cash/fees use Decimal with 28-digit precision. Exact threshold comparisons are as specified; no rounded comparisons or fitted parameters.
