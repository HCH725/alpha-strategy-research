## Economic mechanism

### Source-reported

Investor-favoritism cycle with volume as the stage identifier, not a liquidity discount: `past trading volume provides information about the level of investor interest, and indirectly, about the imminence of price reversals` — low-volume winners are early in the favoritism cycle so their momentum persists (`exhibit future price momentum over a longer horizon`, continuation `for three to five years` with confirming announcement-period CARs `for the next eight quarters`); high-volume winners are late-stage so `the price momentum in these stocks reverses faster`; symmetrically, high-volume losers are early-stage losers (downward continuation, the paper's short leg) while low-volume losers are late-stage losers (slower, years-two-and-three reversals). The paper positions this against the prior volume literature it cites (`Datar, Naik and Radcliffe (1998)` low-minus-high volume premium) by showing the volume effect `is most pronounced among the extreme winner and loser portfolios` — i.e. the interaction, not the level, does the work. (All mechanism framing fenced to the paper's 1965–1995 NYSE/AMEX sample, never adopted as a crypto claim here.)

### Research interpretation

Early-stage-continuation holding, long-only, monthly cadence. Unlike pure momentum records there is no winner-takes-all ranking: the position exists if and only if the trailing-six-month formation return is non-negative AND the trailing-six-month dollar-volume mean sits at or below its own twelve-month median. Unlike volume-shock or volume-oscillator records raw volume never triggers anything by level — volume appears only as the regime partition that qualifies or disqualifies a winner (a high-volume winner is flat here, long under Gervais; a low-volume winner is long here, invisible to every pure-momentum record's volume-blind eye). Unlike the Amihud illiquidity record there is no per-unit-move normalization and no friction-level statistic — the two records can disagree in both directions from disjoint arithmetic (thin-volume drift months, fat-volume chop months). The bet, as derived, is purely that the documented early-stage interaction partition ports to BTCUSDT monthly bars — and the position absorbs the paper's own late-stage-reversal and early-stage-loser drawdowns by sitting them out, with no guardrail inside a held month.

## Signal

Exact rule as pinned (calendar-month evaluation; 126-bar formation; 12-month volume pool; month-end-close decision; next-bar-open fills; long-flat — any other month definition, return definition, volume definition, window, pool length, cutoff statistic, tie rule, or holding length is a different, unpinned rule):

- Bar grid: completed `1d` bars of BTCUSDT only. Evaluations occur at calendar month-ends in UTC (last bar dated in the month), in month order, one per month.
- Daily return (pinned): `r(t) = (C(t) − C(t−1)) / C(t−1)` on consecutive completed daily bars (`C` = close; simple, not log; close-to-close, never high/low/open-based). The first history bar carries no `r` (no predecessor — skipped, pinned).
- Daily dollar volume (pinned declared derivation): `DVOL(t) = C(t) × V(t)` where `V(t)` is the bar's base-asset volume, i.e. USDT notional traded (the venue's kline quote-volume field is the same quantity to tape precision; either field is the identical input, pinned to the product form). Venue-checked guards: closes are strictly positive on any traded tape, so no divide-by-zero branch can trigger; any bar with a non-positive close or a missing/non-positive `DVOL` is excluded from that month's window means and the window still ends on the month's last bar (defensive pin, disclosed, never exercised on this venue — verified live 2026-10-14 that the venue's first three daily bars from 2017-08-17 carry strictly positive closes and volumes).
- Formation window (pinned): exactly the 126 completed daily bars ending on month M's last bar (researcher port of the paper's headline six-month formation, ≈ 6 × 21 trading days; the paper's equity calendar skips weekends — the 24/7 tape keeps them, fenced, never adjusted).
- Formation return (pinned): `F(M) = C(end) / C(first) − 1` — total simple return over the 126-bar window (ports the paper's `returns over the past J months`). Winner iff `F(M) ≥ 0` (weak inequality; the exact-zero tie maps to winner — measure-zero tie rule, pinned).
- Formation volume (pinned): `V(M) = mean DVOL over the same 126 bars` (ports the paper's `average daily turnover during the portfolio formation period` with the no-shares-outstanding substitution).
- Trailing pool (pinned declared derivation): `S(M) = {V(M−12) … V(M−1)}` — the 12 completed monthly formation-volume values strictly preceding M (ports the paper's cross-sectional tercile partition as a time-series median split; any quantile or pool length is a different, unpinned rule).
- Cutoff (pinned median, no interpolation): `med(M)` = mean of the 6th and 7th smallest values of `S(M)` (even-count median, pinned). Low-volume regime iff `V(M) ≤ med(M)` (weak inequality; the exact tie maps to low-volume — measure-zero tie rule, pinned).
- Position target (pinned, paper early-stage direction): the target for calendar month `M+1` is long iff `F(M) ≥ 0 AND V(M) ≤ med(M)` (low-volume winner — the R10V1-side entry condition), else flat (high-volume winners, all losers, and high-volume-regime months — the late-stage, early-stage-loser, and late-stage-loser cells all map to flat, each for its own source-verified reason in Negative evidence 6).
- Orders (pinned): at most one order event per month boundary, placed for the next month's first bar open (next-open fill after the month-end decision bar — the declared causal port of the paper's one-week formation-to-holding microstructure lag; the paper ships no fill convention): flat→long target buys 100% equity at the first bar's open of month M+1; long→flat target closes all at the first bar's open of month M+1; unchanged target places nothing. No intrabar evaluation, no intrabar orders, no adds, no partials. Decisions on distinct month-ends fill on distinct month-opens — no same-bar dual-fire exists to sequence.
- Risk legs (pinned absence, not invented): the paper ships no stop/target/trailing concept for the early-stage continuation — the sole position-closing path is the regime exit to flat at a month-end close (filled next open). The derived posture is explicitly no stop, no target, no trailing, no opposite-signal exit, no calendar extension; a fully ridden within-month slide is absorbed exactly as printed by construction.

## Limitations

- Single-asset port: the paper's evidence is an equity cross-section (plus its market-wide MLC narrative); BTCUSDT volume-regime behavior is unevidenced by the source and may differ arbitrarily — no performance is claimed here for exactly this reason.
- Mechanism mismatch: whatever investor-favoritism/neglect clientele sits behind the equity interaction has no established crypto analogue (24/7 global tape, concentrated venues, perpetual-funding holders, stablecoin quote, no shares outstanding) — the port bets on interaction persistence, not on a verified crypto mechanism.
- Median-rigidity: the trailing-12-month pool adapts slowly — a structural liquefaction (or desiccation) of the venue reprices the cutoff only as old months roll off; a permanently deep tape goes nearly always-flat on the volume half, a permanently hot drift goes nearly always-long, both by construction.
- Month-definition rigidity: a volume-regime shift arriving across a month boundary is attributed to whole months; a first-of-month gap against the fresh regime is taken at the post-move open, never at the edge.
- Warmup cost: everything before the first computable-target month-end close (2018-12-31) is flat by record rule, so the 2017–2018 tape is deliberately untraded — pinned, not recovered.
