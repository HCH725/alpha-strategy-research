### Research interpretation

Unlike pool hammer/shooting-star records (fixed candle shapes with momentum-context or long-only books) and unlike pool engulfing records (two-bar coverage patterns), this machine keys off a **range-normalized directed pin-bar**: the dominant wick must exceed 0.6 of the bar's own range while the body stays under 0.3 of the range *and* points the reversal way — then the bar must print on the trend side of SMA(50) with above-mean volume. The conjunction is the edge object: shape (rejection) × side (trend) × participation (volume), traded two-sided with a self-scaling 2-body-stop / 3-body-target box that breathes with each bar's own size. Unlike pool ATR-box siblings (ATR sets stop distance), here the *current bar's body* is the ruler — quiet bars risk/take little, expansion bars risk/take more. Live-window rate is 672 post-warmup predicate bars per 62,689 1h bars (363 bull / 309 bear) driving 633 entries and 637 exits (365 SL / 268 TP / 4 reversals) — the SL-heavy split (57.6% of exits) is the structural signature of a 2:3 body box under 1h noise, not tuned. The dead RSI leg is page decoration, never order logic.
