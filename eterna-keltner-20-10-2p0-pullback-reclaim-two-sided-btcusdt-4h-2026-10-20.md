## Economic mechanism

### Source-reported

Pullback-reclaim inside an ATR channel under a trend regime (strategy README: `Volatility-based channel system using EMA and Average True Range (ATR). Identifies trending markets and enters on pullbacks to the channel edges. More adaptive than Donchian Channels due to ATR-based bands`; `Trading Style: Mean reversion within trend`): in an uptrend the long never chases an upside breakout — it waits for price to dip to the lower band, touch it, and close back above it; the short mirrors at the upper band in a downtrend. Either leg exits on the first cross back through the middle (basis) line. There is deliberately no stop, no target, no trailing exit, and no always-in-market claim beyond post-signal holds — the order block ends with the two `strategy.close` guards and no risk leg ships anywhere, by design gap rather than by hidden leg.

### Research interpretation

Regime-gated countertrend pullback-reclaim with a midline take — the first reclaim-polarity Keltner record in the pool. Unlike breakout systems that buy strength through the band, this rule buys weakness that PROVES itself: the touch confirm (`low <= lowerBand` / `high >= upperBand`) requires the bar to actually reach the band, and the cross leg requires the close to recover back inside — a close hovering just above the lower band without ever touching it fires nothing, and a bar that touches but closes below the band fires nothing either. Note the two pinned asymmetries, stated plainly: first, the entry needs the trend regime on its side (longs only above the 50 EMA, shorts only below) while the exit needs only the basis cross — a long entered in an uptrend that later sees price sink under the 50 EMA is NOT exited by the regime loss itself, only by the basis crossunder, so a regime flip without a midline cross is held, disclosed; second, the exit is a single basis-cross edge, not a hold-below-midline state — a bar that gaps far under the basis in one move still exits exactly once at that close with no trailing guard for the further slide, and a bar that merely kisses the basis without crossing holds, disclosed. The bet is on trend-regime pullback recovery at frozen `20/10/2.0/50` geometry, not on any breakout extreme, volume print, momentum sign, calendar effect, or second timeframe.

An entry and its OWN-side exit co-print only in the degenerate `atrValue[1] == 0` collapse (proof: entry needs `close > lower` with `close[1] <= lower[1]`, own-exit needs `close < basis` with `close[1] >= basis[1]`; since `lower <= basis` always (`2.0 * atrValue >= 0` as true ranges are non-negative), the priors jointly force `lower[1] >= basis[1]`, hence equality throughout — pinned, disclosed, practically unreachable on a volatile perpetual). Source order evaluates entries before exits and is frozen here: a flat book opens the fresh side then the opposite-exit leg no-ops (no opposite position) — net entry; a held opposite book reverses through the entry leg (Pine pyramiding-0 parity) then the exit leg no-ops — net reversal; a held same-side book ignores the entry leg then exits only in the degenerate own-side co-fire — net flat. At most one net position change per bar; no same-bar double-fill ambiguity survives. Backtest economics of the timings differ by construction — the adaptation is disclosed, not hidden, and this record makes no claim about the source timing's performance. Any live intrabar behavior is out of scope.
- Derived market pin: the code names no symbol (`{{ticker}}` alert templating only) — BTCUSDT is a researcher market choice for the house universe, never a source claim.
- Source economics are Pine-default plus one commission term: beyond `percent_of_equity 10 / initial_capital 10000` and `commission 0.075%` the source ships no slippage, no funding, and no margin assumption — derived evaluation must carry the full house cost load (fees plus historical funding; no hypothetical zero funding claimed), and this record reports no net performance of its own. The README's undated prose ranges are omitted for want of traceable provenance.
- Entries-first resolution under pyramiding-0 parity is a source-order pin: opposite-book reclaim bars reverse through the entry leg with the paired exit leg no-op; degenerate same-bar entry-plus-own-exit (zero-ATR collapse only) resolves to flat. Pine's own next-bar double-queue semantics are not claimed. Any other tiebreak would be a different, unpinned rule.
- README tuning variants not adopted: `1.5x`, `2.5-3.0x`, and any per-asset parameter retune are disclosed-but-not-adopted — each would be a different, unpinned rule.
- No price stop, no time stop, regime-loss hold, post-exit flat gaps: adverse excursion after placement has no guardrail beyond the basis edge; a regime flip without a midline cross is held; after any exit the system sits flat through potentially long reclaim-failing stretches. This is the coded posture, disclosed as the record's principal risk.
- Seed bars: the 50-bar trend EMA plus the cross lookback need 51 completed bars and every trigger needs one lookback bar; the first 60 `4h` bars are flat by record rule, so early-history signals never enter any evaluation.
- Alert-handoff quarantine: the `webhookMessage` template with `leverage`/`positionSize` parameters is payload text with no order-leg effect — treating it as order semantics would be a different, unpinned rule, disclosed here.
- Display quarantine: the 4 `plot` calls, `fill`, `bgcolor`, and 2 `plotshape` markers feed rendering only — promoting any of them into an order leg would be a different, unpinned rule, disclosed here.
