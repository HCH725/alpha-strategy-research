## Economic mechanism

### Source-reported

Short-horizon reversal harvesting with body-confirmed exhaustion plus a single-average rally guard on the short side. The machine waits for three straight same-direction candles whose combined body dominates the current range (70% rule), buys the three-down washout and shorts the three-up stretch (shorts only below the SMA200), then exits each side on the first prior-bar extreme reclaim. The prose claims simple universal short-term reversal character with qualitative risk/optimization language (stricter reversal criteria, stop-loss, BOLL/MACD/volume add-ons) that ships nowhere. No numeric performance figure of any kind ships anywhere (stanza window 2023-12-07→2023-12-14 is a seven-day 5m sample — cited as provenance, never as performance evidence).

### Research interpretation

Body-gated snap-back with asymmetric trend discipline, event-persistent, two-sided. Unlike close-sequence records (`three-down-three-up-consec-close` — `close` vs prior `close` chains with mirror 3-up exit, 1h; `botnet101-three-lower-closes-prior-high-exit` — `close<close[1]` downCount with the same `close>high[1]` long exit, 1h spot long-only), this machine counts intrabar direction (`open>close` streaks, not interbar close declines) and demands a same-bar body-dominance ratio above 0.70 with an explicit zero-guard — a triple-down of doji/indecisive bars can never fire here, while it fires the close-sequence cousins. Unlike MA-cross records, no average ever crosses another average — the sole SMA200 only gates the short entry as a level (`close<ma`), never a cross. Unlike oscillator/BB records, no averaged-gain/loss, stochastic, standard-deviation or band object exists in the signal path; the widest objects are the prior-bar high/low reclaims. Five-axis distinction from the nearest `botnet101` cousin holds on mechanism (body-confirmed intrabar streak + asymmetric SMA gate + symmetric reclaim exits vs bare close-decline count + single reclaim exit), signal construction (open/close direction + 0.70 ratio + SMA200 level vs close/close chain), horizon (5m vs 1h), direction (two-sided with id-specific closes vs long-only) and source identity (FMZ 435519 stormis/hackertrader/QuantpT vs TradingView Botnet101).
