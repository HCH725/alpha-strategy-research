## Economic mechanism

### Source-reported

Three consecutive red daily bars (close below open three days running) mark a washout; the long fires only when that triple-red prints while current ATR(12) stands above its 30-day SMA — a volatility-confirmation gate that blocks entries in dead tapes. The position closes on the mirror triple-green (three consecutive green bars) or on a 22-day time cap, whichever comes first. Between entry and either exit leg the book holds without acting. No protective stop, target, or trailing exists anywhere in the program.

### Research interpretation

This derivative is long only inside confirmed washout-plus-expansion episodes: the triple-red demands three days of sequential selling, while the ATR-above-average gate demands that the washout also carry above-normal realized range — a pattern-plus-volatility confirmation, not a single-bar dip, not a cross edge, and not a midline reclaim. The exit is dual but bounded: the mirror triple-green takes profits when buyers retake the tape three days running, while the 22-day cap guarantees no position rests longer than 22 daily bars by construction (longest observed hold is exactly 22 bars). Because the sole risk mechanism is the mirror-or-time exit with no stop, a market that drifts sideways mid-pattern keeps the book exposed up to the cap; the median observed hold is 10 bars. The ATR gate is material, not decorative: over the full 1d replay 259 triple-red bars print but only 121 pass the ATR gate (138 blocked, ~53%).

## Signal

1. **Input and availability:** one chronological, contiguous sequence of actual Binance BTCUSDT spot UTC **1d** candles indexed t=0,1,... . Native opening time T[t] must be UTC midnight-aligned and T[t+1]-T[t]=86400 seconds. Require finite positive O,H,L,C, H>=max(O,C), L<=min(O,C), finite nonnegative V. Reject duplicates, gaps, out-of-order bars, missing/zero/nonfinite observed prices or incomplete candles; never interpolate or forward-fill. Completed bar t is available at E[t]=T[t]+86400, not T[t]. Float64 chronological arithmetic, no intermediate rounding. Only **O/H/L/C** are consumed (O/C for color triples, H/L/C for true range); V is validity-checked only.
2. **Constants (researcher-pinned to payload defaults):** `maxTradeDuration=22`, `useGreenExit=true`, `atrPeriod=12` pinned (the payload's three `input()` defaults; the source menu is fenced, not adopted). ATR average length 30 and the triple lookback 3 are literals in code, not inputs.
3. **Pinned indicator semantics (Pine v6 built-ins, warmup-fenced):** `redDay[t]=C[t]<O[t]`, `greenDay[t]=C[t]>O[t]` (doji `C==O` fires neither leg); `threeRed[t]=redDay[t] AND redDay[t-1] AND redDay[t-2]`; `threeGreen[t]=greenDay[t] AND greenDay[t-1] AND greenDay[t-2]`. True range `TR[t]=H[t]-L[t]` at t=0 else `max(H[t]-L[t],abs(H[t]-C[t-1]),abs(L[t]-C[t-1]))`. `ATR[t]` is Wilder RMA of TR with period 12: first valid value at t=11 as `mean(TR[0..11])`, thereafter `ATR[t]=(ATR[t-1]*11+TR[t])/12` (SMA-seeded RMA per the Bookstaber `ta.atr`/`ta.rma` precedent; researcher pin with full-window seed convention). `averageATR[t]=mean(ATR[t-29..t])` over the same 30 ATR values (Pine `ta.sma` literal). Pine yields `na` until full windows exist; this derivative fences bars 0..40 as seed-only and never decides before t=41, so only full-window values are ever consumed (first decidable bar t=41; disclosed researcher pin, same fencing shape as the admitted FMZ-521670 record). `atr_entry[t]=(ATR[t]>0 AND averageATR[t]>0) ? (ATR[t]>averageATR[t]) : true` — the payload's explicit zero-guard, pinned verbatim; pre-warmup bars never reach it by the fence. No variable division exists anywhere, so a zero-variance window cannot produce NaN through an explicit `/`.
4. **Entry latch (verbatim, strict comparisons):** at t>=41 while FLAT with no pending action and t strictly later than the last SELL-fill bar: `threeRed[t] AND atr_entry[t]`. Equality on any color leg (`C==O`) blocks entry. Queue exactly one BUY. Dispatch on row t+1 only, independently of that row's signal, and model a full 0.001 BTC MARKET fill at its **actual observed completed close** C[t+1], effective E[t+1]. No t+1 price or indicator is used to decide at t. If row t+1 does not exist, leave unfilled. Require cash >=Decimal("0.001")*Decimal(str(C[t+1]))*(1+Decimal("0.001")); insufficient funds or ambiguous/missing fill aborts rather than assuming borrowing.
5. **Exit latch (verbatim, dual leg):** when confirmed LONG and no pending action, with `tradeDuration[t]=abs(t-entrySignalBar)` where entrySignalBar is the entry-latch bar index: `exitCondition[t]=(threeGreen[t]) OR (tradeDuration[t]>=22)` (with `useGreenExit` pinned true, so the first disjunct is live; the `na(entryBarIndex)` pre-entry state never decides because LONG is required). On the first completed bar u after the BUY-fill bar with `exitCondition[u]` true, queue a full 0.001 BTC SELL for row u+1 at actual observed C[u+1], effective E[u+1]. No stop/target/trailing leg exists in the payload; all are expressly disabled, not hidden defaults. The mirror-or-time signal itself is the sole risk mechanism.

Entry and signal-exit predicates are **mutually exclusive by construction** (they require three consecutive reds vs three consecutive greens on the same three bars; a bar cannot be both red and green, and doji blocks both) — verified zero co-fires over the full 2,493-bar replay, so no tie-break is needed.
