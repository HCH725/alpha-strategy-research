## Economic mechanism

### Source-reported

Staircase-trend persistence with a fixed asymmetric payoff, both directions. Three consecutive two-step rises in bar lows mark a higher-low staircase (uptrend footprint); three consecutive two-step falls in bar highs mark a lower-high staircase (downtrend footprint). The record bets the staircase extends — long on the completed rising-low staircase, short on the completed falling-high staircase — guarded by a 2-point leash and a 6-point cap (1:3 risk-reward). The source ships no sample statistics, trade count, fee basis, win rate, or performance figure of any kind — no source-reported performance is adopted or restated.

### Research interpretation

Triple-staircase ignition machine with a latched 1:3 bracket, event-driven, two-sided, flat except when a completed staircase fires. Unlike clock-only records (`monday-drift` — UTC day-of-week + hour predicates with time exits), there is no clock predicate anywhere here: quiet bars print nothing without the staircase. Unlike day-open threshold records (`dual-thrust`, `holmberg-orb` — displacement measured against the 00:00 UTC open with first-breach entries), the test is staircase-relative (each bar against its two predecessors, no open anchor, no lookback beyond two bars, no breach direction vote). Unlike narrow-range records (`nr7-price-breakout` — narrowest range of 7 bars, relative cross-bar comparison, long-only daily), this trigger is a directional staircase count, two-sided, with a fixed bracket. Unlike color-sequence records (`1-3-1` — one red bar plus three rising green closes, long-only, red-low-anchored 1R bracket), the alphabet here is lows-vs-lows and highs-vs-highs staircases (never open-vs-close colors), the pulse is a counter reaching exactly 3 (not a 4-bar color alternation), and the bracket is fixed 2/6 points off the signal close (not pattern-anchored). Unlike reversal-pattern records (`forven-three-bar-reversal` — three consecutive lower lows plus a higher-close confirmation with a cumulative-decline gate, long-only, new-low exit, no bracket), this machine buys rising-low staircases (continuation, not washout-reversal), needs no decline gate and no confirmation bar, and always brackets every trade. Unlike oscillator records (RSI/CCI threshold fades — fixed level rails, no staircase, no bracket), there is no oscillator anywhere. The edge, if any, lives in the documented intuition that three completed two-step staircases mark a footed short-horizon trend worth riding 6 points while risking 2 — a bet on days-scale drift persistence after structural footing, not on any level, average, oscillator, volume flow, calendar effect, or second timeframe.

## Signal — Gate 1, fully pinned derivative

Index completed UTC daily bars `t = 0, 1, ...` with real Binance BTC-USDT futures `O[t], H[t], L[t], C[t]` (volume not read). A bar with missing/nonfinite/non-positive prices, or with `H < max(O,C)` / `L < min(O,C)`, is invalid; never interpolate. All inputs pinned at source defaults: `SL_D = 2`, `TP_D = 6` price points (any other values are a different, unpinned rule).

- **Rising-low step `U[t]` (t ≥ 2):** `L[t] > L[t−1] AND L[t−1] > L[t−2]` (strict inequalities — equality breaks the chain, never extends it).
- **Falling-high step `D[t]` (t ≥ 2):** `H[t] < H[t−1] AND H[t−1] < H[t−2]` (strict — equality breaks the chain).
- **Counters (exact `var` semantics):** `CU = 0, CD = 0` after warmup; on each valid bar `t ≥ 2`: if `U[t]` then `CU := CU+1, CD := 0`; else if `D[t]` then `CD := CD+1, CU := 0`; else `CU := 0, CD := 0`. Counters persist across missing-bar gaps unchanged until a new valid bar evaluates (gap-recovery pin: no timeout, no interpolation, no reset-without-a-bar); pre-warmup bars are flat by record rule and never update counters (latch-consumption-during-warmup pin: warmup bars neither fire nor pre-charge the count).
- **Long signal `EL[t]`:** `CU == 3` exactly (a fourth consecutive step reads `CU == 4` and fires nothing — single pulse per run). **Short signal `ES[t]`:** `CD == 3` exactly.
- **Mutual exclusivity (proven, no invented priority):** each bar executes exactly one of the three branches and the winning branch zeroes the other counter, so at most one counter is nonzero on any bar; `EL` and `ES` can never co-fire (analytic proof plus 20,000-tape exclusivity fuzz with zero dual-fire and the analytic squeeze case resolving to the rising branch by file order, run in scratch this cycle: `SELF-CHECK OK`). A signal bar arriving while positioned on the same side is discarded unseen (explicitly no queue, no add — Pine `pyramiding = 0` default behavior pinned as an explicit gate, same pin class as the merged Super-Scalper, SMMA17, PowerZone and candle-size records).
- **Bracket legs (latched at the signal bar, never re-anchored):** for a long signal on bar `s`, `LTP = C[s] + 6`, `LST = C[s] − 2`, resting until filled or until an opposite signal reverses the book (short mirror: `STP = C[s] − 6`, `SST = C[s] + 2`). Levels never move after the signal bar (symmetric latch, NOT a ratchet, NOT floating).
- **Exit precedence (explicit researcher pin):** from the fill bar onward, each held bar resolves in this order: (1) bracket first-touch against the bar's `H/L` with the **stop-first** same-bar rule (both touched → stop price wins); (2) only if the bracket is untouched, the opposite staircase signal at the bar close. A bar touching the bracket AND printing the opposite signal closes the old unit at the bracket price and still opens the reversed unit at the next bar's open (matches Pine intrabar-exit-before-next-open-entry sequencing; disclosed, never a file literal — same bracket class as the merged PowerZone, SMMA17, 1-3-1 and candle-size records, here over a latched bracket).

**Direction (Gate 2):** BUY-to-open / SELL-to-close on the long leg; SELL-to-open / BUY-to-close on the short leg. The short leg requires margin-short permission, realistic on the pinned futures venue (same two-sided posture as the merged PowerZone, Super-Scalper, SMMA17 and candle-size records; the stanza venue is natively futures, so no re-venuing is needed). No hedge, no cross-market trade.

**Risk (Gate 3, explicit):** latched-bracket-plus-reversal book. Fixed 2-point stop / 6-point take latched at each signal close, opposite-staircase close-and-reverse. No trailing ratchet, no breakeven move, no time stop, no partial exit, no averaging, no forced end-of-data liquidation, no silent executor default. An end-of-sample open position is marked at the last real valid close, not closed with an invented fill.

**State (Gate 4):** `FLAT`, `BUY_PENDING`, `LONG`, `SELL_PENDING`, `SHORT`, `COVER_PENDING`, plus counters `CU/CD`. Initialize `FLAT` with `CU = CD = 0` after 10 valid completed bars. Entries fire only from confirmed `FLAT` or as one-step reversals from the opposite confirmed state; a missing/failed/unconfirmed fill **halts** this research run, never synthesizes inventory. While `LONG`, further `EL` bars are no-ops and `ES` reverses (bracket canceled on reversal — Pine `from_entry` semantics); symmetrically while `SHORT`. A pending order prohibits additional actions. **No DCA/safety orders (explicitly disabled — the downstream 6%+6%+6% house overlay is not active in this admission and never silently overrides single-unit semantics)**, no pyramiding, no concurrency, no compounding. No cooldown is specified (explicitly none, not invented) — re-entry needs a fresh `== 3` pulse.
