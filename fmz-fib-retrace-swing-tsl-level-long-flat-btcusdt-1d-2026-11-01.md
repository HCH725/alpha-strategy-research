## Economic mechanism

### Source-reported
Rolling-range Fibonacci grid plus swing trailing stop: the highest high and lowest low of the last 50 bars freeze a rolling range, fixed Fibonacci fractions mark five interior rails below the range top, and a direction latch (close clearing the prior-bar high/low, held by `valuewhen`) selects the swing stop — the recent swing low while bullish, the recent swing extreme otherwise. A close above the stop that also sits above any of the five rails holds long (trend absorption with a range-position veto that refuses entries trapped in the bottom fifth of the range); a close below the stop that also sits below any rail stands flat (trend failure with a veto that refuses exits printed at the range top); vetoed and equality bars hold posture. The prose admits whipsaw risk in violent moves, parameter-setting risk, and trend-misjudgment risk in chop (disclosed, unported future work — adding confirmation filters, dynamic parameters, position management, all disclosed unported). The stanza window 2023-04-23→2024-04-28 is a one-year 1d sample — cited as provenance, never as performance evidence.

### Research interpretation
Veto-gated swing-stop level holder, event-persistent, long-flat. Unlike the pool Fibonacci cousin (`fibonacci-retracement-reclaim-long` — FMZ 430984: cross-event machine, entry on the single-bar reclaim-cross of the deep 0.618 rail, exit on the crossunder of the shallow 0.236 rail, no latch, no stop, no veto), this machine never trades a cross — entries and exits are close-vs-latched-stop LEVEL states vetoed by a five-rail OR range-position gate, and the stop itself breathes with a `valuewhen` direction latch rather than sitting on a fixed rail. Unlike trailing-stop cousins (`fmz-atr-dual-trail-t3-envelope-trend` — ATR-scaled dual trails on a T3 envelope, 5m; `fmz-dual-ema-cloud-donchian-stop-breakout` — dual-EMA cloud with a Donchian stop, 1m two-sided; `sma-atr-dynamic-trailing-stop` family), no ATR, no second average and no Donchian stop ship anywhere — the stop is the raw 1-bar swing extreme selected by the latch, and the Fibonacci grid is a veto, never the trigger. Unlike oscillator-gated machines (RSI/CCI/Stoch/CMO kin), no oscillator ships anywhere — the only objects are two rolling extremes, five multiplications and one latch. Five-axis distinction from the nearest pool cousin (430984 fib-reclaim) holds on mechanism (latched swing-stop level hold with range-position veto vs two-rail reclaim-cross events), signal construction (stop-vs-close gated by OR-of-five-rails vs two single-rail crosses), horizon/venue parity with different timing semantics (both futures 1d, but level-state next-bar-open holds vs cross-event edges), exits/economics (stop-plus-veto id-specific close with researcher 0.1% fee and fixed 0.01 BTC vs rail-crossunder close), and source identity (FMZ 449846 Fibonacci BFSP vs FMZ 430984 Zer3192 reclaim script).
