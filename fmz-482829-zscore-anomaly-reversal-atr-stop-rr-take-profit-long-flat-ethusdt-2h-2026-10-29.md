## Economic mechanism

### Source-reported

The page prose presents a volume-and-body anomaly filter over a candle-reversal trigger: z-scores of trading volume and candle-body size flag abnormally active bars, an adjacent-candle reversal (red bar making a lower low followed by a green bar, and mirror for shorts) confirms direction, and each position carries a dynamic stop (the worse of a 2-bar structural extreme and an ATR stop) plus a risk-reward-derived profit target. No source performance metric is adopted.

### Research interpretation

This is an **informed-participation reversal**: the traded thesis is that a reversal candle printed on statistically unusual activity (volume ≥ 1.5σ above its 20-bar mean) is less likely to be noise than the same shape on a quiet bar, and the combined structural-plus-ATR stop then caps exactly the failure mode where the "informed" bar was actually the start of a slide rather than its end. The RR-2.0 target makes the expectancy hinge on win-rate clearing one-third net of the modeled fee, not on trend persistence — winners are cut at a fixed multiple of entry risk, so a slow drift that never reaches +2R round-trips back into the stop. The failure mode is plain: in chop, anomaly bars cluster (volume itself mean-reverts slowly), printing repeated entries that each pay the modeled fee and stop out, while a gap across 2h closes jumps clean over both frozen levels. No profitability is asserted.

Use real Binance ETH-USDT spot `O[t],H[t],L[t],C[t],V[t]`; only closes, lows, highs, opens and volumes are read. A bar with missing/nonfinite/non-positive close or missing/negative volume is invalid; never interpolate.

A zero stdev or any undefined input makes `z[t]` undefined; `z>=1.5` on undefined is false — never imputed, never a tie-break.
3. `green[t] = (C[t] >= O[t])`; `red[t] = (C[t] < O[t])`. `ATR[t] = ta.atr(14)[t]` (Wilder RMA of true range) for `t >= 14`, undefined before.
4. On simulator bar `t` **only after its close**, evaluate with the already-completed **previous** bar `u = t-1`. `longSignal[u] = red[u-1] and (L[u] < L[u-1]) and green[u]`, all three bars' OHLC valid; `long[u] = longSignal[u] and (z[u] >= 1.5)`. This mirrors the source's `barstate.isconfirmed` semantics: no current-bar `t` indicator may influence the action; `C[t]`/`V[t]` are solely modeled fill-price/cash-check data, never signal inputs.
5. If confirmed FLAT and not pending, `long[u]` queues one BUY of 0.05 ETH at simulated `C[t]` subject to warmup/affordability/guards below. No exit predicate is consulted while FLAT.
6. At entry computation (still on completed bar `u`), freeze: `structSL = min(L[u-1], L[u])`; `atrSL = C[u] - ATRMULT*ATR[u]` (requires finite `ATR[u]`); `SL = max(structSL, atrSL)`; `TP = C[u] + (C[u]-SL)*RR`. Entry guard: require `TP > C[u] > SL` (strictly); otherwise queue nothing. With `ATR>0` this holds structurally (`structSL <= L[u] <= C[u]`, `atrSL < C[u]`); the guard exists only for degenerate zero-range/zero-ATR bars.
7. If confirmed LONG and not pending: on completed bar `t` with `u = t-1`, (i) if `C[u] <= SL`, queue one SELL of the confirmed 0.05 ETH at simulated `C[t]` (stop, checked **first** — pinned stop-first priority when both levels are touched by the same close); (ii) else if `C[u] >= TP`, queue the same SELL at simulated `C[t]` (take-profit). Never reverse or re-enter on the same bar. The entry predicate is not consulted while LONG.
8. There is no opposite-signal exit (short leg disabled), no trailing order, no time exit, no scale-out, no cooldown, no pyramiding, and no end-of-sample forced liquidation. An unclosed final position remains open, marked at the last observed valid price only. The `triggerAlarm` function, `barcolor`, all four `plotshape` markers, both `alertcondition`s and the `exitLong`/`exitShort` plot accumulators have no decision function and are pinned as display-only.
