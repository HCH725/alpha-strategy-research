- **MACD (Pine v5 causal `ta.macd` 12/26/9: `ta.ema` α=2/(L+1) SMA-seeded, na before each leg):** `E12[t]=ema(C,12)[t]` (na before 11), `E26[t]=ema(C,26)[t]` (na before 25), `M[t]=E12[t]-E26[t]`, `G[t]=ema(M,9)[t]` (signal seeded on the first 9 defined `M` bars), `D[t]=M[t]-G[t]` unused by the entries (the discarded `_` third return and the signal line are never read for entries — disclosed; the edge is on the MACD line vs zero, not vs signal).

- **Latched bracket (fixed at the signal bar, never moved):** on a long signal at bar `s`, `SL=C[s]-1.5·A[s]`, `TP=C[s]+2·A[s]`; on a short signal, `SL=C[s]+1.5·A[s]`, `TP=C[s]-2·A[s]` (price legs, never ticks; levels are tickSize-snapped at 0.10 — the record's sole precision pin). - **Exit precedence (explicit researcher pin, same class as the merged Simons/dual-EMA9/21/reversal-BB/SMA100 records):** each held bar resolves the latched box first-touch against the bar's `H/L` with the **stop-first** same-bar rule (both touched → stop price wins). An opposite dual-gate arriving while holding REVERSES the book at the next open (close at next-open, open opposite at the same next-open, old resting pair cancelled, new pair armed from the new signal bar) — Pine `pyramiding = 0` default reversal pinned as an explicit gate. A same-side dual-gate while holding is a no-op (default single-unit pin). The closed side's resting stop+limit order dies with the position (Pine exit-order lifecycle — pinned, never smoothed).
