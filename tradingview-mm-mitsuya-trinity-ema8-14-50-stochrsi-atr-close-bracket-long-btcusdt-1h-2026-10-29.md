## Hypothesis and deterministic signal (Gate 1)

**Falsifiable research hypothesis, not a return claim:** Requiring 8/14/50 EMA alignment before a smoothed StochRSI bullish crossover may select pullback recoveries in established uptrends; the 3ATR/2ATR completed-close bracket may have substantially different tail risk from the author's original broker-emulated stops. No profitability, backtest, Survivor, Sharpe, win rate or TradingView statistic is independently reproduced.

**Data contract:** One `binance` connector `BTC-USDT` **spot 1h** OHLCV stream, UTC, bar index t=0,1,..., opening timestamp T[t] (Unix seconds), exactly `T[t]-T[t-1]=3600`. Full OHLCV of t becomes available **only at E[t]=T[t]+3600**, after completion. Require finite positive O,H,L,C, H>=max(O,C), L<=min(O,C), finite nonnegative base-asset volume, unique ascending timestamps, no gaps, duplicates, unfinished bars, interpolation, synthetic prices, forward fill, or silent bridging over missing data. Reject an invalid tape before preparing features. Float64 indicators in ascending chronological order, no fitting/optimization, no future sample dependence.

1. **EMA:** For n in {8,14,50}, `EMA_n[0]=C[0]`; for t>0, `EMA_n[t]=(2/(n+1))*C[t]+(1-2/(n+1))*EMA_n[t-1]`. `UP[t] = EMA8[t]>EMA14[t] AND EMA14[t]>EMA50[t]`; equality is neutral. Do not use future-bar EMA alignment or any higher timeframe.
2. **Wilder RSI14:** For i>=1, delta=C[i]-C[i-1], gain=max(delta,0), loss=max(-delta,0). At t=14 seed average gains/losses with the arithmetic means of i=1..14. For t>14 recur `G[t]=(13*G[t-1]+gain[t])/14`, `L[t]=(13*L[t-1]+loss[t])/14`. RSI=100 when L=0,G>0; RSI=0 when G=0,L>0; RSI=50 when both zero; otherwise `100-100/(1+G/L)`. RSI undefined before t=14.
3. **StochRSI:** For t>=27, `lo=min(RSI[t-13..t])`, `hi=max(RSI[t-13..t])`; `raw[t]=100*(RSI[t]-lo)/(hi-lo)` if hi>lo. If hi=lo, set `raw[t]=raw[t-1]` if defined, otherwise 50. The 14-period window includes only completed RSI observations. `K[t]=SMA(raw[t-2..t],3)` first defined at t=29; `D[t]=SMA(K[t-2..t],3)` first defined at t=31. No threshold/overbought gate; `CROSSUP[t]=(K[t-1]<=D[t-1] AND K[t]>D[t])` for t>=32. Exact equality at t is **not** a crossing. The author describes a StochRSI crossover; the RSI/Stoch/SMA seeds and strict edge are researcher-defined.
4. **Wilder ATR14:** `TR[0]=H[0]-L[0]`; for i>=1, `TR[i]=max(H[i]-L[i],abs(H[i]-C[i-1]),abs(L[i]-C[i-1]))`. Seed `ATR[13]=mean(TR[0..13])`; t>=14 recur `ATR[t]=(13*ATR[t-1]+TR[t])/14`. A buy requires finite strictly positive ATR[t]. No future ATR.
5. **Long entry edge:** At a completed t>=120, while FLAT and no pending order, if UP[t], CROSSUP[t], and ATR[t]>0, queue exactly one BUY for **bar t+1**; freeze `risk_ATR=ATR[t]` with the pending order. Crosses that occur while LONG or pending are consumed, never saved for later. Do not enter solely because trend remains aligned.
6. **Entry fill and bracket:** At completed t+1, after verifying actual finite positive C[t+1] and adequate fee-inclusive cash, send one `MARKET BUY` via pinned `OrderExecutor` at that row's observed completed **close**. Only a confirmed full Q fill at real P=C[t+1] creates LONG. Set `stop=P-3*risk_ATR`, `target=P+2*risk_ATR`, both fixed for the position; reject if stop<=0, stop>=P or target<=P. Do not recompute on fill-bar ATR or modify brackets later. The completed fill bar does not also trigger an exit.
7. **Close-based exit:** For every completed bar u>=t+2 while LONG and with no pending exit, evaluate `C[u]<=stop` first, else `C[u]>=target`. If either true, queue one full-Q SELL for **u+1**; freeze exit reason (stop or target). The stop/target are **thresholds for completed-close decisions only**. They are **not** intrabar high/low touches, stop-market/limit orders, or guaranteed exit prices. At completed u+1 fill the SELL at the **actual observed C[u+1]**, not at stop or target. A gap or further price move may produce a much worse fill. Do not enter or process another signal on an exit fill bar.
8. **State and re-entry:** `FLAT -> BUY_PENDING -> LONG -> SELL_PENDING -> FLAT`. Exactly one position, one pending action and no same-bar reverse or duplicate fill. After confirmed full SELL, the next fresh UP+CROSSUP signal may re-enter from the next eligible completed bar; no extra cooldown. The source's short EMA alignment, bearish StochRSI crossover and short entry are **disabled**; a bearish cross alone is not an exit. No pyramiding, hedging, DCA/safety orders, partial exits, compounding, time limit, breakeven, trailing, regime gate, discretionary exit, forced terminal liquidation, leverage or futures margin. If no bracket close trigger occurs, LONG persists and terminal inventory is marked at the last real valid completed close without a fabricated SELL.
9. **Failure behavior:** A missing next row leaves a pending order **unfilled** at tape end; no fictional fill. A missing/invalid candle, insufficient cash, failed/rejected/partial/duplicate executor fill, invalid bracket, inconsistent inventory or fee ledger **aborts** the bounded research run; never silently assume complete fills or substitute missing prices. Stop and target cannot both trigger on a valid positive-ATR long bracket; the explicit stop-first priority still applies.
10. **Warmup and availability:** Compute indicators from the first valid real bar, but prohibit trading on t=0..119; first decision t=120 after **120 prior fully completed contiguous candles** and valid K/D/ATR. A decision at E[t] may only dispatch on the next row t+1 and fills no earlier than E[t+1]. Raw Binance `timestamp` is **candle OPEN**, not the information-availability or executable fill time. No future pivots, future bars, incomplete HTF candles, full-sample normalization, negative shifts, lookahead, repainting or early fill labels.
