## Economic mechanism

### Source-reported

Naked momentum-threshold acceptance, long side only. The 10-bar Rate of Change measures the unit return over the trailing window; the position opens when that return exceeds +0.02 (upward momentum confirmed across the window) and ends it when the return falls below −0.02. Between the bands the machine holds its posture — a dip back toward zero that never prints −0.02 keeps a long alive, and a recovery toward zero that never prints +0.02 never enters. The equity-index universe and the unmodeled-costs stance are source facts, not this record's venue or economics. No performance figure is carried anywhere.

### Research interpretation

Band-hysteresis level machine raced against its own dead zone — the threshold cousin of the lane's dual-line cross family (ROC-Crossover's fast-vs-slow race) and the band sibling of the pool's zero-line ROC machines. Unlike the Forven ROC12 zero-cross edge (which trades every zero transition — entry needs `prev ≤ 0 < curr`, exit the mirror), this machine never reads the prior bar: any post-warmup bar printing above +0.02 while flat enters, any bar printing below −0.02 while long exits, and the whole [−0.02, +0.02] interval is a hold zone — strictly fewer, strictly later events than any zero-edge reader of any ROC series. Unlike the Pine ROC30 zero-line level-hold (long on ANY `ROC > 0` bar, flat on ANY `ROC ≤ 0` bar), the ±0.02 bands add a symmetric insensitivity sleeve: small wiggles around zero trade nothing here and flip everything there. Unlike dual-ROC cross machines no second series exists — the trigger is a fixed level pair, not a cross. Unlike smoothed/composite momentum (Coppock, KST, smoothed-ROC trough) the scalar is the raw two-point return — no average of any kind is computed anywhere, so entries fire earlier and choppier than any smoothed cousin, at the cost of every whipsaw the smoothing siblings filter out. The failure mode is plain: a sustained ramp pins the ROC above +0.02 and the machine enters once on the first post-warmup bar and holds indefinitely with no stop and no profit-taking leg (planted +1%/day tape: 1 entry / 0 exits ending LONG — disclosed); a sustained bleed never arms the entry at all (planted −1%/day tape: 0/0 ending FLAT — opportunity cost, disclosed); chop that whips the 10-bar return across both bands prints repeated band-to-band round trips (planted chop tape: 5 entries / 5 exits ending FLAT — disclosed); a dead-constant tape pins ROC at exact zero inside the dead zone forever (planted flat tape: 0/0 ending FLAT — disclosed, not patched).
