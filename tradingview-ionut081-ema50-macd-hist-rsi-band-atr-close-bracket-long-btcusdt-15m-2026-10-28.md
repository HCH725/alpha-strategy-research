## Economic mechanism

**Source-reported:** Trade only when trend (EMA50), momentum (MACD histogram) and bounded bullish strength (RSI14 in 50–70) agree; bound intended loss/target distance with ATR14. This is a hypothesis, not an independently measured edge.

**Research hypothesis:** A newly established, jointly confirmed 15-minute momentum state may yield positive net expectancy when held until the first subsequent completed close outside a 1:2 ATR-anchored band. This derivative does not claim any measured profitability, source-native execution equivalence, Survivor status or Hummingbot/Qlib parity.

## Signal

Use a single strictly chronological Binance **BTC-USDT spot 15m** series of actual OHLCV bars indexed `t=0,1,...`, with UTC candle **open** timestamps `T[t]` exactly 900 seconds apart. All numeric inputs must be finite; `0<L<=min(O,C)<=max(O,C)<=H`, `V>=0`. No incomplete candle or synthetic gap filling. The following researcher-defined indicator conventions are frozen:

1. **EMA(n) of close**, for `n=12,26,50`: first defined at `t=n-1` as `mean(C[0..n-1])`; thereafter `EMA_n[t]=(2/(n+1))*C[t]+(1-2/(n+1))*EMA_n[t-1]`. Earlier values are undefined, not zero.
2. **MACD histogram:** `M[t]=EMA12[t]-EMA26[t]` first defined at `t=25`. `S[t]=EMA9(M)[t]` first defined at `t=33` as `mean(M[25..33])`, then recursive EMA with alpha `2/10`. `Hist[t]=M[t]-S[t]`. MACD histogram must be strictly positive for entry; equality zero fails.
3. **RSI14 Wilder:** `delta[t]=C[t]-C[t-1]` for `t>=1`; `gain=max(delta,0)`, `loss=max(-delta,0)`. Seed `avgGain[14]=mean(gain[1..14])`, `avgLoss[14]=mean(loss[1..14])`; subsequently `avgGain[t]=(13*avgGain[t-1]+gain[t])/14` and analogously for loss. If both averages zero RSI=50; if loss=0 and gain>0 RSI=100; otherwise `RSI=100-100/(1+avgGain/avgLoss)`. The long band requires **strict** `50<RSI14[t]<70`.
4. **ATR14 Wilder:** `TR[0]=H[0]-L[0]`; `TR[t]=max(H[t]-L[t], abs(H[t]-C[t-1]), abs(L[t]-C[t-1]))` for `t>=1`. Seed `ATR[14]=mean(TR[1..14])`, then `ATR[t]=(13*ATR[t-1]+TR[t])/14`. Require `ATR[t]>0` at entry; a zero/nonfinite ATR disables the entry, never fabricates a bracket.
5. **Combined long predicate** `E[t]=(C[t]>EMA50[t]) AND (Hist[t]>0) AND (50<RSI14[t]<70) AND (ATR14[t]>0)`. **Fresh edge** `NewE[t]=E[t] AND NOT E[t-1]`; `E[t-1]` is calculated only from previously completed bar data. If any component is undefined, `E=false`. A sustained true state does not trigger repeated buys.
6. **Warmup and causality:** exactly the first **100 fully completed, contiguous, validated 15m candles** (indices 0–99) are warmup-only. First eligible decision is `t=100`. Indicator recursion is built only from `<=t`; no future extrema, higher timeframe, future training, repaint, lookahead, intrabar close prediction or post-sample optimization. On a gap, duplicate/invalid timestamp, malformed OHLCV, incomplete final candle, missing actual close or insufficient actual buffered history, **abort this research segment** without synthetic candles or invented orders; restart a new clean 100-bar warmup only in a separately declared segment with initially FLAT state (do not silently discard an existing LONG).
7. **Entry:** Start `FLAT`, zero BTC, `cash=Decimal("10000") USDT`. On a completed bar `t>=100`, if FLAT, no pending order, `NewE[t]`, and `cash >= Q*C[t]*(1+f)`, issue one BUY MARKET `Q=Decimal("0.001") BTC` at that row's **actual observed completed close `P=C[t]`** in the pinned historical same-close simulator, `f=Decimal("0.001")` hypothetical fee each filled side. On confirmed full fill set `entryBar=t`, `stop=P-ATR14[t]`, `target=P+2*ATR14[t]`, state LONG(Q); `stop` and `target` remain **frozen**. Unaffordable signal is skipped and the edge discarded. Missing/failed/partial/ambiguous fill aborts instead of inventing inventory or price.
8. **Exit priority:** Only on a **subsequent completed bar** `u>entryBar`, while LONG and with no pending order: if `C[u]<=stop`, close all Q with one SELL MARKET at actual `C[u]` (reason CLOSE_STOP, priority 1); else if `C[u]>=target`, close all Q at actual `C[u]` (reason CLOSE_TARGET, priority 2); else hold. The stop and target are **close thresholds, not intrabar stop/limit orders**. Never assume an intrabar touch fill, a fill at the threshold, a guaranteed 1x ATR maximum loss or a 2x ATR realized gain; gaps can materially exceed those distances. If both predicates somehow cofire due to invalid state, stop wins and the inconsistent state is flagged.
9. **Position/re-entry:** After a confirmed SELL, return FLAT, credit `Q*C[u]*(1-f)`, record `lastExitBar=u`. No BUY on that same bar. Re-entry requires a new `NewE[v]` with `v>u`; signals occurring while LONG or during an unresolved action are discarded, not queued. No shorts, reversal, pyramiding, concurrent positions, scaling, DCA, safety orders, dynamic equity sizing, cooldown, time exit, trailing stop, extra opposite-MACD exit, intrabar orders, maker/limit, leverage, margin, funding or sample-end forced liquidation. These are intentionally **disabled researcher decisions**, not asserted source defaults. At the final valid close an open Q remains marked-to-market and separately reported, not fictitiously closed.
10. **Cash/fees/accounting:** BUY `cash -= Q*P*(1+f)`; SELL `cash += Q*C[u]*(1-f)`. Gross round-trip `Q*(C[u]-P)`; total modeled fees `f*Q*(P+C[u])`; net realized PnL is gross minus both-side fees. Verify filled Q and available base before SELL, cash nonnegative, and reconcile the actual Hummingbot `BacktestPositionHold` gross realized, remaining inventory and `cum_fees_quote` with the research Decimal ledger. Fail closed on unaccounted fills, nonfinite prices, position mismatch or unsupported precision. Model slippage=0 is **only a historical simulator assumption**, not measured market execution; 0.1% is **hypothetical**, not the exchange's verified fee tier. Spot has no perpetual funding/margin; do not record zero as observed futures funding.
