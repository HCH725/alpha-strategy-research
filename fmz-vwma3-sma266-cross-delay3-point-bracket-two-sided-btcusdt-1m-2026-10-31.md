## Economic mechanism

### Source-reported

Fast-slow consensus-cross ignition with a lagged trigger and a fixed payoff box, two-sided. A 3-bar volume-weighted average is the nervous consensus; a 266-bar simple average is the slow consensus. A golden cross (fast piercing the slow from below) is read as uptrend ignition; a death cross as downtrend ignition. Entries wait three candles after the edge to shake out whipsaw (the prose's explicit lagged-entry doctrine). Risk is a fixed point box: 10000 ticks of profit target against 2000 ticks of stop (5:1 nominal). The prose block adds an explicit risk admission: single-indicator blindness, signal drought in strong trends (price running long above/below the fast line), parameter instability across products and periods, and unmodeled sizing/stop granularity. The source ships no sample statistics, trade count, fee basis, win rate, or performance figure of any kind — a full-token census for Sharpe/胜率/win-rate/profit-factor/drawdown/annualized returns zero performance tokens — no source-reported performance is adopted or restated.

### Research interpretation

Nervous-vs-patient consensus-cross machine with a three-bar confirmation fuse and a wide-target point bracket, event-driven, two-sided, flat except when a delayed edge fires. Unlike clock-only records (`monday-drift` — UTC day-of-week + hour predicates with time exits), there is no clock predicate anywhere here: quiet bars print nothing without the cross. Unlike day-open threshold records (`dual-thrust`, `holmberg-orb` — displacement measured against the 00:00 UTC open with first-breach entries), the test is consensus-relative (fast average against slow average, no open anchor, no breach direction vote). Unlike band-stretch records (`bollinger-band-breakout-long-midline-exit` — close crossing the SMA20 ± 1.5σ UPPER band with a midline-reclaim exit and no coded bracket), both trigger lines here are averages (not a dispersion-normalized stretch), and risk is a fixed tick-box bracket (not a midline reclaim). Unlike the sibling consensus-cross record (`fmz-tbma-sma20-cross` — raw close crossing its own SMA20 with a 7-low-anchored 2R bracket, long-only, 1d), this machine crosses two averages against each other (never price against its own mean), delays every entry by three bars, brackets both sides in fixed points (not footprint-anchored ratios), and runs two-sided on 1m. Unlike gate records (`alma-cross-volume-gate` — ALMA60/120 cross admitted only when a volume-oscillator gate reads positive, two-sided 1d; `adx-di-cross` — ADX/DI strength-gated cross, two-sided 1d), there is no gate anywhere here: the cross alone fires, and the only filter is the three-bar fuse. Unlike band records that also read volume weighting (`gkv-adaptive-vwma-band-breakout` — VWMA14 center ± Garman-Klass-scaled width, band-breakout entries, lower-band close exit, long-flat, 1d), this trigger is an average-vs-average cross edge (not a band touch), exits only on the fixed box (no band exit), and runs both directions. Unlike centerline records (`forven-vwap-rolling-cross` — rolling session-VWAP centerline cross, long-flat, 1h), this crosses a 3-bar VWMA against a 266-bar SMA with a delay fuse and a bracket, two-sided. Unlike open-PRs (`freqtrade-adx-smas` — SMA3/6 cross ADX-gated long-flat 1h; `larry-williams-3ema-channel` — 3-EMA channel streak long 1d), this cross is volume-weighted-fast vs 266-slow, ungated, delayed, bracketed, two-sided, 1m.

## Signal — Gate 1, fully pinned derivative

Index completed UTC minute bars `t = 0, 1, ...` with real Binance BTC-USDT futures `O[t], H[t], L[t], C[t], V[t]` (all five fields read; volume only inside VWMA). A bar with missing/nonfinite/non-positive prices or volume, or with `H < max(O,C)` / `L < min(O,C)`, is invalid; never interpolate. All inputs pinned at source defaults: `length_fast = 3`, `length_slow = 266`, `take_profit = 10000.0` ticks, `stop_loss = 2000.0` ticks (any other values are a different, unpinned rule).

- **Fast `VWMA3[t]` (t ≥ 2):** `sum(C[i]·V[i]) / sum(V[i])` over `i = t−2..t` (Pine `vwma` — closed-form exact window, no seed state; `na` before index 2, and `na` comparisons never fire — disclosed). The 3-bar window must be fully populated by consecutive valid bars with strictly positive total volume (a zero-volume window never occurs on BTCUSDT 1m; the pin governs it analytically — disclosed).
- **Slow `SMA266[t]` (t ≥ 265):** exact arithmetic mean of `C[t−265..t]` (Pine `sma` — non-recursive, no seed state; `na` before index 265).
- **Edge `BUY[t]` / `SELL[t]` (t ≥ 266):** `BUY[t] = F[t] > S[t] AND F[t−1] <= S[t−1]`; `SELL[t] = F[t] < S[t] AND F[t−1] >= S[t−1]` (Pine v4 `crossover`/`crossunder` semantics, file-literal construction — disclosed). Single pulse per bar per side by construction; both pulses on one bar is arithmetically impossible (strict `>` and strict `<` cannot co-hold — disclosed).
- **Delayed entry decision (t ≥ 269):** a long decision fires on bar `d` iff `BUY[d−3]`; a short decision fires iff `SELL[d−3]` (file-literal `[3]` history index on both sides — the three-bar fuse; structural first-entry decision bar is 269 — self-check verified first-signal 300 → first-entry 303 on a jump tape, and zero pre-266 signals over 2000 noise tapes).
- **Bracket legs (latched at the fill reference, never re-anchored):** for a long fill at reference `R` (the fill bar's open — see Execution assumptions), `TP = R + 1000.0`, `SL = R − 200.0`; for a short fill, `TP = R − 1000.0`, `SL = R + 200.0` (10000/2000 ticks × pinned tickSize 0.10 — the record's sole unit mapping). Levels never move after the fill bar (latched box, NOT a ratchet, NOT floating).
- **Exit precedence (explicit researcher pin):** from the fill bar onward, each held bar resolves the box first-touch against the bar's `H/L` with the **stop-first** same-bar rule (both touched → stop price wins). An opposite delayed edge arriving while holding REVERSES the book at the next open (close at next-open, open opposite at the same next-open, old box cancelled, new box latched at the new reference) — Pine `pyramiding = 0` default reversal pinned as an explicit gate, same pin class as the merged two-sided Super-Scalper/SMMA17/PowerZone/candle-size/staircase records.
- **Gap/contiguity pin (explicit):** the 266-bar and 3-bar windows must be fully populated by consecutive valid calendar bars; a gap pauses signals until the windows refill (no interpolation, no carry-forward of stale windows — the stateless construction makes this the only consistent reading — disclosed).

**Direction (Gate 2):** BUY-to-open / SELL-to-close on the long leg; SELL-to-open / BUY-to-close on the short leg. Both legs ship in the file (`strategy.long` + `strategy.short`). The two-sided stance on the pinned futures venue is realistic and needs no spot-short permission. No hedge, no cross-market trade.

**Risk (Gate 3, explicit):** latched 1000/200-USDT point box per side. Fixed 200-USDT stop and fixed 1000-USDT target latched at each fill reference (5:1 nominal). No trailing ratchet, no breakeven move, no time stop, no partial exit, no averaging, no forced end-of-data liquidation, no silent executor default. An end-of-sample open position is marked at the last real valid close, not closed with an invented fill.

**State (Gate 4):** `FLAT`, `BUY_PENDING`, `SELL_PENDING`, `LONG`, `SHORT` — plus no counters (stateless signal). Initialize `FLAT` after 280 valid completed bars. Entries fire only from confirmed `FLAT` (fresh edge) or as an explicit reversal from the opposite held side; a missing/failed/unconfirmed fill **halts** this research run, never synthesizes inventory. A pending order prohibits additional actions. **No DCA/safety orders (explicitly disabled — the downstream 6%+6%+6% house overlay is not active in this admission and never silently overrides single-unit semantics)**, no pyramiding, no concurrency, no compounding. No cooldown is specified (explicitly none, not invented) — re-entry needs a fresh delayed edge. A position whose box never touches is held indefinitely with no time exit (disclosed as a principal holding risk alongside prose-admitted signal drought and chop whipsaw).
