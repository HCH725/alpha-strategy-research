## Economic mechanism

### Source-reported

Medium-normalized moving-average needle cross with dual-EMA regime filter, by the author's wished-for DIDI reading: the `short`/`long` ratios divide the 3- and 20-bar SMAs by the 8-bar SMA (the DIDI "needle" is the fast/medium/slow convergence), and a cross of the two ratios fires only when price and the 35-bar EMA both stand on the same side of the 150-bar EMA. Risk is a self-scaling box: the stop is the 15-bar swing extreme, the two targets are 1.5 and 3.0 ATRs beyond the signal bar's extreme, half the book leaves at TP1 and the runner's stop moves to breakeven. The page description corroborates entries plus ATR-TP structure (with stale multipliers, fenced above). The script ships no stop/target philosophy beyond the levels, no regime theory beyond the EMA pair, and no performance figure.

### Research interpretation

Unlike pool dual-EMA-cross records (raw price-average crosses — this machine crosses two *medium-normalized ratios*, so the trigger is dimensionless convergence, never a price level), unlike pool EMA-band records (distance objects — the EMA150/35 pair here is a pure side gate, never a distance), and unlike pool ATR-box siblings (fixed-unit boxes — this box mixes a swing-anchored stop with ATR-anchored targets plus a 50%-at-TP1 split and a breakeven runner, a three-stage exit object no pool record trades). The edge object is gate × needle × staged box: trend-side permission (close and EMA35 above/below EMA150), dimensionless timing (ratio cross), and a box that de-risks halfway then rides free. Live-window signature is SL-heavy by construction: 1,355 entries / 1,355 exits across 62,543 post-warmup 1h bars (929 SL / 749 TP1-legs / 411 TP2 / 15 reversals — the 15-bar swing stop sits closer than the 1.5-ATR first target in 1h noise, disclosed, not tuned). The natgas-tuned defaults are frozen as shipped — this record makes no crypto-specific tuning claim.

## Signal

Index completed UTC 1-hour bars `t = 0, 1, ...` with real Binance BTC-USDT futures `C[t]` for signals/averages/closes, `H[t]`/`L[t]` for extreme/box-touch legs, `O[t]` for fills, `V[t]` never read by any order-gating line. A bar with missing/nonfinite/non-positive OHLC, or `H < max(O,C)` / `L < min(O,C)`, is invalid for signals. All constants are shipped literals (zero tuned inputs).

- **Averages (causal, per-bar, Pine-faithful):** `SMA3/8/20[t]` = 20/8/3-bar means of `C` (na until full — bars 0–18 na-gated, never interpolated); `E150[t]`, `E35[t]` = Pine-`ta.ema` recursion seeded at bar 0, actionable only `t ≥ 154` by record rule; `ATR[t]` = RMA(14) of true range (same recursion, same gate); `SL15[t]` = lowest low / highest high over `t-14..t`.
- **Predicates (actionable only t ≥ 154, at bar `t`'s close):** `short[t]=SMA3[t]/SMA8[t]`, `long[t]=SMA20[t]/SMA8[t]`; `bull[t] = (short[t]>long[t] and short[t-1]<=long[t-1]) and (C[t]>E150[t]) and (E35[t]>E150[t])`; `bear[t]` mirrored (`<`, `>=`, below, below). Strict inequalities match source exactly (equality never fires; the dead-constant tape proves the all-flat path).
- **Dynamic box (levels frozen at the signal bar, never moved inside the test bar):** long `SL=SL15_low[t]`, `TP1=H[t]+1.5·ATR[t]`, `TP2=H[t]+3.0·ATR[t]`; short mirrored on `SL15_high[t]` / `L[t]−1.5/3.0·ATR[t]`.
- **Resolution (explicit researcher pin):** entries/reversals fill next-bar-open MARKET; each held bar `v` (fill bar +1 onward) evaluates at its close in code order — opposite signal first (reversal dominates: close old and open new at the same next open, counting one exit plus one entry), else SL-touch (`L[v]≤SL` long / `H[v]≥SL` short), else TP-touch (`H[v]≥TP1/TP2` long / `L[v]≤TP1/TP2` short); SL+TP co-fire is one flat close (stop wins). TP1 touch halves the book at the next open and arms the runner (`stop := entry print`); TP2/SL then close the remainder. Single position, no adds: same-side refire is a no-op (verified on tape — gated crosses never re-fire into a held side because the `position_size` legs block them).
