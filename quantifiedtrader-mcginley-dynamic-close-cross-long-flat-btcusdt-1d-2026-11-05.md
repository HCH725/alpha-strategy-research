class McGinleyDynamic(Strategy):

## Economic mechanism

### Source-reported

Self-adjusting smoothing-speed trend composite, long side only. The McGinley line speeds up when price stretches from its prior print (the `(C/MG)⁴` divisor shrinks the effective window in fast markets) and slows down when price hugs it, so the rail tracks without the lag-whip tradeoff of a fixed EMA; the position opens when the close crosses above its own adaptive rail (trend confirmed at the rail) and ends it on the reverse cross. The equity-index universe and the unmodeled-costs stance are source facts, not this record's venue or economics. No performance figure is carried anywhere.

### Research interpretation

Close raced against its own speed-adaptive shadow — the smoothing cousin of the lane's fixed-average cross family (QStick body-cross, Elder-Ray, DPO) and the adaptive sibling of the pool's FRAMA/KAMA/VIDYA-CMO machines (fenced — different adaptivity: fractal-dimension, efficiency-ratio, and CMO-slope triggers, never a price-ratio-powered window). Unlike fixed-EMA cross machines, the rail's effective window breathes between fast and slow prints, so entries key off trend confirmation relative to a rail that hurries to meet breakouts and dawdles through chop. Unlike level-state machines the entry is a one-bar cross edge — consecutive above-rail bars enter exactly once on the crossing bar and hold silently after. Unlike stop-carrying trend machines there is no capital guardrail besides the reverse cross. The failure mode is plain: a dead-constant tape pins Close and MG at the identical print (seeded equal, zero displacement, zero update), so the strict edge never arms (planted dead-flat tape: 0 entries / 0 exits ending FLAT — disclosed, not patched); sustained geometric ramps ride permanently above (or below) the hurrying rail after the single seed cross ages out inside the warmup (planted ±1%/day tapes print 0 entries / 0 exits ending FLAT — disclosed never-trades degeneration by construction, deterministic, not patched); chop that weaves the close through its shadow prints repeated cross/reverse round trips with no stop and no profit-taking leg (the planted chop tape exercises entry, exit, and re-entry: 11 entries / 11 exits ending FLAT — disclosed).
