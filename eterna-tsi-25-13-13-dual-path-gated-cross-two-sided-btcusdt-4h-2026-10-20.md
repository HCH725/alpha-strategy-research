## Economic mechanism

### Source-reported

Double-smoothed momentum with level-aware entries (strategy README: `TSI reduces noise through its double smoothing technique while maintaining sensitivity to significant price changes`; `Zero-line provides strong trend bias`; `Less whipsaws than single-smoothed indicators`): the raw signal-line cross is only tradeable on the trend side of zero (bull crosses below zero, bear crosses above zero), and an extreme cross carries its own bounce leg (oversold-up cross, overbought-down cross). Either leg exits on the opposite leg's print. There is deliberately no stop, no target, no trailing exit, and no always-in-market claim beyond post-signal holds — the order block ends with the two `strategy.close` guards and no risk leg ships anywhere, by design gap rather than by hidden leg.

### Research interpretation

Gated-cross trend-continuation with an extreme-bounce second path, two-sided with opposite-signal flat exits — the first TSI-gated record in the pool. Unlike raw-cross systems that trade every signal-line touch, this rule never trades a cross on the wrong side of zero: the zero gate is a standing directional permission (bull crosses must print under zero, bear crosses over zero), so late-trend crosses that arrive after the oscillator has already crossed zero fire nothing. Unlike pure level systems, the oversold/overbought legs still require the cross itself plus a rising/falling confirm — a TSI sitting deep in extreme without turning prints no entry. Note the two pinned asymmetries, stated plainly: first, a cross that prints exactly while the oscillator straddles the gate (bull cross with `tsi ≥ 0`, bear cross with `tsi ≤ 0`) fires nothing unless the bounce leg's own strict predicates hold — mid-oscillator chop crosses are ignored by construction, so range-bound markets produce flat stretches; second, the exit needs the full opposite leg (a gated cross the other way), not merely losing the gate — a long entered under zero is held through the zero crossing itself until a bear leg prints, with no profit-taking leg and no trailing guard. The bet is on gated-cross persistence at frozen `25/13/13/±25` geometry, not on any breakout extreme, volume print, calendar effect, or second timeframe.
