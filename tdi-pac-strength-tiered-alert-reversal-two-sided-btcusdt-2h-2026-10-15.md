## Economic mechanism

### Source-reported

Strength-tiered trend-alert reversal (code header plus bilingual prose, no performance section ships): the Traders Dynamic Index compresses RSI momentum, its own moving averages, and RSI-space Bollinger geometry into one panel — the RSI price line (`mab`, 2-bar SMA of RSI-13) against the trade signal line (`mbb`, 7-bar SMA of RSI-13) inside a 34-bar 1.6185-sigma volatility band with a market-base midline. The Price Action Channel (5-bar SMMA of high/low) marks the tape's own support/resistance. An alert fires only when three witnesses agree: the TDI pair is stacked the right way inside the 22–78 RSI corridor, price stretches beyond the PAC rail on its side, and the candle body points the same way. Alerts are tiered strong/medium/weak by where the pair sits against the midline; the pinned `strength = 2` admits strong and medium tiers. The header's hints admit the design plainly: trade with the alert candle, skip ranging markets (flat near-zero MACD histogram), skip wide-PAC chop — but those hints are manual-discipline prose, and the executable block encodes none of them as order legs (fenced in Negative evidence). The prose's forex/commodity framing, 5-minute-chart suggestion, and binary-options expiry advice describe the author's original venue, never this record's pinned market.

### Research interpretation

Triple-conjunction edge-triggered reversal machine, two-sided, single-unit, always-in-market after seeding. Unlike every cross record in the pool (MACD-cross, DSS never-crosses, StochRSI-cross, KDJ) this system never trades a line cross: its only comparisons are level-and-corridor conjunctions, and the order legs fire on rising edges of a tiered state (`L != L[1]`), so a bar where the pair merely holds its stack fires nothing. Unlike every breakout record (Donchian, Darvas, Turtle-55d, NR7, BB-breakout) the breakout leg alone is insufficient — a PAC-rail stretch without the TDI stack, the corridor, and the body-direction witnesses prints no alert by construction. Unlike stop-carrying records there is no protective leg: the book flips only on the opposite alert edge, so a vertical-against move that never prints the opposite conjunction is ridden in full. The edge, if any, lives in the documented intuition that TDI-stack + PAC-stretch + body-agreement on 2h crypto bars marks push-exhaustion worth fading with the indicator's own tier map as chaperone — a bet on witnessed reversal, not on any trend level, volume print, funding regime, session, or second timeframe.
