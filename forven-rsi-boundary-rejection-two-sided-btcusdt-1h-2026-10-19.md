### Research interpretation

Touch-and-prove bounce machine, two-sided, confirmation-gated both legs. Unlike level-hold RSI records (classic 30/60: parked-below-30 is itself the state; cumulative-RSI: summed extremes), this system never fires on the touch bar itself — the touch only opens a 3-bar probation window, and the entry prints only when RSI proves the rejection by recrossing the inner confirm line (35 up / 65 down) while price holds the EMA200 side. Unlike single-cross RSI records (S00139 washout: one cross event with no touch memory), every entry here carries a causal memory of an extreme printed within the trailing 3 bars — chop that wiggles across 35 without ever visiting 30 is turned away. Unlike dip-buy long-only records (catching-the-bottom: RSI<40 plus a 3-point fall plus an SMA crossunder), the gate here is trend-alignment rather than trend-change: price must already sit on the entry side of EMA200, so the system fades the overextension back toward the prevailing trend instead of buying a breakdown. The coded consequence (Negative evidence 1) is a rental book: because the fallthrough exit fires on any off-EMA non-entry bar, a position survives only across consecutive confirming bars — entries are picky, and exits are impatient.

## Signal

Exact rule as pinned (any other RSI variant, period, threshold, window, gate, exit leg, fill, frame, book, or ordering is a different, unpinned rule). All identifiers below are the program's own; the single `generate_signal` path (lines 89–171) is the single source of truth:

```text
rsi[t]      = Wilder RSI(14) on close: ewm(alpha=1/14, adjust=False) gains/losses, zero-loss floored at 1e-10
ema200[t]   = close.ewm(span=200, adjust=False).mean()
touch_lo    = any(rsi[t-2..t] <= 30)            // trailing 3-bar window incl. decision bar
touch_hi    = any(rsi[t-2..t] >= 70)
long_entry  = touch_lo AND (rsi[t] > 35) AND (close[t] > ema200[t])
short_entry = touch_hi AND (rsi[t] < 65) AND (close[t] < ema200[t])
long_exit   = close[t] < ema200[t]              // fallthrough only
short_exit  = close[t] > ema200[t]              // fallthrough only
guard       = fewer than 200 completed bars → flat by source rule
```

## Crypto portability

Direct for the long leg, margin-gated for the short leg: single-venue BTCUSDT perpetual `1h` completed bars with a standard close series (the rule reads `close` alone — no high/low/open/volume dependency at all). No session calendar (crypto trades continuously; the 3-bar touch window needs no session-gap semantics), no corporate actions, no dividends, no funding/OI reads, no cross-venue state. 24/7 continuity means every window bar is always the immediately prior print — no weekend-gap discontinuity to normalize. Tick-size rounding follows the venue; the record's 4-decimal price echo is display precision only and changes no event. Leverage/margin follow the house overlay, not the source's bare `3.0` default. The short leg requires a margin venue (perpetual qualifies under the house overlay); a spot-only deployment expresses the long leg alone and would not be the same rule.
