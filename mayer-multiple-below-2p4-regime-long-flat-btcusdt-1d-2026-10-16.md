## Economic mechanism

### Source-reported

Long-run trend-distance valuation with a bubble partition. All four sources frame the Multiple as price distance from its own 200-day trend: BeThePuck (`Price divided by its 200-day average`, `below 2.4` accumulation vs `bubble territory`); CoinDesk (the `price significantly exceeds its intrinsic value` bubble vs `seller exhaustion` framing, the `2.4` bubble line, the below-`2.4` accumulation finding); Alpha Finance (`whether Bitcoin is expensive or cheap relative to its own recent history`, `needs nothing but the price`); the TradingView script (a backtestable level system on the same ratio). No source prints any economic rationale beyond this distance-from-trend description, and no source ships any stop, target, or risk paragraph — exits are regime-driven only.

### Research interpretation

Single-asset slow-trend participation book, long-flat with a bubble gate. `MM[t] = C[t] / SMA200[t]` reads how far the daily close sits above its own 200-day mean: below `2.4` the book participates (long); at or above `2.4` the market is in the sources' bubble partition and the book stands aside (flat). Unlike pool `faber-gtaa` (price vs its own 10-month average of month-end closes on a monthly grid with a `1.0` line — a monthly-regime object, never a daily `2.4` bubble gate); unlike pool `golden-cross-sma-regime` (a fast/medium dual-average cross event on `1h` bars — a cross object, never a price-vs-own-average level); unlike open-PR #251 (Swift 2-year MA multiplier `1x–5x` zones on a ~730-day average — a multi-zone object on a 3.65× slower average, never a single `2.4` line on SMA200); unlike pool `bollinger-band-breakout`, `keltner-momentum-breakout`, `starc-band-breakout`, `nw-endpoint-envelope` (volatility-scaled bands that expand/contract — never a fixed-multiple ratio); unlike pool `nvi-255ema-regime` (a cumulative volume-filtered line vs its own 255-EMA — a volume-regime object that never reads price distance); unlike oscillator-threshold records (`rsi-classic`, `mfi-threshold`, `williams-r-level-cross` — bounded 0–100 oscillators, never an unbounded price ratio). Magnitude beyond the threshold never sizes the book — only the side trades (single position), so parabolic extensions deeper into the bubble zone change timing, never exposure. The failure mode is plain: a bubble that keeps running while the book sits flat is missed in full, and a breakdown immediately after re-entry is ridden with no guardrail — disclosed, not upgraded.

## Signal

Exact rule as pinned (any other series, average variant, length, threshold, mapping, fill, frame, book, or tie treatment is a different, unpinned rule):

- Inputs (pinned): `close` on completed `1d` bars only — BeThePuck's rule sentence keys off `the close` against `its 200-day average`; Alpha Finance pins the denominator as the `200-day simple moving average` of price. No `open`, no `high`, no `low`, no `volume`, no funding/OI/mark-index, trade, book, on-chain, options, sentiment, macro, session, calendar, breadth, or cross-venue state is read by any order-gating line.
- Construction (pinned): `SMA200[t] = mean(C[t−199..t])` (Simple, closes only, 200 prints); `MM[t] = C[t] / SMA200[t]`. Crypto closes and hence every SMA200 print are strictly positive, so the quotient is always finite on live tape; any non-finite input maps to NaN → flat by record rule, never interpolated or seeded.
- State machine, evaluated once per completed bar in fixed priority (strict on the entry leg): (0) non-finite `MM[t]` → target FLAT; (1) `MM[t] < 2.4` → target LONG (enter if flat, hold if long); (2) `MM[t] >= 2.4` → target FLAT (exit if long, hold if flat). Exact `2.4` prints take the flat path (exit, never entry). Under the single-position no-pyramid book, level-hold and cross-counting entries are event-identical; the pin is level-with-no-op, never a cross-counting variant.
- Exits: regime exit only (rule 2), plus the NaN flat (rule 0). No stop-loss, no take-profit, no trailing, no time exit (no source ships any risk leg — carried over exactly, disclosed in Provenance adaptation (4), never upgraded into a source claim).
- Direction: long-flat overall (long in the below-`2.4` regime, flat in the bubble partition and on undefined prints; the short side is explicitly disabled by the source In/Out pair, not by researcher choice, and no short order exists in this spec).
- Position rule (predeclared researcher pin): single-position 100%-equity long-or-flat at 1× with no leverage; already-long bars repeating the long regime are no-ops (no pyramiding, no averaging, no scaling by Multiple magnitude); flat-regime bars while flat are no-ops. Re-entry re-arms on the next below-`2.4` bar after a flat exit only (no cooldown specified — explicitly none, not invented). Pre-first-signal bars (before the adopted warmup completes) and any NaN-multiple bars are flat by record rule.

- No price stop, no time stop: a breakdown immediately after a below-`2.4` entry is ridden in full until the next `>= 2.4` print or the next below-`2.4` bar — by the carried-over source design — the record's principal risk.
