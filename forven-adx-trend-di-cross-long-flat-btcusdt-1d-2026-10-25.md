## Economic mechanism

### Source-reported

The source claims only what its code prints: hold long exposure entered when the 14-bar ADX reads above 25 while the smoothed +DI crosses up through the smoothed −DI, stand back to flat when the ADX falls back under 25 or the −DI crosses back up through the +DI. The strength gate and the cross trigger are a conjunction on entry but independent legs on exit — a strong-trend +DI cross arms the long, while either strength decay or an opposite cross disarms it. No stop, no target, no trailing, no time exit, no sizing, no cost basis ships anywhere — this record adopts no source-reported performance (see Evidence).

### Research interpretation

Trend-following cross-catcher with a strength chaperone, long-only, edge-triggered entry with a mixed edge/level exit, zero-stop. The entry leg fires only on fresh +DI crosses (a sustained +DI-over-−DI hold re-stamps nothing), and only while ADX clears 25, so entries are selective by construction: directionless chop that never lifts ADX above 25 never arms, and a strong trend whose DI cross printed bars ago is watched, not chased. Verified this cycle on an 800-bar synthetic tape (pinned seed 99): 13 entries against 377 exits — the exit leg is level-dominant (any bar with ADX under 25 exits, even without a cross) and fires an order of magnitude more often, usually from flat (absorbed as no-ops by the position pin). The failure mode is plain: a trend that ignites (ADX > 25) after the +DI cross has already printed is missed until the next fresh cross, and a slow ADX decay under 25 exits a still-rising trend with no reclaim except a brand-new cross (the exit reads strength alone on that leg — price direction never vetoes it).

## Signal

Exact rule as pinned (any other ADX/DI length, threshold, frame, fill, book, or risk posture is a different, unpinned rule). `ADX(14)` is the scanner ewm directional system; the ±DI pair is the file's own `span=14, adjust=False` ewm smoothing over `atr(df, 14)`:

```text
adx_strong_t   = (ADX14_t > 25)                                  // strict
plus_cross_t   = (plus_DI_{t-1} <= minus_DI_{t-1}) & (plus_DI_t > minus_DI_t)    // EDGE
minus_cross_t  = (minus_DI_{t-1} <= plus_DI_{t-1}) & (minus_DI_t > plus_DI_{t-1}) // EDGE
entry_t        = adx_strong_t & plus_cross_t
exit_t         = (ADX14_t < 25) | minus_cross_t                  // level OR edge
```

- Trade events: `entry_signal` on a fresh +DI-cross bar with the ADX gate green; `direction` long-only; `exit_signal` on any bar with ADX under 25 or a fresh −DI-cross bar. A flat book enters one full unit (`0.001` BTC) on an entry bar; a long book exits the full unit on the first exit-leg bar.

## Limitations

- Exit-heavy by construction: the ADX-under-25 level leg fires on any weak-trend bar — verified 377 exits per 800 synthetic bars against 13 entries. Live `1d` tape may print long flat stretches punctuated by brief longs; exits firing from flat are absorbed as no-ops, disclosed.
- Completed-bar exit latency: a bar whose ADX collapses from 40 to 15 triggers the exit on that same completed bar, with the full-unit exit at that bar's effective close under the declared derived fill model. There is no intrabar stop order to front-run that close, disclosed.
- Late-entry profile: a trend that lifts ADX above 25 after the +DI cross has already printed is missed until the next fresh cross — the entry never chases a held +DI-over-−DI stance, disclosed.
- Strength-decay whipsaw: a dip of ADX to 24.9 exits a still-rising trend, and re-entry demands a brand-new +DI cross — chop around the threshold can print exit/entry pairs with no price-continuation requirement, disclosed.
- No risk leg: no stop loss, take profit, ROI rung, trailing-stop order, or time limit exists in source — recorded as explicitly disabled; adverse excursions have no capital guardrail besides the ADX-decay/opposite-cross exit.
- Downtrend blindness: declines are watched from flat with no short participation — the zero-`short` posture is a stance choice with a return cost in sustained downtrends, disclosed.
- Single-market scope: derived pin is BTCUSDT spot `1d` only; portability to other symbols/timeframes is untested and claimed nowhere.
- Research overlay dependence: fills, sizing, and costs follow the derived pins and the stated economics identity — not the source engine's own semantics, and not the engine summary labels fenced above.
- No trailing, no breakeven, no short leg, no cooldown — all explicitly absent, never to be added by repair.
