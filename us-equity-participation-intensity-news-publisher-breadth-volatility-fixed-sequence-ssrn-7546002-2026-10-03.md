## Economic mechanism

### Source-reported

The paper's stated framing (Sections 1 and 5): online market discourse has at least two separable dimensions - **what participants say** (tone / sentiment) and **how much participation occurs** (activity, breadth, concentration). Participation is explicitly *unsigned*: `participation is unsigned. A surge in attention or discussion can mark a consequential market state without implying the direction of the next return`, which is why the source picks subsequent volatility rather than subsequent return as the outcome. The source cites Antweiler and Frank (2004) for message-board activity and later volatility, Barber and Odean (2008), Da, Engelberg and Gao (2011), Tetlock (2007), Sprenger et al. (2014), Behrendt and Schmidt (2018), Audrino, Sigrist and Ballinari (2020), Jiao, Veiga and Walther (2020) and Ballinari, Audrino and Sigrist (2022), and explicitly disclaims novelty for the broad claim: `This paper does not claim novelty for the broad proposition that attention, online activity, or media coverage can relate to later volatility.` The claimed contribution is narrower - a **point-in-time participation framework for heterogeneous historical media data** that separates social activity, publisher breadth, contributor breadth and contributor concentration while conditioning on contemporaneous sentiment and conventional market context.

The claim under test is a nested-increment claim, not a raw correlation: does the block {social activity intensity, news publisher breadth} add information about subsequent 10-session realized volatility **beyond** sentiment, recent volatility, the anchor return, relative volume, and asset/date fixed effects (Section 1)? The ordered sequence then admits contributor breadth (H2) and contributor concentration (H3) only if the preceding gates open (Section 3.1).

### Research interpretation

Normalized as a falsifiable, unsigned, horizon-specific prediction:

- **H-participation (magnitude):** a same-day, prior-normalized count of *how many distinct sources and publishers* produced timing-safe, positive, asset-specific content carries incremental information about **future realized variance** that contemporaneous sentiment and volatility persistence already do not carry. Candidate mechanism: participation breadth is a shock-arrival / disagreement-intensity proxy - heterogeneous beliefs and information production raise future trading and price revision, so volatility rises even when the *sign* of the news is neutralized by controlling for sentiment. This is a **risk-forecast** claim only; the source itself states the design `does not establish causation, directional-price predictability, or trading profitability`.
- **Component roles (hybrid structure as decoded from the source):**
  - Regime / timing qualification: collection-lag ceiling `L` (24 hours primary; 6 and 72 hours as sensitivities) applied as `0 <= (timestamp_collected AT TIME ZONE 'America/Los_Angeles') - created_at <= L`, with negative lags failing closed.
  - Primary signal block (H1): `social_activity_intensity_robust_z` plus `news_publisher_breadth_robust_z`.
  - Secondary signals, gated: `social_contributor_breadth_robust_z` (H2), `social_contributor_hhi` (H3).
  - Controls: contemporaneous `combined_compound` sentiment, `ln(1 + prior_RV10)`, absolute anchor log return, relative volume, asset fixed effects, anchor-date fixed effects.
  - Outcome: `Y10 = ln(1 + RV10)` with `RV10 = sqrt((252/10) * sum r(t+k)^2)` over the next 10 sessions (Section 2.8).
- **What is not claimed by us:** no component is asserted to be tradable, and the source reports that the primary joint test **fails** its own threshold. Do not assume any component adds return; gates F4-F9 below exist for exactly that reason. Whether participation adds anything *economically* (as opposed to statistically) is separately gated in F13.

## Signal

Everything in this section is **source-reported** unless explicitly tagged `research-proposed`. The source specifies **no entry, no exit, no holding period and no sizing rule**: it is a conditional-mean panel regression, not a trading rule. Any operational rule that would turn these coefficients into a position is `research-proposed` and is labeled below.

**Formation timestamp and availability (Section 2.2, 2.3).** Source families: Reddit, Bluesky and News; `X was treated as legacy-only`, `YouTube was forward-only and was not retrospectively inferred into this study`. Same-date market alignment is anchored to `the UTC calendar date of the content event timestamp`; `Weekend, holiday, and after-hours observations are not remapped to later market sessions`. Event time, provider indexing time, collection time and calendar date are distinguished explicitly: for Bluesky, `created_at` is the canonical event instant, `indexed_at` is the provider indexing instant used only as a quality-control bridge, `timestamp_collected` is a Pacific-local wall clock normalized with `AT TIME ZONE 'America/Los_Angeles'`, `date_utc` is a calendar alignment field explicitly `not used as an intraday event clock`, `negative collection lag fails closed`, and `no blanket fixed-hour offset is applied`. The UTC date of `created_at` remains the event-date key, kept separate from the collection-availability rule. Primary social collection-lag ceiling is 24 hours; timing sensitivities use 6 hours and 72 hours.

**Lookback / parameterization (Sections 2.1, 2.5, 2.6, 2.7 - all source-reported):**

- Base anchor requires: (1) same-date non-null contemporaneous sentiment (`combined_compound`); (2) `at least 90 prior market-session keys`; (3) `at least 10 later market-session keys`. At a given lag ceiling an anchor enters H1 only when **both** social activity intensity and news publisher breadth are supportable under prior-only history rules.
- Social activity intensity, per source, ten fixed steps: retain the 60 most recent prior positive source observations; require at least 20 prior positive observations; estimate a prior-only 99th-percentile cap (linear interpolation at index `(n-1)*0.99`); cap the current positive count at that p99; apply `log1p`; compute the prior median and MAD on log1p prior counts; scale MAD by `1.4826`; form a robust z-score; if MAD is zero, assign 0 when the current transformed value equals the prior median and otherwise assign the signed z-cap; clip to `[-3, +3]`. The anchor-level measure is `the equal mean of available qualifying Reddit and Bluesky components`.
- News publisher breadth: the count of distinct non-empty News publishers (`articles_tbl.source`) for the same asset/date, transformed under the same prior-only 60-observation, minimum-20, p99-cap, log1p, median/MAD robust-z procedure. The source stresses `This is a breadth measure, not generic news volume.`
- Contributor breadth (H2): Reddit and Bluesky contributor counts standardized **within source** under the same prior-only robust-z policy, then equally averaged - `Raw cross-platform contributor counts are therefore not pooled directly`; contributor identity is `source-local and platform-namespaced; no cross-platform person matching is performed`.
- Contributor concentration (H3): HHI over timing-safe social-item shares by identified contributor. Eligibility requires `at least two timing-safe social-item`, `at least one identified contributor`, and identified-contributor coverage of `at least 80%` of timing-safe social items.
- Missing or unqualified source evidence `remained unresolved rather than being recoded as zero` and `does not enter the anchor-level mean` (Sections 1, 2.1, 2.5).

**Estimation, hypotheses and inference (Sections 3.1, 3.2, 3.3):** linear panel OLS with an explicit intercept, asset fixed effects and anchor-date fixed effects; baseline and enhanced models use `identical complete-case rows`; two-way cluster-robust covariance by asset and anchor date via the inclusion-exclusion form `asset cluster meat + date cluster meat - asset/date intersection meat`, each meat carrying the finite correction `G/(G-1) * (N-1)/(N-K)`; Wald p-values use the asymptotic chi-square reference and coefficient intervals use the normal reference with the same covariance. Two-sided fixed-sequence gatekeeping at `alpha=0.05` (Westfall and Krishen, 2001): H1 is a joint two-degree-of-freedom Wald test; H2 is `confirmatory only if H1 rejects`; H3 is `confirmatory only if H1 and H2 reject`. The tested H1/H2/H3 covariance blocks are positive definite, full rank and positive on the diagonal, while `Full covariance matrices are indefinite for some nuisance fixed-effect coefficients` - treated by the source as a limitation, not as a reason to change the estimator. Two H1/H2 sentiment entries are non-finite NaN and are dropped for complete-case eligibility, reducing H1 from 6,865 to 6,863 and H2 from 6,817 to 6,815; H3 remains 6,242.

**Sensitivity suite (Section 3.4, prespecified):** equal-total-weight-by-asset estimation; leave-one-asset-out estimation; joint exclusion of the five most represented assets (`COIN, NVDA, GOOGL, TSLA, AAPL`); 6-hour timing; 72-hour timing; a 5-session volatility outcome. These `are sensitivity checks and do not replace the 24-hour / 10-session primary result`.

**Secondary inference triangulation (Section 4.6, bounded):** after the primary and sensitivity analyses were complete, a package was specified before execution reusing `the exact 6,863 H1 complete-case observations, the same Y10 outcome, the same baseline controls and fixed effects, the same two-variable H1 block, and the same two-way clustering`, with `No new outcome, horizon, subset, predictor, transformation, or model family`. Three diagnostics: the primary two-way clustered Wald test; a restricted wild-cluster score bootstrap with `9,999 valid Rademacher draws`; and a restricted cluster-robust score/LM test. The pre-specified classification rule required all three p-values below 0.10.

**Entry / exit / holding / sizing.** **None specified by the source.** The artifact contains no long or short rule, no rebalance, no position size and no stop (`portfolio` 0, `rebalance` 0, `backtest` 0 in the census). The source's own closing boundary is `No causal, directional-price, trading, or product-performance conclusion follows from the study.`

**Research-proposed (not in the source):** converting the participation block into a volatility-forecast overlay (e.g. adding `social_activity_intensity_robust_z` and `news_publisher_breadth_robust_z` as regressors in a HAR-type model), any QLIKE/DM acceptance threshold, any risk-target or position cap derived from the forecast, any crypto adaptation of the 10-session window, and the calendar window itself (currently a `data gap`).
