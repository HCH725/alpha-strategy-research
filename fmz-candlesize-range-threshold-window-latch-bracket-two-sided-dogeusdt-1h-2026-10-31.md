## Economic mechanism

### Source-reported

Big-bar momentum ignition inside the US-morning liquidity window, both directions, one shot per day. A 1h bar whose high-low range spans at least 25 DOGE ticks marks an institutional-scale displacement; if it prints inside 7:00–9:15 AM CST, the record bets the displacement extends — with the candle's own direction (bullish → long, bearish → short) — guarded by a 50-tick cap and a 40-tick leash. One trade per day caps overtrading. The source ships no sample statistics, trade count, fee basis, win rate, or performance figure of any kind — no source-reported performance is adopted or restated.

### Research interpretation

Range-threshold ignition machine with a clock gate and a daily fuse, event-driven, two-sided, flat except when a qualified ignition fires. Unlike clock-only records (`monday-drift` — UTC day-of-week + hour predicates with time exits; `bitcoin-intraday-first-last-halfhour` — half-hour momentum with no range test), the window alone fires nothing here: a quiet bar inside 7:00–9:15 prints no signal without the 25-tick range expansion. Unlike day-open threshold records (`dual-thrust`, `holmberg-orb` — displacement measured against the 00:00 UTC open with first-breach entries), the test is bar-local (|H−L| of one bar, no open anchor, no lookback) and the side comes from the bar's own close-vs-open vote, not from the breach direction. Unlike narrow-range records (`nr7-price-breakout` — narrowest range of 7 bars, relative cross-bar comparison, long-only daily), this trigger is an ABSOLUTE fixed-tick expansion test on a single bar, two-sided, intraday. Unlike pattern records (`wick-reversal-hammer`, outside-day/body-fade — shape-gated entries), there is no shape test beyond range size and body sign. The edge, if any, lives in the documented ignition intuition that unusually large hourly displacements during peak US-morning liquidity tend to extend 40–50 ticks before fading — a bet on minutes-to-hours expansion persistence inside one venue window, not on any level, streak, average, oscillator, volume flow, calendar effect beyond the window, or second timeframe.

## Signal — Gate 1, fully pinned derivative

Index completed UTC 1-hour bars `t = 0, 1, ...` with real Binance DOGE-USDT perpetual `O[t], H[t], L[t], C[t]` (volume not read). A bar with missing/nonfinite/non-positive prices, or with `H < max(O,C)` / `L < min(O,C)`, is invalid; never interpolate. All inputs pinned at source defaults: `TRIG_TICKS = 25`, `TP_TICKS = 50`, `SL_TICKS = 40`, `TICK = 0.00001`, window `[07:00, 09:15]` on the fixed `GMT-6` offset, start gate `2025-01-01 00:00 GMT-6` (any other values are a different, unpinned rule).

- **Range `R[t]`:** `|H[t] − L[t]|` (Pine `math.abs(high - low)` semantics; bar-local, no lookback).
- **Window `W[t]`:** the bar's OPEN instant mapped to the fixed GMT-6 wall clock falls inside `[07:00, 09:15]` (bar-date reading — researcher pin replacing the `timenow` construction). On the 1h grid exactly three opens qualify per day: **13:00, 14:00, 15:00 UTC** (verified in scratch this cycle).
- **Date gate `G[t]`:** bar-open instant ≥ `2025-01-01 06:00 UTC` (the GMT-6 midnight literal, carried over inert — always true on research data).
- **Daily latch `F[t]`:** `false` after warmup; resets to `false` on the first bar of a new UTC calendar date (`dayofweek` is exchange-timezone-dated; Binance exchange timezone is UTC — pinned); persists across missing-bar gaps until a bar with a new UTC date prints (gap-recovery pin: no timeout, no interpolation, no reset-without-a-bar); set `true` on any trigger bar (see below) even when no entry prints.
- **Trigger `T[t]`:** `G[t] AND W[t] AND R[t] ≥ 0.00025 AND NOT F[t]`.
- **Long signal `EL[t]`:** `T[t] AND C[t] > O[t]`. **Short signal `ES[t]`:** `T[t] AND C[t] < O[t]`.
- **Mutual exclusivity (proven, no invented priority):** `EL[t]` needs `C[t] > O[t]` while `ES[t]` needs `C[t] < O[t]` (doji equality fires neither entry but still consumes the latch by code rule); the latch admits at most one trigger per UTC date, and the window admits at most three candidate bars per day — verified by 20,000-case exclusivity fuzz plus the analytic doji case in scratch this cycle: `SELF-CHECK OK`. A signal bar arriving while positioned on the same side is discarded unseen (explicitly no queue, no add — Pine `pyramiding = 0` default behavior pinned as an explicit gate, same pin class as the merged Super-Scalper record).
- **Bracket legs (both floating, re-anchored every held bar):** for a position filled on bar `e`, on every held bar `t ≥ e` the long legs are `LTP[t] = C[t] + 0.0005`, `LST[t] = C[t] − 0.0004` (short mirror: `STP[t] = C[t] − 0.0005`, `SST[t] = C[t] + 0.0004`) — recomputed from that bar's close, never latched at entry, free to move adversely (symmetric re-anchor, NOT a ratchet).
- **Exit precedence (explicit researcher pin):** from the fill bar onward, each held bar resolves in this order: (1) bracket first-touch against the bar's `H/L` with the **stop-first** same-bar rule (both touched → stop price wins); (2) only if the bracket is untouched, a same-bar opposite trigger (possible only on the ≤3 window bars and only if the latch were free — in practice the latch is consumed, so same-day reversal needs no rule; a LATER-day opposite trigger while holding reverses). A bar touching the bracket AND printing an opposite trigger closes the old unit at the bracket price and still opens the reversed unit at the next bar's open (matches Pine intrabar-exit-before-next-open-entry sequencing; disclosed, never a file literal — same bracket class as the merged PowerZone and SMMA17 records).

**Direction (Gate 2):** BUY-to-open / SELL-to-close on the long leg; SELL-to-open / BUY-to-close on the short leg. The short leg requires margin-short permission, realistic on the pinned perpetual venue (same two-sided posture as the merged PowerZone, Super-Scalper and SMMA17 records; the source's spot stanza could not carry the coded short leg — hence the disclosed perp re-venuing). No hedge, no cross-market trade.

**Risk (Gate 3, explicit):** floating-bracket-plus-reversal book. Symmetric re-anchored 50/40-tick cap/leash re-armed every held bar, opposite-trigger close-and-reverse. No trailing ratchet, no breakeven move, no time stop, no partial exit, no averaging, no forced end-of-data liquidation, no silent executor default. An end-of-sample open position is marked at the last real valid close, not closed with an invented fill.

**State (Gate 4):** `FLAT`, `BUY_PENDING`, `LONG`, `SELL_PENDING`, `SHORT`, `COVER_PENDING`, plus the daily latch `F`. Initialize `FLAT` with `F = false` after 5 valid completed bars. While `LONG`, further `EL` bars are no-ops and a later-day `ES` reverses (bracket canceled on reversal — Pine `from_entry` semantics); symmetrically while `SHORT`. A pending order prohibits additional actions. **No DCA/safety orders (explicitly disabled — the downstream 6%+6%+6% house overlay is not active in this admission and never silently overrides single-unit semantics)**, no pyramiding, no concurrency, no compounding. No cooldown beyond the daily latch is specified (explicitly none, not invented) — re-entry needs a fresh trigger on a later UTC date.
