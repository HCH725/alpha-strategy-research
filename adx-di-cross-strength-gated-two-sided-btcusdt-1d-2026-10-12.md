## Economic mechanism

### Source-reported

Trend-strength-filtered directional trading (strategy README: ADX `measure[s] trend strength` while `DI+/DI-` `determine direction`; `Only trades when ADX confirms strong trending conditions, filtering out weak/choppy markets`): a DI crossover opens the leg only while ADX certifies a strong trend and price confirms the regime side; either leg exits on the opposite DI cross or when ADX decays below 70% of the entry threshold — weakness itself is an exit, not just the opposite signal. There is deliberately no stop, no target, no trailing exit, and no flat state beyond pre-signal bars — the order block ends with the two `strategy.close` guards and no risk leg ships anywhere, by coded posture rather than by hidden leg.

### Research interpretation

Strength-gated DMI-cross trend-following, two-sided with weakness exits — the first DI-crossover record in the pool. Unlike the pool's `dmi-swings` record, which holds a level-persistent hot-ADX state against the dominant DI side (contrarian fade of the swing), this record trades *with* the fresh cross and only while strength is certified: the cross without `adx > 25` fires nothing, and a certified trend that softens below `17.5` is exited even with no opposite cross printed. Unlike the pool's bare MA-cross records, two independent gates stand in front of every entry (the ADX strength gate and the EMA50 regime gate), and unlike trailing-stop records the exit needs no price extreme — decaying ADX alone closes the leg. Note the two pinned asymmetries, stated plainly: first, DI crosses in weak ADX print nothing — choppy markets produce long flat stretches by construction, so the system can sit out entire ranges; second, the decay exit can close a leg that is still directionally correct (DI alignment intact, price still on-regime) purely because trend strength softened — whipsaw around the `17.5` line can exit and re-enter across consecutive bars, disclosed as the record's principal friction. The bet is on ADX-certified mid-trend moves at frozen `14/25/14/50` geometry, not on early reversals, breakouts, volume prints, calendar effects, or any second timeframe.
