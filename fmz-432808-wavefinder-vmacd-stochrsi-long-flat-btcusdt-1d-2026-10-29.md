## Economic mechanism

### Source-reported

The prose claims a three-indicator reversal buyer (StochRSI bounce off oversold + fast-EMA cross over slow EMA + rising VMACD confirmation, plus an SMA10-breakout aux) that opens a fixed-size long and stops on "5% drawdown or below the SMA line". The prose is **not** the rule (see Negative evidence): the code's aux leg is a 5%-spread + double-rising conjunction, the code's stop is 1%-below-SMA10, the 25/75 Oversold/OverBought inputs gate nothing, and the title's VRSI chain computes nothing that gates. This record pins the code only; the prose is fenced as non-authoritative marketing.

### Research interpretation

A long-only buyer of StochRSI recoveries confirmed by a volume-weighted trend filter, with two independent exit rails. Leg L1 buys StochRSI momentum (K snapping up through D under 55) only when the tape has held above its low-average (lcount streak) and the VMACD histogram is turning up — shallow wobbles in dead tape print nothing. Leg L2 buys the StochRSI V-hook itself (a −2 fade followed by a +5 snap inside the 35–80 band) with no regime ask. The exits are a slow-average breakdown rail (two consecutive closes under ema(low,20)) and a fast mean rail (close 1% under SMA10) — no stop, no target, no clock. The VMACD leg reads volume nominally (ema(volume·close)/ema(volume)); everything else is price-only.
