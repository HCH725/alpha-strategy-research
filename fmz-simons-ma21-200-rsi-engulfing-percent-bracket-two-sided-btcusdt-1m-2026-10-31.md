## Economic mechanism

### Source-reported

Triple-filtered trend-reversal fade with an asymmetric fixed box, two-sided. The MA21/MA200 golden/death cross is the primary reversal call, admitted only when price already sits on the cross side of both MA21 and MA50 (chop filter), when RSI does not contradict the side (exclusion rails 70/30 — avoids buying peaks and selling valleys), and when the cross bar itself prints an engulfing reversal candle (pattern confirmation). Every position carries a +4% limit target and a −1% stop (1:4 nominal asymmetry — small stops, wide targets). ### Research interpretation

Edge-gated reversal switch with a latched asymmetric collar, event-driven, two-sided, flat except after a fully admitted cross. Unlike clock-only records (`monday-drift` — UTC day-of-week + hour predicates with time exits), there is no clock predicate anywhere here: chop prints nothing without a cross edge. Unlike level-agreement records (`fmz-dual-oscillation-rsi14-stoch143` — RSI+Stoch joint 20/80 level agreement with a symmetric floating box, two-sided 1m), the trigger here is a cross EDGE of slow SMAs (not a level), the RSI legs are vetoes rather than joint triggers, the box is latched at the signal close rather than re-anchored every bar, and the box is asymmetric (+4%/−1%) rather than symmetric. Unlike gate records that admit a cross only under a second indicator (`alma-cross-volume-gate` — ALMA60/120 cross admitted only when a volume-oscillator gate reads positive, two-sided 1d), this machine reads no volume at all and its pattern gate is candle geometry (engulfing body relations), not an oscillator.
