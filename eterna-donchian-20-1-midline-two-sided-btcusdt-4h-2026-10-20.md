## Economic mechanism

### Source-reported

Classic Donchian breakout trend riding in its Turtle lineage (strategy README: `Classic breakout system based on Richard Donchian's trend-following method (used in the famous Turtle Trading system). Enters when price breaks above/below the highest high/lowest low of the past N periods`; `Trading Style: Breakout/momentum`; Risk Warning: `High whipsaw risk during consolidation! Many false breakouts will occur. Expects to lose small amounts frequently but capture massive trends occasionally`). The channel is the memory of the last 20 bars; a close beyond the lagged band edge means the market just printed something the trailing window has never contained — the system joins it, then retreats to the midline (half the range back) at the first cross. There is deliberately no stop, no target, no trailing order leg, no time exit, and no pyramiding add anywhere — the order block ends with the two `strategy.close` guards and no risk leg ships, by design gap rather than by hidden leg.

### Research interpretation

Edge-triggered range-expansion breakout with a midline retreat exit — the first midline-exit Donchian record in the pool. Two pinned subtleties carry the economics, stated plainly. First, entries are EDGES, not levels: `close > upperBand[1] and not (that same comparison one bar ago)` fires once on the breakout bar and then goes quiet — a trend leg that keeps closing above the trailing band holds without re-firing (pyramiding-0 parity would no-op it anyway), so the system rides rather than compounds; a level machine would re-signal every bar. Second, the exit is the MIDLINE, not the opposite band: the long gives back roughly half the channel width on a crossunder instead of riding the full round trip to the lower band — earlier, smaller exits that cut whipsaw losses short at the price of surrendering mega-trends the Turtle original would ride to the 10-day extreme (that Turtle variant is fenced out above, never substituted). The bet is on 20-bar range expansion continuing at frozen `20/1/true` geometry with the midline banking half the range, not on any volume print, volatility filter, higher-timeframe confirm, or retest.

An entry CAN co-print with its OWN-side exit on a violent bar (e.g. a fresh long edge while the close simultaneously crosses under the current midline after the channel jumps): source order opens the fresh side first, then the own-exit leg closes it — net flat on the bar, both fills at the same derived close (round-trip cost only, disclosed). An entry and the OPPOSITE-side exit CAN co-print (e.g. fresh long edge plus midline crossover): the flat book opens the fresh side then the opposite-exit leg no-ops (no opposite position) — net entry; a held opposite book reverses through the entry leg (Pine pyramiding-0 parity) then the exit leg no-ops — net reversal; at most one surviving position per bar. Pine's own next-bar double-queue behavior is not claimed, and substituting any other tiebreak would be a different, unpinned rule.
