## Economic mechanism

### Source-reported

Statistically clean trend measurement: an ordinary-least-squares line fit to the last 20 closes distills direction from noise, and the sign of its slope separates upward drift from downward drift. A fresh turn of the slope from non-positive to positive marks trend birth and aligns long; a turn of the slope below zero marks trend death and stands flat. The design bets that the zero-crossing of the trailing linear fit on the daily frame times durable drift better than raw price crosses, and that sidestepping negative-slope regimes harvests more drift than it surrenders to fit lag at regime births.

### Research interpretation

Edge-triggered slope-sign trend system, long side only — the only pool machine that trades the sign of a closed-form OLS fit. Unlike the pool QT Keltner/ATR/Bollinger band machines, the rail is not manufactured from volatility at all: the signal carries price units per bar with no normalization, so steep nominal climbs print large positive slopes while flat chop prints exact zeros that hold (never enter — strict `> 0` required). Unlike the pool Coppock FMZ record (WMA-smoothed summed-ROC momentum held as a persistent zero *state*), this machine trades the crossing *edge*: a tape that trends from the first finite print never re-crosses and never enters (planted +1%/day tape: 0/0 ending FLAT — the edge exists only during seeding, disclosed, not patched); a single level jump enters once on the jump bar and then holds indefinitely because the decaying slope never prints strict `< 0` (planted single-jump tape: 1/0 ending LONG with entry fill t=61 — disclosed hold-by-construction, not patched). Unlike stop/trailing-stop machines, the exit carries no memory and no ratchet: `slope[t] < 0` is recomputed each bar from the live 20-close window. Unlike two-sided reversal systems there is no short leg and no always-in-market grind — sub-zero regimes are flat, so chop below zero costs only opportunity, never adverse exposure. The equity-index universe and the unmodeled-costs stance are source facts, not this record's venue or economics. No performance figure is carried anywhere.
