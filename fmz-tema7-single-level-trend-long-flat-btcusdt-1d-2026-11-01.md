## Economic mechanism

### Source-reported
Triple-smoothed trend following: three cascaded EMAs of one period cancel single-EMA lag, and the moviment of close against the resulting TEMA line marks the mid-to-long trend posture — above the line holds long, below it stands flat. The prose frames the line as a noise filter for long-horizon holding, admits black-swan reversal loss, missed turning-point exits and overfit risk, and suggests volatility-adapted parameters, stops, drawdown-linked sizing and cross-timeframe filters (all unported future work — disclosed). ### Research interpretation
Single-rail level holder, event-persistent, long-flat. Unlike cross-event TEMA machines (`tema-dema-hma-cross-trend-two-sided-btcusdt-1d` — TEMA8-vs-DEMA43 cross gated by HMA52 slope, two-sided, always-in; `forven-tema-cross-long-flat-btcusdt-1h` — dual-TEMA 12/26 cross pair, long-flat, 1h), this machine never computes a second average, a cross, or a regime gate — entries and exits are close-vs-one-TEMA LEVEL states that stay true across consecutive bars (first such bar trades while flat; the position then blocks the rest), and equality is an explicit hold.
