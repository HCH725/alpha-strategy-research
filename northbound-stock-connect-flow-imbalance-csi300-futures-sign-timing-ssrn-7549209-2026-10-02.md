## Economic mechanism

### Source-reported

The authors frame the design as a test of whether foreign investors bring information to an emerging equity market or merely transmit global risk appetite (Section 1, citing Bekaert and Harvey 2000 and Froot et al. 2001). Disaggregated daily buy-sell imbalances for three investor groups are defined by Eq. (1) as $F_{k,t}=(B_{k,t}-S_{k,t})/(B_{k,t}+S_{k,t})$ for $k$ in {northbound, large order, small order}. The reported division of labour is: northbound flow is associated with same-day returns and predicts the next session without reversal, so it is read through the informed-trading lens of Kyle (1985), Glosten and Milgrom (1985) and Hasbrouck (1991); domestic large-order flow moves prices the same day and then partially reverses over two sessions, read as temporary price pressure with limited depth; small-order flow mainly mirrors large orders as counterparty and carries no incremental predictive content, read against Barber and Odean (2000) and Kelley and Tetlock (2013). The information-timing claim that makes the design testable is stated in Section 5.2.2: on ordinary days part of the information in northbound flow is incorporated into prices the following day, while on scheduled-release days the underlying information becomes public at a known time and is fully impounded within the session, leaving nothing to forecast. Section 6.1 adds that stress episodes work in the opposite direction by magnifying the informational advantage, and Section 7.1.2 records the authors' own competing demand-pressure explanation, which the announcement result is said to disfavour.

### Research interpretation

The falsifiable mechanism this record captures is **delayed incorporation of cross-border informed flow into an index price**, with a scheduled-public-information kill switch and a volatility amplifier:

```text
Regime: announcement calendar (PBOC / domestic macro / FOMC, FOMC assigned to the
        next Chinese session) plus a high-volatility indicator (20-day realized
        volatility above its 80th percentile at the previous close)
Primary signal: lag-1 northbound buy-sell imbalance (long next session when positive,
        short when negative), jointly with lag-1 to lag-3 domestic large-order
        imbalance whose lagged sign is negative (fade institutional price pressure)
Controls, not alpha: preceding S&P 500 return, lagged CNH change, lagged index returns
Position rule: sign of the combined forecast, one CSI 300 futures position, flipped
        only when the sign flips
```

Under the information reading, the tradable content is the next-session component of $NB_{t-1}$ that survives global controls; under the competing demand reading the same coefficient is persistent foreign demand with limited arbitrage capital and no forecastable content. The source does not distinguish the two with account-level data (Section 7.1.2 states both may operate simultaneously), so the mechanism remains a hypothesis rather than an established fact. The record treats the announcement calendar and the volatility gate as **regime conditioning of a single flow signal**, not as independent alpha, and does not assume each component contributes (ablation is required, see Falsification plan).

## Signal

All rules below are source-reported unless explicitly labeled `research-proposed` or `research-defined`.

- **Formation timestamp (source-reported):** predictors are dated at the previous close and are computed from daily statistics released after the market close, so the forecast for session $t$ uses information available at the close of $t-1$ (Section 2.2.2: "all predictors are dated at the previous close, so that the forecasts could have been computed in real time from the daily statistics released after the market close"; OOS note: "using only information available at the previous close"). The preceding US session return is observed before the Chinese market opens. FOMC decisions published after the Chinese close are assigned to the next Chinese session.
- **Lookback (source-reported):** three lags of each of the three imbalances plus, in the richest specification, three lagged index returns, the preceding US return and the lagged CNH change (Eq. (5)); the VAR uses two lags for the four-variable system $(NB, LOI, SOI, R)$ although information criteria select one (Section 4.1.1). Announcement interactions use the indicator of the previous session (Table 7). High-volatility regime uses a 20-day realized-volatility window measured at the previous close against its 80th in-sample percentile (Section 6.1.1). Rolling diagnostics use 250-day windows (Fig. 2). Sample starts 13 January 2017 and the estimation sample is 1,790 trading days ending 16 August 2024.
- **Forecast models (source-reported):** same-day regressions (Eq. (2)), conditional quantiles at $\tau$ from 0.05 to 0.95 (Eq. (3)), reduced-form VAR with orthogonalized impulse responses ordering flows before returns (Eq. (4)), predictive distributed lags (Eq. (5)), announcement interactions same-day and lagged (Eqs. (6) and the Table 7 structure), local projections with a regime interaction (Eq. (7)), and expanding-window forecasts from January 2020 evaluated against the recursive historical mean by $R^2_{OS}$ (Eq. (8)) and the Clark-West statistic (Eq. (9)). All predictive regressions are re-estimated each day (Section 6.2.1).
- **Long entry (source-reported):** a long CSI 300 futures position is held on session $t$ when the combined flow forecast for $t$ is positive.
- **Short entry (source-reported):** a short CSI 300 futures position is held when the forecast is negative; the Table 9 note states strategies "hold a long or short CSI 300 futures position according to the sign of the forecast", and Section 6.2.2 states short positions were available throughout the evaluation period although margin requirements and position limits were tightened temporarily after the 2015 turbulence.
- **Exit / holding period (source-reported):** one session; the position changes only when the sign of the forecast changes, and the source reports position changes on 37% of sessions (Section 6.2.2).
- **Re-entry (not stated in source):** `data gap` for the treatment of a zero forecast, for flat/zero positions and for sessions when Connect is closed (Hong Kong holidays are excluded from the sample because northbound imbalance is undefined).
- **Parameters (source-reported):** Newey-West lag 5 for inference (t-statistics with 10 lags differ by less than 3%); moving-block bootstrap with 10-day blocks and 150 replications for quantile standard errors; size classes large above CNY 200,000 and small below CNY 40,000 with Lee-Ready trade signing; announcement counts 165 PBOC, 271 domestic macro, 61 FOMC, 437 combined release sessions; high-volatility days 355 and stress-window days 277; cost 5 basis points per position change; benchmark recursive historical mean and buy-and-hold.

- **Within-session entry price, contract symbol, multiplier, margin rate, roll calendar and fill model (not stated in source):** `data gap`; any choice such as entering at the next session open or at the close of the signal session, unit notional, or one-tick slippage is `research-proposed`.

## Required data

- **Instrument / universe (source-reported):** the CSI 300 index, the 300 largest and most liquid A-shares listed in Shanghai and Shenzhen, described as the underlying of the most actively traded stock index futures in China; the tradable instrument is a CSI 300 index futures contract. The specific contract symbol, exchange listing, multiplier, tick value and roll rule are `not stated in source` (data gap).
- **Venue / market type (source-reported):** Shanghai and Shenzhen exchanges plus the Shanghai-Hong Kong and Shenzhen-Hong Kong Stock Connect northbound channel for flow inputs; Chinese index futures for implementation. Market type is traditional equity index futures, not crypto.
- **Timeframe / fields (source-reported):** daily log returns of the CSI 300; daily northbound buy and sell turnover in CNY for Shanghai and Shenzhen combined from the daily Stock Connect statistics; Level-2 transaction data for CSI 300 constituents with each trade Lee-Ready signed and the originating order value used to assign the CNY 200,000 / CNY 40,000 size classes; preceding S&P 500 close-to-close return; daily percentage change in the offshore renminbi (CNH); a daily announcement calendar covering PBOC operations, loan prime rate fixings, announced reserve-requirement or policy-rate changes, CPI/PPI, activity data and official PMI releases, and FOMC decisions; a 20-day realized-volatility series; the six enumerated stress windows (2018 trade-dispute sell-off, May 2019 tariff escalation, January-April 2020 COVID shock, February-March 2021 correction, March-May 2022 Shanghai lockdown, January-February 2024 small-cap liquidity squeeze).
- **Point-in-time / availability (source-reported):** daily statistics are released after the market close so the forecast is computable at the previous close; the sample explicitly ends on 16 August 2024 because of "the reduction in the public disclosure of daily northbound buy and sell turnover by the exchanges in August 2024, after which the northbound measure can no longer be constructed at daily frequency" (Section 2.1.1). Post-August-2024 daily northbound turnover is therefore `data gap` from the sources used by the paper.
- **Timestamp / timezone:** the source does not state the timezone convention for the daily boundary, the release timestamps of Connect statistics, or the alignment of US and CNH sessions beyond the verbal statements above; `underspecified`.
- **Missing data:** days on which Connect was closed for Hong Kong holidays are dropped; the authors state no observations required winsorisation because the imbalance ratios are bounded by construction; no imputation rule is given for partial Connect days (`data gap`).
- **Funding / fee / spread needs:** maker-taker fees, spread, slippage, borrow, funding and capacity are `not stated in source` beyond the single 5-basis-point-per-change assumption; futures basis and margin costs are explicitly excluded by the source.

## Execution assumptions

Source-reported: signal formed at the previous close; one long or short CSI 300 futures position per session set by the sign of the forecast; position changed only on sign changes, occurring on 37% of sessions; 5 basis points charged per position change; returns and volatilities annualized; shorting available throughout the evaluation window with temporary post-2015 margin and position-limit tightening; out-of-sample design is an expanding window re-estimated daily from January 2020 over 1,096 sessions.

Source-stated omissions (Section 6.2.2 and Section 7.2.2): the strategy "ignores futures basis, margin costs and the possibility that northbound data might be released with delay", and the out-of-sample strategy "abstracts from futures basis risk, from intraday execution prices and from the possibility that the predictive relations change after disclosure reforms or after further growth in quantitative trading".

Not stated in source, therefore `data gap` rather than assumed zero: entry price and time within the session, order type, limit versus market, fill and partial-fill model, latency and market-impact/participation limits, commission, exchange fees, bid-ask spread, slippage, borrow cost, leverage and margin interest, capacity, position sizing, roll mechanics, dividend and corporate-action treatment, and any treatment of data-release delay. ### Negative evidence

1. The core input cannot be constructed after the sample: the source states the sample ends 16 August 2024 because of the reduction in public disclosure of daily northbound buy and sell turnover, after which the northbound measure "can no longer be constructed at daily frequency" - so the published signal is not reproducible in live form from the sources the paper used.
2. Section 7.2.2 states that stability of the documented relations after the 2024 disclosure reform is an open empirical question testable only "as data from alternative sources accumulate over the coming years".
3. The source's own caveat that the strategy ignores futures basis, margin costs and possible data-release delay means every friction beyond the flat 5 basis points is a data gap, not a modeled zero.
4. Cost sensitivity is severe by the source's own arithmetic: the advantage over buy-and-hold disappears at costs above about 9 basis points per position change, only about 4 basis points above the assumed rate.
5. Out-of-sample explanatory power is tiny: $R^2_{OS}$ of 0.78% for the best model, below the 1% level the source itself calls typical, with a 54.3% hit rate against 51.7% for a trivial return-lag model.
6. The conditional refinement fails out of sample: adding the northbound-release interaction cuts $R^2_{OS}$ from 0.78% to 0.24% and Sharpe from 0.88 to 0.48, which the source attributes to imprecise early-window estimation of the extra parameter.
7. The northbound-only strategy underperforms buy-and-hold (Sharpe 0.62 versus 0.69) and the return-lag and small-order strategies lose money (Sharpe -0.11 and -0.19); only the three-flow combination and the large-order reversal strategy beat the benchmark, so the headline number depends on combining a reversal leg with the flow leg.
8. Predictability is estimated to be zero after roughly one quarter of sessions: the combined northbound coefficient after release days is 0.043 with $p=0.945$, and the source notes the full-sample coefficient therefore understates ordinary-day predictability while the calendar-conditioned forecast failed to help out of sample.
9. Performance is concentrated in 2023 and 2024 after weak 2021 and 2022, a period the source attributes to unpredictable policy-driven moves, so the result is regime-concentrated rather than uniform (Section 6.2.2 and Fig. 6b).
10. No multiple-testing control exists anywhere in the document: zero hits for Benjamini, Hochberg, Bonferroni, multiple testing, deflated, walk-forward, holdout and validation-set terms, against a large specification grid (three flow groups, three lag lengths, four announcement types, two regime definitions, five forecast models and nine tables of estimates).
11. Announcement indicators do not measure the surprise component of releases (source-stated limitation), so the information-timing mechanism is tested with day-type dummies rather than news surprises.
12. Order size is an imperfect proxy for investor identity and account-level data would be needed for sharper identification (source-stated limitation); misclassification would attenuate or distort both large-order and small-order legs.
13. Index-level daily-frequency data cannot separate flows from prices within a session, and the source notes same-day magnitudes describe association rather than causal price impact.
14. Orthogonalized impact responses are explicitly upper bounds under the Cholesky ordering, so the 42.2 and 43.5 basis-point impact numbers are ordering-dependent (source-stated).
15. Inference is thin: Newey-West with 5 lags, only 150 bootstrap replications with 10-day blocks for quantiles, and interaction terms with $p$ = 0.050 to 0.065 at the regime margin, which the source itself calls limited precision from small subsamples concentrated in a few episodes.
16. The small-order out-of-sample model has negative $R^2_{OS}$ of -0.23% and an insignificant Clark-West statistic of 0.30, and the return-lag benchmark model is worse than the historical mean (-0.73%).
17. No capacity, market-impact, participation or latency model exists, so 5 basis points per change is an assumption rather than a measured execution cost for an index-futures book sized to absorb a 37%-of-sessions turnover.
18. Position sizing, leverage, margin fraction, contract specification and roll rule are absent, so the reported Sharpe and drawdown are not reconstructible as an implementable portfolio without `research-proposed` choices.
19. Same-day regressions are simultaneous by construction and the source says so; the tradable content rests entirely on the lagged predictive coefficients.
20. Shorting was subject to temporary post-2015 margin and position-limit tightening and may constrain larger investors (source-stated).
21. Publication status is a non-peer-reviewed SSRN preprint with zero downloads and three abstract views at fetch time, no journal, no funder, no code release and no data release.
22. Author identity is inconsistent across the three primary artefacts (three contradictions in the frontmatter), so provenance of the work itself is not clean.
23. A single index in a single market over one 7.6-year window with one implementation instrument provides no cross-market or cross-asset replication.
24. The mechanism faces a live competing explanation the source cannot reject: persistent foreign demand pressure with limited arbitrage capital rather than informed trading, distinguished only by the announcement interaction.
25. Post-August-2024, alternative proxies would substitute a different measurement of the same latent concept, which is a material data-dependency change and not a like-for-like replication.
26. The paper's own conclusion that "flow-based forecasts retain modest out-of-sample accuracy and economic value after transaction costs" is explicitly hedged by the authors as sensitive to execution costs.

## Falsification plan

Every threshold below is `research-defined`; every operational choice not specified by the source is `research-proposed`. Each gate states data, sample, metric, threshold and action.

- **F1 - Printed-value reproduction.** Rebuild Table 9 from the pinned sample definition and check the all-flows row (15.6% return, 17.7% volatility, Sharpe 0.88, drawdown 22.5%, hit rate 54.3%, $R^2_{OS}$ 0.78%, Clark-West 3.12) at 5 basis points per change. Fail if any of return, volatility or Sharpe differs by more than 0.5 percentage points, 0.5 points or 0.02 respectively, or if the Sharpe ordering all-flows > large-order > buy-and-hold > northbound > return-lags does not hold. Action: report the reproduction as failed, reclassify all quoted performance as unverified, and do not advance the candidate.
- **F2 - Signal-availability gate (primary go/no-go).** Reconstruct daily northbound buy and sell turnover for a post-2024-08 window from public sources. Fail if the daily measure cannot be constructed at all, or if any proxy requires information not published before the next session close. Action: mark the strategy non-reproducible as published, keep the record research-only, and require an explicit measurement-equivalence study before any test proceeds.
- **F3 - Cost ladder.** Rerun the OOS rule at 0, 2, 5, 9 and 15 basis points per position change with all other parameters frozen. Fail if net Sharpe falls below the buy-and-hold 0.69 at 9 basis points. Action: conclude the edge is execution-cost dominated and stop.
- **F4 - Timing and staleness stress.** Compare entering at the next session open, at the signal session close and with the northbound statistic delayed by one session. Fail if Sharpe drops below 0.69 under a one-session delay or if the entry-convention spread exceeds 0.15 Sharpe. Action: treat the reported edge as a same-close timing artifact.
- **F5 - Calendar-conditioning ablation.** Compare the plain three-flow forecast against (a) excluding release-day signals and (b) including the release interaction, exactly as in Table 9. Fail if neither variant improves net Sharpe over the plain model by at least 0.05, since the source's own conditional variant degraded results. Action: report that the announcement channel has no demonstrated tradable value and strip it from any future hypothesis.
- **F6 - Subperiod stability.** Compute net Sharpe separately for 2020-2022 and 2023-2024 (the split the source itself describes) and for each calendar year. Fail if at least two of the three subperiods have net Sharpe at or below zero, or if the pre-2023 half contributes less than 25% of cumulative net return. Action: classify the effect as regime-concentrated and require a fresh forward window before any use.
- **F7 - Selection-luck gate.** Enumerate the specification grid actually available to the design (3 flow groups x 3 lag lengths x 4 announcement types x 2 regime definitions x 5 forecast models) and compute a deflated Sharpe ratio at the 1,096-session sample size. Fail if deflated Sharpe is below 0.95. Action: treat 0.88 as selection-contaminated and downgrade confidence.
- **F8 - Basis and margin realism.** Add CSI 300 futures basis cost, margin financing cost and a 1-tick-per-side slippage term. Fail if net Sharpe falls below buy-and-hold. Action: stop; the source already warns these are omitted.
- **F9 - Mechanism discrimination.** Test the source's own signature jointly out of sample: northbound predictability must be significantly positive on ordinary days, statistically indistinguishable from zero after domestic macro and FOMC releases, and larger in the high-volatility regime (Table 7 and Table 8 signs). Fail if any of the three sign conditions reverses out of sample at the 10% level. Action: reject the information-timing interpretation and retain only a generic flow-reversal description.
- **F10 - Placebo universe.** Apply the identical forecast and sign rule to an index with no northbound channel (for example a domestic-only small-cap index) over the same window. Fail if the placebo net Sharpe is within 0.10 of the CSI 300 result. Action: conclude the rule captures generic flow-reversal or vol-timing rather than cross-border information.
- **F11 - Point-in-time audit.** Rebuild the forecast using only values published before the session open, logging publication timestamps for the Connect statistics, the US close and the CNH fix. Fail if any predictor is unavailable at the assumed time or if any same-close value leaks into the signal. Action: mark look-ahead and discard the affected specification.
- **F12 - Frozen forward window.** Freeze the design from 2026-10-02 forward for at least 12 months of whatever data exists under F2. Fail if forward net Sharpe is not strictly positive or if forward hit rate is below 51%. Action: close the hypothesis as falsified.

## Crypto portability

`adapted`, economic viability unproven.

The mechanism is a ported hypothesis, not crypto empirical evidence: the source contains zero crypto content (census: `crypto` 0, `bitcoin` 0, `ethereum` 0, `perpetual` 0, `24/7` 0) and demonstrates the effect only in Chinese index equities and their futures. What ports mechanically is the shape of the test: a normalized buy-sell imbalance, a next-session sign forecast, a scheduled-announcement kill switch and a volatility amplifier, none of which depends on equity market structure per se.

What does not port directly: (a) there is no crypto analogue of Stock Connect northbound disclosure, so the material data dependency would have to be replaced by a different measurement of the same latent cross-border informed flow (cross-venue taker imbalance, stablecoin netflows, exchange net inflows), which is a material data-dependency change; (b) crypto trades 24/7, so "previous close", the announcement-calendar timing convention and the session-based holding period all require redefinition and candle boundaries differ across venues; (c) perpetual funding, mark and index price divergence, and the basis term that the source already omits would become first-order; (d) venue fragmentation, listing churn, survivorship in the token universe, auto-deleveraging, custody and withdrawal risk, and stablecoin quote effects have no counterpart in the paper; (e) index-futures shorting, margin and roll mechanics map only approximately onto perpetual futures, where funding and liquidation replace margin interest; (f) the announcement channel is specific to PBOC, Chinese macro releases and FOMC, and its crypto counterpart would be a different, undocumented event set. Crypto portability is not authorization to trade.

## Limitations

- `not independently reproduced`; all performance figures are source-reported from a non-peer-reviewed preprint.
- `data gap`: post-August-2024 daily northbound turnover, so the published signal cannot be constructed live from the paper's own sources; any substitute proxy changes the material data dependency.
- `data gap`: contract symbol, multiplier, tick, roll rule, entry price and time, position sizing, leverage, margin rate, fill model, spread, slippage, impact, borrow, commission, capacity and latency are all `not stated in source`.
- `underspecified`: timezone and release-timestamp conventions, handling of zero forecasts and flat positions, treatment of Connect-closed sessions inside the trading record, and any rule for partial sessions.
- `underspecified`: the announcement calendar is day-type based with no surprise measure, and the stress windows are hand-enumerated historical episodes.
- Source-quality limitation: SSRN preprint, not peer reviewed, zero downloads at fetch time, no code and no data release, and three cross-artefact author-identity contradictions.
- Statistical limitations: no multiple-testing control across a large specification grid, small bootstrap replication count, regime interactions significant only at the 10% margin, ordering-dependent impulse responses, and $R^2_{OS}$ below 1%.
- Identification limitation: simultaneity in same-day relations, order size as a proxy for investor identity, index-level aggregation, and an unrefuted persistent-demand competing explanation.
- Economic limitation: cost sensitivity at roughly 9 basis points per change, no capacity model, no basis or margin cost, and performance concentrated in 2023-2024.
- Scope limitation: one index, one market, one implementation instrument, one sample window; absence of negative results elsewhere is not evidence of robustness.
- Incremental-write check: this record adds a new source identity (SSRN 7549209), a new signal construction (cross-border plus size-classified imbalance with announcement and volatility conditioning for an index-futures sign rule) and new negative evidence (the 2024 disclosure break) relative to the repository's existing order-imbalance and A-share records.
