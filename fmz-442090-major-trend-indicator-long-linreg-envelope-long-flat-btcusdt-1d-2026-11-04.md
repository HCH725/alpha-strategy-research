## Economic mechanism

### Source-reported

Nested linear-regression envelope with a slope-order gate, long side only. The program's construction is self-describing: 5-bar highest-high / lowest-low extremes seed two smoothed series (`X_i + high`, `Y_i + low`); a 23-bar regression over the high-seeded series and a 75-bar regression over the low-seeded series form the fast/slow cores; a second 23-bar regression layer over each core forms the `upper` / `lower` envelope; `bull` requires the high core above its envelope AND the low core above its envelope AND the 3-bar close regression above the 75-bar close regression. The page prose claims only adaptability across instruments via parameter tuning plus-handler prose (`stop losses, trade cost control`, `price-volume confirmation` as future work) — this record adopts none of it. The embedded backtest header (`Futures_Binance BTC_USDT`, `1d`, 2023-02-12→2024-02-18) is a source fact, not this record's venue. No return, win-rate, or trade-count figure from any source is carried into the derived specification.

### Research interpretation

Two-layer OLS trend envelope with a fast-over-slow slope-order confirmation, long-flat. Unlike the pool's Forven cousin (a single 50-bar regression channel whose breakout is chased) or the Eterna cousin (two-sided band-touch fades), this rule never trades a price-vs-band distance: it trades the *position of a regression core relative to its own second-layer regression* on both the high-seeded and low-seeded paths at once, then demands the short-horizon close drift sit above the long-horizon close drift. The nested layer makes the gate slow to arm (both cores must climb above their own smoothed shadows) and the slope-order leg makes it release as soon as short drift rolls under long drift — entries arrive mature into trends, exits cut without a stop leg. The failure mode is plain: a core oscillating across its envelope with positive slope-order prints full open/close cycles at each flicker, and the flat leg rides out every sub-envelope chop with no guardrail — disclosed, not upgraded.
