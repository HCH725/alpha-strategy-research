## Economic mechanism

### Source-reported
ATR-breakout pole plus SMA-anchored flag containment with a fixed-horizon exit: a single-bar body exceeding 3× the prior 10-bar ATR after 1–3 consecutive rises freezes a flagpole; the pole-top close minus the prior 10-bar SMA measures the pole height; the flag is the top-third band (pole top down to 33% below); three consecutive bars fully contained (open AND close) in that band confirm the bull flag and open long (trend-absorption entry that refuses poles without follow-through consolidation); the sleeve closes exactly 6 bars after the most recent flag bar (fixed-horizon exit that refuses to overstay). ### Research interpretation
Pole-anchored containment pattern with a clock exit, event-persistent, long-flat. Unlike the pool formation-breakout cousins (`darvas-box-breakout` — frozen highest-high box with a level breakout, 1d; `quantifiedtrader-fractal-breakout-22` — fixed-lag fractal pivots, 1d; `fmz-gbs-top-bottom-confirmed-breakout` — top/bottom confirmation breakout, 1d), this machine never trades a level break — the entry is a three-bar CONTAINMENT sequence inside an ATR-pole-anchored band (volatility-scaled pole, SMA-anchored height, open-and-close inside tests), and the exit is a `barssince` CLOCK, never a stop, target, crossunder or opposite signal. Unlike trailing-stop/ATR cousins (`ut-bot-atr-trailing-stop-flip`, `fmz-atr-dual-trail-t3-envelope-trend` — ATR-scaled trails), ATR here sizes the pole gate only and never trails anything.
