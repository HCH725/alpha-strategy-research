## Economic mechanism

### Source-reported

Triple-window buying-pressure reversal (strategy README: the UO `combines three different timeframes (short, medium, and long) to reduce false signals`; `trades crossovers` of the weighted oscillator `in extreme zones` plus confirmed pivot divergences): UO crossing up through 30 while still under the 50 midline opens a long; UO crossing down through 70 while still over 50 opens a short; a lower price low against a higher UO low (both pivot-confirmed) with UO under 30 opens a long; the mirror with UO over 70 opens a short; the opposite signal closes the position. There is deliberately no stop, no target, no trend filter, and no flat state beyond pre-signal bars — the order block ends with the two `strategy.close` guards and no risk leg ships anywhere, by design gap rather than by hidden leg.

### Research interpretation

Zone-gated triple-weighted pressure reversal with a confirmation-lagged divergence kicker, two-sided with reversal exits. Unlike the pool's Forven UO record (long-only cross gate, no midline filter, no divergence, no short leg — a one-directional reclaim machine), this system trades both exhaustion turns symmetrically and its divergence legs fire only after 5-bar pivot confirmation, so divergence entries arrive late by construction and never anticipate the pivot bar. Unlike the pool's TradingView UO breakout record (long-only level-break machine on a 6/14/20 SMA variant with close-gated orders — no cross pair, no midline, no divergence), the trigger here is a K/D-style cross inside extremes, never a level break. Unlike the pool's StochRSI/MFI Eterna siblings (single-window oscillators with same-bar extremes), the UO smooths three BP/TR windows with 4:2:1 weights, so single-bar wicks move it less and zone entries need multi-bar pressure, not one hot print. Note the two pinned asymmetries, stated plainly: first, entries need a fresh cross inside the zone or a freshly evaluable confirmed divergence — a UO that merely sits under 30 or over 70 without crossing fires nothing, so flat congestion inside extremes is ignored by construction; second, the midline test reads the crossing bar's own UO (`uo < 50` long / `uo > 50` short, strict), so a cross that prints already past midline fires nothing — the setup is extreme-only, disclosed. The bet is on triple-smoothed buying-pressure exhaustion at frozen `7/14/28/70/30` geometry, not on any price level, volume print, trend-rail position, or calendar effect.

## Signal

Exact rule as pinned (`length1 7 / length2 14 / length3 28 / overboughtLevel 70 / oversoldLevel 30` frozen — any other value is a different, unpinned rule):

- Declaration (source-faithful): `strategy("Ultimate Oscillator - Eterna MCP", shorttitle="UO", overlay=false, default_qty_type=strategy.percent_of_equity, default_qty_value=100, initial_capital=1000)` semantics preserved with the derived `BTCUSDT 4h` market/frame pin and same-bar-close execution adopted (see derived declaration) and house sizing/costs replacing the source economics (see Execution assumptions). Pine-default no-pyramiding semantics hold (no `pyramiding` argument ships): at most one open position, same-direction refires while positioned are blocked by the `position_size == 0` entry gates, opposite entries arrive only via the close-then-enter path below.
- Indicator (pinned Williams arithmetic — evaluated from the frozen inputs, not tuned): `bp[t] = close[t] - min(low[t], close[t-1])`; `tr[t] = max(high[t], close[t-1]) - min(low[t], close[t-1])`; `avgN[t] = sum(bp, N)[t] / sum(tr, N)[t]` for `N in (7, 14, 28)`; `uo[t] = 100 * (4*avg7[t] + 2*avg14[t] + avg28[t]) / 7` — causal recursions on completed bars. A zero `sum(tr, N)` print (N consecutive zero-range bars) yields `na` by Pine division semantics and every `na` comparison below is false — fenced, never interpolated. First defined print needs 28 completed closes plus one lookback close (verified empirically: first defined index 28 on a synthetic tape, see Evidence).
- Cross legs: `bullishSignal[t] = crossover(uo, 30)[t] and uo[t] < 50` (UO crosses up through 30 with UO strictly under 50 on the crossing bar); `bearishSignal[t] = crossunder(uo, 70)[t] and uo[t] > 50` (UO crosses down through 70 with UO strictly over 50). `crossover`/`crossunder` are the Pine edge forms (`prior <= level and current > level` / `prior >= level and current < level`); ties on the strict midline/level tests fail — predeclared, never left ambiguous.
- Divergence legs (confirmed-pivot construction, pinned semantics): pivots are `ta.pivotlow/pivothigh(source, 5, 5)` — a pivot at bar `j` becomes known only on bar `j+5` (confirmation bar), prior bars print `na`; `lastPriceLow/lastUoLow` (`lastPriceHigh/lastUoHigh`) latch the latest simultaneously confirmed price/UO pivot pair and persist until the next confirmation. `bullishDiv[t] = defined(lastUoLow) and defined(lastPriceLow) and low[t] < lastPriceLow and uo[t] > lastUoLow and uo[t] < 30`; `bearishDiv[t]` is the mirror with `high[t] > lastPriceHigh and uo[t] < lastUoHigh and uo[t] > 70`. Strict inequalities throughout — equality prints fire nothing. No future bar enters: every term is the current completed bar or a confirmation-lagged latch, disclosed.
- Combined signals: `buySignal[t] = bullishSignal[t] or bullishDiv[t]`; `sellSignal[t] = bearishSignal[t] or bearishDiv[t]`. The two cannot co-print both directions' extremes on one bar by construction (buy needs `uo < 30` or cross-under-50 context; sell needs `uo > 70` or cross-over-50 context — the zone requirements are mutually exclusive on every bar), so at most one entry leg fires per bar.
- Entry long: `strategy.entry("Long", strategy.long)` under `if buySignal and strategy.position_size == 0` — fires on completed bars with same-bar-close execution under the derived declaration.
- Entry short: `strategy.entry("Short", strategy.short)` under `if sellSignal and strategy.position_size == 0` — mirrored.
- Exits (coded, the sole position-closing path): `strategy.close("Long")` under `if sellSignal and strategy.position_size > 0`; `strategy.close("Short")` under `if buySignal and strategy.position_size < 0`. No stop, no target, no trailing exit leg, no time exit — pinned as written, explicitly none, not invented.
