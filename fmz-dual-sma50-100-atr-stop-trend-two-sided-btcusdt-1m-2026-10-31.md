### Research interpretation

Edge-gated trend switch with a latched single-stop collar, event-driven, two-sided, flat except after a fully admitted cross. Unlike clock-only records (`monday-drift` — UTC day-of-week + hour predicates with time exits), there is no clock predicate anywhere here: chop prints nothing without a cross edge. Unlike filter records (`fmz-simons-ma21-200-rsi-engulfing` — MA21/200 cross admitted only under MA50 trend plus RSI exclusion plus engulfing confirmation, two-sided 1m with a latched +4%/−1% box), the trigger here is a bare cross EDGE of medium SMAs with zero conjuncts — no trend filter, no oscillator veto, no pattern gate — and the risk is a single ATR stop with no target leg rather than a two-legged percent box. Unlike level-agreement records (`fmz-dual-oscillation-rsi14-stoch143` — RSI+Stoch joint 20/80 level agreement with a symmetric floating box, two-sided 1m), the trigger here is a cross EDGE (not a level), and the stop is latched at the signal reference rather than re-anchored every bar. Unlike gate records that admit a cross only under a second indicator (`alma-cross-volume-gate` — ALMA60/120 cross admitted only when a volume-oscillator gate reads positive, two-sided 1d), this machine reads no volume at all and its only risk leg is the ATR stop. The economic hypothesis is falsifiable per side: post-cross drift persists often enough and far enough that a 4-ATR stop survives chop while the reverse edge eventually harvests the excursion — against the disclosed alternative that the 4-ATR stop (only ~0.13% at probe volatility) chops the book to flat before any trend pays.

## Signal — Gate 1, fully pinned derivative

A bar with missing/nonfinite/non-positive prices, or with `H < max(O,C)` / `L < min(O,C)`, is invalid; never interpolate. All inputs pinned at source defaults: `SMA 50 / SMA 100 / ATR 14 / ATR multiplier 4` (any other values are a different, unpinned rule).

- **Moving averages (exact windowed means, no seed state):** `SMA50[t]`, `SMA100[t]` are exact `n`-bar means of closes (Pine `ta.sma` — non-recursive; `na` before index 49/99 respectively, and `na` comparisons never fire — disclosed).
- **ATR `A[t]` (t ≥ 13):** Wilder-RMA ATR over 14 bars (Pine `ta.atr` — recursive: needs the 14-bar true-range seed plus convergence margin; `na` before index 13). True range uses `H/L/C` only, never volume.
- **Edge triggers (Pine v5 `ta.crossover`/`ta.crossunder` — strictly one-bar edges):** gold edge `G[t] = SMA50[t−1] <= SMA100[t−1] AND SMA50[t] > SMA100[t]`; death edge `D[t] = SMA50[t−1] >= SMA100[t−1] AND SMA50[t] < SMA100[t]`.
- **Full predicates (t ≥ 99 by construction, actionable only t ≥ 159 — see warmup):** long predicate `LONG[t] = G[t]`; short predicate `SHORT[t] = D[t]` (file-literal bare-edge predicates on both sides — no filter conjunct of any kind). A single bar cannot print both (gold and death edges are mutually exclusive — disclosed).
- **Latched stop legs (fixed at the signal bar's close and ATR, never moved):** on a long signal at bar `s`, `STOP_LONG = C[s] − 4 × A[s]`; on a short signal, `STOP_SHORT = C[s] + 4 × A[s]` (4×ATR is an ATR-unit distance, never ticks or percents; levels are tickSize-snapped at 0.10 — the record's sole precision pin). Levels never move after arming (latched stop, NOT floating, NOT a ratchet, NOT trailed — no `trail_` qualifier ships anywhere).
- **Exit precedence (explicit researcher pin):** each held bar resolves the single latched stop first-touch against the bar's `L` (long) / `H` (short). There is no limit leg, so no same-bar stop/limit precedence exists to pin (disclosed difference from the two-legged VWMA/Simons bracket class). An opposite edge predicate arriving while holding REVERSES the book at the next open (close at next-open, open opposite at the same next-open, old resting stop cancelled, new stop armed from the new signal close and ATR) — Pine `pyramiding = 0` default reversal pinned as an explicit gate, same pin class as the merged two-sided records. **Direction (Gate 2):** `"Buy"`-to-open / reverse-to-close on the long leg; `"Sell"`-to-open / reverse-to-close on the short leg. Both legs ship in the file (`strategy.long` + `strategy.short`). **State (Gate 4):** `FLAT`, `LONG`, `SHORT` — plus no counters (stateless signal apart from the ATR recursion seed). Initialize `FLAT` after 160 valid completed bars. Entries fire only from confirmed `FLAT` (fresh edge predicate) or as an explicit reversal from the opposite held side; a missing/failed/unconfirmed fill **halts** this research run, never synthesizes inventory.
