## Economic mechanism

### Source-reported

Steady-path winners as a stronger continuation signal than spike-built winners, plus year-end tax-loss trading as the seasonal overlay: `Achieving a high past return with a series of steady positive months appears to generate a larger expected return than a high past return achieved with just a few extraordinary months`; candidate channels fenced to the paper are disposition-driven reference-price updating per Grinblatt-Han 2002 (`consistent winning stocks necessarily have investors who acquired the stock at a basis below the current price`), Bayesian learning about discount rates per Watkins 2002 (`consistent price increases tend to be those experiencing dissemination of their low discount rate type`), and volatility-as-risk (`Consistency can also proxy for the inverse of volatility`); the paper finds `winner consistency is important` at all three horizons with `46 basis points per month` marginal at one year while `Consistent losers have little impact`, and documents `December persistence` for losers with January bounceback plus high-turnover/small-stock concentration consistent with tax-loss selling. All mechanism framing fenced to the paper's 1966–1995 equity sample, never adopted as a crypto claim here.

### Research interpretation

Monthly-count-filtered momentum, long-only, one-month cadence. Unlike bare-sign momentum systems there is no formation level that alone triggers exposure: an 11-month gain built from 2 jump months prints flat here and long in JT/TSMOM — the position exists if and only if the gain arrived through at least 8 green monthlies. Unlike daily-composition records the statistic is 11 monthly closes, not ~365 daily signs: a formation with 8 green monthlies but a negative-day majority prints long here and discrete-flat in FIP. Unlike volume-conditioned records volume never appears anywhere: high-volume and low-volume 8-of-11 winners are treated identically. Unlike spike-avoidance records the statistic is a count, not an extremum: a formation with no outsized month but only 7 greens still prints flat. Unlike cost-basis/overhang records there is no reference price, no turnover weighting, and no holdings input. The bet, as derived, is purely that the documented steady-winner continuation ports to BTCUSDT monthly blocks — and the position absorbs full one-month drawdowns with no guardrail plus the paper's own spike-winner pain (missed inconsistent continuations).

## Signal

- Bar grid: completed `1d` bars of BTCUSDT only. Month membership is the UTC calendar date of each daily bar — deterministic on the 24/7 tape.
- Month-end close (pinned): `E(X)` is the close of the last completed daily bar dated in calendar month `X`. The first history month `2017-08` is a partial-month base (tape opens 2017-08-17, `E(2017-08)` = 2017-08-31 close 4724.89 verified live): it serves only as the predecessor for the September-2017 monthly return and its own monthly return is never computed (no predecessor — skipped, pinned).

Exactly zero (`R == 0`, measure-zero on live tape) does not count as positive — pinned.
- Formation for holding month `H` (pinned, skip-latest-month plus gap): the 11 calendar months `H-12 … H-2` (month `H-1` is the one-month gap, observed but excluded from the count — the declared port of the paper's skip for microstructure orthogonality). The derived posture is explicitly no stop, no target, no trailing, no opposite-signal exit, no calendar extension; a one-month adverse move inside a consistent-winner block is ridden in full by construction.

## Limitations

- Single-asset port: the paper's evidence is a cross-sectional equity study; BTCUSDT steady-winner behavior is unevidenced by the source and may differ arbitrarily — no performance is claimed here for exactly this reason.
- Mechanism mismatch: whatever disposition/learning clientele sits behind the equity effect has no established crypto analogue (24/7 global tape, concentrated venues, perpetual-funding holders, stablecoin quote, thin early history) — the port bets on regime persistence, not on a verified crypto mechanism.
- Binary-collapse rigidity: the fixed `>= 8 of 11` partition never adapts — a tape where every formation prints 7 greens goes nearly always-flat on winners, while a drift-dominated tape goes nearly always-long, both by construction.
- Month-definition rigidity: gap-month information is observed at the decision but excluded from the count by rule; a shock arriving across a month boundary is attributed to whole months; a first-of-month gap against the fresh regime is taken at the post-move open, never at the edge.
- Decile-diversification loss: the source's cross-sectional averaging and benchmark hedging are lost by declared derivation; replication disputes are decided against this record's pin, never patched silently.
- No price stop, no intra-month exit: adverse excursion inside the one-month holding block has no guardrail; the next decision is weeks away by construction.
- Warmup cost: everything before the September-2018 first holding month is flat by record rule, so the 2017–2018 tape is deliberately untraded — pinned, not recovered.
- Partial-base month: `E(2017-08)` rests on a 15-day partial month (tape opens 2017-08-17); it anchors only the September-2017 monthly return and never a position — pinned, not re-estimated.
