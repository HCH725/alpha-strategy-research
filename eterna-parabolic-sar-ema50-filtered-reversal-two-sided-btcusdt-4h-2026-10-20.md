## Economic mechanism

### Source-reported

Wilder's "Stop and Reverse" trailing-dot trend riding, optionally filtered to the main trend (strategy README: `"Stop and Reverse" indicator developed by J. Welles Wilder. Places dots above/below price that act as trailing stops. When price crosses the SAR, it signals a trend reversal and position flip`; `Exit: Automatic on opposite signal (always in market)`; `Optional: EMA filter to trade only with main trend`; Risk Warning: `High whipsaw risk in ranging markets! SAR is "always in" - it flips between long/short constantly. Use trend filter or only trade during established trends. Can have multiple small losses before catching big trend`). The SAR dot starts far from price and accelerates toward it as the trend extends (AF stepping 0.02 toward the 0.20 cap), so a close crossing through the dot reads as trend-exhaustion confirmation in a single event, and the EMA50 side condition vetoes flips that fight the medium trend. There is deliberately no stop, no target, no trailing order leg beyond the dot itself, no time exit, no `strategy.close` leg, and no pyramiding add anywhere — the opposite filtered flip is the entire exit path, by design gap rather than by hidden leg.

### Research interpretation

Always-in-tendency accelerating-stop reversal with a medium-trend veto — the only SAR record in the pool that filters flips through an EMA. Two pinned subtleties carry the economics, stated plainly. First, signals are EDGES, not states: `ta.crossover`/`ta.crossunder` fire once on the flip bar and then go quiet — a book correctly riding a multi-week trend fires nothing further (a same-side re-flip is impossible without an intervening opposite cross, and pyramiding-0 parity would no-op a re-fire anyway), so the system rides rather than compounds; a state machine keyed on `sarValue < close` would re-signal every trend bar. Second, there is NO flat state past seeding: with no `strategy.close` leg anywhere, the book exits a side only by reversing into the other — but unlike the pool's bare SAR twin, which reverses on EVERY flip, this machine sits through counter-trend flips that fail the EMA side condition, holding the old side flat-free until a qualified flip arrives. The veto is the whole bet: whipsaw cost concentrates in two places by construction — ranging markets where qualified flip pairs still alternate, and trending markets where an early counter-trend flip is correctly vetoed but a late qualified flip gives back open profit waiting for the dot to catch price. The trade is on 0.02-step accelerating reversal timing persisting with the 50-bar medium trend at frozen `0.02/0.02/0.2/50` geometry — not on any volume print, ADX certification, retuned AF pair, or disabled-filter variant.
