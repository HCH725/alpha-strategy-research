## Economic mechanism

### Source-reported

The source claims only what its code prints: buy the bar whose 10-bar window shows a bullish divergence (second-half price trough strictly below the first-half price trough while the second-half RSI trough prints strictly above the first-half RSI trough) with current RSI strictly below 30; stand back to flat on the bar whose RSI prints strictly above 70. The RSI is a Wilder-style EWM recursion (`alpha = 1/14`, `min_periods = 14`) on closes only. No stop, no target, no trailing, no time exit, no sizing, no cost basis ships anywhere — this record adopts no source-reported performance (see Evidence).

### Research interpretation

Oversold-exhaustion reversal timer, long-only, setup-gated, no veto beyond the gate itself. A divergence that prints while RSI has already recovered above 30 fires nothing (exhaustion-inside-the-zone only); an oversold RSI without the lower-low/higher-low trough pair fires nothing (no level-only entries). Unlike the pool's Pine pivot-divergence sibling (confirmed `ta.pivothigh`/`ta.pivotlow` prints on both price and RSI with register storage, two-sided, `4h` — confirmation-lagged pivots fire there on bars where this record's fixed 10-bar split-window troughs see nothing, and its short leg reverses where this record stands flat), flat here means only "no fresh trough-pair printed" (or "RSI out of zone"), never "pivot vetoed". Unlike RSI level/cross cousins (threshold holds, recrosses, washouts), there is no level-only trigger — chop that tags 30 without printing the trough pair holds flat. The failure mode is plain: a divergence that keeps diverging (trough pair prints, RSI stays under 30, price keeps bleeding) enters early with no stop; an uninterrupted all-gain ramp prints RSI NaN (zero-loss windows divide by a NaN-replaced zero) and never exits — disclosed, with the ramp-neutral behaviour empirically confirmed in Evidence R9.

## Signal

Exact rule as pinned (any other RSI period/lookback, price input, trigger polarity, zone level, frame, fill, book, or risk posture is a different, unpinned rule). `RSI_t` is the pinned Wilder-style recursion:

```text
RSI_t  = 100 - 100 / (1 + RS_t), RS = EWM_gain(1/14) / EWM_loss(1/14)   // min_periods 14, zero-loss -> NaN
W_t    = last 10 closes ending at bar t; H = 10 // 2 = 5
p1     = min close over W_t[0:5] (first occurrence on ties); p2 = min close over W_t[5:10]
r1     = RSI at p1's bar; r2 = RSI at p2's bar
div_t  = (p2 < p1) AND (r2 > r1)
ready  = positional length >= 26 AND RSI_t is not NaN
entry_t = div_t AND (RSI_t < 30) AND ready
exit_t  = (RSI_t > 70) AND ready
```

- Trade events: `entry_signal` on a divergence-plus-oversold bar, `direction` long-only; `exit_signal` on an overbought bar. A flat book enters one full unit (`0.001` BTC) on an entry bar; a long book exits the full unit on the first exit-leg bar.

## Limitations

- Ramp-no-exit by construction: an uninterrupted all-gain tape prints RSI NaN (zero-loss windows divide by a NaN-replaced zero — coded arithmetic, confirmed in R9), so the overbought exit may never fire and the book holds indefinitely — disclosed arithmetic profile.
- Late entries by construction: the RSI-14 EWM plus the 10-bar trough window lag price after any V-turn, so recoveries are entered strictly after the trough pair prints — disclosed lag profile.
- Chop whipsaw: alternating trough-pair prints with RSI dipping under 30 print repeated enter-then-exit round trips with no cooldown and no structural filter — disclosed chop profile.
- Diverging-divergence blindness: a trough pair that keeps diverging (price bleeds further while RSI stays under 30) enters early with no stop to front-run it — disclosed.
- No risk leg: no stop loss, take profit, ROI rung, trailing-stop order, or time limit exists in source — recorded as explicitly disabled; adverse excursions have no capital guardrail besides the overbought exit.
- Downtrend blindness: declines are watched from flat with no short participation — the zero-`short` posture is a stance choice with a return cost in sustained downtrends, disclosed.
- Single-market scope: derived pin is BTCUSDT spot `1d` only; portability to other symbols/timeframes is untested and claimed nowhere in this record.
- Research overlay dependence: fills, sizing, and costs follow the derived pins and the stated economics identity — not the source engine's own semantics, and not the engine summary labels fenced above.
