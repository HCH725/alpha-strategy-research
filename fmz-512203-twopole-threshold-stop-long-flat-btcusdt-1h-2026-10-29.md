## Economic mechanism

### Source-reported

The page prose presents a detrended, normalized, double-smoothed oscillator: subtract the 25-bar average, remove the residual's own 25-bar average, divide by its 25-bar standard deviation, then pass the z-like residual through two cascaded EMA legs ("two poles"). Long on an upward cross of −0.8, short on a downward cross of +0.8, flat again on the opposite cross or on a 10-point adverse close within 5 bars of entry. No source performance metric is adopted.

### Research interpretation

This is a **threshold-cross on a smoothed detrended z-score with a time-boxed close stop** — the traded object is not price but a twice-filtered stationarity residual, and the position survives only while no opposite cross prints and no early adverse close trips the boxed stop. The double-EMA cascade (total lag ≈ 2·(2/α−1)/2 ≈ 19 bars at α=2/21) makes the oscillator slow to turn, so entries arrive after the residual has already traveled far from −0.8; the 5-bar stop box then acts as a FAILED-breakout fuse, cutting exactly those late entries that do not follow through, while winners run under the opposite-signal exit only. The failure mode is plain: in chop the oscillator can hover near a threshold and print repeated cross entries after each boxed-stop exit, and a slow bleed that never prints a 10-point down-close inside the box stays exposed under the midline-free exit. No profitability is asserted.

## Signal

**Gate 1 — exact researcher-pinned arithmetic.** Index completed UTC hourly bars by `t=0,1,...`. Use real Binance BTC-USDT spot `O[t],H[t],L[t],C[t]`; only closes are read. A bar with missing/nonfinite/non-positive close is invalid; never interpolate.

1. Pinned inputs: `LENGTH=20`, `BUY_TRIG=-0.8`, `SELL_TRIG=0.8`, `STOP_PTS=10` (USDT price points).
2. `SMA25[t] = mean(C[t-24]..C[t])` for `t >= 24`, undefined before.
3. `D[t] = C[t]-SMA25[t]` for `t >= 24`; `DSMA[t] = mean(D[t-24]..D[t])` for `t >= 48`; `DEV[t] = D[t]-DSMA[t]`; `SD[t] = population-stdev(D[t-24]..D[t])` (divide by 25, Pine `ta.stdev` biased default) for `t >= 48`, requiring `SD[t] > 0`; `NORM[t] = DEV[t]/SD[t]`, undefined otherwise. No imputation: any undefined input yields no signal on that bar.
4. `alpha = 2/21`; `S1[t] = NORM[t]` at the first defined bar (`t=48`), else `S1[t] = (1-alpha)*S1[t-1]+alpha*NORM[t]`; `S2[t] = S1[t]` at `t=48`, else `S2[t] = (1-alpha)*S2[t-1]+alpha*S1[t]`; `OSC[t] = S2[t]`.
5. On simulator bar `t` **only after its close**, evaluate with the already-completed **previous** bar `u = t-1`. `isLong[u] = (OSC[u-1] <= -0.8 and OSC[u] > -0.8)`; `isShort[u] = (OSC[u-1] >= 0.8 and OSC[u] < 0.8)`; all four referenced values finite. Pine `ta.crossover`/`ta.crossunder` semantics pinned as `<=`/`>` and `>=`/`<` respectively. `isLong` and `isShort` cannot co-occur (thresholds disjoint). No current-bar `t` indicator may influence the action; `C[t]` is solely the modeled fill price and cash check.
6. If confirmed FLAT and not pending, `isLong[u]` queues one BUY of 0.001 BTC at simulated `C[t]` subject to warmup/affordability. The exit predicates are not consulted while FLAT.
7. If confirmed LONG and not pending: (i) `isShort[u]` queues one SELL of the confirmed 0.001 BTC at simulated `C[t]` (opposite-signal exit, checked first); (ii) else, if `f <= t-1 <= f+5` where `f` is the fill-bar index and `C[t-1] <= stopRef-10` with `stopRef` the modeled fill close, queues one SELL at simulated `C[t]` (boxed close stop, bar-close evaluated — never intrabar). Never reverse or re-enter on the same bar. The entry predicate is not consulted while LONG.
8. There is no take-profit, no trailing order, no time exit beyond the stop box, no safety order, no scale-out, no cooldown, no pyramiding, and no end-of-sample forced liquidation. An unclosed final position remains open, marked at the last observed valid price only. The stats accumulators, `prevOsc`, `tf`, and all six `plotshape` markers have no decision function.

**Gate 2:** LONG/FLAT on Binance BTC-USDT **spot**, leverage 1, short side explicitly disabled. **Gate 3:** complete threshold-cross entry, opposite-signal exit, boxed 10-point close stop, and intentionally absent further risk legs as specified above (verified absent in source: zero `strategy.exit`/`strategy.order`).

## Position, risk and state

State is `FLAT -> BUY_PENDING -> LONG -> SELL_PENDING -> FLAT` **only after an observed filled executor**, not on signal submission. The fixed size is a backtest design, not an assertion of exchange lot/min-notional viability. The stop box covers only bars `f..f+5`; gap risk across hourly closes inside and outside the box is real and undisguised.

## Limitations and adoption boundary

This is an independent derived hypothesis, not a faithful reproduction of the FMZ Pine source. It inherits a slow oscillator's whipsaw: the SMA-25 plus double-EMA stack means threshold crosses arrive late, so a range expansion can print long entries just as the residual mean-reverts, and the boxed stop then exits exactly those entries at a fixed 10-point loss while chop repeats the cycle. The z-normalization divides by trailing stdev, so identical price paths at different volatility levels produce different signals — volatility-regime dependence is real and undisguised. A completed-close MARKET simulator is a bounded research abstraction; real gaps across hourly closes bypass the modeled exit price with no slippage accounting. The 10-point box is tuned to BTC-scale prices and does not transfer to other symbols. No positive return, real trading viability, Qlib parity, OOS result, Survivor/HB Certified status, Paper/Testnet/Live authorization or deployment is asserted. Optional campaign coins/timeframes, DCA BO/SO 6/6/6 and futures leverage are not entry requirements and are not part of this derivative.
