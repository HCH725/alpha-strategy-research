## Economic mechanism

### Source-reported
Single-average envelope tracking: one 50-period EMA marks the slow consensus price, and symmetric ±0.3% rails around it form a hysteresis band — a close above the upper rail holds long (trend absorption), a close below the lower rail stands flat (trend failure), and closes inside the band hold posture (chop filter). The prose admits EMA lag at turning points, false signals in consolidation from the fixed-percentage rails, overfit risk, and the absence of any stop-loss (unported future work — adding stops, parameter optimization, confirmation filters, all disclosed unported). The stanza window 2023-01-17→2024-01-23 is a one-year 1d sample — cited as provenance, never as performance evidence.

### Research interpretation
Tight-envelope level holder, event-persistent, long-flat. Unlike cross-event EMA machines (dual-EMA-cross kin, `dual-ema-engulfing-volume` — average-vs-average crosses with volume gates, two-sided edges), this machine never computes a second average or a cross — entries and exits are close-vs-two-rails LEVEL states with an explicit dead-zone hold, and the 0.3% band buys hysteresis with width instead of confirmation lag. Unlike wide-band cousins (`ehma-range-band-breakout` — custom 3-call-site borserman exponential-Hull recursion period 180 with ±2% bands, spot, same-bar-close fills, 100%-equity compounding with 0.085% declared commission; `bll-trb-200`/`bll-vma-1-200` — 200-period percent bands, two-sided; `flying-dragon-offset-ma-band` — HMA35/SMA22 historical-offset levels, two-sided, same-bar-close, pyramiding 3), this machine uses the plain single-stage built-in EMA at period 50 with a 10×-tighter symmetric band on futures 1d with next-bar-open fills and fixed-unit sizing — different smoother family, different scale and width, different venue/timing/economics. Unlike oscillator-gated machines (RSI/CCI/Stoch/CMO kin), no oscillator ships anywhere — the only objects are one EMA and two multiplications. Five-axis distinction from the nearest pool cousin (EHMA range-band) holds on mechanism (plain single EMA50 vs custom Hull recursion), signal construction (±0.3% vs ±2% symmetric bands), horizon/venue (futures 1d next-bar-open vs spot 1d same-bar-close), exits/economics (id-specific close with researcher 0.1% fee and fixed 0.01 BTC vs 100%-equity compounding with declared 0.085% commission), and source identity (FMZ 439859 PingEMA50V.3 vs hasnocool EHMA Range Strategy).
