## Economic mechanism

### Source-reported

Coarse mean-reversion/momentum building blocks. The paper frames its universe coarsely: `one can think of alpha signals as based on mean-reversion or momentum`, where `a mean-reversion alpha has a sign opposite to the return on which it is based`, and adds that `in complex alphas elements of mean-reversion and momentum can be mixed`. Alpha#21 is a Bollinger-style band-fade block with a volume-tilted mid-band in one line: when the 2-bar mean prints above the 8-bar mean plus its 8-bar dispersion, the score is `-1` (fade the fast upside); when it prints below the 8-bar mean minus dispersion, the score is `+1` (fade the fast downside); inside the band the score follows the volume regime — at-or-above-average volume prints `+1`, below-average volume prints `-1`. The paper ships no behavioural, structural, or risk-premium mechanism beyond this measurement construction, and no stop, target, or risk leg of any kind.

### Research interpretation

Band-breach contrarian with a volume-regime mid-band default, two-sided, threshold-free, always-in-market. Unlike sibling worldquant-alpha7 (an `adv20 < volume` gate selecting between a 7-day time-rank contrarian leg and a `-1` short default) this rule never ranks anything: its band object is a mean-plus/minus-dispersion envelope and its mid-band clause is a bare volume-ratio comparison, with every defined bar resolving to exactly `+1` or `-1`. Unlike sibling worldquant-alpha12 (a 1-day bilinear product `sign(volume-change) × (−price-change)` with no window, no average, and no band) this rule's memory is 20 bars deep and its volume read is a regime default, not a signed multiplier. Unlike sibling worldquant-alpha6 (a single negated 10-day open-volume correlation with no branch at all) this rule has three mutually exclusive branches and no correlation. Unlike pool `alma-cross-volume-gate` (ALMA 60/120 trend cross gated by an EMA volume oscillator with fixed 2%/2.5% take/stop exits) there is no moving-average trend cross, no oscillator, and no stop/target of any kind. Unlike weekly-momentum or run-length records (liu-tsyvinski-1w Sunday-log momentum, double-seven Connors pullback channel, eight-day-run SMA5 run reversal) there is no calendar week, no Donchian-style channel, and no run counter — the memory objects are two trailing means, one trailing dispersion, and one trailing volume average. The failure mode is plain: the mid-band volume clause keeps the book permanently tilted (high-volume chop sits long, quiet drift sits short) with no guardrail, and a band breach into a genuine breakout fires the fade at the worst print of the move — disclosed, not upgraded.

## Signal

Exact rule as pinned (any other lookback, series, dispersion variant, gate, threshold, fill, frame, book, or tie treatment is a different, unpinned rule):

- Inputs (paper A.2): `close[t]`, `volume[t]` on completed `1d` bars — the kline prints as-is (base-asset volume, no quote conversion, no normalization). No `open`, no `high`, no `low`, no vwap/cap/sector, no cross-sectional rank, no second asset or timeframe is read by any order-gating line.
- Band (paper A.1, pinned impl lines 100–105): `mean8[t] = mean(close[t-7 .. t])`, `mean2[t] = mean(close[t-1 .. t])`, `sd8[t] = stddev(close[t-7 .. t])` with `ddof=1` per the pinned implementation (fenced in Negative evidence 5). Undefined before 8 consecutive finite closes exist, by the `_rolling` all-finite-block convention.
- Volume ratio (paper A.2, pinned impl lines 185–190 and 235–236): `adv20[t] = mean(volume[t-19 .. t])`, `ratio[t] = volume[t] / adv20[t]` with non-finite quotients (including any zero-`adv20` print) mapping to NaN — flat by the warmup rule, never traded. Paper-dollar definition fenced in Negative evidence 5; this record implements the pinned implementation literally.
- Score (paper Appendix A, pinned impl lines 325–330): `score[t] = -1.0` if `mean8[t] + sd8[t] < mean2[t]` (strict `<`: equality falls through); else `score[t] = +1.0` if `mean2[t] < mean8[t] - sd8[t]` (strict `<`: equality falls through); else `score[t] = +1.0` if `ratio[t] >= 1` (the paper's `(1 < ratio) || (ratio == 1)` — identical to `>=`), else `score[t] = -1.0`. Undefined (NaN-preserving per pinned `_if_else`) while any of `mean8/mean2/sd8/adv20/volume` is non-finite — flat by the warmup rule, never traded. The two breach branches are mutually exclusive by construction (`mean2 - mean8 > sd8 >= 0` and `mean8 - mean2 > sd8 >= 0` cannot co-fire), the chain is total, and every defined print is exactly `+1.0` or `-1.0` — there is no zero print and no flat branch once warmed.
- Direction mapping (source-grounded): `score[t] == +1` → long signal; `score[t] == -1` → short signal, per the §2 positive→long convention (the paper's own Alpha#101 worked example). The entry fills at the next bar's open, one single unit, only if the book does not already carry that side (see position rule).
- Exit: the first opposite signal reverses the full unit in one step (close plus opposite entry at the same next-bar-open fill) — reversal is the sole exit path by construction. Consecutive same-side bars extend one continuous hold, never re-enter. No flat signal exists once warmed, so there is no flatten-to-flat path. No stop-loss, no take-profit, no trailing, no time exit (the paper ships no risk leg — carried over exactly, disclosed in Provenance adaptation (4), never upgraded into a source claim).
- Direction: two-sided (long leg fires on lower-band breaches and high-volume mid-band bars; short leg fires on upper-band breaches and quiet mid-band bars). Shorts are native to USDT-M margin (no uptick rule, no borrow desk).
- Position rule (predeclared researcher pin): single-unit; an already-long book treats further long-signal bars as no-ops (no pyramiding, no averaging), and symmetrically for shorts. Re-entry re-arms on the next opposite signal only (no cooldown specified — explicitly none, not invented). Pre-first-signal bars are flat.

## Limitations

- No price stop, no time stop, no flat: the always-in-market book rides every adverse excursion with no guardrail before the opposite branch, by the carried-over paper design — the record's principal risk.
- Mid-band tilt bias: inside-band chop rests long whenever volume prints at or above average and short whenever quiet — the book leans with the volume regime rather than standing aside, disclosed.
- Breakout-fade exposure: a genuine directional breakout that holds outside the band keeps the fade leg pinned against the trend until the fast mean re-enters the envelope — the paper provides no breakout filter and this record invents none.
- Dispersion-collapse note: when the trailing 8 closes are near-constant, `sd8` approaches zero and the band collapses onto `mean8`, so microstructure noise around the mean flips the book — disclosed.
- Single-venue port residual: paper-regime U.S. equities vs Binance perpetual execution (prints, fees, funding, volume-definition per Negative evidence 5, dispersion convention per Negative evidence 5, margin mechanics beyond the house model) is unclaimed, disclosed.
- Performance evidence is absent: nothing here is calibrated, fitted, or tuned to any backtest.
- Author scope: the paper presents production formulaic alphas as research objects, not a vetted retail trading system — this record is research-only and claims no live suitability.
